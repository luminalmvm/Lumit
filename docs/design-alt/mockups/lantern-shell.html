Lumit — Lantern

A from-scratch shell on the Lyrica cues

Dark cards lit inside a light room, which is the reference's own arrangement. Every function in the UI spec is kept — this is the style meant to replace Round, and it is a whole style rather than a shape: the cards, the pills, the transport, the floating tool dock and the type are all its own. Night is the same design with the room turned down. Toggle the notes to see the structural moves numbered.

The structural moves

  1. The floating tool dock. Every tool from §1.7, in order and in groups, as round buttons in one vertical capsule floating in the Viewer card's margin — over the neutral surround, never over the picture. The armed tool is a filled accent circle. Flyouts open beside it; options open as a second capsule.
  2. The transport is one pill with a large accent play button at its centre — the Lyrica cue — and the clock is a capsule beside it. Loop, preview mode, quality, audio and the cache-ready bar sit on the same deck band. The Viewer bar above keeps only ways of looking.
  3. Cards in a room. Every pane is a dark card with an 18px radius, 12px apart, on a light room; the card's name and its header dot on a 40px line. The dock is untouched — cards still split, tab, float and drop exactly as panes do.
  4. The top band belongs to the room, not a card: the mark, the menus, a workspace pill with the fronted name filled, and the command palette as a pill at the right.
  5. Sections as sub-cards. The inspector is one list, but each section is a rounded sub-card with a toggle switch for enable, its cost, and its ×; selected takes an accent inset ring. Wells are capsules; a slider is an accent track with a white dot thumb.
  6. The playhead is a pin — a round accent head on a 2px line — and the work area is an accent capsule on the ruler with round handles. Cache runs are rounded segments; markers are dots with a pill label.
  7. Rows are pills too. Outline rows have a 10px radius and an avatar circle for the layer type; the selected row fills with the accent at 22%. Layer bars are capsules in the label colour; keys are the same three marks, softened.
  8. Readouts float. The status line, cache meters and frame cost live in one pill over the room's bottom-right corner rather than a strip.

The system under it

Room
Day: a cool light grey room around dark cards — the reference's own arrangement. Night: the same cards in a near-black room. The Viewer surround is #7f7f7f in both.
Accent
One violet-blue, spent on filled pills: the armed tool, the fronted workspace and mode, the play button, the selection, the playhead, the work area, Add effect and Export. Never a word on a card.
States
on · attention · fault as small marks; the header dot is decorative and never a status light.
Type
Plus Jakarta Sans for words, DM Mono for numbers. Card titles 12px semibold, body 11, pills 10.5, the clock 15. Sentence case; no caps, no kickers.
Shape
Cards 18, sub-cards and stages 12, rows 10, everything else a capsule. Controls are stadiums; buttons are circles.
Elevation
Real: a card casts a soft shadow into the room; a floating capsule casts a deeper one. No blur, no glass, no bevel.
Motion
This is the style where the spring lives: ≤150ms with the one signature overshoot on the drag ghost, and filled pills that slide between positions.
