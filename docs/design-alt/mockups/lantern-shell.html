Lumit, Lantern

Lumit's dark ramp, arranged as cards in a light room

Every function in the UI spec is still here: the dock, every tool, the Viewer bar's ways of looking, the transport, the whole outline, the inspector, the status line. What changed is the room the panels sit in and how each control is drawn. Every colour is Lumit's own: the dark ramp becomes cards floating in a light room, the spruce accent is spent on fills only, and the toolbar can stand along the top or down the left edge. Toggle the notes to see the structural moves numbered on the shell.

The structural moves

  1. The toolbar has two positions, and that is a new setting. Every tool from §1.7 is here in the spec's order and groups: six simple tools, a seam, the seven groups with their corner triangle, a seam, the snapping switch. Along the top it is a strip in the room under the top band with the tool options at its right; down the left it is a rail of rounded squares with snapping at the foot, and the options open from the armed tool instead. The armed tool is the lime square with a black glyph. Flyouts, shortcuts and the "shown but disabled" rule for unbuilt tools are unchanged.
  2. Cards in a room. Each pane is a near-black card with a 16px radius and a soft shadow, 10px from its neighbours, in a light room by day and a near-black one by night. No hairline separates panes; the room does. Docking is untouched.
  3. The card title is centred, uppercase, 10px, tracked, muted, with a small lime dot at the card's top-left corner. The pane's own controls stand at the left and right of that line as pills and rounded rects.
  4. The top band lives in the room. The mark, nine menu words in room ink, the workspace strip as a segmented pill with the fronted workspace filled lime, and the command palette as a dark pill at the right. On macOS the menus go to the system bar.
  5. The Viewer bar keeps only ways of looking. Magnification, resolution, the channel mark, board, view menu, 3D view, exposure, the snapshot pair, the colour pipeline and the degradation reading. Everything about playing went to the deck under the picture, which stays on a strictly neutral stage in both rooms.
  6. The transport is one pill with a lime play button at its centre and the four marks either side; the clock is the one big number, in mono, with its frame count. Loop mode, preview mode, quality, audio and the cache-ready meter sit beside it. This is §9's Preview panel given a fixed seat.
  7. A property row is stopwatch, navigator, a muted label in a fixed column, the control, the value in its own cell, the unit, and a reset arrow. Sliders are a thin dark track with a small pale thumb; a parameter with no range gets a field or a dropdown; a colour gets a swatch and a dropper. Icon rows carry Easing and Direction.
  8. Effect sections carry a toggle switch at the right of the heading with the render cost and the close mark, and are separated by air rather than sub-cards; the selected section is the one filled a step lighter. Add effect is the lime pill at the foot, Save preset a grey one.
  9. Outline rows are rounded fills on a 24px pitch: six switch glyphs, the number, the twirl, a coloured type avatar, the name, six mode glyphs, three rounded-rect pickers and the cost. The selected row is filled grey; nothing is stroked. The chrome row above holds the search field, shy and motion blur, and the three group toggles.
  10. Lanes are bright, labelled bars with near-square corners and pale fade wedges at the ends, on a slightly lighter lane ground that dims outside the work area. Under them a minimap compresses the whole composition with a lighter window over the visible span. The foot carries the zoom slider, the magnet and the scrollbar.
  11. The playhead is a lime pin with a round head, the work area a translucent lime band with round handles, the cache bar rounded runs with a gap, the marker a grey dot with a label pill.
  12. The bottom bar lives in the room. Two round buttons, a wide dark pill for the status message, a second pill with the vram, ram and disk meters and the frame cost, then the pill actions at the right.

The system under it

Room
Lumit Light's own canvas by day, surface_0 by night. The cards are surface_1 in both, and at night they keep a one-pixel edge so they still read. The Viewer stage is the same neutral surround as ever, bounded by a hairline rather than by value.
Cards
Nothing here is a new colour. A card is surface_1, a section inside it surface_2, a control surface_3, a pressed fill surface_4, and a lane well surface_0. The arrangement changed, the ramp did not.
Accent
Spruce, spent on fills: the armed tool, the fronted workspace, the play button, the selection chip, the playhead and work area, the keyed stopwatch, Add effect, Export. Its label is text_primary rather than surface_0, which is the one place this parts from 7.1, because near-black on spruce only reaches 3.7.
States
Lumit's own success, warning and error, each a small filled mark or badge. A two-state option fills its active half surface_4; a mode fills it spruce. Toggle switches for enable.
Type
Plus Jakarta Sans for words, DM Mono for every number. Card titles 10px uppercase tracked; labels 9.5 to 10 muted; values white; the clock 16 mono.
Shape
Cards 16, sub-cards 12, rows 8, dropdowns and fields and icon buttons 7, rail buttons 9 to 10, bars 3. Full pills only for actions, active halves and chips.
Elevation
A soft shadow under every card and every room control; nothing else casts. No blur, no glass.
Motion
At most 150ms, ease-out; a filled half slides between positions when a segmented pill changes. Nothing animates at rest.

Specimen data is invented. Every control named in docs/07-UI-SPEC.md §1.7, §2.2, §3, §4.1 to 4.3, §6, §9 and the status line is on this shell; what moved is stated in the notes, and the written system is docs/design-alt/15-DESIGN-LANTERN.md. Effects & presets and Scopes are tabbed behind the inspector in this arrangement, as in the shipping Edit preset.
