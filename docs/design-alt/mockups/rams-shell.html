Lumit — the grey room

A from-scratch shell on Rams' ten principles

Every function in the UI spec is still here — the dock, every tool, both Viewer strips' worth of controls, the whole outline, the graph, the inspector, the status line. What changed is where each thing sits and how it is drawn: controls are placed by how often the hand goes to them, the way a Braun panel is laid out, and everything stands on a 4px module. Toggle the notes to see the structural moves numbered on the shell.

The structural moves

  1. The tool rail. Every tool from §1.7 stands in a 44-wide column down the left edge, in the spec's order and groups, with flyouts and shortcuts unchanged. Each cell is 44×44 — the full hit target the horizontal strip gave up down the page. The armed tool wears a 2px index on its edge, like a Braun switch. Snapping sits at the foot.
  2. The deck. Transport, clock and frame count, loop mode, preview mode, quality, audio, the cache-ready meter move off the Viewer bar into one 32px instrument strip under the picture — §9's Preview panel, given a fixed seat. The clock is the one big number in the application.
  3. Bare panes, engraved labels. No tab bars in the default arrangement: each pane's name is a 9px lowercase label in its top-left corner, and the pane's own controls sit on the same 24px line. Docking is untouched — right-click a pane, or Window ▸.
  4. The Viewer bar keeps only ways of looking. Magnification, resolution, channel, board, view menu, 3D view, exposure, the snapshot pair, the colour pipeline and the degradation reading. Everything about playing went to the deck.
  5. The menu bar and workspaces share one 28px line with the wordmark and the command palette. On macOS the menus go to the system bar and the line keeps the rest.
  6. The inspector is one list. Transform, then one section per effect, exactly as §6 rules — but with the section heading carrying its enable tick, reset, render cost and close mark in one row, and the value column at a fixed edge.
  7. The outline rests pared down. The row is switches · number · name · modes · three pickers · ms, on a 24px module, each control a bare glyph on the row with no box round it. The foot's toggles hide the groups by name; the ease strip and the measuring clock live there too.
  8. The ruler is an engraved scale. Four-frame ticks, a taller tick every second and a labelled one every four, on the upper half; the work area, markers and cache bar on the floor. The playhead is the one orange index and its head is the grab. Outside the work area the ground steps darker — nothing is painted in.
  9. The dial. The lane zoom is a real dial with a tick scale and an index, beside its own reading. The one control this style proposes that the canonical language has no counterpart for.
  10. The readout strip. The status line, the three cache meters, the frame cost and the dropped-frame count: a 20px row of engraved readouts, and the measuring switch beside the column it fills.

The system under it

Room
Braun light grey, warm-neutral, never white. Graphite is the same design at the dark end. The Viewer surround is #7f7f7f in both.
Signal
One orange: the playhead, the armed tool's index, the fronted tab's rule, the selection edge, the focus ring, the one filled action. Never a word.
States
on · attention · fault, each a small mark. No amber; a keyed property is a filled stopwatch and a mark on its lane.
Type
IBM Plex Sans throughout, tabular figures on. Labels 9px lowercase, body 11, wells 11, the deck clock 15. Nothing bold.
Module
4px. Six heights: 16 / 20 / 24 / 28 / 32 / 48. Top bar 28, deck 32, rail cells 44, readout 20.
Radius
2 everywhere; full only where a control is genuinely round.
Elevation
Hairlines. Shadow 0/1/3 at 14% on menus and modals only.
Motion
≤100ms ease-out, no springs, no overshoot. Nothing animates at rest; an idle panel issues no repaint.

Specimen data is invented. Every control named in docs/07-UI-SPEC.md §1.7, §2.2, §3, §4.1–4.3, §6, §9 and the status line is on this shell; what moved is stated in the notes, and the written system is docs/design-alt/15-DESIGN-RAMS.md. Effects & presets and Scopes are tabbed behind the inspector in this arrangement, as in the shipping Edit preset.
