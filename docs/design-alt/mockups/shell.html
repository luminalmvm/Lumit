One markup, three design languages, two rooms each

The same panel tree — Project, Viewer, Effect controls, Timeline — drawn three times. Every difference you can see is a token value or a grammar switch; no panel is laid out twice. Style 1 is the shipping language in docs/15-DESIGN.md; styles 2 and 3 are the envisionings in this folder. The page's own surround is a fixed neutral grey and ignores your system theme, because three palettes are being judged against each other and a ground that moved would move the judgement.
