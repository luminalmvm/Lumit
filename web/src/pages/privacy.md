Lumit is an editor that runs on your computer. Your projects and footage stay there unless
you send them somewhere. This page covers the two things that do talk to a server of ours:
a Lumit account, and Lumit Pro.

If you never sign in, we hold nothing about you. Lumit is run by Mackenzie Reed, who is
responsible for what is kept.

## What we keep

- **Your email and your name.** The name is the one other people see.
- **How you sign in.** For Discord or Google that is their number for your account and the
  address of your picture. For a password it is a scrambled form that can't be turned back
  into the password, which never leaves your computer.
- **Where you are signed in.** The name of each computer, so that you can sign out of one.
- **Your subscription.** Whether you have Pro, when it renews, and Paddle's numbers for
  you and for it. Never your card.
- **The settings you sync.** Appearance, shortcuts, panel layout, preferences, and what you
  have saved, for the kinds you leave ticked in Settings.
- **Review links you make.** The video you sent, a picture of it, the comments left on it
  with the names people typed, and when it was opened and under which name.
- **Backups of your projects.** Each saved project file, a small picture of it, and the name
  of the computer that saved it. A project file holds no footage unless you packed it.
- **Projects you keep online.** These are sealed on your computer with a key that comes
  from the invite, which we are never given. We hold the sealed project, its edits and its
  footage, and can see how much there is and when it was last opened. We can't see inside.

## What we never see

- Your projects, your footage, or anything you export, unless you send it as a review link
  or a backup.
- What passes through the relay, or what is in a project kept online. Both are encrypted
  with the invite's key before they leave your computer, and we are never given that key.
- Your card. Paddle take the payment and only tell us that it happened.

## What a server notices anyway

Any server sees the internet address of whoever connects to it. Ours uses yours to limit
how fast sign-ins can be tried, and forgets it within the hour. The relay sees which
addresses are connected through it and how much they send, and keeps no record once they
leave. A review link uses the address of whoever opens it to count one opening an hour and
to limit how fast comments can be left, and forgets it within two days.

## If you comment on someone's link

You need no account. The name you type and what you write are kept with the link until its
maker deletes it or the link. You can delete your own comment from the browser you wrote it
in. Your browser remembers your name on your own device, and we set no cookies for it.

This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers.

## Who else is involved

- **Cloudflare** run the servers and send the emails with your sign-in codes.
- **Paddle** sell Pro on our behalf and take the payment. They hold what you give them
  under [their own privacy policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).
- **Discord** or **Google**, only if you choose to sign in with them. They tell us your
  email, your name, and your picture, and nothing else.

## Keeping and deleting

We keep an account until you delete it. **Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account** in Lumit
removes it straight away: the account, the synced settings, every review link, backup and
project kept online, every sign-in, and it ends any subscription. A sign-in that isn't used
for 60 days expires on its own.

Review links, backups and projects kept online can each be deleted on their own from
Lumit. Backups thin out by themselves: every save is kept for a week, then one a day, then
one a week, and a version you have named is kept until you delete it.

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, or for a correction, at
[hello@lumitlab.com](mailto:hello@lumitlab.com).
