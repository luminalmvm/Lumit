## Lumit for iPhone

**Pairing.** In Lumit on your computer, open Settings ▸ Account ▸ Add a phone and scan the code with your
iPhone's camera. A code lasts five minutes.

**Exports.** Lumit has to be open on the computer, with the project open. A computer that's asleep shows as
offline.

**Starting exports from a phone.** Turn it off on the computer in Settings ▸ Account ▸ Devices.

**Lumit Pro.** Exports and your own previews need Pro. Pro bought in the app is managed in your iPhone's
Settings, under your name ▸ Subscriptions.

**Deleting your account.** Devices ▸ Delete account in the app, or Settings ▸ Account in Lumit. Pro bought in
the app is cancelled in Settings, not by deleting the account.
