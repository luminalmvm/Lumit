If Pro isn't for you, ask within 14 days of a payment for a full refund, no questions asked.

## How to ask

Email [hello@lumitlab.com](mailto:hello@lumitlab.com) from the address on your account, or
use the link on the receipt Paddle sent you. Refunds may take a few days to appear in your account once requested.

## Cancelling is not a refund

Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps Pro until that date. 
You can do it yourself from **Manage subscription** in Lumit.
