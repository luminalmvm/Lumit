If Pro isn't for you, ask within 14 days of a payment and you get all of it back. That
covers the first payment and each renewal, and you don't have to say why.

## How to ask

Email [hello@lumitlab.com](mailto:hello@lumitlab.com) from the address on your account, or
use the link on the receipt Paddle sent you. Paddle send the money back the way it was
paid, which can take a few days to show.

## After a refund

Pro ends when the refund is made. Your account and your settings on each machine stay,
and everything in the free editor works as it did.

## Cancelling is not a refund

Cancelling stops the next renewal and leaves Pro on until the end of what you have paid
for. You can do it yourself from **Manage subscription** in Lumit.
