3D models have come to Lumit, with materials, shadows, animation, and Blender files, along with 3D shapes, extruded text, an Environment light, volumetric light, and smoke and fire. Particulate now runs on the graphics card with up to a million particles, the whole interface has had a small update, and Lumit can now run in the browser.

Several of these change how an existing project can look, so make sure to check the Before you update section.

## What is new

### 3D models

- glTF, GLB, FBX, and OBJ files import as 3D models, and drop into a comp as a **Model layer**, lit by the comp's lights and passing through other 3D layers by depth.
- .blend, USD, Alembic, PLY, STL, and Collada files come in through Blender, if it's installed. Saving the .blend converts it again, and **Edit in 3D app** opens it.
- Models cast and take shadows. A **Holdout** switch hides the CG behind something in the footage.
- Each material in a model has its own row, from Base colour and Roughness to Transmission for glass, with presets such as Chrome, Gold, and Frosted glass. A layer of the comp can be a material's picture, so a video can play on a phone screen.
- Animations in glTF and FBX files play on the layer, with Take, Loop, and Offset rows. **Parts** lists the model's parts, each with its own Transform rows.
- **Layer ▸ Create cameras and lights** makes layers from the cameras and lights a file holds.
- **Layer ▸ Project from camera** projects footage onto a model from a Camera layer, and **Layer ▸ New ▸ Card** makes a plane to project onto.
- **Scene pass** hands a model's or a 3D precomp's Normals, Position, and Part id to the next effect, and **Part matte** cuts a layer down to the parts you pick.
- Each view shows models as Final, Shaded, Wireframe, Shaded with wire, or Normals, and Shift+Z switches Wireframe on and off.

### 3D shapes and extruded text

- A cube, sphere, cylinder, cone, torus, plane, or pyramid can be made with the 3D shape tool or **Layer ▸ New ▸ 3D shape**, with keyable size, segments, bevel, and slice.
- Text and Shape layers have **Geometry options**, with extrusion depth, bevels, and Front, Bevel, Side, and Back materials.
- Text animators on extruded text have Position z, Rotation x, and Rotation y.

### Light, smoke, and fire

- A new **Environment light** lights the scene from an HDR or EXR panorama, footage, or a precomp, with reflections on models and a Reflection material option on 3D layers.
- Lights have a **Volumetric** row, which shows them in the air as a beam, a glow, or a soft haze. 3D layers and models cut shafts through it.
- **Smoke and fire** simulates smoke and fire on the graphics card, and .vdb volumes open as Model layers.

### Particles

- Particulate is simulated on the graphics card, with up to a million particles, and **Bake** saves its frames.
- **In the scene** puts the particles in the comp's 3D space, lit and focused like a 3D layer, with Box, Sphere, and Layer emitters. A Model layer can be the emitter, or the shape of each particle.
- **Depth points** draws footage as 3D points from its depth.

### New effects

- **Film** shoots the picture on one of 17 film stocks and prints it on one of 4, with halation, bloom, grain, gate weave, and flicker.
- **Timewarp** retimes footage, precomps, and adjustment layers as an effect.
- **Jolt** adds sudden hits of blur, colour, light, scale, slide, and time, at random or on markers and beats.
- **Energy line** draws a hot core of light with a glow along a line, a mask, a shape's paths, or a title's letters.
- **Luma fade** takes a layer away in order of brightness, and built-in presets now show on the effect menu.
- **Audio spectrum** draws a sound as bars, a line, or a ring.
- Shake has Channels and Crop groups, Mood lighting has Digital and Streaks styles, and Radial blur has a Rays type.

### Interface

- Every surface has a right-click menu.
- Rows rest quiet at their defaults, and the lines that explained things are gone.
- Tool bars, tab strips, and splits make room instead of overflowing on smaller screens.
- The first time setup asks for a workspace, Edit, Cut, or Nodes, with Lumit's own choices marked for everything else.
- **Drive with** sits on every row, properties link by dragging, and Effect controls and the Node panel can filter their rows.
- The Grade panel can save a grade as a look, copy and paste a grade, and pick a neutral for white balance. Scopes have an RGB parade.
- **Compare** sets the Viewer against a snapshot or another comp.
- Every row in Settings resets to its default from its right-click.

### Windows, styles, and languages

- Right-clicking the Viewer's tab has **Pop out**, which moves it into its own window for a second monitor.
- Resting on the maximise button opens Windows 11's snap layouts.
- Styles can bring their own fonts, make the window see-through, and be shared as a .lumstyle.
- Icons drawn in more than one colour keep their colours.
- Arabic reads right to left.

### Audio and Cut

- The Audio timeline has buses and sends. **Insert bus** is on the right-click under the tracks, and **Output** and **Send to** are on a track name's right-click.
- The master measures loudness and true peak, and sound can be normalised.
- In Cut, clips can ramp, hold, and freeze, and take a wipe at an edit point. Titles and solids can be put in from the Cut timeline.
- Solids drag onto Cut and the Timeline, Cut shows where a drag will land, and files dropped from outside Lumit go where they're let go.
- Ctrl+D duplicates in every workspace, and the default transition moves to Ctrl+Shift+D.

### Node graph, drivers, and keys

- Shift and a right-drag across wires puts a reroute point on each, and wire ties now work on a Custom shader's graph.
- Boxes can be coloured and collapsed.
- A precomp of a node graph shows the graph's Inputs in Effect controls and the Timeline, and a picked box's keys show under the Timeline's ruler.
- Value and Time drivers, a Drivers menu, a Clone deformer, and more points effects (thanks to @andrea68git).
- The acceleration graph has handles, and typing an acceleration on a key can lay a Speed profile or Gravity motion (thanks to @andrea68git).
- Motion path handles drag in the Viewer, and points an expression drives show where it puts them.
- Ctrl+D in Effect controls duplicates the picked effects.

### Browser and Intel Macs

- Lumit runs in the browser at [web.lumitlab.com](https://web.lumitlab.com), all on your own machine.
- Intel Macs have their own download option.

### Smaller things

- Flow works on Precomp layers.
- Camera track has a Focal length row, for footage shot on a known lens.
- The Frames row works on node graph boxes, Camera track, Planar track, and Track points.
- Depth, Remove background, Roto brush, and tracker results are sent to everyone in a shared project, so nobody runs them twice.
- A .psd's adjustment layers come in switched on where they match.
- .aep projects bring more effects across, onto Luma fade, Timewarp, Energy line, and Jolt.
- Scripts and MCP can read and write every part of a layer or comp, with `layer.read`, `layer.write`, and `comp.write`.
- Settings ▸ Addons no longer asks to load the model runtime, which loads itself.

## Performance

- Particulate runs on the graphics card, so a million particles simulate much faster.
- Orbiting round a large model in a 3D view is smoother.
- The Viewer draws faster on integrated graphics cards.

## Fixed

- Keys moved as a group on a 3D layer snapped back, and moving it with the arrows made keys the Timeline didn't show.
- A precomp shown at two times in one frame drew the same frame at both.
- Roto brush mattes came back grey across the frame.
- See-through parts and soft edges in a precomp came out darker than the same layers drawn flat.
- Glow and the other effects that spread light showed a faint box behind each letter of a Text layer.
- Shake kept twisting and zooming when its Amplitude was keyed down to 0.
- Some OFX plugins failed every frame.
- Points and 3D handles an expression drives were missing from the Viewer, or did nothing when dragged.
- Double-clicking a preset or theme file did nothing.
- Pan/Crop started off-centre on clips that weren't the comp's size.
- Reversing eased keys bent the curve.
- Easing a layer style's keys from the Timeline did nothing.
- Rows that changed nothing still left an undo step.
- The Viewer could keep showing an old frame.

## Before you update

- Roto brush cut-outs have a harder edge, and are worked out again the first time the project opens.
- A Shake with its Amplitude keyed down to 0 now settles to a still picture, where it kept twisting and zooming.
- See-through parts and soft edges inside a precomp look lighter, and now match the same layers outside it. Motion blur trails lighten too.
- A precomp used twice at different times now shows the right frame in each place.
- An effect that uses a Precomp layer, as a matte or a map for instance, now sees that layer's own effects too.
- 3D layers inside a collapsed precomp are now lit by the lights around it.
- A layer set to only take shadows, or with Accepts lights off, now catches shadows even when facing away from the light.
- Corner pin and Bezier warp in a node graph are no longer cut off at the edge of the picture.
- The faint box behind each letter of a Text layer with Glow on it is gone.
- A Precomp layer using Flow now makes the frames in between.
- Some EXR files saved with PIZ compression can look slightly different.
- Shape layers stay sharp when scaled up, and their outlines stay even.
- Math points set to Rose no longer jumps at a whole Denominator.

## Known gaps

- A bus can't feed another bus yet.
- Particulate's Trails don't run on the graphics card yet, and Collide with, flocks, and sparks grey out in the scene.
- A model's materials don't change its shadow, so glass casts a whole shadow.
- The Environment light doesn't cast shadows yet.
- Smoke and fire, and Depth points, don't take motion blur yet.
- Warp, Twirl, and Spherize still stay inside the picture they're handed in a node graph.
- A node graph precomp from an older project shows no Inputs rows until one is edited.
- Flow on a Precomp layer is slower than on footage.
- Lumit in the browser needs WebGPU.
- Intel Macs have no model runtime, so Depth, Remove background, and the other model packs aren't offered there.
