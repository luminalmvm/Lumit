Lumit can now caption speech, edit by transcript, cut multicam, grade in a Colour workspace, mix through a live Mixer, take noise out of pictures and sound, and steady a shaky shot. It also has graphics templates, a transitions pack, a library shared between your computers, and exports that another of your computers can do.

A few of these change how an existing project can look or sound, so make sure to check the Before you update section.

## What is new

### Cut

- **Captions from speech.** Layer ▸ Audio ▸ Create captions and makes a caption layer. Captions follow the layer's clips, trims, and Retime. In the Captions panel they can be retyped, retimed, split, joined, and found and replaced, with seven styles. SRT, WebVTT, Timed Text, ASS, and LRC import, and SRT, WebVTT, Timed Text, and text export. The speech model is an addon, from Settings ▸ Addons.
- **Editing by transcript.** The Transcript panel shows what the comp says. Deleting, moving, or copying words cuts the comp to match. The Fillers and Pauses bars find hesitations and long gaps, Assemble from script lays a script's lines down in order, and Export transcript writes text, Word, rich text, Markdown, a web page, CSV, SRT, or WebVTT.
- **Multicam.** New multicam comp syncs angles by sound, timecode, marks, markers, or starts, and the angle viewer cuts to an angle as it plays. A cut's angle can be switched, rolled, joined, or flattened, and Sync to angle lines a camera up again.
- **Transitions pack.** New Move, Slices, and Light leak effects, hits such as Flash, Dip to black, Glitch, and RGB split, and a Pattern row on Luma fade. Pushes, whips, zooms, and spins now move the incoming clip too. The Transition menu is grouped into Wipes, Moves, and Hits, and **Layer ▸ Transition** puts them between two overlapping layers.

### Colour

- A **Colour workspace** beside Edit, Cut, and Nodes, for comp layers and Cut clips. **Correction** is a whole grading node in one effect, with wheels, log and HDR wheels, curves, hue curves, a qualifier, and four windows.
- Nodes run in serial, in parallel, or as layers, and shots keep versions, groups, and memories. A gallery of stills compares by wipe, split, difference, mix, or grid.
- Auto balance, shot and still match, and a colour chart solver. Window tracking, a matte view, CDL in and out, the Colour warper, and LUT export.
- Scopes hold several instruments, with a CIE plot. Tangent panels and MIDI controllers can drive the grade.
- The Lift, Gamma, and Gain wheels drag in any direction, at a fifth of the pointer's speed. Shift or Ctrl gives a twentieth.
- In Cut, the Grade panel and Copy and Paste grade act on the picked clip, not its whole layer.

### Sound

- The mix is now a live graph of strips, buses, and the Master. Faders, pans, and sends are heard while they move, and a reverb rings on across a cut. A bus can feed another bus, and the Master ends in a true-peak limiter.
- A full **Mixer** panel, with Read, Touch, Latch, Write, and Trim automation recorded while the comp plays.
- A layer's Gain is separate from Volume. A comp can have a loudness target, and an export can aim at one.
- Five sound clean-up effects: Noise reduction, Hum removal, Click removal, De-esser, and Reverb reduction. **Voice isolation** keeps speech, with the DeepFilterNet3 addon.

### Effects

- **Denoise** takes noise out of footage, learned from the flattest patch at the playhead. 
- **Stabilise** takes the shake out of a shot, or adds it on another layer. 
- **Mesh warp** bends any kind of layer with a grid that drags in the Viewer (thanks to @andrea68git).
- **Colorama** and **DisplaceX** are new as well (thanks to @andrea68git).
- Echo, Motion blur, Timewarp, and Posterize time follow the layer's own motion.
- Time loop can loop the layer it is on, and Particulate has a **Composite on original** switch.
- Gradients have a Blend row, and Gradient has a Paint row that can keep it inside the layer.

### Graphics and export

- **Graphics templates.** A comp can expose rows as controls, so one design gives many copies, each with its own values. Templates save as .lumt files, and a CSV, TSV, or JSON file can feed them rows.
- **File ▸ Export as Lottie or SVG** writes Lottie JSON, dotLottie, or an animated SVG, and checks what will not come across for the players you pick. Lottie files import too.
- WebP, QuickTime Animation, and VP9 with alpha join the export dialogue, with VBR and CBR bitrates.
- **Also write** puts up to three extra files beside the main export, such as a ProRes file or a PNG sequence.
- With Lumit Pro, **Export on** sends an export to another of your computers. Only the footage it lacks is sent, and the finished file can come back or stay there.

### Library and windows

- **Window ▸ Library** keeps presets, node groups, styles, themes, fonts, shortcut sets, lenses, flares, export presets, LUTs, and templates in one place. With Lumit Pro it is the same on every computer, with history, Restore, and Recently deleted for 30 days.
- Any panel can open in a window of its own, from its tab menu or by dragging the tab off the window. **Window ▸ Video monitor** shows the Viewer's picture alone on a second display.

### Smaller things

- Text layers have variable font axes, and text animators have Range, Proximity, and Wiggly selectors (thanks to @andrea68git).
- A handle in the Graph editor can reach past the neighbouring key.
- In the Graph editor a middle-button drag pans, and with Ctrl it zooms. The colour picker has an eyedropper (thanks to @andrea68git).
- Flow on a Precomp layer has an Engine row, Built in or RIFE.
- The Viewer's deck has switches for Shown size, Wireframe drags, and Suspend updates, and Caps Lock suspends the Viewer.
- The status line says Power saving while a laptop is on battery.
- The welcome screen shows a waiting update, and the update button shows its progress. With no project open, the Viewer lists recent projects.
- Ctrl+V pastes a picture copied in a browser, a screenshot tool, or a file manager. It imports as footage and, with a comp open, goes on a new layer at the playhead.
- 259 new Simplified Chinese strings and 32 rewordings, thanks to @lc01n.

## Performance

- Video decodes on the graphics card on Linux, with NVDEC on NVIDIA and VA-API on AMD and Intel.
- Text, shapes, and stills no longer redo their work every frame, so they play and drag much faster.
- **Decode footage at shown size**, in Settings ▸ Preview and cache, decodes footage for the preview no larger than it is shown. It is on by default, and exports still decode at full size.
- **Wireframe while dragging** and an **Adaptive resolution limit** keep drags quick on a slower machine.
- Under memory pressure, Lumit gives back half of its frame cache.
- OFX plugins start when they are first used, so Lumit uses less memory with many installed.

## Fixed

- The Linux AppImage would not start on a machine without WebKitGTK. Only extension panels need it now.
- The Cut razor did nothing on an uncut footage layer, or on its sound row. It now makes the layer a Sequence layer and cuts it.
- Roto brush edges were rough on 4K footage, and its card stayed on Not stroked yet after strokes were drawn.
- Reset on a 3D layer's Scale set Scale z to 0 and flattened it.
- A comp made from footage shorter than 30 seconds opened 30 seconds long. It is now as long as its longest footage.
- A Transform effect's anchor landed off-centre when scaling a layer that was not the comp's size. A new effect now starts centred on its layer.
- The Vegas preset left Cut's select tool with no key. It is on Shift+V.
- The clip colour menu in Cut opened as wide as the window.
- Settings ▸ Addons offered addons the build could not read, and refused them after the download.
- Closing Lumit on Windows could leave it running out of sight.
- GIFs over 50 fps played at the wrong speed.
- Settings ▸ Account failed for a Pro account.
- Easings added on two computers lost one.
- Lens flare's Lights mode ignored the camera, so the flare stood still as the camera moved. It now follows the camera, and an area light's size follows its distance.
- Retyping centred or right-aligned point text kept its left edge still, so a centred title drifted as it grew. It now grows from the side it is lined up on.
- A preset with text animators moved the words one text size down and to the right on text that had none.
- The Text tool opened a text layer that was not on screen at the playhead, and the click made no new layer. Only text on screen counts now.
- A U pressed a moment after another opened the same rows again. It now shuts what the last U opened, and a quick double tap is still UU.
- After the Audio timeline had been used, Delete and Copy could do nothing on a picked effect in Effect controls.
- Dragging a file over the Timeline could draw an error over the Sound mix lane, and threw the lanes back to the start of the comp.
- The Timeline's zoom strip showed the wrong window after a fit or a zoom, until the pointer touched it.
- Scrubbing could be heard but the Mixer's meters stayed at -inf. They now read what a scrub plays.

## Before you update

- The default video export is now tagged Rec. 709, not sRGB.
- Soft-edged wipes between footage and solid clips in Cut were dimmed twice and no longer are, so they look lighter.
- Echo, Motion blur, Timewarp, and Posterize time now take in the layer's own motion and its parents', so a moving layer with one of them can look different.
- A mix that went over the Master's ceiling was clipped. The limiter now holds it under, so a loud mix can sound different.
- Text layers stay sharp when scaled up.
- Round corners on a Clone deformer measures Radius along each side, so rounded corners can change size.
- Roto brush mattes are worked out again the first time the project opens, and edges on frames over about 2880 pixels can change.
- Lens flare in Lights mode now places its lights through the comp's camera, so a comp with a camera can look different.
- With Caps Lock on, the Viewer stops updating and a red bar says so. **Caps Lock suspends Viewer updates** in Settings ▸ Preview and cache turns this off.
- On Linux, **Hardware video decoding** now works and is on. If importing video freezes or fails, turn it off in Settings ▸ Preview and cache.
