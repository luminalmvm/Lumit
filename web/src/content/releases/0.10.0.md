3D has had a big overhaul. You get lights that cast real shadows, materials, extra 3D views beside the Viewer, and camera tools that move the way you'd expect from After Effects. Shape layers now have modifiers and groups, there are new and rebuilt panels for easing, aligning, and keeping notes, and the default workspaces have been refreshed.

Some of this changes how existing projects look, mostly 3D comps and shape layers, so it's worth a quick read of **Before you update** first.

## What's new

### 3D, properly

- **Lights cast shadows.** 3D layers throw shadows onto each other, and each one has **Material options** to set how it takes light and whether it casts or catches shadows.
- **Move things right in the Viewer.** 3D handles and rotation rings work on layers, cameras, and lights, with Local, World, and View axes in the tool options.
- **See your scene from any angle.** Open a 3D viewport beside the Viewer, or set new panes to Top, Front, or Right. Each one remembers its own view.
- **Get around like you're used to.** Orbit, dolly, track, and fly with Alt, as in After Effects. F10 to F12 jump between saved views, and **Look at selected layers**, **Look at all layers**, and **Reset 3D view** are in the View menu.
- **Layers sit where they should.** Nearer 3D layers draw in front and cut through each other where they cross, whatever their order in the Timeline. A comp with no camera gets a default one.
- **Real depth of field.** Each part of a 3D layer blurs by its own distance from the camera, as much as it would in After Effects.
- **3D in expressions.** toComp, fromComp, lookAt, Orientation, and the rest of the 3D conversions are all there.
- **More to work with.** 3D layers have Orientation, Anchor Z, and Scale Z, the arrow keys nudge a 3D layer in z, and lights get their own rows in the Timeline.

### Shapes you can keep building on

- **Modifiers and groups.** Repeater, trim, offset, twist, deform, and smooth now sit in a shape layer's contents and act on the shapes above them. Stack them (a repeater under a repeater makes a grid) and group shapes together.
- **Expand copies** turns a repeater's copies into real shapes you can edit one by one.
- **Change shapes after you draw them.** Rectangles, ellipses, polygons, and stars keep their size, roundness, and points as numbers.
- **Animate more.** Colours, outline width, and opacity can all be keyed, and you can add or take away a fill or outline whenever you like.
- **Bring in Illustrator art.** **Layer ▸ Create ▸ Shapes from vector layer** turns an Illustrator layer into shapes, and **Split into shape layers** gives each letter, path, or group a layer of its own.
- **Draw straight into a shape layer.** With one selected, a new shape goes into it.

### Panels that do more

- **A rebuilt Easing panel.** Built in and Yours tabs, and you can import and export your own easings to share or back them up.
- **Align panel.** Line layers up in a click, and Align in the Viewer can use any point of a layer's box.
- **Notes panel.** Your notes live inside the project, so they travel with the file.
- **Better default workspaces.** The built-in layouts make more use of stacks, so more panels are a click away.
- **Acceleration graph** in the Graph editor.

### Smaller things you'll notice

- **Multi-edit** is the new name for shared projects. Add friends in **Settings ▸ Account** and send them previews and Multi-edit invites directly.
- **Particulate** gets Trails, flocking, and sparks on death, and colliders can sit anywhere in 3D.
- **New effects.** Vector blur, a Lines type for Grid, and Repeat start and Repeat end in node graphs.
- **Animated GIF export.**
- **Hardware exports on Linux** with VAAPI on AMD and Intel cards.
- **Edit path handles** right in the Viewer.
- **Better After Effects imports.** Shape and text layers, light keyframes, Material options, and Orientation all come across.
- **Ctrl+Alt+Home** centres the anchor point.
- Text animator values have a **Reset**.
- Presets are grouped by folder in Effects & presets, and you can make, rename, and delete groups from the panel.
- The font list shows each font in its own face.
- Text uses After Effects' fill and stroke order, so imported titles look the same.
- Shift-drag a curve in the Graph editor to move the middle of an ease.
- Scrubbing past the edge of the Timeline scrolls it along.
- Lumit checks with you before quitting while an export is still running.
- The disk cache shares one size limit across all your projects and always leaves 10 GB free on the drive.
- Plugins installed inside the Flatpak are found.
- Making an account asks for your date of birth.
- 76 more Italian strings (thanks to @andrea68git), and other languages have had gaps filled in.

## What we fixed

- Phone clips filmed upright showed up on their side.
- Dragging a value box added an undo step for every tiny move. Now it's one.
- Shape path keys, self-crossing fills, and clicking the right shape item were off in places.
- A shape item's opacity jumped while you dragged it, and dragging a point added keys to items you didn't touch.
- The track overlay showed points from every frame, not just the current one.
- Roto propagation could leave old frames sitting in the cache.
- Deleting the last clip on an Audio timeline row left an empty row behind.
- Soloing a picture layer muted the sound.
- An image sequence frame that wouldn't read left a gap. Now the frame next to it fills in.
- Updates on Windows could stop if a file was in use, or if a plugin helper closed on its own.
- On Linux, the window wasn't called Lumit.

## Before you update

- **3D comps can look different.** Nearer 3D layers now draw in front and cut through each other, whatever their order in the Timeline, as in After Effects.
- **3D layers with no camera** are now seen through a default camera, with perspective.
- **Depth of field** blurs each part of a layer by its own distance, and as strongly as After Effects, so blurred layers may look softer or sharper.
- **Shapes that cross themselves** are now filled where they cross, like in After Effects. They used to leave a hole.
- **Shape modifiers** from older projects open as entries in the layer's contents and look the same. One exception: if combined shapes had a repeater, offset, twist, or deform on the first one, the layer's box now fits the whole run instead of cutting it off at the edge.
- **Upright phone clips** now show upright.
- **No going back to 0.9.1.** Projects saved with shape modifiers or groups won't open in 0.9.1 or earlier.
