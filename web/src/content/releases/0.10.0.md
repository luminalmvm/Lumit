3D has received a large overhaul, with lights, shadows, materials, multiple 3D views alongside the viewer, and improved camera tools. Modifiers and groups have been added to shape layers, and Easing, Align, and Notes panels are added or improved, along with updated default layouts for the workspaces.

With these changes, they may alter how your existing projects look, largely due to the revamp of 3D comps and shape layers, so make sure to check the Before you update section.

## What is new

### 3D

- Lights can cast shadows from 3D layers, and 3D layers now have a **Material option** for how it interacts with light and whether it casts or accepts shadows.
- The Viewer has 3D handles and rotation rings, for layers, cameras, and lights, with Local, World, and View axes in the tool options.
- A 3D viewport can open alongside the Viewer. Each view keeps its own 3D view.
- Orbit, dolly, track, and fly round the scene, with Alt. F10 to F12 jump between saved views, and **Look at selected layers**, **Look at all layers**, and **Reset 3D view** are in the View menu.
- 3D layers now draw and occulude based if they are in front of other layers, and a comp with no camera uses a default until a camera is added.
- Added camera's depth of field for 3D layers.
- Expressions have toComp, fromComp, lookAt, Orientation, and other 3D conversions.

### Shape layers

- Repeater, Trim paths, Offset paths, Twist, Deform, and Smooth are now entries in a shape layer's contents. Each one acts on every shape above it, so they can be stacked, a repeater under a repeater makes a grid, and shapes can be put in groups (requested by @andrea68git).
- **Expand copies** turns a repeater's copies into shapes of their own, or into layers.
- Rectangles, ellipses, polygons, and stars stay as numbers that can be changed after they're drawn.
- A shape's fill, outline colour, outline width, and opacity can be keyframed, and a fill or outline can be added or removed.
- **Layer ▸ Create ▸ Shapes from vector layer** turns an Illustrator layer's paths into a shape layer, and **Split into shape layers** makes a layer for each letter, path, or group.
- Drawing with a shape tool on a selected shape layer adds the new shape to it, as in After Effects.

### Panels

- The **Easing** panel has been rebuilt, with a larger curve and Built in and Yours tabs. Your own easings can be saved, imported, and exported.
- An **Align** panel lines layers up to each other or to the composition (requested by @andrea68git and Kingdom85).
- A **Notes** panel, with its text kept in the project (requested by Nano).
- The shipped workspaces have been updated to make more use of stacks.
- The Graph editor has an acceleration graph beside the speed graph (requested by @andrea68git).

### Multi-edit and friends

- **File ▸ Shared project…** is now **Multi-edit…**, and **File ▸ Previews…** is now **Share preview…**.
- **Settings ▸ Account** has a username and friends. Add someone by their username, and send them previews and Multi-edit invites directly. Invites are sealed on your computer, so Lumit's server can't read them.

### Effects

- Particulate has **Trails**, flocking, and sparks on death, and colliders can sit anywhere in 3D.
- **Vector blur**.
- Grid has a **Lines** type (requested by @andrea68git).
- Repeat start and Repeat end boxes in node graphs.

### After Effects

- After Effects projects bring across shape and text layers, light keyframes, Material options, and Orientation.
- After Effects preset folders are kept when importing, and Effects & presets groups presets by folder, with groups that can be made, renamed, and deleted (requested by @politohh).
- **Ctrl+Alt+Home** centres the anchor point.
- Shift-dragging the curve in the Graph editor moves the middle of an ease (requested by Nano).
- Text has After Effects' fill and stroke order (requested by hazim_beats).

### Smaller things

- Animated GIF export.
- Exports on Linux can use VAAPI on AMD and Intel cards.
- Path handles can be edited in the Viewer.
- Right-clicking under the rows in the Cut and Audio timelines inserts an empty Audio, Sequence, or Adjustment layer (requested by ruze).
- Text animator values have a Reset.
- The font list shows each font in its own face, and U shows text animator keys (requested by hazim_beats).
- Scrubbing past the edge of the Timeline scrolls it (requested by Nano).
- Lumit asks before quitting while an export is running (requested by Nano).
- The disk cache has one size cap across all projects, and keeps 10 GB free on the drive.
- Plugins installed in the Flatpak are now found.
- Making an account asks for a date of birth.
- Italian has 76 more strings (thanks to @andrea68git), and other languages have had their translations filled in.

## Performance

- The Viewer no longer asks the engine about the comp on every pointer move.
- The shader graph's cache no longer grows on every drag.
- A plain 3D solid under a depth of field camera is only drawn at full size when it's actually blurred.

## Fixed

- Phone clips filmed upright showed on their side.
- Dragging a value box made an undo step for every move.
- A shape item's path keys couldn't be edited in the Graph editor, a shape that crossed itself had holes in its fill, and clicking a shape item's name didn't pick it (reported by @andrea68git and wazzabi).
- A shape item's opacity jumped while it was dragged, and dragging a point keyed shape items it didn't touch.
- The track overlay drew points from every frame, not just the current one.
- Roto brush frames drawn before a propagation kept showing no cut-out.
- Moving or deleting the last clip on a row in the Audio timeline left an empty row behind (reported by ruze).
- Soloing a picture layer silenced the sound (reported by Nano).
- A frame of an image sequence that wouldn't read left a gap instead of its neighbour.
- Updating on Windows could stop when a file was in use, or when a plugin's helper closed by itself.
- On Linux the window was not called Lumit.

## Before you update

- 3D layers now draw nearest in front and cut through each other where they cross, as in After Effects, whatever their order in the Timeline. A 3D comp can look different.
- 3D layers in a comp with no camera layer are now seen through a default camera, with perspective, as After Effects does.
- A camera's depth of field now blurs each part of a 3D layer by its own distance, and as much as After Effects does, so a blurred layer can look softer or sharper than before.
- A shape whose outline crosses itself is now filled where it crosses, as After Effects fills it. It used to leave a hole there.
- A shape layer's modifiers are now entries in its contents, and older projects open as entries and draw the same. One case differs: where shapes were combined and the first carried a repeater, an offset, a twist, or a deform, the layer's box now holds the copies of the whole run, which it used to cut off at the edge.
- A phone clip filmed upright now shows upright. It used to show on its side.
- A project saved with shape modifiers or groups won't open in 0.9.1 or earlier.

## Known gaps

- The Easing panel can't apply an ease to one side of a key only yet.
- Corner pin and other warps still stay inside the picture they're handed in a node graph.
- Arabic still reads left to right.

Thanks very much to everyone who sent in reports and requests for this release, a lot of it came straight from your posts on the Discord. I hope you enjoy using it.
