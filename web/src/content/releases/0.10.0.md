A big one. 3D is now properly usable, with lights that cast shadows, materials, 3D views beside the Viewer, and camera tools that move like After Effects. Shape layers get modifiers and groups, and there are new Easing, Align, and Notes panels.

A few changes alter how existing projects look, mainly 3D comps and shape layers, so have a read of Before you update first.

## What is new

### 3D

- Lights cast shadows from 3D layers, and every 3D layer has **Material options** for how it takes light and whether it casts or accepts shadows.
- Lights have rows of their own, and a light's settings are asked for when it's made.
- 3D layers have Orientation, Anchor Z, and Scale Z, and the arrow keys step a 3D layer in z.
- The Viewer has 3D move handles and rotation rings, for layers, cameras, and lights, with Local, World, and View axes in the tool options.
- A 3D viewport can open beside the Viewer, and new Viewer panes open on Top, Front, and Right. Each view keeps its own 3D view, with the ground and axes under **Guides**.
- Orbit, dolly, track, and fly round the scene, with Alt as in After Effects. F10 to F12 jump between saved views, and **Look at selected layers**, **Look at all layers**, and **Reset 3D view** are on the View menu.
- Nearer 3D layers draw in front and cut through each other where they cross, and a comp with no camera is seen through a default one.
- A camera's depth of field blurs each part of a 3D layer by its own distance, as much as After Effects does.
- Expressions have toComp, fromComp, lookAt, Orientation, and the rest of the 3D conversions.

### Shapes

- A shape layer's repeater, trim, offset, twist, deform, and smooth are entries in its contents, acting on the shapes above them, and shapes can be grouped.
- **Expand copies** turns a repeater's copies into shapes of their own.
- Rectangles, ellipses, polygons, and stars stay as numbers you can change after drawing them.
- A shape's colours, outline width, and opacity can animate, and its fill and outline can be added or taken away.
- **Layer ▸ Create ▸ Shapes from vector layer** turns an Illustrator layer's paths into a shape layer, and **Split into shape layers** makes one layer per letter, path, or group.
- Drawing on a selected shape layer adds a new shape to it.

### Panels

- The **Easing** panel has been rebuilt, with Built in and Yours tabs, and saved easings can be imported and exported.
- An **Align** panel, and Align in the Viewer can use any point of the box.
- A **Notes** panel, whose text is kept in the project.
- The shipped workspaces make more use of stacks.
- The Graph editor has an acceleration graph.

### Everything else

- **Multi-edit** is the new name for shared projects, and **Settings ▸ Account** has a username and friends, so previews and Multi-edit invites can be sent straight to a friend.
- Particulate has Trails, flocking, and sparks on death, and colliders can sit anywhere in 3D.
- **Vector blur**, a Lines type for Grid, and Repeat start and Repeat end in node graphs.
- Export animated GIFs.
- Exports on Linux can use VAAPI on AMD and Intel cards.
- Path handles can be edited in the Viewer.
- After Effects projects bring across shape and text layers, light keyframes, Material options, and Orientation.
- **Ctrl+Alt+Home** centres the anchor point.
- A text animator's values have a Reset.
- Saved presets are grouped by folder in Effects & presets, and groups can be made, renamed, and deleted from the panel.
- The font list shows each font in its own face.
- Text has After Effects' fill and stroke order.
- Shift-dragging the curve in the Graph editor moves the middle of an ease.
- Scrubbing to the edge of the Timeline scrolls it.
- Lumit asks before quitting while an export is running.
- The disk cache has one size cap across all projects, and keeps 10 GB free on the drive.
- Plugins installed in the Flatpak are found.
- Making an account asks for a date of birth.
- Italian has 76 more strings (thanks to @andrea68git), and other languages have had their translations filled in.

## Fixed

- Phone clips filmed upright showed on their side.
- Dragging a value box made an undo step for every move.
- Shape path keys, self-crossing fills, and picking the right shape item were wrong in places.
- A shape item's opacity jumped while it was dragged, and dragging a point keyed shape items it didn't touch.
- The track overlay drew points from every frame, not just the current one.
- Roto propagation could leave the old frames in the cache.
- Deleting the last clip on a row in the Audio timeline left the row behind.
- Soloing a picture layer silenced the sound.
- A frame of an image sequence that wouldn't read left a gap instead of its neighbour.
- Updating on Windows could stop when a file was in use, or when a plugin's helper closed by itself.
- On Linux the window was not called Lumit.

## Before you update

- 3D layers now draw nearest in front and cut through each other where they cross, as in After Effects, whatever their order in the Timeline. A 3D comp can look different.
- 3D layers in a comp with no camera layer are now seen through a default camera, with perspective, as After Effects does.
- A camera's depth of field now blurs each part of a 3D layer by its own distance, and as much as After Effects does, so a blurred layer can look softer or sharper than before.
- A shape whose outline crosses itself is now filled where it crosses, as After Effects fills it. It used to leave a hole there.
- A shape layer's modifiers are now entries in its contents, and older projects open as entries and draw the same. One case differs: where shapes were combined and the first carried a repeater, an offset, a twist, or a deform, the layer's box now holds the copies of the whole run, which it used to cut off at the edge.
- A phone clip filmed upright now shows upright. It used to show on its side.
- A project saved with shape modifiers or groups won't open in 0.9.1 or earlier.
