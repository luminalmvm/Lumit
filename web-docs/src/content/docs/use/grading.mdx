The **Colour** workspace grades a composition shot by shot. A shot's grade is the Colour
effects in its own stack.

<Shot file="colour-workspace.png" caption="The Colour workspace: the Gallery, the Viewer over the Shots panel, Grade nodes over Scopes, and the Grade panel along the bottom." eager/>

## Shots

A **shot** is a picture clip on a Sequence layer, or any other layer with a picture of its
own: footage, a precomp, a solid, text, a shape, a 3D model or a 3D shape. Sound-only and
adjustment layers are not shots. The [Shots panel](/panels/shots/) lists them in time
order, the upper layer first where two start together.

### The current shot

The **current shot** is the clip picked in the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/) while its
layer is the selected layer, else the selected layer. The Grade, Grade nodes and Shots
panels follow it. A clip's grade is the clip's own stack, not its Sequence layer's. A
Sequence layer selected with no clip picked is graded in its own stack, which runs after
every clip's.

While the Grade, Grade nodes, Shots or Gallery panel is in the workspace, the current shot
and the playhead move together:

- A shot made current while the playhead is outside it brings the playhead to its first
  frame, or for a clip that starts under the one before it, to the first frame it shows
  alone.
- The playhead moved out of the current shot makes the shot under it current: for a clip,
  the clip on the same Sequence layer, the later one where two overlap; for a layer, the
  top layer shot there.

Over a gap nothing changes.

An edit in the Grade panel lands on the current shot and on every other picked shot of its
kind: the picked clips for a clip, the selected layers for a layer. See
[Grade](/panels/grade/#the-pages).

## Nodes

Every effect in a shot's stack is a node, in stack order. The usual one is
[Correction](/effects/colour/correction/): wheels, adjustments, a mixer, curves, a
qualifier and four windows in one effect. Any other effect, such as a LUT or a blur, is a
node too.

The Grade panel edits the **current node**: the one picked, else the shot's last Correction.
The first touch on a shot with no Correction adds one, in the same undo step.

| Node | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Serial** | Grades what the node before it made. |
| **Parallel** | Grades the picture the node before it graded, and adds its change to theirs. |
| **Layer** | Grades the picture the node before it graded, and lays its result over theirs through its key. |
| **Outside** | Grades what the current node leaves alone: its Matte is wired from the current node's key, inverted. |

**Layer ▸ Grade**, a right-click in the Grade or Grade nodes panel, and the
[shortcuts](#shortcuts) add them. A new node goes after the current one and becomes
current. A Parallel or Layer node is a Correction whose **Combine** says so. An outside
node needs the current node to be a Correction on a layer, not on a clip. **Add colour
warper node**, on **Layer ▸ Grade** and the Grade nodes panel's add menus, adds a
[Colour warper](/effects/colour/colour-warper/).

Each node has a **key**: its Matte, times its qualifier, times its windows, each white
while off. The grade lands where the key is white.

### Key wires

A Correction's **Key** wired into the Matte of an effect below it in the same stack mattes
that effect, which then works only where the key is white. An outside node is a Correction
wired this way. In the [Grade nodes panel](/panels/grade-nodes/), a drag from a
Correction's key out dot onto a later node wires it, and the wire is drawn dashed. The
[node graph](/use/nodes/#key-wires) shows the same wire.

### Shared nodes

A **shared node** is one node used by several shots. An edit to it shows in every shot
using it. **Share with picked shots**, on a node's menu in the Grade nodes panel, makes the
node shared and puts it in every other picked shot, after that shot's last grade node.
**Add shared node ▸**, on the same menu, puts one of the project's shared nodes in the
current shot.

**Rename…** and **Reset node** act on a shared node in every shot. **Bypass node** and
**Delete node** act on this shot's use of it. **Unshare** gives every shot using it its own
copy, where it stood.

## The Grade panel

The [Grade panel](/panels/grade/) edits the current node, a page at a time: **Wheels**,
**Adjust**, **Curves**, **Qualifier**, **Windows**, **Match** and **Look**. The Wheels page
shows the wheels as **Wheels**, **Bars**, **Log** or **HDR**, six zones by brightness in
stops. A node that is not a Correction shows its own rows, and a
[Colour warper](/effects/colour/colour-warper/) its mesh.

Holding **Before** shows the shot without its grade.

## The qualifier

The qualifier keys a node by colour: **HSL**, **RGB**, **Luma** or **3D**.

**Pick**, **Add to pick** and **Subtract from pick** on the Qualifier page arm the picture.
A drag on the Viewer draws a circle, and the colours inside it, read from the picture
coming into the node, set the ranges:

- **Pick** sets the ranges to cover them and nothing else.
- **Add to pick** widens the ranges to cover them too.
- **Subtract from pick** pulls the nearest edge of one range in past them.

A click reads a few pixels. On a qualifier that is off, Pick and Add to pick turn HSL on,
and Subtract from pick does nothing.

The ranges show as the drag goes, in Highlight when the matte view is off, and the release
writes them as one undo step. The tool stays armed until its button is pressed again or the
picture is right-clicked.

### The 3D qualifier

A **3D** qualifier keys on colours sampled from the picture, some kept and some dropped, up
to 32 of each. Past 32, the two closest are merged. On a node keyed in 3D, the pick tools
draw a stroke on the Viewer in place of a circle, and the colours along it are the samples:

- **Pick** keeps only this stroke's colours, and drops nothing.
- **Add to pick** keeps this stroke's colours as well.
- **Subtract from pick** drops this stroke's colours.

The key shows as the stroke is drawn, and the release writes it as one undo step. Each node
keeps its strokes while the project is open, and the Viewer shows them faintly while a tool
is armed. The key is white near a kept colour, as far as **Size** and **Softness** reach,
and black near a dropped one. With nothing kept, every colour is kept.

### The matte view

The matte view shows the current node's key on the Viewer, on the shot alone:

| Matte view | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | The picture. |
| **Highlight** | The graded picture where the key is, over mid grey, black, white, or the picture in black and white. In a Parallel or Layer run, the run's picture with this node joined. |
| **Key** | The key in black and white. |

`Shift+H` steps through them. **Highlight** on the Qualifier page turns it on and off, and
a right-click there offers all three. A chip on the Viewer's bar names it while it is on: a
click turns it off, and a right-click picks what Highlight shows around the key, kept until
Lumit closes. The matte view is never exported, and it turns off when the current node is
not a Correction.

## Windows

A Correction has four window slots. Each is an **Ellipse**, a **Rectangle**, a **Polygon**
or a **Gradient**, placed in the shot's own picture. A window joins the slots before it, or
with **Subtract** cuts itself out of them. A slot turned on from the Windows page starts at
the middle of the shot's picture.

The picked slot's handles show on the Viewer:

- The centre moves the window.
- A corner sizes it about the centre. `Shift` keeps its proportions.
- An edge changes its width or height.
- The handle above it turns it. `Shift` snaps to 15°.
- The handles on the dashed rings set the outer and inner softness.
- On a rectangle, a handle on the top edge sets the roundness.
- On a gradient, the aim handle sets its direction, and two handles its outer and inner
  softness.

Other slots show a dot at their centre. Click one to pick it. Right-click a handle to change
the window's shape or turn it off. A keyed row takes a key at the playhead, and each drag is
one undo step. `Escape` lets a drag go.

A picked polygon with fewer than three points is drawn on the picture: each click adds a
point, up to 32. `Enter`, or a click on the first point, closes it, and `Escape` lets it go.
Drag a point to move it. Click a point and press `Delete` to remove it.

### Tracking a window

**Track forwards** and **Track back** on the Windows page follow the picture coming into the
node, inside the picked window, from the playhead to the end or the start of the shot. They
key the window's centre, size and angle on every frame, linear, as one undo step, in place
of any keys already in that span. **Stop** keeps the frames done. A window that loses the
picture stops there, keeps the frames done, and the status line names the frame.

Only footage and precomps track, as layers or as clips. A track is **refused** while
another analysis runs. A window in a group's grade or in a shared node cannot be tracked.

## Matching

The **Match** page solves values into the current node, a new Correction when there is none,
as one undo step.

- **Auto balance** takes the cast out of the shot at the playhead and sets its black and
  white points. It writes Temperature, Tint, Lift and Gain.
- **Match** brings the shot at the playhead to look like the one in **Match to**: the
  Viewer's reference, or another shot of the composition at its first frame. It writes Lift,
  Gamma, Gain and Saturation.
- **Show chart** draws the grid of a 24-patch ColorChecker Classic on the Viewer. Drag its
  four corners onto the chart in the picture, with dark skin at the top left, or its middle
  to move it whole. **Match chart** reads the patches at the playhead, sets Space to Linear
  and writes the mixer and Exposure, and the page shows the mean ΔE before and after. The
  chart's colours are taken into the project's working space.

**Match to current shot**, on a tile's menu in the Shots panel, matches that shot to the
current one. **Auto balance** is also on **Layer ▸ Grade**.

## Looks and LUTs

A **look** is a grade kept as a preset in the library's **Looks** group, which every
project sees. **Save look…** writes the current shot's grade as one. Picking a look on the
Look page puts its grade in place of the grade on every picked shot.

The Look page's **LUT** row reads a `.cube` file into the shot's first LUT node, added after
the grade.

**Export LUT** writes the shot's grade as a `.cube`, at 17, 33 or 65 points a side, for
**Video** (Rec. 709 at a 2.4 gamma), **Log** (ACEScct) or **sRGB** pictures. It bakes the
shot's enabled Colour effects as they stand at the playhead, Parallel and Layer nodes
included. Qualifiers stay, without their Denoise, Grow and Blur radius. Anything that
depends on where a pixel sits is left out: windows, mattes, key wires, Midtone detail and a
Vignette. Each node bakes as if its whole picture were keyed. A group's grades and shared nodes are
left out. From the Shots panel and **Layer ▸ Grade** it writes 33 points for Video.

## CDL

**Import CDL…** reads a `.cdl`, `.cc` or `.ccc` file into a [CDL](/effects/colour/cdl/) node
after the current node, or after the node right-clicked, and makes it current. A file
holding several corrections asks which.

**Export CDL…** writes a Correction or CDL node as a `.cc`, or as a `.cdl` decision list
when the name ends so. A Correction that moves only Offset, Lift, Gamma, Gain and Saturation
exports exactly. Any other is fitted, and the status line says *CDL exported, fitted*. The
numbers are in the node's Space, read at the playhead, as if its key took the whole picture.

Both are on the Look page, **Layer ▸ Grade** and a node's menu in the Grade nodes panel.

## Versions

A shot can keep **versions** of its grade: *Version 1*, *Version 2*. One is live, in the
stack, and the others are kept aside. The version button in the Grade panel's header, and
**Versions ▸** on a tile's menu in the Shots panel, list them.

- Picking a version swaps it into the stack where the grade was.
- **New version** starts an empty grade. **Duplicate version** starts a copy of the live
  one. Either becomes the live version.
- **Rename…** names the live version.
- **Delete version** deletes the live version and shows the one before it in its place, or
  the one after for the first. The last version going leaves its grade in the stack.
- **Versions in a grid**, on the version button, shows them in the Viewer's
  [grid](#the-grid).

A group's grade has no versions.

## Groups

A **grade group** is a set of shots sharing a grade laid on before their own and one laid
on after. **Group ▸** on a tile's menu in the Shots panel makes one from the picked shots,
puts them in another, or takes them out. A shot is in one group at a time.

Each member carries two [Group grade](/effects/colour/group-grade/) effects, a Pre and a
Post. In the [Grade nodes panel](/panels/grade-nodes/), **Group pre** and **Group post**
show the group's nodes, edited once for every member. The Grade panel and the Viewer's
grading tools follow, and the first touch adds a Correction to the group's grade.

**Rename group…** and **Delete group**, on **Group ▸** while the picked shots share one
group, name it or delete it. Deleting a group takes its grades out of every member, after
asking.

## Copying grades

- **Copy grade**, `Alt+Shift+C`, puts the current shot's grade on the clipboard.
- **Paste grade**, `Alt+Shift+V`, puts it in place of the grade on every picked shot. Both
  are on the **Edit** menu.
- **Apply grade from previous shot**, `=`, puts the grade of the shot before the current
  one in time in place of the grade on every picked shot. **Apply grade from two shots
  back**, `-`, takes the one two before it. Where that shot has no grade, the picked shots
  lose theirs.
- **Reset grade** puts every node back to its defaults, its Space and Combine kept. From
  the Grade panel, a keyed row keeps its keys and takes its default at the playhead. From
  the Grade nodes panel, the Shots panel and **Layer ▸ Grade**, the keys go.
- **Bypass grade** switches the whole grade off, and on again.

A grade copied, pasted, versioned, kept in a memory or saved as a look is the shot's Colour
effects. It never carries the shot's group or its shared nodes.

### Memories

The project keeps eight **memories**, each a grade. `Ctrl+Alt+1` to `Ctrl+Alt+8` keeps the
current shot's grade in that memory, with a small picture of the shot. `Alt+1` to `Alt+8`
puts a memory's grade in place of the grade on every picked shot. The
[Gallery](/panels/gallery/#memories) shows them as a strip of slots.

## Stills and the Gallery

A **still** is a frame as the Viewer showed it, kept with the grade that made it. **Grab
still**, `Ctrl+Alt+G`, keeps the composition's frame at the playhead, with the current
shot's grade, in the [Gallery's](/panels/gallery/) open album, named by its shot and the
Viewer's clock. Stills live in the library, and every project sees them.

A still's grade goes on a shot from the Gallery, or by dragging the still onto a tile in
the Shots panel. **Export still…** writes a still's picture as a PNG, and **Export grade as
LUT…** its grade as a 33-point `.cube` for Video.

### Comparing against a reference

A still, or another shot's frame, can be the Viewer's **reference**. Double-click a still
in the Gallery, or press **Compare** on the Match page, which takes the one in **Match
to**. `Ctrl+Alt+C` shows or hides it.

| Style | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Wipe** | The reference on one side of an upright divider. |
| **Wipe across** | The reference on one side of a level divider. |
| **Split** | The same part of both, side by side. |
| **Difference** | The difference between the two. |
| **Mix** | The two blended, the divider setting how much. |

Drag the divider to move it, and double-click it to put it back in the middle. A chip on
the Viewer's bar names the reference and its style: a click shows or hides it, the cross
clears it, and a right-click offers **Show reference**, the styles, **Swap sides**, **Reset
divider** and **Clear reference**. A shot's frame is read once, when it becomes the
reference.

## The grid

The Viewer can show up to nine pictures in place of the picture, each in the picture's
shape. Two stand side by side, or one over the other in a tall Viewer, three or four two by
two, and more three by three:

| Grid | Shows | Double-click a cell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Versions in a grid** | The current shot's versions. | Makes that version live. |
| **Stills in a grid** | The stills selected in the Gallery. | Puts the still's grade on the current shot. |
| **Shots in a grid** | The picked shots. | Puts that shot's grade on the current shot. |

Each cell is named, and the live version or the current shot is outlined in the accent. A
chip on the Viewer's bar names the grid: a click or its cross closes it, and a right-click
switches what it shows. `Escape` closes it too.

## Shortcuts

**Colour panels** keys work while the Grade, Grade nodes, Shots or Gallery panel is active.

| Key | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Alt+S` | Colour panels | Adds a serial node. |
| `Alt+P` | Colour panels | Adds a parallel node. |
| `Alt+L` | Colour panels | Adds a layer node. |
| `Alt+O` | Colour panels | Adds an outside node. |
| `Shift+H` | Anywhere | Steps the matte view through Off, Highlight and Key. |
| `Alt+Shift+C`, `Alt+Shift+V` | Anywhere | Copy grade, paste grade. |
| `Ctrl+Alt+G` | Colour panels | Grabs a still. |
| `↑`, `↓` | Colour panels | The previous and the next shot. |
| `Home`, `End` | Colour panels | The first and the last shot. |
| `=` | Colour panels | Applies the previous shot's grade. |
| `-` | Colour panels | Applies the grade from two shots back. |
| `Alt+1` to `Alt+8` | Colour panels | Applies that memory. |
| `Ctrl+Alt+1` to `Ctrl+Alt+8` | Colour panels | Keeps the grade in that memory. |
| `Ctrl+Alt+L` | Colour panels | Shows or hides the [Lightbox](/panels/shots/#the-lightbox). |
| `Ctrl+Alt+C` | Colour panels | Shows or hides the reference. |

**Settings ▸ Shortcuts** rebinds each.
