Lumit grades with two kinds of hardware: **Tangent** panels, through Tangent Hub, and **MIDI**
controllers of any make, through MIDI learn. Both are set up in **Settings ▸ General ▸
Control surfaces**.

A surface drives the [Grade panel's](/panels/grade/) current node on the current shot, a
layer or a Cut clip, or on the group's grade while that is in view. When the current node is
not a [Correction](/effects/colour/correction/), it drives the last Correction, and with no
Correction a turn does nothing. A turn shows at once and is written as one undo step once
the hardware has been still for a quarter of a second. It lands on the current shot alone.
A keyed row takes a key at the playhead. The transport and undo work whatever is selected.

## Tangent panels

**Tangent panels** connects to Tangent Hub on this machine, which is installed from Tangent.
The row under it says **Waiting for Tangent Hub**, or which panels are connected.

Each time panels are turned on, Lumit hands the Hub its list of controls and a map for each
panel. Maps made in Tangent's Mapper are kept by the Hub and left alone. The panels'
displays show each control's name and value, the shot's name and the Correction's number.

A trackball moves a wheel's colour, and its ring the wheel's master. **Alt** and the bank
buttons are the panel's own.

### What the buttons do

| Button | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Previous shot**, **Next shot** | Makes the shot before or after current. |
| **Previous node**, **Next node** | Steps to the Correction before or after. |
| **Add serial node**, **Add parallel node**, **Add layer node**, **Add outside node** | A new node after the current one. See [Nodes](/use/grading/#nodes). |
| **Bypass node** | Switches the current Correction off and on. |
| **Bypass grade** | Switches the whole grade off and on, on every picked shot. |
| **Reset node** | Puts the current Correction's rows back to their defaults, its **Space** and **Combine** kept. |
| **Reset lift**, **Reset gamma**, **Reset gain**, **Reset offset** | Puts that wheel back. |
| **Grab still** | Keeps the frame at the playhead in the Gallery, with the current shot's grade. |
| **Wheels page**, **Bars page**, **Log page**, **HDR page** | Shows the Wheels page with its wheels that way. |
| **Grade page** | Steps the Grade panel through **Wheels**, **Adjust**, **Curves**, **Qualifier**, **Windows**, **Match** and **Look**. |
| **Wheel mode** | Shows the Wheels page and steps its wheels through **Wheels**, **Bars**, **Log** and **HDR**. |

**Reset shadow**, **Reset midtone** and **Reset highlight** are on no map of Lumit's own.
Tangent's Mapper and MIDI learn list them.

## MIDI controllers

Each MIDI input on the system has a row with a switch that opens it. A device switched on
keeps its row while it is unplugged, and opens again when it comes back. With no inputs,
**MIDI devices** says **None found**.

**MIDI learn** ties a control to something it drives:

1. Pick what it drives from the list: a row the knobs above reach, a wheel's **X** (across),
   **Y** (up) or **master**, or a button. **Grade page** and **Wheel mode** are not in the
   list.
2. Press **Learn**. It reads **Move a control**, and a second press stops it.
3. Move the knob or fader, or press the key, on a device that is switched on.

One control drives one thing. Learning a control again replaces what it drove.

Each learned control has a row: the control, as **Note**, **CC** or **CC 14-bit** with its
number and channel, the device under it, what it drives, and **Remove**. A 7-bit CC also has
how it is read:

| Reading | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Absolute** | A knob or fader that says where it is. |
| **Relative, two's complement** | An endless knob sending 1 for a step up and 127 for a step down. |
| **Relative, sign bit** | An endless knob sending 1 for a step up and 65 for a step down. |
| **Relative, offset 64** | An endless knob sending 65 for a step up and 63 for a step down. |

Learn guesses the reading from the control's first two messages. **Relative, sign bit** is
only ever picked by hand.

- An absolute control moves its row by how far it moved, its whole travel the row's whole
  range. Its first message only says where it is.
- An endless knob moves its row ten of the row's smallest steps a click.
- A key or pad resets the row it drives, or presses the button it drives.
- A CC tied to a button presses it past half way, or on each click of an endless knob.
- A 14-bit CC drives a row, never a button.
