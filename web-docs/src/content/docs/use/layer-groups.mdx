A **layer group** is a header row in the Timeline over a run of layers. It folds them away
and switches them together. It does not change the picture unless effects are put on it.

## Make a group

Select layers that sit next to one another in the stack and choose **Layer ▸ Group
layers**, or press `Ctrl+G` in the Timeline. **Group layers** is also on a layer's
right-click menu. The group is named **Group**.

A selection that is not one unbroken run of the stack is **refused**, and so is one holding
a layer that is already in a group.

## The header row

| Part | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Twirl** | Folds the group, hiding its layers' rows, and unfolds it. |
| **Colour** | Opens the nine-colour picker a layer's label opens. |
| **Name** | Double-click it to rename the group. `Enter` renames the selected group. |
| **Count** | How many layers the group holds. |
| **Visible**, **Audible**, **Solo**, **Lock** | Sets that switch on every layer in the group, as one undo step. A switch reads on only when it is on for every layer. |

Clicking the header selects every layer in the group, so a command or a drag on the
selection acts on all of them.

In the lanes the group draws one bar, from its earliest layer's in point to its latest
layer's out point. Drag the bar to move every layer in the group together. It has no ends
to trim.

Right-click the header or the bar for **Rename group**, **Select all**, **Ungroup**,
**Precompose group** and **Label colour…**.

## Take layers out

- **Layer ▸ Ungroup**, `Ctrl+Shift+G`, or **Ungroup** on the header's right-click menu
  removes every group the selection touches. The layers stay where they are.
- A layer dragged out of the group's run in the stack is drawn outside the group. Dragged
  back into the run, it is in the group again.

**Precompose group** opens the Precompose dialogue on the group's layers. The group's
effects move onto the new Precomp layer and the group is removed. See
[Compositions](/use/compositions/#nest-one-composition-in-another).

## Effects on a group

Click a group's header and [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/) shows the group's
own effect stack. Add an effect there, or drag one onto the panel from Effects & presets.

A group with effects renders its layers together, on their own, and runs the effects on
that one picture. A [driver](/effects/#drivers) is **refused**.

The header carries an **fx** mark while the group has effects. Press it to open the
effects' rows and keyframes under the header.
