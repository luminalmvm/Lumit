A **review link** is an address where an export of a composition is watched in a browser.
Anyone who has it is a **reviewer** and can comment on a frame. The comments come back to
Lumit. It is part of [Lumit Pro](/use/profiles/#lumit-pro): the person sending needs it, and
reviewers do not.

## Send a composition

1. Front the composition, or select it in the Project panel.
2. Choose **Composition ▸ Send for review…**.
3. Set the fields and press **Create link**.

| Field | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | What the link is called. It starts as the composition's name. |
| **Size** | **1080p** at 6 Mb/s, or **720p** at 3 Mb/s. The composition is fitted inside it, turned for a portrait composition, and never enlarged. |
| **Span** | The whole composition, or the work area when one is set. |
| **Reviewers** | **Can comment** lets reviewers leave comments. |

Lumit exports an H.264 MP4 at the composition's frame rate, with its sound, and uploads it.
**Cancel** stops either. When the upload finishes, the link is on the clipboard.

- Missing footage is drawn as colour bars.
- The export waits behind one that is already running. Items waiting in the export queue are
  not started.
- The window can be closed while a composition is being sent. A ring on the bottom strip
  turns until it finishes.
- A send is refused when the account's storage is full, and when the file is over 2 GB.
- An account holds up to 1,000 links.

## Versions

A composition that has been sent before is sent as a **new version** of its link.
**Separate link** makes a link of its own instead. A comment belongs to the version it was
made on. A link holds up to 50 versions.

**What changed** is a line of up to 200 characters about a new version. It is shown beside
the version, in Lumit and in the browser. Lumit fills it in with the two largest changes to
the composition's layers and effects since the last version, and it can be typed over. One
layer or effect is named, as in `Glow added to Title text`, and more than one are counted. It
starts empty for a link sent from another machine.

A link is marked **Changed** when its composition has been edited since the newest version
was sent. **Send a new version** on the link sends one. An edit is a change to the
composition's settings, to one of its layers, or to a composition nested in it. Markers do
not count, the composition's or a layer's. The mark shows in the profile the version was
sent from.

## The Review links window

**File ▸ Review links…** opens the window. The links are listed on the left, newest
activity first, and the chosen one is on the right.

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Copy the address | **Copy** |
| Open the link in the browser | **Open** |
| Choose a version | The list beside the title |
| Send the fronted composition | **Send for review…** |
| Copy, open, send a new version or delete | Right-click the link in the list |

A dot marks a link with comments or approvals that have not been seen. While there are any,
a button on the bottom strip shows how many and opens the window on the newest. The first
time a profile lists an account's links, the comments already on them count as seen.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Up`, `Down` | Moves through the links, or through the comments once the keys are on them |
| `Right`, `Enter` | Puts the keys on the chosen link's comments |
| `Left` | Puts the keys back on the links |
| `Enter` on a comment | Goes to its frame |
| `Home`, `End` | Goes to the first or the last |
| `Esc` | Closes the window |

The foot of the window shows how much of the account's storage is used.

### The Link page

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Can comment** | Whether reviewers can leave comments. |
| **Can attach pictures** | Whether reviewers can attach a picture to a comment. Off while **Can comment** is off. Turning it off removes no picture already on the link. |
| **Can download** | Whether reviewers can download the file. |
| **Email me** | Whether you are emailed when a comment, a reply or an approval lands on this link. **Settings ▸ Account ▸ Email me about new comments** turns the emails off for every link. |
| **Video only** | A second address for the link that shows the video and nothing of the review: no comments, likes, approvals or other versions. **Copy link** makes it on the first press and copies it. It shows the version that was newest when it was made and stays on that one. The row says which, and **Move to v…** points it at the newest. **Remove** stops it working. The password and the expiry apply to it too. |
| **Password** | A password every reviewer types before watching, of up to 100 plain letters, numbers and symbols. **Clear** removes it. |
| **Expires** | When the link stops working: never, or in a day, 7 days or 30 days. A link that has expired is deleted. |
| **Seen by** | Who has opened the link in a browser: the name they gave, or **Someone**, the version they were on, and when they were last there. The newest six are listed, and **and … more** shows the rest. Opening the link from Lumit is not counted. |
| **Compare** | Opens the browser with that version beside the one before it. |
| **Delete…** | Deletes that version and its comments. A link keeps at least one version. |
| **Delete link…** | Deletes the link, its versions and its comments. |

## Comments

The **Comments** page lists the comments on the chosen version. **Open** shows the ones
that are not resolved, and **All** shows every one. Each says how many it holds. A comment
made while the window is open appears in the list.

A comment shows who wrote it, when, its timecode in the composition, and its text. A comment
on a range of frames shows the two timecodes it runs between.

A comment with a pin, an arrow or a box also shows the name of the layer it points at. That
is the top layer under the pin, the arrow's head or the middle of the box, on the comment's
frame of the composition as it is now. Hidden layers, layers outside their in and out
points, and camera, light, null, adjustment and audio layers are passed over.

A pencil marks a comment with a drawing. Pressing it shows the drawing over that frame of
the composition as it is now, and pressing it again hides it.

A comment sent with a picture shows it small under its text. Pressing it shows the picture
whole, and pressing again or `Esc` puts it away. A comment can be a picture with no text.

A mention is `@` and the name of someone on the link: its sender, anyone who has commented,
or anyone who has approved a version. It is drawn in the accent colour, and a mention of you
sits on a tint of it.

A like is one line: who liked that moment, and its timecode. Likes in the same second share
a line. A like is never counted as unseen.

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Go to a comment's frame | Press its timecode |
| Show or hide a drawing | Press the pencil |
| Show a picture whole | Press the picture |
| Resolve or reopen | Point at the comment: **Resolve** or **Reopen** |
| Reply | Point at the comment: **Reply**, then type and press `Enter` |
| Copy a link to a comment | Point at the comment: **⋯**, or right-click it, then **Copy link to this comment** |
| Delete | Point at the comment: **⋯**, or right-click it, then **Delete**. Its replies go with it. |
| Remove an approval | Point at the **Approved by** line above the comments: **Remove**, or right-click it. A reviewer approves a version in the browser. |

A comment's menu holds everything its row does. **Copy link to this comment** copies the
link's address with the comment's version and frame, so the browser opens there.

Pressing a timecode fronts the composition the link was sent from, moves the playhead, and
selects the layer the comment points at. The composition is found in the open project by
the one it was sent from, or by its name. Where the open project does not have it,
timecodes do nothing, no layer is named, a drawing is shown without its frame, and
**Add to Timeline** is off.

## Add comments to the Timeline

**Add to Timeline** puts every open comment on the chosen version onto the Timeline as
[markers](/use/markers/), in one undo step.

- A comment that points at a layer becomes a layer marker on that layer, so it moves with
  the layer. Every other comment becomes a marker on the composition.
- A marker's label is the reviewer's name and the comment: `Ana: Hold this a beat longer`.
  A picture with no text is the name alone.
- A comment on a range of frames becomes a [region](/use/markers/#regions).
- Comments on the same frame of the same layer, or of the composition, share one marker.
- Each reviewer has one colour.
- A marker already on that frame is replaced. Beat markers stay.
- Replies, likes and resolved comments are not added, and nor is a comment whose marker is
  already on the Timeline.

A version exported from the work area puts its markers on the frames it was exported from.
