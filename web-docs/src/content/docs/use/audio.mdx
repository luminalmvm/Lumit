Audio plays in preview, and exporting will retain audio unless specifically turned off.

## Audio layers

An **Audio layer** is sound with no picture. Placing an audio file in a composition makes
one. To take just the sound of an item that has a picture too, right-click the item in the
Project panel and choose **Add audio only**.

An Audio layer has no visibility switch.

With **Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Sound moves in whole frames** off, an Audio layer dragged by
its bar lands where the pointer is, between frames.

## Waveforms

In the Timeline, a Footage layer draws its audio waveform in its lane, and clips inside a
[Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) draw theirs inside their own box. The drawing is
trimmed and retimed along with the layer.

There are several options in **Edit ▸ Settings ▸ Interface** for how to display waveforms.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Waveform frequency stack** | Splits the waveform into low, mid, and high frequency bands. |
| **Waveforms from the bottom** | Draws the waveform from the bottom of the row instead of centred. |

## Audio streams

A file can carry several **audio streams**, such as a screen recording with the game on
one and the microphone on another. A file placed in a composition plays every stream,
mixed. The Project panel's preview card states how many streams a file has.

Right-click a layer in the Timeline, or a clip in the Cut timeline or the Audio timeline.
The commands appear when the file has more than one stream.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Audio streams** | Lists the file's streams. The layer plays the ticked ones, mixed. |
| **Split audio streams** | Gives each stream a row of its own. |
| **Merge audio streams** | Puts the streams back on one row, mixed. |

**Split audio streams** and **Merge audio streams** are also in **Layer ▸ Audio**.

- A split Footage layer keeps its picture and its first stream. Each other stream becomes
  an Audio layer directly below, with the same span, Volume and effects.
- A split clip keeps its first stream. Each other stream becomes a
  [linked clip](/use/cutting/#linked-clips) on an audio-only Sequence layer.
- A split row is named after its stream where the file names it, and after its number
  otherwise.
- Merging removes the Audio layers of the same file that start at the same time, along
  with their own Volume keyframes and effects. Locked layers stay.
- Merging a layer that was never split sets it to every stream of its file.

**Edit ▸ Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Several audio streams arrive as** sets what such a file
becomes when it is placed in a composition. **One layer, mixed** is the default.

| Choice | What is placed |
| --- | --- |
| **The first stream** | One layer playing the first stream. |
| **One layer, mixed** | One layer playing every stream. |
| **A layer each** | A layer for each stream. |

A layer in a project from Lumit 0.7.0 or earlier plays the first stream until it is set to
others.

The source view and the Project panel's preview follow the same setting. They play the
first stream, or every stream mixed for either of the other two choices.

## Scrubbing

Hold `Ctrl` and drag along the Timeline ruler to hear the composition as the playhead
moves. Each frame the playhead lands on plays a twelfth of a second of the mix from that
frame. `Ctrl`+click on the ruler plays the frame under the pointer.

## Beat markers

Lumit can read a composition's audio and place a **beat marker** on each detected beat.

- **Composition ▸ Detect beats** runs the detection.
- **Composition ▸ Clear beat markers** removes all beat markers in the composition.

:::note[Partially built]
Neither sensitivity tuning nor tapping beats in by hand is built yet.
:::
