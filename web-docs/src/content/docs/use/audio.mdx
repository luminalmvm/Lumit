Audio plays in preview, and exporting will retain audio unless specifically turned off.

## Audio layers

An **Audio layer** is sound with no picture. Placing an audio file in a composition makes
one. To take the sound of a clip that has a picture too, right-click the item in the
Project panel and choose **Add audio only** — the sound arrives as its own layer, which
you can trim, fade and keyframe on its own.

An Audio layer has no visibility switch, because there is nothing to see, and hiding or
muting one never costs you rendered frames.

## Waveforms

In the Timeline, a Footage layer draws its audio waveform in its lane. The drawing
is trimmed and retimed along with the layer.

There are several options in **Edit ▸ Settings ▸ Interface** for how to display waveforms.

| Settting | What it does |
| **Waveforms show the frequency stack** | splits the waveform into low, mid, and high frequency bands to help you differentiate them or make it more obvious when a beat occurs. |
| **Waveforms rise from the bottom** | Instead of the waveform being centred in the row, and displaying the same audio on each side, this displays effectively one half of that, but it can take up to twice the height. | 

:::note[Partially built]
Clips inside a [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) do not draw waveforms yet.
:::

## Beat markers

Lumit can read a composition's audio and place a **beat marker** on each detected beat.

- **Composition ▸ Detect beats** runs the detection.
- **Composition ▸ Clear beat markers** removes all beat markers in the composition.

:::note[Partially built]
Neither sensitivity tuning nor tapping beats in by hand is built yet. See
[Markers](/use/markers/).
:::
