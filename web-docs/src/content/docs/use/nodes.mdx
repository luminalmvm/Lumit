The **Node graph** draws the selected layer's [effect stack](/use/effects/) as boxes
joined left to right by wires. It is the same stack the Effect Controls panel lists, plus
one thing the list cannot do: wire a **driver** into a parameter.

<Shot file="graph-panel.png" caption="The Node graph: a layer's image chain wired left to right, with a driver feeding one of its parameters." eager/>

Open it from **Window ▸ Node graph**, or switch to the **Nodes** workspace. The panel
follows the layer selected in the Timeline.

## The image chain

Every graph starts with **Source**, one box per effect in stack order, and **Layer out**.
An effect's **Input** takes exactly one wire — the effect above it — so nothing here can
branch the picture, skip a stage, or merge two chains.

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Drop a node onto a wire | Inserts it at that point in the stack. |
| Drag a box's body | Moves it. A drag that starts inside the selection moves the whole selection. |
| Drag from a socket | Pulls out a wire; drop it on a socket that takes the same kind of value. |
| `Delete` | Removes the picked boxes. |

Two switches sit in the panel's toolbar. **Auto-wire** joins a dropped node to the wire in
hand as it lands. **Heal** reconnects the neighbours of a deleted box.

## Getting about the canvas

- **Middle-drag** pans. The wheel zooms about the pointer.
- **Frame all** fits every box on screen.
- A **primary drag on empty canvas** sweeps a selection box. Click replaces the selection,
  `Ctrl`-click toggles one, `Shift`-click adds, and `Ctrl+A` selects everything.
- **`Tab`** opens the node search. With a wire in hand the results are filtered to the
  kinds that wire can join, and picking one connects it.

Each box carries two small badges. **B** bypasses it — the box draws with a dashed border
and the picture passes through untouched. **E** exposes it, growing a hollow socket for
every parameter; a socket that already has a wire is drawn either way.

## Drivers

A **driver** makes a value rather than a picture, and a wire from a driver into a
parameter's socket makes that parameter follow the value.

| Driver | Hands out |
| --- | --- |
| [**Wiggle**](/effects/drivers/wiggle/) | A number wandering by an Amount at a Frequency. |
| [**Audio level**](/effects/drivers/audio-level/) | The loudness of a named layer's sound at this moment. |
| [**Colour cycle**](/effects/drivers/colour-cycle/) | A colour whose hue turns at a Rate. |
| [**Math**](/effects/drivers/math/) | Two numbers and an operation. |
| [**Remap**](/effects/drivers/remap/) | Maps a number from one range onto another, with a clamp. |
| [**Smooth**](/effects/drivers/smooth/) | Its input, softened over Time. |
| [**Points sample**](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) | Reads a points stream: how many points are alive, and how far the nearest one is from a position. |
| [**Layer points**](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) | Another layer's points, brought into this graph. |

Add one from the `Tab` search — they are grouped under **Drivers** — and wire its output to
a parameter's socket.

Wires never cross between layers. The two drivers that read another layer name it instead:
Audio level has an **Audio** row and Layer points a **Points layer** row, both ordinary
layer pickers.

A driven parameter's row in Effect Controls names the driver in place of a value. Its
keyframes are overridden while the wire is there, and come back when it is pulled.

## Wires and their colours

A wire's colour is the kind of value it carries; the **Types** legend in the corner of the
canvas lists them. Seven kinds share five colours, grouped as the legend groups them:

- **image · matte**
- **number**
- **colour**
- **shape · points**
- **audio**

A wire dropped on a socket of another kind is refused.

## Mattes and points

An effect's **Matte** socket takes a wire as well as the layer picker on its row. Only the
graph can feed an effect the layer's **own** masked picture at that point in the chain —
the Source box's matte output.

**Particulate** and the rest of the points family hand their points out on a socket of their
own, for another box to read. A box whose Points socket has nothing in it draws a **No
stream** mark and behaves as if it had been given an empty one.

## Seeing the picture at a box

Selecting exactly one effect — a box here, or a heading in Effect Controls — puts an **at
&lt;effect&gt;** chip over the Viewer's picture. Turn it on and the Viewer renders the
composition with that layer's stack stopping there. A driver offers no chip: it makes no
picture.

## The Nodes workspace

<Shot file="nodes-workspace.png" caption="The Nodes workspace: the graph across most of the window, a small Viewer and the Node panel down the right." />

The **Nodes** workspace puts the graph where the Timeline usually is, with a short
Timeline underneath and a right-hand column carrying a small Viewer over the **Node
panel**, which lists the picked box's parameter rows. There is no Project panel.

:::note[What the graph does not draw]
There are no branching image chains and no merge node: joining two pictures is what layers
and [blend modes](/use/blend-modes/) already do. The Layer out box draws an **Audio**
input that takes no wire — a layer's sound comes from its own footage.
:::
