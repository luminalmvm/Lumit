The **Node graph** draws the selected layer's [effect stack](/use/effects/) as boxes
joined left to right by wires. It is the same stack the Effect Controls panel lists, 
with the ability to wire a **driver** into parameters.

<Shot file="graph-panel.png" caption="The Node graph: a layer's image chain wired left to right, with a driver feeding one of its parameters." eager/>

Open it from **Window ▸ Node graph**, or switch to the **Nodes** workspace. The panel
follows the layer selected in the Timeline.

## The image chain

Every graph starts with **Source**, one box per effect in order, and **Layer out**.

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Drop a node onto a wire | Inserts it at that point in the stack. |
| Drag a box's body | Moves it. A drag that starts inside the selection moves the whole selection. |
| Drag from a socket | Pulls out a wire. Drop it on a socket that takes the same kind of value. |
| Drag a wire off an effect's input | Bypasses that effect. The picture goes past it and it keeps its place in the stack. |
| Drop a wire back on it | Switches it back on, and moves it to where the wire says. |
| Drag the wire off **Layer out** | The layer draws nothing until something is plugged back in. |
| `Delete` | Removes the picked boxes. |

Two switches sit in the panel's toolbar. **Auto-wire** joins a dropped node to the wire in
hand as it lands. **Heal** reconnects the neighbours of a deleted box.

## Getting about the canvas

- **Middle-drag** pans. The wheel zooms about the pointer.
- **Frame all** fits every box on screen.
- A **primary drag on empty canvas** sweeps a selection box. Click replaces the selection,
  `Ctrl`-click toggles one, `Shift`-click adds, and `Ctrl+A` selects everything.
- **Dragging an effect** from [Effects & presets](/panels/effects-presets/) onto the canvas
  adds it: an effect joins the stack, a driver lands where it is let go.
- A **right-click on empty canvas**, or **`Tab`** or **`Shift+A`** with the canvas focused,
  opens the node search at the pointer, as `ctrl+space` does. Each of the three can be turned
  off in [Settings ▸ Timeline](/use/settings/#timeline).
- **`ctrl+space`** opens the node search. With a wire in hand the results are filtered to the
  kinds that wire can join, and picking one connects it.

Each box carries two small badges. The checkbox enables or disables a specific node and the 
picture passes through untouched. The twirl opens the node, displaying a socket for every 
parameter. In a [node graph](/use/node-graphs/) an open node also carries the parameters' 
controls.

The checkbox and the wires say the same thing. A bypassed effect is drawn unwired, with
hollow sockets and the chain running from the box before it to the box after. Switching one
off in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/) unplugs it here, and unplugging it here
switches it off there.

## Groups

A **group** is a named, coloured frame behind some boxes. It follows them as they move, and
a group can sit inside another.

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl+G`, or **Group** in the toolbar | Frames the picked boxes and starts typing the group's name. Boxes picked inside a group are framed inside it, and whole groups picked together are framed as one. |
| Click a group's bar | Picks every box in it. |
| Drag the bar | Moves the group. |
| Double-click the bar, or `F2` | Renames it. |
| Right-click the bar | Opens its menu: **Rename**, **Colour**, **Save group** and **Ungroup**. |
| Drop boxes on a frame | Moves them into that group. |
| `Ctrl+Shift+G` | Takes the picked boxes out of the innermost group they are in. With a group's bar picked, removes that frame and leaves its boxes. |

The two shortcuts are the ones set for grouping layers in the Timeline. Zoomed out, a
group's name keeps its size on screen.

**Save group** asks for a name, a description, a colour and whether to show the group on
the shelf, and writes it to the preset library as a `.lumgrp` file: its effects and drivers, the wires among them and their layout. Source and Layer out
are never saved, and the groups inside it are. Picked boxes that are not a group become one
under that name. A saved group is listed in the node search under **Group**. Inserting one adds its effects to the
end of the layer's stack and puts its drivers where the search was opened.

A group shown on the **shelf** sits in a strip above the canvas, and a click there inserts
it.

The folder button beside **Save group** opens **Saved groups**. Click a group there to
insert it. Each row has a pin for the shelf, an edit mark and a delete mark, and **Show
folder** opens the library in the file manager.

## Notes

A **note** is a few words left on the canvas. It is not a box and is wired to nothing.

- **Add note** in the toolbar starts one in the middle of the canvas. Clicking away keeps
  it, and one left empty is not kept.
- **Drag** a note to move it.
- **Double-click** it to change its words.
- **The twirl** folds it down to its first line.
- **Right-click** it for **Edit**, **Collapse**, **Colour** and **Delete**.

## Drivers

A **driver** makes a value rather than a picture, and a wire from a driver into a
parameter's socket makes that parameter follow the value.

| Driver | Hands out |
| --- | --- |
| [**Wiggle**](/effects/drivers/wiggle/) | A random continuous number changing by an Amount at a Frequency. |
| [**Audio level**](/effects/drivers/audio-level/) | The loudness of a named layer's sound at this moment. |
| [**Colour cycle**](/effects/drivers/colour-cycle/) | A colour whose hue turns at a Rate. |
| [**Math**](/effects/drivers/math/) | Two numbers and an operation. |
| [**Remap**](/effects/drivers/remap/) | Maps a number from one range onto another, with a clamp. |
| [**Smooth**](/effects/drivers/smooth/) | Its input, softened over Time. |
| [**Points sample**](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) | Reads a points stream: how many points are alive, and how far the nearest one is from a position. |
| [**Layer points**](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) | Another layer's points, brought into this graph. |
| [**Clone index**](/effects/drivers/clone-index/) | Which copy Clone to points is stamping a layer as. |

Add one from the effect console, `ctrl+space`, where they are grouped under **Drivers**,
and wire its output to a parameter's socket.

Wires don't cross between layers. The two drivers that read another layer name it instead:
Audio level has an **Audio** row and Layer points a **Points layer** row, both ordinary
layer pickers.

A driven parameter's row in Effect Controls names the driver in place of a value. Its
keyframes are overridden while the wire is there, and come back when it is pulled.

## Wires and their colours

A wire's colour is the type of value it carries:

- **image · matte**
- **number**
- **colour**
- **shape · points**
- **audio**

A wire dropped on a socket of another kind is refused.

## Mattes and points

An effect's **Matte** socket takes a wire as well as the layer picker on its row. 

**Particulate** and the rest of the points family have a socket of their own, for another 
box to read the point stream.

A points effect added below a producer is wired to the nearest one above it. The **Points**
row at the top of its card in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/) shows the same
wire and changes it.

Some points effects, such as [Vary points](/effects/generate/vary-points/) and
[Pick points](/effects/generate/pick-points/), take a stream and hand a changed one on.
[Merge points](/effects/generate/merge-points/) has four Points sockets, and takes a
different producer above it on each.

## Seeing the picture at a box

Selecting exactly one effect, a box here or a heading in Effect Controls, puts an **at
&lt;effect&gt;** chip over the Viewer's picture. Turn it on and the Viewer renders the
composition with that layer's stack stopping there.

## The Nodes workspace

<Shot file="nodes-workspace.png" caption="The Nodes workspace: the graph across most of the window, a small Viewer and the Node panel down the right." />

The **Nodes** workspace puts the node graph in the centre of your view, to allow for work
dedicated to nodes. It also keeps the timeline visible to change the selected layer or the 
current frame. As well as the viewer to the right and the **Node panel**, which lists the 
picked box's parameter rows.

:::note[What the graph does not draw]
A layer's graph draws no branch and no merge: it is the layer's effect stack, seen as boxes.
Branching and merging live in a
[**node graph composition**](/use/node-graphs/), made with **New node graph**. The Layer
out box draws an **Audio**
input that currently takes no input, this is planned to be changed.
:::
