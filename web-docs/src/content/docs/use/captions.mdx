A **caption layer** is a Text layer that shows the words being said, a few at a time. Lumit
hears the speech in a layer, writes it down word by word, and cuts the words into captions.
The hearing runs on your own computer.

<Shot file="captions-panel.png" caption="The Captions panel." eager/>

## Make captions

1. Select a layer with sound: footage, a Sequence layer or a Precomp layer.
2. Choose **Layer ▸ Audio ▸ Create captions…**.
3. Pick the **Language**, the **Style** and how many **Lines**, then press **Create
   captions**.

The first time, Lumit asks to install the speech model. See [Addons](/use/addons/#captions-whisper).

Footage that has not been heard yet is transcribed first. The status line shows how far it
has got, how fast it is going and how long is left. **Cancel** stops it, and keeps every
file already finished. A file is only heard once: making captions from it again uses the
words already written down.

The new caption layer goes at the top of the stack, and the **Captions** panel opens on it.
**Window ▸ Captions** opens the panel at any time.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Language** | The language spoken. **Detect** hears it from the first speech. |
| **Speakers** | **Detect** tells the voices apart. **One** puts every word to one speaker. A number tells that many apart. |
| **Audio stream** | Which of the file's sounds to hear, for a file that carries several. |
| **Style** | How the captions look. See [styles](#styles). |
| **Lines** | One or two lines a caption. |
| **Characters per line** | The longest a line may be. |

**More** shows the rest:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Words per caption** | The most words a caption holds. **1** shows one word at a time. |
| **Gap** | Frames left empty between two captions. |
| **Shortest**, **Longest** | How long a caption stays up, in seconds. |
| **Reading speed** | Characters a second. A caption stays up longer to meet it, when there is room. |
| **Speaker names** | **Names** puts the speaker's name in front when the speaker changes. **Dashes** puts a dash in front of each speaker's line when two share a caption. |
| **Keep um and uh** | Keeps the sounds people make between words. |
| **Words to expect** | Names and words the speakers use, so they are heard and spelt right. |
| **Into English** | Writes what was said in English. Only with a speech pack that translates. |

Captions break at pauses, at the ends of sentences and where the speaker changes, and never
run past the limits.

## Transcripts

A **transcript** is the words said in a footage item, each with when it is said and who
says it. It is saved with the project, and every caption layer made from that footage is cut
from it.

Right-click footage in the Project panel and choose **Transcribe** to write one without
making captions. **Remove transcript** lets it go. Relinking the footage to a different file
removes it too.

## Captions follow the edit

A caption layer made from a layer stays linked to it. Cut, trim, move or slow down that
layer, or the clips in it, and the captions follow: each word stays on the moment it is
said, and words cut out of the edit leave the captions with them.

Captions imported from a file keep their own times on the caption layer.

## Edit captions

The **Captions** panel lists the captions of the selected caption layer, or of the top one
in the composition, in the order they show. The caption being said is marked. Click one to
move the playhead to it.

- Double-click a caption, or press `Enter`, to retype it. `Shift+Enter` starts a new line.
  Words you keep keep their timing.
- Double-click a time to change it.
- **Split** cuts the caption at the playhead, between the words either side of it.
- **Join** makes the selected captions one.
- **Add** puts a new caption at the playhead.
- `Delete` removes the selected captions.
- **Find** searches the captions. **Replace** changes what it finds, one at a time or **All**.
  **Match case** and **Whole word** narrow what is found.

The figure beside each caption is its reading speed, in characters a second. It turns
amber above the layer's **Reading speed**.

Twirl a caption layer open in the Timeline to see its captions as blocks. Drag an end to
retime one, or the middle to move it. Double-click one to retype it in the Captions panel.
Right-click one for **Split at the playhead**, **Join with next**, **Raise to top** and
**Delete**. Double-clicking a caption layer in the Viewer retypes the caption showing.

**Raise to top** moves the selected captions to the top of the picture, off a title at the
bottom. **Put back down** returns them. **Position…** moves them by a number of pixels.

Right-click a word in a caption to **Emphasise** it. The built-in styles colour it.

**Limits** sets the lines, characters per line and timing the layer keeps. **Make captions
again** cuts the captions again from their words, under those limits. Splits, joins and
retiming done by hand are not kept.

**More** holds **Import captions…**, the layer's **Language**, which subtitle files are
written with, **Speaker names** and **Make captions again**.

[Captioning a talking clip](/tutorials/captions/) goes through it step by step.

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl+Shift+D` | Split the caption at the playhead |
| `Ctrl+J` | Join the selected captions |
| `Ctrl+Alt+C` | Add a caption at the playhead |
| `Ctrl+Alt+↑`, `Ctrl+Alt+↓` | Go to the previous or next caption |

The first three work while the Captions panel has focus. All of them change in
[Settings ▸ Keymap](/use/settings/).

## Styles

A caption layer is styled like any Text layer: its font, size, fill, outline and background
in the [Text panel](/use/text/#style-it), its box in the Paragraph panel. Its animators can
follow the words as they are said.

**Style** in the Captions panel sets all of that at once:

| Style | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Plain** | White words with a thin dark outline. |
| **Box** | White words on a dark box, a line at a time. |
| **Highlight** | The word being said turns yellow. |
| **Karaoke** | The words fill with colour as they are said. |
| **Pop** | Each word pops in as it is said. |
| **Word box** | Bold capitals, with a box round the word being said. |
| **One word** | One large word at a time. |

**Save style…** keeps the current caption layer's look in the list.

## Subtitle files

**Export** in the Captions panel writes the captions as **SRT**, **WebVTT**, **Timed Text**
or plain **Text**, on the composition's clock. WebVTT carries the time of every word.

**File ▸ Import captions…** makes a caption layer from an `.srt`, `.vtt`, `.ttml`, `.dfxp`,
`.ass`, `.ssa` or `.lrc` file. Words without their own times are spread over each caption by
length.

The [export](/use/export/) dialog's **Captions** row chooses how caption layers leave with
the video: burned into the picture, as a subtitle file beside it, or carried inside it.

:::note[Limitation]
Captions are made from footage, Sequence and Precomp layers. Speech mixed into a
composition only as its sound mix is not heard on its own.
:::
