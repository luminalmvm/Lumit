**Puppet** lays a mesh over the opaque part of a layer and bends the picture between
**pins**. It bends the layer after its paint and masks, and before its effects.

For a walkthrough, see [Pinning a character with Puppet](/tutorials/puppet/).

## The pin tools

Four tools share one toolbar button. `Ctrl+P` arms it, and pressing again steps through
them.

| Tool | Its pin |
| --- | --- |
| **Puppet position pin** | Holds a spot of the picture, or moves it. |
| **Puppet starch pin** | Stiffens the mesh round it. |
| **Puppet overlap pin** | Says which part draws in front where the picture folds over itself. |
| **Puppet bend pin** | Turns and scales the mesh round it. |

With a pin tool armed, the selected layer shows its mesh as a wireframe.

- **Click** the mesh to place a pin of the armed tool's kind. A click on a pin places
  nothing.
- **Drag** a pin to move it. With the Bend pin tool, dragging a bend pin turns it and
  `Alt`-dragging scales it.
- `Delete` removes the pin under the pointer.
- `Escape` abandons a drag.
- **Right-click** a pin for **Rename…**, **Delete all pins** and **Delete pin**.

The wireframe and the pin follow a drag, and the picture bends when it is released. A
drag on a pin whose stopwatch is on sets a keyframe at the playhead.

## The mesh

The mesh is cut from the layer's alpha at the frame the first pin is placed on. Pixels
under 10 % opaque are outside it. The mesh is not saved in the project.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Density** | The size the mesh's triangles aim for, in the layer's pixels, from 2 to 500. Starts at 24. |
| **Expansion** | How far past the opaque edge the mesh reaches, in pixels, from 0 to 100. Starts at 3. |

Both are on the toolbar while a pin tool is armed. Changing one with a pinned layer
selected rebuilds that layer's mesh.

A mesh holds at most 1,500 points. A **Density** finer than that allows is coarsened to
fit, and a layer too large for the coarsest mesh is left unbent.

## Pins in the Timeline

A layer with pins has a **Puppet** heading in the Timeline, a row for each pin, and a row
under it for each of the pin's numbers. Every number row has a stopwatch. A pin is named
after its kind and numbered.

| Pin | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| **Position pin** | **Position x**, **Position y** |
| **Starch pin** | **Position x**, **Position y**, **Amount**: how stiff, from 0 to 100 % |
| **Overlap pin** | **Position x**, **Position y**, **Amount**: from −100 to 100 %, positive in front |
| **Bend pin** | **Position x**, **Position y**, **Rotation** in degrees, **Scale** from 1 to 1,000 % |

**Amount** starts at 0, so a new starch or overlap pin changes nothing until it is raised.

A starch, overlap or bend pin's own row carries its **extent**: how far it reaches, in
pixels, from 1 to 10,000. It starts at 50, cannot be keyframed, and is drawn as a circle
round the pin while the pin is dragged.

Double-click a pin's name to rename it. Right-click it for **Rename** and **Delete pin**.

A pin left outside the mesh after the mesh is rebuilt is drawn hollow and does nothing.

## Refusals

| Status line | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Select a layer to pin** | No layer is selected. |
| **Nothing opaque here** | The layer has no mesh at this frame. |
| **That point is outside the puppet's mesh** | The click missed the mesh. |

:::note[Limitation]
A pin's keyframes draw as diamonds on its Timeline lane and have no curve in the
[graph editor](/panels/graph-editor/). A pin placed on a puppet that is already bent
pulls its spot back to where it was before the bend. On a footage layer the mesh is cut
from each frame's own alpha.
:::
