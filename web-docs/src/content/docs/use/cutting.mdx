The **Cut** workspace arranges Lumit for cutting footage: a **source view** beside the
composition in the Viewer, and the **Cut timeline** across the bottom.

<Shot file="cut-workspace.png" caption="The Cut workspace: the Project panel, the source view beside the composition, and the Cut timeline." eager/>

The Cut timeline shows the open composition. Each track is a layer of that composition, and
the same composition opens in the [Timeline](/panels/timeline/) and the Audio timeline.

## Tracks

<Shot file="cut-timeline.png" caption="Picture tracks above the line and sound tracks below, with two dissolves and a fade out." />

- A picture track is a [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/). Picture tracks stand above
  the dividing line, and the top one wins the picture.
- A sound track is an audio-only Sequence layer. Sound tracks stand below the line.
- Any other layer draws as one plain bar on a track of its own.
- A footage layer with sound also draws its sound on a track below the line. Both tracks are
  the same layer.

With **Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Cut tracks in any order** on, the tracks stand in the
composition's layer order and there is no line. Drag a track's name up or down to move it.
With the setting off, a track moves only among the tracks on its side of the line.

Drag the lower edge of a track's header to make the track taller or shorter. Double-click
the edge to put it back. Heights are saved with the project.

Right-click the empty ground under the tracks for **Insert audio layer**, **Insert video
layer** and **Insert adjustment layer**. Each adds an empty track above the selected one, or
at the top. With nothing selected an audio layer goes to the bottom.

Double-clicking the bar of a Precomp layer opens its composition.

## The source view

Double-click footage in the [Project panel](/panels/project/) to load it into the source
view. Drag along the strip under the picture to move through it.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Play** | Plays the footage from the frame shown, with its sound. |
| **In**, `I` | Marks the frame shown as the start of the stretch to place. |
| **Out**, `O` | Marks the frame shown as its end. |
| **Insert**, `,` | Places the marked stretch at the playhead and moves everything after it later. |
| **Overwrite**, `.` | Places the marked stretch at the playhead, over what is there. |

With no marks set, the whole item is placed. The clip lands on the selected picture track,
or the lowest one, or a new track when the composition has none. The playhead moves to the
end of the placed clip.

In and Out are kept on the footage item and saved with the project. Setting them is one
undo step, **Mark In and Out**. An Out that is not after the In is **refused**.

A video with marks brings only its marked stretch wherever it is placed. Dropped in the
Timeline, its In lands on the composition's start. Dropped on a track, the clip holds the
marked stretch, and several files dropped together are spaced by their marked lengths.

`I` and `O` mark the source while the source view has the keyboard. With the Cut timeline
active they set the start and end of the work area at the playhead.

With the source view in front and the Viewer active, the transport keys play and step the
clip instead of the composition: `Space` plays it, `Page Up` and `Page Down` or `Ctrl+←`
and `Ctrl+→` step a frame, `Shift+Page Up` and `Shift+Page Down` step ten, and `Home` and
`End` go to its start and end.

Choosing any workspace other than Cut takes the source view away. **One view** in the Viewer
keeps the composition.

Footage dragged from the Project panel onto a track makes a clip where it is dropped.
Dropped on empty ground it makes a new track. Several files dropped together land one after
another. A still takes five seconds.

A clip placed past the end of the composition lengthens the composition to fit.

`F` loads the footage of the clip under the playhead into the source view, on the frame the
composition is showing. Double-clicking a clip does the same and shows the clip's own
stretch as In and Out. Neither changes the marks kept on the item.

## Linked clips

Footage with picture and sound is placed as two **linked clips**: the picture on a picture
track, its sound on a sound track. While **Linked** is on they select, move, trim and cut
together. `Alt`+click selects one alone. `Shift`+`L` turns **Linked** on and off.

The picture clip is silent, and its sound is heard from the linked clip. With **Settings ▸
Timeline ▸ Video sound in Cut** set to **With the picture**, the sound stays on the picture
clip and is drawn along its foot. A picture track whose audible switch is off keeps its sound
on linked clips either way.

Right-click a picture clip for **Detach audio**, which puts its sound on a sound track as a
linked clip, and **Attach audio**, which puts it back. **Attach audio** needs the sound clip
to cover the same stretch as the picture.

A sound file dropped on a picture track plays on that track, and sound clips move between
sound tracks and picture tracks. A picture clip never goes on a sound track.

Footage with several [audio streams](/use/audio/#audio-streams) places every stream, mixed,
unless the **Several audio streams arrive as** setting says otherwise. Right-click a clip
for **Split audio streams**, which puts each stream on a sound track of its own as a linked
clip, and **Merge audio streams**, which puts them back on one clip.

## Tools

<Clip name="cut-razor-ripple" caption="Two cuts with the razor through a picture clip and its linked sound, then Ripple delete on the piece between."/>

A tap of a tool's key arms it. Holding the key arms it for one gesture and goes back to the
tool before on release.

| Tool | Key | What a drag does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Select** | `V` | Moves clips in time and between tracks. An edge trims, and `Shift`+drag on an edge stretches. A drag over empty ground selects every clip it crosses. `Alt`+drag leaves the clips and moves copies. |
| **Razor** | `C` | A click cuts the clip under the pointer. `Shift`+click cuts every track. |
| **Ripple** | `B` | An edge trims, or with `Shift` stretches, and everything after the clip follows. |
| **Roll** | `N` | An edit point moves: one clip trims as the next extends. |
| **Slip** | `Y` | The clip shows earlier or later source, keeping its place and length. |
| **Slide** | `U` | The clip moves between its neighbours, which trim and extend to meet it. |

Pressing `B` during an edge drag with **Select** turns the ripple on for that drag, and
releasing it turns it off.

While an edge is dragged the clip draws a faint outline of its whole source, and a readout
beside the pointer counts the frames moved.

**Snap** holds a dragged edge to edit points, the playhead and markers. `S` turns it on and
off, and `Ctrl` suspends it. `Esc` abandons a drag.

## Switches

| Switch | Key | While it is on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Auto ripple** | `Shift`+`B` | `Delete` closes the gap, an edge dragged with **Select** is a ripple trim, and a moved clip takes everything after it along. |
| **Auto crossfade** | `Shift`+`X` | A clip dragged across the end of another keeps the overlap, which dissolves or crossfades. A clip dropped wholly over another is **refused**. |
| **Quantise** | `Alt`+`F8` | Sound clips move in whole frames. Off, a sound clip dragged alone lands where the pointer is, between frames. |

With **Auto crossfade** off, a moved clip overwrites what it lands on.

A sound clip that sits between two frames shows how far past the frame it starts.

**Quantise** is also **Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Sound moves in whole frames**. Sound clips in
the Audio timeline and Audio layers in the Timeline follow it.

## Editing at the playhead

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Q`, `W` | Removes the clip's start, or its end, up to the playhead and closes the cut. With nothing selected it acts across every track, to the nearest edit point. |
| `Alt`+`Q`, `Alt`+`W` | The same, leaving the gap. |
| `Shift`+`Q`, `Shift`+`W` | Rolls the previous, or the next, edit point to the playhead. |
| `Ctrl`+`K` | Cuts the selected clips' tracks at the playhead, or every track with nothing selected. |
| `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`K` | Cuts every track at the playhead. |
| `D` | Selects the clip under the playhead. |
| `↑`, `↓` | Moves the playhead to the previous or the next edit point. |
| `Alt`+`←`, `Alt`+`→` | Moves the selected clips one frame. With `Shift`, five. |
| `Alt`+`↑`, `Alt`+`↓` | Moves the selected clips up or down a track. |

## Removing and copying

- `Delete` removes the selected clips and leaves a gap. `Shift`+`Delete` removes them and
  closes it.
- A clip's menu has **Delete clip**, which leaves a gap, and **Ripple delete**, which closes
  it.
- Right-click a gap for **Close gap**.
- `;` lifts the work area: everything inside it is removed and the gap is left. `'` extracts
  it: the gap is closed.
- `Ctrl`+`C` copies the selected clips, `Ctrl`+`X` cuts them, and `Ctrl`+`V` pastes at the
  playhead, onto the selected track when there is one. The playhead moves to the end of what
  was pasted.

## Fades and dissolves

Two picture clips that overlap on a track **dissolve** across the overlap. Two sound clips
that overlap crossfade.

- `Ctrl`+`D` puts a one-second dissolve on the edit point nearest the playhead. Each clip is
  extended into its own source to make the overlap, as far as its media allows.
- Drag either edge of a dissolve to lengthen or shorten it.
- A clip's menu offers **Fade in** and **Fade out**, which bring a picture clip up from or
  down to transparent, and a sound clip from or to silence.
- With **Select**, drag the handle at a clip's top corner to set the length of its fade.
  Dragged back to the corner, the fade is removed.
- A dissolve between two clips with no spare media at the cut is **refused**.

## Effects and blend mode on a clip

A clip carries an effect stack of its own, which runs ahead of its track's. Selecting one
clip shows its stack in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), marked **Clip**. An
effect or a preset dragged from [Effects & presets](/panels/effects-presets/) onto a clip
lands on that clip, and a double-click there applies to the selected clip. Click a track's
name to see the track's own stack.

A clip with effects wears an **fx** mark. **Bypass effects** on the clip's menu turns its
stack off.

**Blend mode** on a picture clip's menu gives the clip a blend mode of its own. **From
layer** hands it back to the track's.

Keyframes on a clip's effects move with the clip. A cut gives each piece its own copy of
the stack.

Inside a dissolve each clip's effects fade out or in with the clip. The blend mode is that
of the clip that is more than half of the picture.

## Clips as compositions

**Open as composition** on a clip's menu turns the clip's footage into a composition and
opens it. The clip plays that composition from then on, showing what it showed. Its linked
sound clip is left as it was.

Double-clicking a clip that already plays a composition opens the composition.

## What a ripple moves

A ripple moves the clips after the edit on every unlocked Sequence layer, and the other
layers that start after it. A clip that straddles the point stays where it is.

A ripple that would move a clip onto one that stays is **refused**, and the status line says
so. Lock the track holding that clip, or clear the space.

## View

`=` and `-` zoom about the playhead. `\` fits the whole composition, and again returns to
the zoom before. During playback the view pages along when the playhead leaves it.
