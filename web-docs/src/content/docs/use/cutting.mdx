The **Cut** workspace arranges Lumit for cutting footage: the composition in the Viewer, with a
**source view** beside it once footage is loaded, and the **Cut timeline** across the bottom
with the [Mixer](/panels/audio/#mixer) at its right. **Window ▸ Panels ▸ Cut timeline** shows the
Cut timeline in any workspace.

<Shot file="cut-workspace.png" caption="The Cut workspace: the Project panel, the source view beside the composition, and the Cut timeline." eager/>

The Cut timeline shows the open composition. Each track is a layer of that composition, and
the same composition opens in the [Timeline](/panels/timeline/) and the
[Audio timeline](/panels/audio/#audio-timeline).

## Tracks

<Shot file="cut-timeline.png" caption="Picture tracks above the line and sound tracks below, with two dissolves and a fade out." />

- A picture track is a [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/). Picture tracks stand above
  the dividing line, and the top one wins the picture.
- A sound track is an audio-only Sequence layer. Sound tracks stand below the line.
- Any other layer with a picture draws as one plain bar on a track of its own. A Camera, a
  Light and a Null have no track here, and stay in the Timeline.
- A footage layer with sound also draws its sound on a track below the line. Both tracks are
  the same layer.
- A [Bus layer](/use/mixing/#buses) is a bus track, below the sound tracks, with the bus
  glyph before its name. Its bar draws the mix routed into it. A clip let go on a bus track
  is **refused**, and the status line says *Clips don't go on a bus track.*

A track's header carries the layer's name and switches: audible, solo, visible and lock on
a picture track, audible, solo and lock on a sound track, and visible and lock on a plain
bar. A footage layer's own sound track has no solo. Click the name to select the layer.
Double-click it, or press `Enter`, to rename the layer.

With **Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Cut tracks in any order** on, the tracks stand in the
composition's layer order and there is no line. Drag a track's name up or down to move it.
With the setting off, a track moves only among the tracks on its side of the line, and a bus
track only among the bus tracks.

Drag the lower edge of a track's header to make the track taller or shorter. Double-click
the edge to put it back. Heights are saved with the project. Drag the headers' right edge to
make them wider or narrower, from 120 to 480 pixels. Double-click that edge, or right-click
it for **Reset width**, to put them back.

A track's header has a **Vol** and a **Pan** slider on a sound track or a bus track and a
**Level** slider on a picture track, where the track is tall enough. Drag across one to
change it, with `Ctrl` for finer steps. Double-click puts it back. On a keyed property the
slider sets a key at the playhead.

A track wears its layer's label colour: its clips are tinted with it and their waveforms
drawn in it. Right-click a track's header, or a plain bar, for the layer's commands:
**Rename**, **Convert to sequence layer** or **Convert to footage layer**, its switches,
**Reset level**, **Select layer**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Minimise all tracks**,
**Reset height** on a track that has been dragged, **Duplicate**, **Label colour** and
**Delete**. A sound track has the menu a track head has in the
[Audio timeline](/panels/audio/#a-tracks-menu), less **Lane ▸**. Right-click a slider
for the Mixer's own rows: **Reset volume**, **Set level…**, **Add keyframe** and so on.

A title, a solid, an adjustment layer or a composition opens at the least height, and so does
every picture track in a short panel. **Minimise all tracks** puts every track at the least
height, and again puts them back. **Clip colour** on a clip's menu gives the picked clips a
colour of their own, and **Track colour** hands it back.

A picture clip shows a strip of its frames. A retimed clip's frames follow its speed. A sound
clip draws its waveform, the left channel over the right where the sound is stereo.

Right-click the empty ground under the tracks for **Insert title** (`Ctrl`+`T`), **Insert
solid**, **Insert audio layer**, **Insert bus**, **Insert Sequence layer** and **Insert
adjustment layer**. Each adds a track above the selected one, or at the top. The first two
are also under **Layer ▸ New**. With nothing selected an audio layer goes to the bottom. A
title or a solid is five seconds long from the playhead, and a title opens for typing with
the composition's name in it. A gap on a track has the title and the solid too, at the frame
under the pointer.

Double-clicking the bar of a Precomp layer opens its composition.

## The source view

Double-click footage in the [Project panel](/panels/project/) to load it into the source
view, which opens beside the composition. Its close button lets the footage go, and the
composition has the Viewer back. Drag along the strip under the picture to move through it.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Play** | Plays the footage from the frame shown, with its sound. |
| **Previous frame**, **Next frame** | Step the footage a frame. |
| **Go to In**, **Go to Out** | Stand the source on its marks. |
| **In**, `I` | Marks the frame shown as the start of the stretch to place. |
| **Out**, `O` | Marks the frame shown as its end. |
| **Insert**, `,` | Places the marked stretch at the playhead and moves everything after it later. |
| **Overwrite**, `.` | Places the marked stretch at the playhead, over what is there. |
| **Replace**, `Shift+R` | Places the footage from its In over the clip picked in the Cut timeline, which keeps its place and length. |
| **Fit to fill**, `Shift+F` | Places the marked stretch over the work area, at whatever speed fills it. |

A [multicam comp](/use/multicam/) loads as the angle viewer, which shows every angle at
once. `M` puts a marker on the footage at the frame shown.

With no marks set, the whole item is placed. The clip lands on the selected picture track,
or the lowest one, or a new track when the composition has none. The playhead moves to the
end of the placed clip. Replace lands on the picked clip's own track, and is **refused**
where the footage ends before the clip does. Each is one undo step. In a narrow source view
**Replace** and **Fit to fill** are on its menu only, and narrower still the steps go.

In and Out are kept on the footage item and saved with the project. Setting them is one
undo step, **Mark In and Out**. An Out that is not after the In is **refused**.

A video with marks brings only its marked stretch wherever it is placed. Dropped in the
Timeline, its In lands on the composition's start. Dropped on a track, the clip holds the
marked stretch, and several files dropped together are spaced by their marked lengths.

`I` and `O` mark the source while the source view has the keyboard. With the Cut timeline
active they set the start and end of the work area at the playhead. `Shift+I` and `Shift+O`,
**Go to In** and **Go to Out** on the source view's menu, stand the source on its marks.

With the source view in front and the Viewer active, the transport keys play and step the
clip instead of the composition: `Space` plays it, `Page Up` and `Page Down` or `Ctrl+←`
and `Ctrl+→` step a frame, `Shift+Page Up` and `Shift+Page Down` step ten, and `Home` and
`End` go to its start and end.

Choosing any workspace other than Cut takes the source view away. **One view** in the Viewer
keeps the composition.

Footage dragged from the Project panel onto a track makes a clip where it is dropped.
Dropped on empty ground it makes a new track, named for its kind: **Picture 1**, **Sound 1**
and so on. Several files dropped together land one after another on one track, in the
Project panel's order. Hold `Shift` to give each a track of its own, all at the drop point.
A still takes five seconds, and so does a solid. Files dragged in from outside Lumit are
imported and land the same way. While dragging, the clip-to-be shows where it will land, and
its start or end snaps as a moved clip does. A composition with no layers reads *Drop
footage here.* across the tracks.

A composition or a solid dragged from the Project panel goes down as one clip, on the
picture track under the pointer or a new one.

A clip placed past the end of the composition lengthens the composition to fit.

`F` loads the footage of the clip under the playhead into the source view, on the frame the
composition is showing. Double-clicking a clip, or **Open in Source** on its menu, does the
same and shows the clip's own stretch as In and Out. None of them changes the marks kept on
the item.

## Linked clips

Footage with picture and sound is placed as two **linked clips**: the picture on a picture
track, its sound on a sound track. While **Linked** is on they select, move, trim and cut
together. `Alt`+click selects one alone. `Shift`+`L` turns **Linked** on and off.

The picture clip is silent, and its sound is heard from the linked clip. With **Settings ▸
Timeline ▸ Video sound in Cut** set to **With the picture**, the sound stays on the picture
clip and is drawn along its foot. A picture track whose audible switch is off keeps its sound
on linked clips either way.

Right-click a sound clip for **Normalise**, which sets the gain of every picked clip so its
loudest sample lands at **Normalise to** in [Settings](/use/settings/#audio), as one undo step,
**Normalise loudness ▸**, which does the same for a measured loudness of −14, −16 or −23 LUFS,
and **Reset gain**, which puts it back at 0 dB. With several sound clips picked, **Normalise
together** and **Normalise loudness together ▸** move them all by one change. The loudest
lands on the target and the rest keep their balance. **Keep pitch** is the
[track's setting](/use/retime/#sound), and **Add audio effect ▸** adds an
[audio effect](/use/audio/#audio-effects) to the clip.

Right-click a picture clip for **Detach audio**, which puts its sound on a sound track as a
linked clip, and **Attach audio**, which puts it back. **Attach audio** needs the sound clip
to cover the same stretch as the picture. Where a picture clip has several rows about its
sound, they are under **Sound ▸**.

A sound file dropped on a picture track plays on that track, and sound clips move between
sound tracks and picture tracks. A picture clip never goes on a sound track.

Footage with several [audio streams](/use/audio/#audio-streams) places every stream, mixed,
unless the **Several audio streams arrive as** setting says otherwise. Right-click a clip
for **Split audio streams**, which puts each stream on a sound track of its own as a linked
clip, and **Merge audio streams**, which puts them back on one clip.

## Tools

<Clip name="cut-razor-ripple" caption="Two cuts with the razor through a picture clip and its linked sound, then Ripple delete on the piece between."/>

The strip above the tracks carries the playhead's time, which can be typed into, the tools
and the [switches](#switches). Right-click the strip for the tools and switches by name. In
a narrow panel the ones that do not fit are under the mark at the strip's end.

A tap of a tool's key arms it. Holding the key arms it for one gesture and goes back to the
tool before on release.

| Tool | Key | What a drag does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Select** | `V` | Moves clips in time and between tracks. An edge trims, and `Shift`+drag on an edge stretches. A drag over empty ground selects every clip it crosses. `Alt`+drag leaves the clips and moves copies. `Ctrl`+`A` selects every clip. |
| **Razor** | `C` | A click cuts the clip under the pointer. `Shift`+click cuts every track. |
| **Ripple** | `B` | An edge trims, or with `Shift` stretches, and everything after the clip follows. |
| **Roll** | `N` | An edit point moves: one clip trims as the next extends. |
| **Slip** | `Y` | The clip shows earlier or later source, keeping its place and length. |
| **Slide** | `U` | The clip moves between its neighbours, which trim and extend to meet it. |

Pressing `B` during an edge drag with **Select** turns the ripple on for that drag, and
releasing it turns it off.

While an edge is dragged the Viewer shows the frame at the edge, the clip draws a faint
outline of its whole source, and a readout beside the pointer counts the frames moved.

**Snap** holds a dragged edge to the start and end of every clip, the playhead and markers.
`S` turns it on and off, and `Ctrl` suspends it. `Esc` abandons a drag, and with no drag
under way it deselects the clips.

## Vegas drags

**Settings ▸ Shortcuts ▸ Clip drags in Cut** set to **Vegas** changes what a drag with
**Select** does. The **Vegas** shortcut preset sets it.

| Drag | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl`+drag a clip | Moves copies. A `Ctrl`+click still adds to the selection. |
| `Ctrl`+drag an edge | Stretches the clip. |
| `Alt`+drag a clip | Slips it. |
| `Ctrl`+`Alt`+drag a clip | Slides it. |
| `Shift` held | Suspends snapping. |
| Drag on empty track space | Sets the work area. `Ctrl` held draws a selection box. |
| `Shift`+click a clip | Selects every clip from the last one clicked, across the tracks between. |

A click on the tracks also moves the playhead there.

With the Vegas preset the wheel zooms with no key held and scrolls the tracks with `Ctrl`.
Its keys are under [The Vegas shortcuts](#the-vegas-shortcuts).

Choosing **Vegas** when Lumit first opens loads the Vegas preset and turns on **Clip drags in
Cut** and **Auto crossfade**.

## Switches

| Switch | Key | While it is on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Auto ripple** | `Shift`+`B` | `Delete` closes the gap, an edge dragged with **Select** is a ripple trim, and a moved clip takes everything after it along. |
| **Auto crossfade** | `Shift`+`X` | A clip dragged across the end of another keeps the overlap, which dissolves or crossfades. A clip dropped over the whole of another, or inside one, overwrites it. An edge pulled into the clip it meets dissolves into it. |
| **Quantise** | `Alt`+`F8` | Sound clips move in whole frames. Off, a sound clip dragged alone lands where the pointer is, between frames. |

With **Auto crossfade** off, a moved clip overwrites what it lands on.

A switch turned on or off from the keyboard says so on the status line.

A sound clip that sits between two frames shows how far past the frame it starts.

**Quantise** is also **Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Sound moves in whole frames**. Sound clips in
the Audio timeline and Audio layers in the Timeline follow it.

## Editing at the playhead

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Q`, `W` | Removes the clip's start, or its end, up to the playhead and closes the cut. With nothing selected it acts across every track, to the nearest edit point. |
| `Alt`+`Q`, `Alt`+`W` | The same, leaving the gap. |
| `Shift`+`Q`, `Shift`+`W` | Rolls the previous, or the next, edit point to the playhead. |
| `Ctrl`+`K` | Cuts the selected clips' tracks at the playhead, or every track with nothing selected. The pieces stay selected. |
| `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`K` | Cuts every track at the playhead. |
| `D` | Selects the clip under the playhead. |
| `↑`, `↓` | Moves the playhead to the previous or the next edit point. |
| `Alt`+`←`, `Alt`+`→` | Moves the selected clips one frame. With `Shift`, five. |
| `Alt`+`↑`, `Alt`+`↓` | Moves the selected clips up or down a track. |

## Removing and copying

- `Delete` removes the selected clips and leaves a gap. `Shift`+`Delete` removes them and
  closes it. With no clip selected, `Delete` removes nothing, unless the track was picked by
  its header.
- A clip's menu has **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste** and **Split**, **Delete clip**, which
  leaves a gap, and **Ripple delete**, which closes it. **Split** cuts at the playhead and
  is there while the playhead is over the clip. **Select to end** selects the clip and
  every clip after it, and **Select layer** every clip on its track.
- **Trim to playhead ▸** on a clip's menu has **Trim start**, **Trim end**, **Ripple trim
  start** and **Ripple trim end**, the same edits as `Alt`+`Q`, `Alt`+`W`, `Q` and `W`.
- A cut that changed nothing, where the clip after carries straight on from the clip
  before, is drawn dotted. **Join** on either clip's menu takes it away.
- Right-click a gap for **Paste**, **Close gap**, **Insert title**, **Insert solid**,
  **Select layer**, **Select all**, **Add marker here**, **Work area starts here**, **Work
  area ends here** and **Clear work area**.
- Right-click the ground under the last track for **Paste**, the insert rows, **Select all**
  and the same marker and work area rows.
- `;` lifts the work area: everything inside it is removed and the gap is left. `'` extracts
  it: the gap is closed. **Lift work area** and **Extract work area** on the ruler's menu do
  the same, and **Split all tracks**, `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`K`, cuts every track at the playhead.
- `Ctrl`+`C` copies the selected clips, `Ctrl`+`X` cuts them, and `Ctrl`+`V` pastes at the
  playhead, onto the selected track when there is one. The playhead moves to the end of what
  was pasted.
- `Ctrl`+`D`, or **Duplicate** on a clip's menu, places a copy of the selected clips straight
  after them, or at the end of their tracks where something is in the way.

## Fades and dissolves

Two picture clips that overlap on a track **dissolve** across the overlap. Two sound clips
that overlap crossfade.

- `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`D` puts a one-second dissolve on the edit point nearest the playhead. Each
  clip is extended into its own source to make the overlap, as far as its media allows.
- Drag either edge of a dissolve to lengthen or shorten it.
- With **Linked** on, the sound linked to either clip crossfades at the same edit point, or
  fades where nothing meets it.
- **Transition ▸** on a clip's menu offers **Fade in** and **Fade out**, which bring a
  picture clip up from or down to transparent, and a sound clip from or to silence. Each is
  one second long, and goes on every selected clip.
- Where another clip meets the end nearer the pointer, a sound clip's **Transition ▸** offers
  **Add dissolve** for that edit point.
- A fade or a dissolve already in place is offered as **Remove fade in**, **Remove fade
  out** or **Remove dissolve**.
- With **Select**, drag the handle at a clip's top corner to set the length of its fade.
  A readout beside the pointer gives the length in frames. Dragged back to the corner, the
  fade is removed.
- An edit point on a picture track can be a wipe rather than a dissolve. **Transition ▸** on a
  clip's menu lists **Dissolve**, **Linear wipe**, **Radial wipe**, **Iris wipe**, **Card
  wipe**, **Venetian blinds** and **Luma fade**, and **Remove transition** where there is an
  overlap. The effect runs over the outgoing clip with the incoming clip beneath it, from
  nothing to everything across the overlap.
- Right-click an overlap on a picture track to change what it is. **Effect controls** on that
  menu opens its rows in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), marked **Transition**,
  less the ones the overlap decides. Bypassed there, it is a dissolve again.
- **Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Default transition** picks what `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`D` adds, and what a
  clip dragged across another under **Auto crossfade** takes.
- Right-click a fade, a dissolve or a crossfade for its curve: **Linear**, **Fast**,
  **Slow**, **Smooth** or **Sharp**. **Length…** on the same menu types its length in frames,
  and the last row removes it.
- A dissolve between two clips with no spare media at the cut is **refused**.

## Effects and blend mode on a clip

A clip carries an effect stack of its own, which runs ahead of its track's. Selecting one
clip shows its stack in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), marked **Clip**. An
effect or a preset dragged from [Effects & presets](/panels/effects-presets/) onto a clip
lands on that clip, and a double-click there applies to the selected clip. Click a track's
name to see the track's own stack.

Every clip has an **fx** button at its top right, lit while the clip has effects that are on.
Clicking it selects the clip and shows its stack, as **Effect controls** on the clip's menu
does. **Bypass effects** on the clip's menu turns the stack off, and **Use effects** turns it
back on.

A picture clip also has a **Pan/Crop** button, and **Pan/Crop…** on its menu. It puts a
[Crop](/effects/utility/crop/) and a **Transform** on the clip, where it has none, and opens
the **Pan/Crop** window: the clip's frame with a box over the part that fills the picture.

- Drag inside the box to pan, a corner or an edge to zoom, and the knob above it to turn it.
- **X**, **Y**, **Width**, **Height** and **Rotation** type the box exactly.
- **Lock aspect** keeps its proportions, and **Fill frame** puts it back over the whole frame.
- Right-click the picture for **Fill frame**, **Centre**, **Reset rotation** and **Lock
  aspect**.

The box writes the Transform's anchor, scale and rotation, at the playhead on a keyed
property. The Crop's edges are set in Effect controls.

**Blend mode** on a picture clip's menu gives the clip a blend mode of its own. **From
layer** hands it back to the track's.

Keyframes on a clip's effects move with the clip. A cut gives each piece its own copy of
the stack.

Inside a dissolve each clip's effects fade out or in with the clip. The blend mode is that
of the clip that is more than half of the picture.

## Speed

Each picture clip carries its speed as a line across it, in per cent, which is edited where it
lies. The **Speed** switch on the strip, `Shift`+`E`, shows the lines on every clip. It is
off to begin with. **Speed line** on a clip's **Speed** menu shows the line on that clip
alone, and a retimed clip shows its line either way. The points and curves behave as in the
[speed envelope](/use/retime/#speed-on-a-clip).

| On a picture clip's **Speed** menu | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Speed…** | Sets the selected picture clips to one speed. |
| **Reset speed** | Sets them back to 100%. |
| **Copy speed** | Copies this clip's speed line. |
| **Paste speed** | Gives the selected picture clips the copied line, stretched to each one's length. |

A picture clip's linked sound clip plays at normal speed.

## Gain

A sound clip's level is a line across it, from −60 dB at the foot to +24 dB at the top. Drag
it to turn the clip up or down, with `Ctrl` for finer steps. Double-click it to put it back
at 0 dB. A readout beside the pointer gives the level, and the waveform follows it.

A picture clip's opacity is the same line along its top edge. Drag it down to make the clip
see-through, and double-click it to put it back at full. Below full the line shows across
the clip.

## Takes

A clip can hold several takes: other footage shown in the same place, as in Vegas.

- Drop footage on a clip with `Alt` held to add it as a take. The clip shows it.
- `T` shows the next take and `Shift`+`T` the previous one, on the selected clips.
- **Take** on a clip's menu lists them, with the one showing ticked. **Delete take** removes
  the one showing.

A take keeps the clip's place, length, speed, effects and fades. A linked sound clip of the
same footage changes take with it.

## Paste attributes

**Paste attributes** on a clip's menu, or `Ctrl`+`Alt`+`V`, gives the selected clips the
copied clip's effects, gain, opacity, blend mode, fades and speed. Copy the clip first with
`Ctrl`+`C`.

## Envelopes

A sound track's volume and pan can be drawn as lines across the whole track, as in the
[Audio timeline](/panels/audio/#audio-timeline). `Shift`+`V` shows and hides the volume envelope
and `Shift`+`P` the pan envelope, on the selected clips' tracks or the track last clicked.
**Volume envelope** and **Pan envelope** on a sound track's header menu do the same for that
track. A track whose volume or pan has keys shows its envelope to begin with.

- Drag the line up or down. Between two keys both move.
- Drag a key to move it in time and value. `Shift` or `Alt` held moves it in time only, and
  `Ctrl` gives finer steps with its time held.
- Double-click the line, or `Ctrl`+click it, to plant a key. `Alt`+click removes one.
- Double-click a key to put it back at 0 dB or centre.
- The value shows beside the pointer while dragging.

Right-click a key for **Set value…**, **Reset** and **Delete**. **Set value…** opens a field
at the key, `Enter` keeps what is typed. Right-click the line for **Add point**, the curve of
the stretch under the pointer (**Linear**, **Fast**, **Slow**, **Smooth**, **Sharp** or
**Hold**), **Thin points**, **Flip all points** on pan, **Copy volume** and **Paste
volume** (or pan), **Reset volume** or **Reset pan**, and the envelope's own switch, which
hides it. **Thin points** takes away the keys the line does not need.

An envelope shows on a track tall enough to take it, under the fade handles. A locked
track's envelope cannot be dragged, and a property driven by an expression has none.

## Groups

`Ctrl`+`G`, or **Group** on a clip's menu, puts the selected clips in a group. While
**Linked** is on, selecting one clip in a group selects the rest, so they move, trim, cut and
delete together. A cut through a group leaves two groups, one each side. `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`G`,
or **Ungroup**, takes the selected clips out of their group. A copy of a clip is outside the
original's group.

## Clips as compositions

**New composition from clip** on a clip's menu turns the clip's footage into a composition and
opens it. The clip plays that composition from then on, showing what it showed. Its linked
sound clip is left as it was. On a clip that already plays a composition the row reads
**Open composition**, and double-clicking the clip does the same.

With more than one clip picked, the row reads **New composition from selection**. The clips
move into a new composition as long as their span, on the same tracks with their effects, fades
and speed, and their linked sound comes too. One clip playing it takes their place. It goes on
the first of their tracks if nothing there would act on it twice, and on a new track otherwise.

## Freezing a frame

**Freeze frame…** on a clip's menu asks how long and holds the frame at the playhead, two
seconds until it is changed. The clip is cut there, a clip of the same source goes between the
halves with its speed line flat at 0, and everything after it moves later. Linked sound is cut
too, with silence under the hold, unless **Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Sound plays on through a
freeze** is on. In the Audio timeline the same row holds a sound clip.

## What a ripple moves

A ripple moves the clips after the edit on every unlocked Sequence layer, and the other
layers that start after it. A clip that straddles the point stays where it is.

A ripple that would move a clip onto one that stays is **refused**, and the status line says
so. Lock the track holding that clip, or clear the space.

## Refused edits

An edit that cannot be made changes nothing, and the status line says why.

| The status line says | When |
| --- | --- |
| *That would overlap another clip.* | A clip the edit carries along would land on one that stays. |
| *Clips can't start before the composition.* | A clip would start before the composition does. |
| *A track this edit needs is locked.* | A layer the edit has to change is locked. |
| *No more footage in that direction.* | An edge would pass the clip's other edge, a neighbour would be left with no length, or a slip would run off the source. |
| *Clips with a Retime expression can't be cut.* | The clip's Retime is driven by an expression. |
| *Nothing there to edit.* | There is no clip under the razor, no gap at that time, or no edit point at that edge. |
| *Picture goes on picture tracks, sound on sound tracks.* | A clip is moved or dropped onto a track of the other kind. |

| `Alt`+`←`, `Alt`+`→` | Moves the playhead one frame. With `Ctrl`, to the previous or the next edit point. |
| `↑`, `↓` | Zooms in and out. |
| `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`U` | Turns **Linked** on and off. |
| `Ctrl`+`L` | Turns **Auto ripple** on and off. |
| `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`X` | Turns **Auto crossfade** on and off. |
| `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`E` | Turns **Speed** on and off. |
| `V`, `P` | Shows and hides the volume and the pan envelope. |
| `Shift`+`V` | Picks **Select**. |
| `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`V` | Pastes a clip's attributes onto the selected clips. |
| `G`, `U` | Groups and ungroups the selected clips. |
| `Q` | Turns looping on and off. |

**Slide** has no key in the Vegas preset.

## View

`=` and `-` zoom about the playhead. `\` fits the whole composition, and again returns to
the zoom before. During playback, or after a key moves the playhead, the view pages along
when the playhead leaves it.

Markers run as lines down through the tracks, and a region, a marker with a length, as a
faint band. `R` adds a region over the work area.

The ruler carries the playhead, the work area and [markers](/use/markers/) as the
[Timeline's](/panels/timeline/#moving-about) does, and the strip above it shows the whole
composition with a window on the part in view. The slider on the bottom bar zooms.
`Shift`+wheel scrolls sideways, `Ctrl`+wheel zooms time about the pointer, and a
middle-button drag pans.
