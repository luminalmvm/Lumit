Lumit composites in **scene-linear Rec. 709**, in floating point, whatever your footage
arrived in.

Without a configuration Lumit reads what footage files contain and shows the working
space encoded to sRGB. An **OpenColorIO configuration** is for when that is not enough.

## Choose a configuration

**File ▸ Project settings… ▸ Colour** holds one row, **Configuration**, with *Choose…* and
*Clear* beside it.

Under the row explains what happened, what was loaded, or why the configuration
could not be used.

Below it, **Working space** states what the arithmetic runs in.

The configuration is a property of the **project**, saved in the `.lum`. A
missing or unusable configuration never stops a project opening: the preview falls back to
the built-in handling and every assigned colour-space name is kept.

## Say what a footage item arrived in

Right-click a footage row in the [Project panel](/panels/project/) and open the **Colour
space** submenu.

- **As the file says** is the default.
- Under **From the configuration** are the configuration's own colour-space names. Pick
  the one the footage was shot or graded in.

## Look at it through a view

The Viewer bar carries a **colour-pipeline picker**. What it reads is the current view/face.

| Face | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Linear → sRGB** | The built-in transform. |
| ***view* — *display*** | A view out of the configuration, on one of its displays. |
| **Config not in force** | The project names a configuration that cannot be used; the built-in transform is used. |

Open it and the built-in transform is the first row, then one heading per display. 
**Tone mapping** sits at the foot, if enabled in Settings.

The Viewer's exposure and tone mapping settings never reach an export; while either is engaged
the badge says *preview*. These are both solely for helping viewing while compositing.

## Writing it out

The export dialogue's **Colour** section gains the configuration's spaces in its **Colour
space** list, under a *From the configuration* heading. **Managed by** reads the
configuration's path.

- A file written through the configuration's transform is left **untagged**.
- An export naming a space this build cannot transform to is **refused**.
