By default Lumit composites in **scene-linear Rec. 709**, in floating point, whatever your
footage arrived in.

Without a configuration Lumit reads what footage files contain and shows the working
space encoded to sRGB. An **OpenColorIO configuration** is for when that is not enough.

## Project settings

**File ▸ Project settings…** (`Ctrl+Alt+Shift+K`) holds the colour depth, the
configuration and the working space. They are saved in the `.lum`, and a change to one is
an undo step.

## Colour depth

**Rendering ▸ Colour depth** sets how many bits each channel is composited in.

| Depth | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **8 bpc** | Nought to one. Anything brighter than white is clipped. |
| **16 bpc** | Half floats, which keep values above white. The default. |
| **32 bpc** | Full floats: the range of 16 bpc at a higher precision, and twice the memory. |

**In use on this machine** appears under the row when the graphics card cannot work at the
depth chosen. The project keeps the depth that was set.

## Choose a configuration

**File ▸ Project settings… ▸ Colour** holds the row **Configuration**, with *Choose…* and
*Clear* beside it.

Under the row explains what happened, what was loaded, or why the configuration
could not be used.

The configuration is a property of the **project**, saved in the `.lum`. A
missing or unusable configuration never stops a project opening: the preview falls back to
the built-in handling and every assigned colour-space name is kept. *Choose…* on a
configuration that has moved points the project at it again.

A [packed project](/use/projects/#packed-projects) carries the configuration and the
look-up tables in its folder.

## Working space

**Working space**, under the configuration, sets the primaries layers are composited in.

- **Linear Rec. 709** is the default.
- **The configuration's scene-linear space** composites in the space the configuration
  names as scene-linear. It is offered while a configuration is loaded. With no usable
  configuration, or one that names no scene-linear space, the project composites in
  Rec. 709.

## Say what a footage item arrived in

Right-click a footage row in the [Project panel](/panels/project/), choose **Interpret
footage…** and open the **Colour** tab.

- **As the file says** is the default.
- The rest are the configuration's own colour-space names. Pick the one the footage was
  shot or graded in. A space Lumit can't read footage from is greyed out, with the reason
  on hover.
- A name the loaded configuration does not have stays on the list.

**Linear light** is read only while the list says **As the file says**, and **Preserve
RGB** switches the list off. See [Importing media](/use/importing/).

## Look at it through a view

The Viewer bar carries a **colour-pipeline picker**. What it reads is the current view/face.

| Face | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Linear → sRGB** | The built-in transform. |
| ***view* — *display*** | A view out of the configuration, on one of its displays. |
| **Config not in force** | The project names a configuration that cannot be used; the built-in transform is used. |

Open it and the built-in transform is the first row, then one heading per display. 
**Tone mapping** sits at the foot, if enabled in Settings, and **Project settings…** under
it.

A view the configuration cannot make is listed and greyed out, with the reason on hover.
Where the configuration is not in force, the reason is the second line of the list.

The Viewer's exposure and tone mapping settings never reach an export; while either is engaged
the badge says *preview*. These are both solely for helping viewing while compositing.

## Writing it out

The export dialogue's **Colour** section gains the configuration's spaces in its **Colour
space** list, under a *From the configuration* heading. **Managed by** reads the
configuration's path.

- A file written through the configuration's transform is left **untagged**.
- An export naming a space this build cannot transform to is **refused**.
- A space the configuration cannot deliver is listed and disabled.

## On a layer

Four effects run a layer through the project's configuration:
[OCIO colour space transform](/effects/colour/ocio-colour-space-transform/),
[OCIO display transform](/effects/colour/ocio-display-transform/),
[OCIO look transform](/effects/colour/ocio-look-transform/) and
[OCIO file transform](/effects/colour/ocio-file-transform/).

## What a configuration is refused for

A configuration that asks for something Lumit does not do is **refused by name**. Where
one colour space or view needs it, that name is greyed out and the rest of the
configuration is used. A configuration written for a profile version above 2 is not in
force at all.

- Context variables in a file path.
- Inverting a 3D look-up table.
- `FixedFunctionTransform`, `GradingRGBCurveTransform`, `ExposureContrastTransform`,
  `LookTransform` and `DisplayViewTransform`.
- A `CDLTransform` that picks its grade by id.
- A `BuiltinTransform` style Lumit does not have.
- A look-up table in a format Lumit does not read, or a CLF table with a process node
  Lumit does not have.

:::note[Not built yet]
A standalone looks picker. Lumit's blend modes and tone-map curve read pixels as Rec. 709
whatever the configuration's primaries are.
:::

## Grade a layer

The **Grade** panel draws the selected layer's colour effects as wheels, primaries, curves
and a look. It is in the **Colour** workspace, and **Window ▸ Panels ▸ Grade** opens it.
With several layers selected, the header counts them and every change lands on all of them
as one undo step. Camera, light, null, audio and bus layers take no grade.

| Section | What it holds | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Balance** | Three wheels: lift, gamma and gain. The bar under a wheel moves its three channels together. | [Colour balance](/effects/colour/colour-balance/) |
| **Primaries** | **Exposure**, **Contrast**, **Saturation**, **Temperature** and **Tint**. | [Exposure](/effects/colour/exposure/), [Contrast](/effects/colour/contrast/), [Saturation](/effects/colour/saturation/), [Temperature](/effects/colour/temperature/) |
| **Curves** | One curve each for the master and the red, green and blue channels. | [Curves](/effects/colour/curves/) |
| **Look** | A look from the preset library, and a **LUT** file with *Choose…*. | [LUT](/effects/colour/lut/) |

A control adds its effect to the layer the first time it is touched, as one undo step with
the value. The effects stack in this order: Exposure, Temperature, Colour balance,
Contrast, Saturation, Curves, LUT. A keyed value takes a key at the playhead.

- **Before**, in the header, shows the picture without the grade while it is held.
- **Reset grade**, in the header, puts every control back to its default.
- A right-click on a section heading or a row has **Reset**, **Bypass** and **Remove** for
  that part, and **Reveal in Effect controls**. A row also has the copy, keyframe and
  expression rows of a property.
- A right-click anywhere on the panel has **Reset grade**, **Bypass grade**, **Reveal in
  Effect controls**, **Copy grade**, **Paste grade** and **Remove grade**.
- The eyedropper beside **Temperature** sets **Temperature** and **Tint** from a pixel that
  should be grey. Drag across the picture to watch it change, and let go to keep it. A keyed
  row takes a key at the playhead. Later effects on the layer are left out of the sum, so
  a pixel changed by one may need a second pick.
- The picker in the **Look** section lists the presets in the **Looks** group of the
  [preset library](/use/presets/). Picking one replaces the colour effects on every selected
  layer, as one undo step. **Save look…** at its foot writes the layer's colour effects as a
  preset in that group, which it makes on the first save. A look of the same name is
  replaced after asking.
- **Copy grade** (`Alt`+`Shift`+`C`) and **Paste grade** (`Alt`+`Shift`+`V`) are on the panel's
  menu and under **Edit**. A paste replaces the colour effects on every selected layer that
  draws a picture, as one undo step.
