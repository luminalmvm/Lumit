Open the settings window from **Edit ▸ Settings…**, or with `Ctrl+Alt+;`. On macOS the row
is in the **Lumit** menu.

<Shot file="settings.png" caption="The settings window, on the Appearance page." eager/>

The search box in the title strip finds a setting by its name, its section or its page, and
lists what it finds under the page names. On the Shortcuts page it searches the shortcuts instead.

Changes apply at once. A dot beside a setting's name marks one changed from how it shipped,
and the same dot beside a page's name says the page holds one.
Right-click a row for **Reset** and, where there is a number, path or colour, **Copy value**.
**Reset page**, at the foot of the window or on a right-click of a page's or section's name,
puts every setting on that page back, after asking. On **Preview and cache** it leaves the
cache budgets as they are. **Addons** and **Account** have no **Reset page**.

The window resizes from its corner and opens where it was left.

## General

- **Display**: **Language**. **Same as this machine** follows the system's. The others are
  English, العربية, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Қазақша, Polski, Português (Brasil),
  Українська, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
- **Workspace**: **Welcome screen on launch**, whether Lumit opens on the
  [welcome screen](/start/install/#the-welcome-screen). **Keep running in the background**:
  closing the window leaves Lumit in the system tray, or the menu bar on macOS, where paired
  phones still see it. **Open Lumit** on the tray icon's menu brings the window back and
  **Quit Lumit** ends Lumit. Opening Lumit again also brings the window back, which is the
  only way on a desktop with no tray, such as GNOME as it ships. **Open Lumit when you sign
  in** appears once that is on, and starts Lumit in the tray when you sign in to the
  computer. **New project template**, a project that every new one starts as a copy of:
  **Choose…** picks it and **Clear** goes back to an empty project. **Panel layout ▸ Reset
  workspace** puts the panels back in the
  [Edit arrangement](/use/workspaces/#the-shipped-workspaces).
- **Updates**: **Automatic updates** checks for a new version at launch, at most once a
  day; downloading waits for a click. **Nightly builds** follows nightly builds instead of
  releases, and asks first. **Nightly icon**, on a nightly build on Windows or macOS,
  chooses between the nightly's own icon and the regular one. **This version** names the
  build installed and what the last check found, beside the same update button as the
  **Help** menu. See [Installation](/start/install/#updating).
- **Shared projects**: **Footage upload limit** and **Footage download limit**, the speed
  footage in a [shared project](/use/projects/#shared-projects) moves at, from 128 KB/s to
  25,600 KB/s, or **No limit**.
- **Connections**: **Discord status** sets what Discord shows while Lumit is open: **Off**,
  **Lumit only**, or **Project and composition**, which adds the names of the open project
  and composition. **Let other programs drive Lumit**, off as shipped, lets a program on
  this computer
  [edit the open project](/use/command-line/#let-another-program-edit-the-project-open-in-lumit).
  **After Effects presets** turns a folder of `.ffx` presets into
  [Lumit presets](/panels/effects-presets/#after-effects-presets). **3D app**, the Blender
  that opens `.blend`, USD, Alembic, PLY and STL files, with its version. Lumit finds one on
  its own; **Choose…** picks another program and **Clear** goes back to the one found.
- **Scripts**: **Scripts folder**, the folder **File ▸ Scripts** lists. **Choose…** picks
  another and **Clear** goes back to Lumit's own. **Export finished**, **Project opened**
  and **Project saved** each name a script from that folder to run after the event, or
  **None**. See [Scripts](/use/scripts/#event-scripts).
- **Control surfaces**: **Tangent panels**, which connects to Tangent Hub, a switch for each
  MIDI input under its own name, **MIDI learn**, and a row for each learned control. With no
  MIDI input, **MIDI devices** reads **None found**. See
  [Control surfaces](/use/control-surfaces/).

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Colour scheme** offers 33 built-in schemes grouped dark and light, plus your
  own, and pointing at one previews it. **Customise…** opens
  [the theme editor](#the-theme-editor). **Your themes** acts on the ones you have made:
  **Duplicate** copies the theme in use to a new one of your own, **Rename…** and **Delete**
  act on one of your own, **Import…** reads a `.lumtheme` file and switches to it, and
  **Export…** writes the colours on screen to one. An imported theme never replaces one of
  your own: a name already taken gets a number. **Accent** and **Style** tune whichever
  scheme is in force. Accent has six colours, with the accent in force written beside them,
  and pointing at one previews it. **Splash uses theme colour** lights the start-up splash
  with the accent. Style is how the interface is built: **Studio**, **Desk** or **Lantern**,
  then any styles of your own. **Your styles** takes a style in with **Import…**, packs the
  one in use into a file with **Make a package**, and opens the folder with **Show folder**.
  See [Make a theme](/make/themes/#a-style). **Reduce transparency** draws a style's
  see-through parts solid. **Label case** sets how panel titles, headings and other labels
  are cased: **Style's choice**, **All caps**, **First letter** or **Lower case**. Left to the
  style, Studio writes the first letter as a capital, Desk is lower case and Lantern is all
  caps.
  **Room**, shown with Lantern only, is **Style's choice**, **Day** or **Night**.
- **Interface**: [**Scale**](/use/workspaces/#how-large-everything-draws), 75 % to 200 %.
  **Tooltips**, on or off. **Motion**, for how much controls animate: **Full**, **Minimal**
  or **None**. **Compact**, which tightens the row spacing throughout. And **Chrome
  labels**, deciding whether the bars carry **Words**, **Icons**, or **Icons everywhere**,
  which turns panel titles into icons too.
- **Viewer**: whether the scopes and an effect's own graphs take the theme's colours or their
  own, and whether the **Surround** behind the picture is **Neutral** or **Theme colour**.
  **Align to the first picked layer** lines the selection up with the first layer picked
  rather than the box round them all. See [Align](/panels/align/).
  **Viewer bars** places the Viewer's bars: **Split, top and bottom**, **One bar, at the
  top**, **One bar, at the bottom** or **Deck**. **Toolbar position** is **Style's choice**,
  **Top** or **Left**, and the toolbar's own right-click menu has the same three. **Range
  sliders** draws a track beside a ranged parameter's number. **Command
  box in the top line** shows the search box that opens the command palette. On Linux,
  **Lumit's own title bar** replaces the system's, from the next start. **Icon set** draws
  the icons **Regular**, **Engraved** or **Bold**. Viewer bars, Toolbar position and Icon
  set can also be left to the **Style's choice**. **Custom icons** reads your own icons from
  the folder it shows, one SVG per icon named after it, and counts how many it replaces.
  The folder is read again when Lumit's window comes back to the front. See
  [Make an icon set](/make/icons/).
- **Panels**: whether footage rows in the Project panel carry thumbnails, whether layer names
  are drawn on the lane bars, whether [markers](/use/markers/) draw lines down the rows
  (**Marker lines**), and whether a minimap of the bars is drawn under the lanes (**Minimap
  under the lanes**, on by default in Lantern). **Wires** sets how each wire in a
  node graph runs between its two sockets: **Curved**, **Angled**, which turns only at 45 or
  90 degrees, or **Hanging**, which droops like a slack cable.
- **Waveforms**: **Style** is **Plain** or **Frequency**, which splits the waveform into low,
  mid and high bands. **Anchor** draws it from the **Centre** or the **Bottom**. **Scale** is
  how its height follows the level: **Linear**, **Square root** or **Logarithmic**.

A `splash.png` in Lumit's settings folder, `%APPDATA%\lumit` on Windows and
`~/.config/lumit` on macOS and Linux, replaces the picture on the start-up splash. The
splash is 640 by 500.

### The theme editor

**Customise…** lists every colour of the theme in use, in groups: surfaces, text, lines,
roles, waveforms, graph curves and layer kinds. A click on a swatch opens the colour picker,
and the interface redraws as the colour changes. Right-click a swatch for **Copy**,
**Paste**, which takes a hex colour from the clipboard, and **Reset**.

- **Save** on a built-in scheme asks for a name and makes a theme of your own. On one of
  your own it updates that theme.
- **Save a copy…** writes the colours under a new name.
- **Cancel** puts the colours back. Closing with unsaved changes asks whether to **Save** or
  **Discard** them.

The Viewer's surround is not among the colours. See [Make a theme](/make/themes/).

## Shortcuts

Every command and its shortcut, grouped by where it works. The defaults are listed under
[Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/).

- Click a shortcut and press the keys to rebind it. `Esc` leaves it as it was, and
  `Backspace` or `Delete` clears it. A key another command in the same group held is taken
  from that command, which then reads **Not set**.
- **Reset** on a row's right-click puts back the shortcut Lumit ships, whichever preset is
  loaded. **Reset page** loads the **Lumit default** preset.
- A shortcut that runs two commands in one group is flagged above the table.
- **Preset** loads a whole keymap: Lumit's own, **After Effects** or **Vegas**, then the
  shortcut sets you have saved. **Save as set…**, last in the list, saves the keymap in
  force under a name, in the [library](/use/library/). The row reads **Custom** once a
  shortcut has changed since. The Vegas preset puts split on `S`, snapping on `F8`, zoom on
  `↑` and `↓` and on the wheel with no key held, looping on `Q`, grouping on `G` and `U`,
  the Cut timeline's switches on `Ctrl`+`L`, `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`X` and `Alt`+`F8`, and sets
  **Clip drags in Cut** to **Vegas**. The other two presets set it to **Lumit default**, and
  a saved set leaves it. See
  [the two presets](/reference/shortcuts/#the-after-effects-preset).
- **Keymap file ▸ Import…** and **Export…** read and write the whole keymap as a `.json`
  file. A file that is not a keymap is refused. A command the file does not name keeps the
  shortcut Lumit ships. An imported file is also saved as a set, named after the file.
- **After Effects shortcuts ▸ Import…** reads a shortcut file saved by After Effects, the
  `.txt` in its `aeks` folder. Commands Lumit has no match for are skipped, and a command
  the file leaves out takes the After Effects preset's shortcut.
- **Keyboard** shows which keys have a shortcut. A filled key works anywhere and an outlined
  key works in one panel. Click **Ctrl**, **Alt** or **Shift** on it to see the keys they
  change, and point at a key to read what it does.

**Scroll wheel** sets the key held with the wheel to zoom the Timeline, scroll it sideways,
zoom the graph editor's values, change the dropper's sample size, and dolly a 3D view:
`Ctrl`, `Alt`, `Shift` or **No key**, which answers the wheel with no key held. Two that
would clash in one panel swap keys.

**Dragging** sets the modifier that breaks or joins handles in the
[graph editor](/use/graph-editor/): `Alt` or `Ctrl`. **Clip drags in Cut** picks
**Lumit default** or **Vegas** drags for the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/#vegas-drags).

## Timeline

- **Editing**: whether [Retime](/use/retime/) opens to the Speed lens rather than Value
  (**Retime opens to Speed**) and whether it reads in seconds rather than as a timecode
  (**Retime values in seconds**), whether video and image sequences are placed in a
  [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) automatically (**Video arrives as a Sequence
  layer**), whether a video's sound in the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/) goes **On its own
  track** or **With the picture** (**Video sound in Cut**), whether its tracks stand in any
  order (**Cut tracks in any order**), whether pasted layers keep their original time rather
  than pasting at the playhead (**Paste layers at their original time**), and whether the
  playhead stays where playback stopped rather than going back to where it started
  (**Playhead stays where playback stopped**). **Several audio streams arrive as** sets what
  a file with more than one audio stream becomes when placed: **The first stream**, **One
  layer, mixed** or **A layer each**. **New expressions are written in** **JavaScript** or
  **Rhai**; each expression keeps its own language afterwards.
- **Panels**: whether Transform appears in Effect controls (**Transform in Effect
  controls**), whether Effect controls shows its keyframes beside the parameters
  (**Keyframes in Effect controls**), whether an ease opens in a popup or the
  [Easing panel](/use/workspaces/) (**Shape eases in a popup**), and whether sound dragged
  in the Cut or Audio timeline lands on whole frames (**Sound moves in whole frames**).
  **Sound plays on through a freeze** and **Default transition** are for the
  [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/#fades-and-dissolves).
- **Node graphs**: whether `Tab` or `Shift+A` on a node graph canvas opens
  [node search](/use/nodes/), both on by default, and whether a right-click on empty canvas
  opens the search instead of the canvas menu, off by default.

## Viewer

- **Picture**: whether the picture is smoothed when zoomed in, and whether the tone-map
  button appears on the Viewer bar. Turning the button off turns the tone map off too.
- **Orbit**: **Invert left and right** and **Invert up and down** turn a
  [3D view](/use/camera/#3d-views) the other way as it is dragged.

## Audio

- **Output device**: **System default**, first in the list, follows the machine. A device
  chosen by name is used while it is connected; while it is not, Lumit plays through the
  system default and the row says so.
- **Audio offset**, −1000 to 1000 ms. Above zero holds the picture back for sound heard
  late, such as through Bluetooth headphones; below zero holds the sound back. It applies
  while playing, not to export, and is kept for this machine.
- **Normalise to**, −24 to 0 dB. Where **Normalise** puts a sound's loudest sample.
- **Scrub when dragging the playhead**: a plain drag on a ruler plays the sound as
  [scrubbing](/use/audio/#scrubbing) does with `Ctrl`. Off by default.
- **Touch return**, 0 to 2000 ms, 250 by default. How long a control let go in
  [Touch or Trim](/use/mixing/#automation) takes to glide back to its keys.

## Autosave

- **Save a copy every**: the minutes between spare copies, 0 to 240. Zero turns autosave
  off. 5 as shipped.
- **Copies kept**: 1 to 50. 5 as shipped.

## Export

What the export dialogue opens on.

- **Default preset**: an export preset, or **None**.
- **Filename template**: the name suggested for a file. `{comp}` is the composition's name,
  `{preset}` the export preset's own file name and `{date}` the date.
- **Destination**: **Ask every time**, **Beside the project**, or **A folder I choose**,
  which **Choose…** names.
- **Notify when exports end**: on by default. The system shows a notification when an export
  ends while Lumit is not the window in front.

## Preview and cache

- **Playback**: **When the machine cannot keep up** is **Every frame**, or **Adaptive**,
  which lowers the resolution to keep time. See [Preview and playback](/use/preview/).
  **Full resolution while dragging** draws a value drag at the Viewer's own resolution
  rather than a reduced one.
- **Hardware video decoding**: Windows and Linux only, on by default. The graphics card
  decodes video. Linux uses NVDEC where NVIDIA's driver has it, and otherwise VA-API. The
  card takes 4:2:0 footage, and 4:2:2 as well through NVDEC. Video the card cannot take or
  fails on decodes on the processor. macOS decodes on the processor. If importing video
  freezes the computer, turn it off and open the project again.
- **Render plugins on the graphics card**: on by default. OFX plugins that support CUDA,
  OpenCL or Metal render on the graphics card.
- **Largest picture**: the widest and tallest picture Lumit makes, 8192 px by default. The
  list offers what this machine's graphics card and memory can take. A frame cut to it shows
  **Cut to the largest picture** in the Viewer.
- **Quality tier**: the resolution playback has stepped down to, **Full**, **Half**,
  **Third** or **Quarter**. **Reset** starts it again at full.
- **Cache budgets** for all three tiers: rendered frames in memory, preview frames on the
  graphics card, and frames parked on disk. Each **Budget** is typed or dragged in
  megabytes, from 64. Each tier shows what is **In use**, with **Clear**, and clearing the
  disk tier asks first. The disk cache's location is set here too: **Where** is **With
  Lumit**, **Beside the project** or **A folder I choose**, and **Applies to** makes the
  choice for **Everything** or **This project**.
- **Resource governor**: how much **Graphics memory** and **Ordinary memory** is in use
  against its budget, the most held at once, and how many requests it has turned away. Under
  each is **Room to spare**, **Getting tight**, **Nearly full**, which pauses cache fill, or
  **Full**, which gives back frames.

## Addons

The model runtime and the model packs, and installed [extensions](/use/extensions/). See
[Addons and models](/use/addons/).

- **Runtime**: the **Model runtime**, with **Install**, or once installed **Remove**. Once an
  effect has asked for it, the line under it says what it is running on, or that it would
  not load. With files missing it says **Files are missing**.
- **Installed**: each addon with its licence, size and what it does, and **Remove**. One
  with files missing says **Files are missing**.
- **Available**: **Catalogue ▸ Check for addons** lists what can be downloaded, each with
  its licence and size and **Install**, or **Update** for a newer version of one already
  here. A download shows a bar and **Cancel**. Nothing installs before the runtime.
  **Install from file** installs from a manifest on disk. **Where they are kept ▸ Show**
  opens the folder.
- **Extensions**: each installed [extension](/use/extensions/), with **Show panel** or
  **Hide panel**, and **Remove**. **Install a package** takes a `.lfx` file and **Install
  from a folder** the folder containing `extension.json`. **Make a package** packs a folder
  into a `.lfx` file. See [Package and share](/make/share/).
- **Extension folders**: **Extensions folder ▸ Show** opens Lumit's own. A **Machine-wide
  folder** is listed where there is one. **Add a folder** names another to read extensions
  from, and each one added has **Remove**. See
  [Extension folders](/use/extensions/#extension-folders).

**Licence**, on a row, opens that licence in the browser.

## Account

The profiles on this machine, the Lumit account the one in use is signed in to, and what
of it is synced. See [Profiles and accounts](/use/profiles/). **Computers** is this
computer's part in [exports on another computer](/use/remote-export/#computers).
