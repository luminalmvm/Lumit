Open the settings window from **Edit ▸ Settings…**.

<Shot file="settings.png" caption="The settings window, on the Appearance page." eager/>

## General

Reset the panel layout to its default arrangement.

## Appearance

- **Colour scheme**: seven built-in schemes, grouped dark and light.
- **Custom colours**: allows you to alter the colour scheme according to your own preferences for complete customisation.
  If it also possible to import and export themes.
- **Corners** and **Motion**: Corners change the style of the application, and motion can be reduced or off if you'd prefer.
- **Scopes** and **Viewer surround**: both default to neutral colours but can use the theme's colours instead.

## Interface

- **Interface scale** and **tooltips**.
- **Transform in Effect controls**: displays a layer's Transform properties in the effect controls.
- **Editing**: whether Retime opens to the Value or Speed lens, whether video footage is automatically
  placed in a sequence layer when adding to a composition, whether the playhead stays where playback paused, 
  and whether pasted layers keep their original time positioning rather than pasting at the playhead.

## Keymap

Allows viewing of shortcuts and rebinding. Rebind by clicking a chord; conflicts are flagged. 
Keymaps can be exported and imported.

## Performance

- **Playback**: Every frame, or Adaptive resolution.
- **Cache budgets** for all three tiers: rendered frames in memory, preview frames on the
  graphics card, and frames parked on disk. The disk cache's location is set here as well,
  per machine or per project.
