Open the settings window from **Edit ▸ Settings…**.

<Shot file="settings.png" caption="The settings window, on the Appearance page." eager/>

The search box in the title strip finds a setting by name on any page, and lists what it
finds under the page names. On the Shortcuts page it searches the shortcuts instead.

Changes apply at once. **Reset page**, at the foot of the window, puts the page in view back
to how it shipped. On **Preview and cache** it leaves the cache budgets and the disk cache's
place as they are.

## General

- **Display**: **Language**. **Same as this machine** follows the system's.
- **Shared projects**: **Footage upload limit** and **Footage download limit**, the speed
  footage in a [shared project](/use/projects/#shared-projects) moves at, from 128 KB/s up,
  or **No limit**.
- **Workspace**: **Welcome screen on launch**. **New project template**, a project that
  every new one starts as a copy of: **Choose…** picks it and **Clear** goes back to an
  empty project. **Reset workspace** puts the current arrangement back to how it
  shipped. **Discord status** sets what Discord shows while Lumit is open: **Off**,
  **Lumit only**, or **Project and composition**, which adds the names of the open project
  and composition. **Keep running in the background**: closing the window leaves Lumit in
  the system tray, or the menu bar on macOS, where paired phones still see it. **Open
  Lumit** on the tray icon's menu brings the window back and **Quit Lumit** ends Lumit.
  Opening Lumit again also brings the window back, which is the only way on a desktop with
  no tray, such as GNOME as it ships. **Open Lumit when you sign in** appears once that is
  on, and starts Lumit in the tray when you sign in to the computer.
- **Updates**: **Automatic updates** checks for a new version at launch, at most once a
  day; downloading waits for a click. **Nightly builds** follows nightly builds instead of
  releases, and asks first. **Nightly icon**, on a nightly build on Windows or macOS,
  chooses between the nightly's own icon and the regular one. **This version** names the
  build installed and what the last check found, beside the same update button as the
  **Help** menu. See [Installation](/start/install/).

## Account

The profiles on this machine, the Lumit account the one in use is signed in to, and what
of it is synced. See [Profiles and accounts](/use/profiles/).

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Colour scheme** offers 33 built-in schemes grouped dark and light, plus your
  own, and pointing at one previews it. **Customise…** opens the theme editor, and **Your
  themes** holds the ones you have made; themes can be exported and imported. **Accent** and
  **Shape** tune whichever scheme is in force. Shape is the interface style: **Studio**,
  **Desk** or **Lantern**. **Label case** sets how panel titles, headings and other labels
  are cased: **Style's choice**, **All caps**, **First letter** or **Lower case**. Left to the
  style, Studio writes the first letter as a capital, Desk is lower case and Lantern is all
  caps.
  **Room**, shown with Lantern only, is **Style's choice**, **Day** or **Night**.
- **Interface**: [**Scale**](/use/workspaces/#how-large-everything-draws), 75 % to 200 %.
  **Tooltips**, on or off. **Motion**, for how much controls animate: **Full**, **Minimal**
  or **None**. **Compact**, which tightens the row spacing throughout. And **Chrome
  labels**, deciding whether the bars carry **Words**, **Icons**, or **Icons everywhere**.
- **Viewer**: whether the scopes and an effect's own graphs take the theme's colours or their
  own, and whether the **Surround** behind the picture is **Neutral** or **Theme colour**.
  **Viewer bars** places the Viewer's bars: **Split, top and bottom**, **One bar, at the
  top**, **One bar, at the bottom** or **Deck**. **Toolbar position** is **Top** or
  **Left**. **Range sliders** draws a track beside a ranged parameter's number. **Command
  box in the top line** shows the search box that opens the command palette. On Linux,
  **Lumit's own title bar** replaces the system's, from the next start. **Icon set** draws
  the icons **Regular**, **Engraved** or **Bold**. Viewer bars, Toolbar position and Icon
  set can also be left to the **Style's choice**. **Custom icons** reads your own icons from
  the folder it shows, one SVG per icon named after it, and counts how many it replaces;
  **Reload icons** reads the folder again.
- **Panels**: whether footage rows in the Project panel carry thumbnails, whether layer names
  are drawn on the lane bars, whether [markers](/use/markers/) draw lines down the rows
  (**Marker lines**), and whether a minimap of the bars is drawn under the lanes (**Minimap
  under the lanes**, on by default in Lantern). **Wires** sets how each wire in a
  node graph runs between its two sockets: **Curved**, **Angled**, which turns only at 45 or
  90 degrees, or **Hanging**, which droops like a slack cable.
- **Waveforms**: **Style** is **Plain** or **Frequency**, which splits the waveform into low,
  mid and high bands. **Anchor** draws it from the **Centre** or the **Bottom**.

## Timeline

- **Editing**: whether [Retime](/use/retime/) opens to the Speed lens rather than Value
  (**Retime opens to Speed**) and whether it reads in seconds, whether video is placed in a
  [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) automatically, where a video's sound goes in the
  [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/), whether its tracks stand in any order, whether pasted
  layers keep their original time rather than pasting at the playhead, and whether the
  playhead stays where playback stopped. **Several audio streams arrive as** sets what a
  file with more than one audio stream becomes when placed: **The first stream**, **One
  layer, mixed** or **A layer each**. **New expressions are written in** **JavaScript** or
  **Rhai**; each expression keeps its own language afterwards.
- **Panels**: whether Transform appears in Effect controls, whether Effect controls shows its
  keyframes beside the parameters (**Keyframes in Effect controls**), whether an ease opens
  in a popup or the [Easing panel](/use/workspaces/), and whether sound dragged in the Cut
  or Audio timeline lands on whole frames (**Sound moves in whole frames**).
- **Node graphs**: whether a right-click, `Tab` or `Shift+A` on a node graph canvas opens
[node search](/use/nodes/). Each is on by default and can be turned off on its own.

## Viewer

Whether the picture is smoothed when zoomed in, and whether the tone-map button appears on
the Viewer bar. Turning the button off turns the tone map off too.

## Audio

- **Output device**: **System default**, first in the list, follows the machine. A device
  chosen by name is used while it is connected; while it is not, Lumit plays through the
  system default and the row says so.
- **Audio offset**, −1000 to 1000 ms. Above zero holds the picture back for sound heard
  late, such as through Bluetooth headphones; below zero holds the sound back. It applies
  while playing, not to export, and is kept for this machine.

## Autosave

How often a spare copy is written, and how many are kept. Setting the interval to nothing
turns autosave off.

## Export

What the export dialogue opens on: a preset, a filename template, and whether it asks for a
destination each time or uses a folder you name.

## Addons

- **Runtime**: the **Model runtime**, with **Install**, or once installed **Load now** and
  **Remove**. The line under it says what it is running on, or that it would not load.
- **Installed**: each addon with its licence, size and what it does, and **Remove**. One
  with files missing says **Files are missing**.
- **Available**: **Catalogue ▸ Check for addons** lists what can be downloaded, each with
  its licence and size and **Install**, or **Update** for a newer version of one already
  here. Nothing installs before the runtime. **Install from file** installs from a manifest
  on disk. **Where they are kept ▸ Show** opens the folder.
- **Extensions**: each installed [extension](/use/extensions/), with **Show panel** or
  **Hide panel**, and **Remove**. **Install from a folder** takes the folder containing
  `extension.json`. **Extensions folder ▸ Show** opens the folder.

**Licence**, on a row, opens that licence in the browser.

## Preview and cache

- **Playback**: when the machine cannot keep up, every frame, or adaptive resolution.
  **Full resolution while dragging** draws a value drag at the Viewer's own resolution
  rather than a reduced one.
- **Hardware video decoding**: on by default. If importing video freezes the computer,
  turn it off and open the project again.
- **Cache budgets** for all three tiers: rendered frames in memory, preview frames on the
  graphics card, and frames parked on disk. Each shows what is **In use**, with **Clear**.
  The disk cache's location is set here too (**Where**), for **Everything** or **This
  project**.
- **Quality tier**: the resolution playback has stepped down to, **Full**, **Half**,
  **Third** or **Quarter**. **Reset** starts it again at full.
- **Resource governor**: how much **Graphics memory** and **Ordinary memory** is in use
  against its budget, and how many requests it has turned away.

## Shortcuts

Every command and its shortcut. Rebind by clicking one; conflicts are flagged. Keymaps export
and import.

- **Preset** loads a whole keymap: Lumit's own, **After Effects** or **Vegas**. The Vegas
  one puts split on `S`, snapping on `F8`, zoom on `↑` and `↓`, and the Cut timeline's
  switches on `Ctrl`+`L`, `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`X` and `Alt`+`F8`.
- **After Effects shortcuts ▸ Import…** reads a shortcut file saved by After Effects, the
  `.txt` in its `aeks` folder. Commands Lumit has no match for are skipped.
- **Keyboard** shows which keys have a shortcut. A filled key works anywhere and an outlined
  key works in one panel. Click **Ctrl**, **Alt** or **Shift** on it to see the keys they
  change, and point at a key to read what it does.

**Dragging** sets the modifier that breaks or joins handles in the
[graph editor](/use/graph-editor/): `Alt` or `Ctrl`.
