Open the settings window from **Edit ▸ Settings…**. Almost everything in it is a preference
about this machine rather than a fact about the project — what belongs to the project is in
**File ▸ Project settings…** instead.

<Shot file="settings.png" caption="The settings window, on the Appearance page." eager/>

The window is resizable, and it remembers where you left it.

## General

**Language**, which follows the system unless you choose otherwise. Whether the welcome
screen appears on launch. And **Reset workspace**, which puts the arrangement you are on
back to how it shipped.

Beneath those, whether Lumit checks for updates, and what it found last time it looked.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: seven built-in schemes grouped dark and light, plus your own. **Customise…**
  opens the theme editor; themes can be exported and imported. **Accent** and **Shape** tune
  whichever scheme is in force.
- **Interface**: [**Scale**](/use/workspaces/#how-large-everything-draws), 75 % to 200 %.
  **Tooltips**, on or off. **Motion**, for how much controls animate. **Compact**, which
  tightens the row spacing throughout. And **Chrome labels**, deciding whether the bars carry
  words, icons, or icons everywhere.
- **Viewer**: whether the scopes and an effect's own graphs take the theme's colours or their
  own, and whether the surround behind the picture is neutral or themed.

## Timeline

Editing habits: whether [Retime](/use/retime/) opens to the Value or the Speed lens and
whether it reads in seconds, whether video is placed in a [Sequence
layer](/use/sequence-layers/) automatically, whether pasted layers keep their original time
rather than landing at the playhead, and whether the playhead stays where playback stopped.

Then the panel habits: whether Transform appears in Effect controls, whether an ease opens in
a popup or the [Easing panel](/use/workspaces/), and whether layer names are drawn on the lane
bars.

## Viewer

Whether the picture is smoothed when zoomed in, and whether the tone-map button appears on
the Viewer bar. Tone mapping is a way of looking and never reaches an export — see [Colour
management](/use/colour-management/).

## Audio

The output device. Follows the system unless you name one.

## Autosave

How often a spare copy is written, and how many are kept. Setting the interval to nothing
turns autosave off.

## Export

What the export dialogue opens on: a preset, a filename template, and whether it asks for a
destination each time or uses a folder you name. These are about how you export, not about
any one project, so a `.lum` sent to somebody else says nothing about where their copy of
Lumit writes files.

## Preview and cache

- **Playback**: every frame, or adaptive resolution.
- **Cache budgets** for all three tiers: rendered frames in memory, preview frames on the
  graphics card, and frames parked on disk. The disk cache's location is set here too, per
  machine or per project.

## Shortcuts

Every command and its chord. Rebind by clicking one; conflicts are flagged. Keymaps export
and import.
