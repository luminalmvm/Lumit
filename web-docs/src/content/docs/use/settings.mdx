Open the settings window from **Edit ▸ Settings…**. A sidebar of pages sits on the
left; each page is a stack of sections.

<Shot file="settings.png" caption="The settings window, on the Appearance page." />

## General

Reset the panel layout to its default arrangement.

## Appearance

- **Colour scheme** - seven built-in schemes, grouped dark and light.
- **Custom colours** - start from any scheme and edit every colour in the theme editor.
  Custom themes can be renamed and deleted here too.
- **Corners** and **Motion** - how rounded controls are, and how much they animate.
- **Scopes** and **Viewer surround** - both default to neutral colours so a grade can be
  judged; either can opt into the theme's colours instead.

## Interface

- **Interface scale** and **tooltips**.
- **Transform in Effect controls** - repeat a layer's Transform rows above its effects.
- **Editing** - whether Retime opens to the Speed lens, whether video arrives as a
  Sequence layer, whether the playhead stays where playback stopped, and whether
  pasted layers keep their original time rather than landing at the playhead.

## Keymap

Every shortcut, grouped by where it is live, with a search field. Rebind by clicking a
chord; clashes are flagged. Two presets ship - **Lumit default** and **After Effects**
- and a keymap can be exported and imported as a file.

## Performance

- **Playback** - Adaptive (keeps time, softens the picture) or Every frame.
- **Cache budgets** for all three tiers: rendered frames in memory, preview frames on
  the graphics card, and frames parked on disk - including where the disk cache lives,
  per machine or per project.
