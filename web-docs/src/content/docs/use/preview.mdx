**Preview** is playback inside Lumit.

<Shot file="cache-bar.png" caption="The cache bar filling above the Timeline." eager/>

## Play

Press play, or `space` by default. The playhead will begin moving and the Viewer will update.

## The cache

The cache has three tiers: VRAM, RAM and disk. You can alter the memory allocated to each of 
these individually under **Edit ▸ Settings ▸ Performance**.

Lumit stores rendered frames to help speed up playback. Frames that are cached are shown by 
the **cache bar** under the time ruler: VRAM and RAM display as a green line, and frames
stored on the disk are blue.

## Preview resolution

Lower the preview resolution to render previews faster: full, half, third, quarter, or auto. 
Changing the preview resolution has no impact when exporting.

Lumit also has two preview resolution options in the Viewer bar. **Every frame** will
render each frame in the composition, pausing audio if playback isn't realtime.
**Adaptive res** will instead dynamically alter the resolution to try and maintain
realtime playback; if even quarter resolution isn't enough, it will only display frames it
manages to render in time.

Personally I'd normally recommend **Every frame** as the default option to use.

## The progress bar

While waiting on a slow frame, a progress bar appears in the bar at the bottom of the
viewer, and fills as the frame passes through decoding, building, and compositing.

## Adaptive degradation

Under load, Lumit reduces quality on its own to keep responding. It may drop resolution
while scrubbing effect values as you change them.

## If playback is slow

1. Lower the preview resolution.
2. Let the cache fill on a first pass, then play again.
3. Turn off heavy effects while you block out timing.
4. Precompose a finished section so it caches as one unit.
