A **profile** is one person's settings: appearance, shortcuts, panel layout, preferences,
saved easings and expressions, and its own [library](/use/library/). A machine can hold
several.

<Shot file="profile-card.png" caption="The profile card, opened from the left of the bottom strip." eager/>

## Profiles

The profile button is at the left of the bottom strip. It opens the **profile card**.

- A click on a profile in the card switches to it.
- **Add profile…** makes a profile from a name and switches to it. It starts with the
  settings in force.
- **Account settings…** opens **Settings ▸ Account**.
- **Settings ▸ Account** renames and removes profiles. The profile in use is not removed.

Removing a profile deletes its settings on the machine. An account it was signed in to
stays.

A profile does not hold the cache settings, the audio device, recent projects, or where
windows were left. Those stay with the machine.

## Lumit account

An account is optional. **Sign in** in the profile card opens the sign-in window, which
takes an email and a password, or **Continue with Apple**, Google or Discord where the
server offers them. An Apple, Google or Discord sign-in finishes in the browser, and is
given up after five minutes. **Cancel** stops waiting for it.

- **Create an account** asks for a name, an email, a password of at least 10 characters
  and a date of birth, and emails a six-digit code that confirms the address. **Send
  another code** emails a new one.
- A first sign-in with Apple, Google or Discord asks for the date of birth too.
- Lumit refuses an account to someone too young for one.
- **Forgot password?** emails a code to the address typed and takes a new password.
- **Privacy** and **Terms** open those pages in the browser.

The password is not sent to Lumit's server. Each profile signs in separately. A profile
with no name takes the name of the account it signs in to.

The profile card names the account's email under the profile, or *On this device only*.
**Sign out** is in the card and on **Settings ▸ Account**.

**Settings ▸ Account ▸ Name** is the name other people see on previews and replies.
**Rename…** changes it, up to 40 characters.

**Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account…** deletes the account, its synced settings, and
any subscription. The settings on the machine stay.

## Friends

Friends are other Lumit accounts, found by username. A
[preview](/use/review-links/#the-previews-window) can be sent to a friend from the Previews
window, and an invite to a [shared project](/use/projects/#shared-projects) from the
Multi-edit window. Friends are free.

- **Settings ▸ Account ▸ Username** sets the name friends find you by: 3 to 20 letters,
  numbers, `_` or `.`. A username another account has is refused.
- **Add friend** asks someone by their username. They become a friend when they accept.
  An ask that is waiting for them reads *Asked*, and **Cancel** takes it back.
- An ask waiting for you shows **Accept** and **Decline**. The **Friends** row in the
  profile card counts them, and opens **Settings ▸ Account**.
- Friends are folded under their count, such as **Friends · 3**. A press opens the list,
  and **×** beside a friend removes them, after asking.

When a friend sends an invite to a shared project, the bottom strip says so, and **Open**
opens the Multi-edit window. See [Shared projects](/use/projects/#shared-projects).

## Lumit Pro

Lumit Pro is a subscription on a Lumit account. It adds:

- Shared projects through Lumit's relay. See [Relays](/use/projects/#relays).
- [Projects kept online](/use/projects/#keeping-a-shared-project-online).
- [Previews](/use/review-links/).
- [Backup and history](/use/backup/).
- [Sync](#sync).
- [Devices](#devices).

**See what is in Pro** in the profile card, or **Help ▸ Lumit Pro…**, opens the **Lumit
Pro** window. It lists what Pro adds. What it offers under that comes from Lumit's server.

| The server says | The window shows |
| --- | --- |
| Pro is on sale | **Monthly** and **Yearly** with their prices, and what the year saves. The line under the button gives the price the plan renews at, and where it is cancelled: **Manage subscription**, in **Settings**. |
| Pro is not on sale | *Pro is not on sale yet.* |
| Pro is free to try | **Sign in to try Pro**, and *Pro is free while we test it.* The badge reads **Pro preview**. |

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Subscribe | Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then **Get Lumit Pro**. The payment page opens in the browser, and the window changes when the payment completes. |
| Subscribe when signed out | **Sign in to get Pro** opens the sign-in window first. |
| Stop waiting for a payment | **Not now** |
| Change or cancel | **Manage subscription**, in the window, in the profile card, or on **Settings ▸ Account**. It opens the page in the browser. |

With Pro, the window says when the subscription renews, or when it ends. It says so when
a payment has failed. A subscription bought in the iPhone app shows **Managed on your
iPhone** in the window and on **Settings ▸ Account** instead.

A profile with Pro wears a **Pro** badge and a ring round its picture. For an account
without Pro, a menu row that needs it wears the badge, and a switch that needs it is
dimmed.

**Settings ▸ Account ▸ Plan** reads *Free* or **Pro**, and **Storage** says how much of
the account's storage is used, and how much of that is backups. Backups, previews and
footage kept online share it.

## Devices

With Lumit Pro, **Settings ▸ Account ▸ Devices** lists **This computer** and the phones
paired with the account.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename…** | Renames the computer or phone, up to 40 characters. |
| **Remove** | Unpairs the phone. |
| **Add a phone** | Shows a QR code for the iPhone camera or the Lumit app, with the time it has left. **Copy link** copies the same pairing link, and **New code** replaces one that has run out. The window closes once a phone has paired, and Lumit names the phone. |
| **Phones can start exports** | On by default. Off, a phone can follow the export queue and change nothing. |

A paired phone follows this computer's export queue, by name: the project's name and each
export's file, never a path. It can:

- start the queue
- cancel or remove an item
- queue a composition with a saved export preset, over the work area or the whole
  composition, and start it
- send a finished export as a [preview](/use/review-links/)

The bottom strip says what a phone did. **Keep running in the background**, in
[Settings](/use/settings/#general), leaves Lumit running for paired phones after its
window is closed.

A phone's request is refused when **Phones can start exports** is off, when no project is
open, when **Settings ▸ Export ▸ Destination** is **Ask every time**, **Beside the
project** with an unsaved project, or **A folder I choose** with none chosen, and while a
preview is already being sent. An export a phone starts never overwrites a file.

## Sync

With Lumit Pro, a signed-in profile is also kept on Lumit's server. Signing in on another
machine brings its settings. A change is sent a few seconds after it is made and reaches
the other machines within two minutes.

The first time a profile syncs on a machine, the settings on the server replace its own.
After that, changes to different settings on two machines are both kept. Where two machines
changed the same setting, the one that syncs last is kept.

**Settings ▸ Account ▸ Keep this profile in sync** turns sync off for the profile. Under it
is a switch for each kind of setting, and **Sync now**.

| Kind | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Appearance and themes** | Theme, accent, shape, and the theme and style in use. |
| **Shortcuts** | The whole keymap. |
| **Panel layout and workspaces** | The arrangement in force and saved workspaces. |
| **Preferences** | The options on the other Settings pages, apart from those below. |
| **Easings, expressions and favourites** | Saved easings and expressions, favourite effects, and recent colours. |

The profile card says where sync stands.

| Line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Settings in sync | The last sync finished. |
| Syncing… | Settings are being sent or fetched. |
| Couldn't sync. Trying again soon. | The last sync failed. The next is within two minutes. |
| Sync is off | **Keep this profile in sync** is off. |

:::note[Not synced]
Interface scale, cache budgets and location, the audio device, **Audio offset**, recent
projects, **Nightly builds**, **Nightly icon**, **Discord status**, the **New project
template**, the shared projects **Upload limit**, **Download limit**, **Stand-in size** and
**Stand-in frames**, **Phones can start exports**, and **Keyframes in Effect controls**.
A kind of setting is left out while it comes to more than 900 KB.
:::

The library syncs by itself. See [Library](/use/library/#on-every-computer).

## Settings ▸ Account

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Profiles** | Each profile, with **Rename…** and **Remove**, and **Add profile…**. The profile in use reads *In use*. |
| **Lumit account** | **Sign in**, or the account's email with **Sign out** and **Delete account…**. **Name**, **Username**, **Plan**, **Storage**, and **Email me about new comments**, see [Previews](/use/review-links/#the-link-page). |
| **Friends** | See [Friends](#friends). It shows for a signed-in profile. |
| **Devices** | See [Devices](#devices). It shows with Lumit Pro. |
| **Sync** | **Keep this profile in sync**, the kinds, and **Sync now**. |
| **Backup** | **Back up projects when they are saved**, see [Backup and history](/use/backup/#settings). |
| **Kept online** | The shared projects the account keeps online, each with its size and when it was last opened, and **Stop keeping online**. One the computer has no file for reads *Unnamed project*, with the day it was put online. After Lumit Pro ends, a **Lumit Pro has ended** row has **Lumit Pro…**. See [Projects](/use/projects/#keeping-a-shared-project-online). |

**Plan** has **Manage subscription**, or **Lumit Pro…**, which opens the Lumit Pro window.
