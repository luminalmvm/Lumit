Compositions hold an ordered stack of **layers**. Layers draw from the bottom up, meaning 
the top layer composites over everything below it.

## Add a layer

- Drag an asset from the Project panel into the timeline. Several dropped together lie end
  to end in the panel's order, and the composition lengthens to fit. Hold `Shift` to stack
  them all at the start.
- Or choose **Layer ▸ New** and pick the type of layer you need.

A layer made from the **Layer** menu lands directly above the selected layer, above the
topmost of several, or at the top of the stack with nothing selected. It runs the length
of the composition and arrives selected.

| Layer ▸ New | Shortcut | Makes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Solid…** | `Ctrl+Y` | Opens **New solid** for a name, a size and a colour, then makes the solid. It starts at the composition's size and on the colour of the last solid made. Its item is filed in the project's **Solids** folder. |
| **Text** | `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T` | A [text layer](/use/text/) in the middle of the composition, open for typing. |
| **Camera** | `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+C` | A [camera](/use/camera/). |
| **Light…** | `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+L` | Opens **Light settings**, then makes the [light](#light-layers). |
| **3D shape ▸** | | A [3D shape](#3d-shapes): **Cube**, **Sphere**, **Cylinder**, **Cone**, **Torus**, **Plane** or **Pyramid**. |
| **Adjustment** | `Ctrl+Alt+Y` | An [adjustment layer](#adjustment). |
| **Null** | `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Y` | A null layer at the middle of the composition. |
| **Card** | | A flat [Model layer](/use/importing/#3d-models) with no file, filling the camera's frame at the playhead. |
| **Sequence** | `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S` | An empty [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/). |
| **Audio** | `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A` | An empty [Audio layer](/use/audio/). With nothing selected it goes to the bottom of the stack. |

In a composition that holds clips, the submenu also carries **Insert title** and **Insert
solid**. See [Cutting](/use/cutting/).

**New layer** on the Timeline's [right-click menu](/panels/timeline/#right-click-menus)
holds **Solid…**, **Text**, **Camera**, **Light…**, **Adjustment**, **Null** and
**Sequence**.

While a layer is soloed, a new layer that draws arrives soloed. A new Audio layer arrives
soloed while another Audio layer is.

## The layer kinds

| Kind | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Footage layer** | One footage item as a source. Supports [Retime](/use/retime/). |
| **Solid layer** | A flat colour. Often used as a base for effects. |
| **Precomp layer** | Another composition, used as a layer. Supports Retime. A [node graph](/use/node-graphs/) placed this way carries its Inputs as a section of the layer's own controls. |
| **[Text layer](/use/text/)** | Editable text. |
| **[Shape layer](/use/shapes/)** | Vector shapes with fills and strokes. |
| **[Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/)** | Similar to Footage, but allows cutting within one layer. |
| **Adjustment layer** | A comp-sized layer with nothing of its own to show, born with the adjustment switch on. |
| **Null layer** | Invisible and transform-only. Used to drive other layers. |
| **[Camera layer](/use/camera/)** | A 3D viewpoint. Affects layers with the 3D switch on. |
| **[Light layer](#light-layers)** | A source of light: **Point**, **Spot**, **Area**, **Parallel**, **Ambient** or **Environment**. Shades layers with Accepts lights on. |
| **[Model layer](/use/importing/#3d-models)** | A 3D model, or a Card, drawn in the comp's 3D space. Always 3D. |
| **[3D shape](#3d-shapes)** | A cube, sphere, cylinder, cone, torus, plane or pyramid, drawn in the comp's 3D space. Always 3D. |
| **[Audio layer](/use/audio/)** | Sound with no picture. Made by placing an audio file, or by **Add audio only** on an item that has a picture too. |
| **Bus layer** | Sound routed to it from other layers. Made by **Insert bus** on the ground of the Audio timeline or the Mixer. |

## Layer settings

**Layer ▸ Layer settings…** (`Ctrl+Shift+Y`) opens on the selected layer's **Name**. On a
Solid layer it also holds **Solid size**, from 1 to 16384 pixels each way, and **Solid
colour**. The solid's item in the Project panel takes the same name.

On a Camera, a Light or a Model layer the command opens that layer's own window:
[Camera settings](/use/camera/#camera-settings), [Light settings](#light-layers) or
[Model settings](/use/importing/#3d-models).

## Order and selection

Drag a layer up or down to change ordering. Select several layers to apply commands 
to all of them at once.

Selection applies across the application. For instance, picking a layer in the Viewer 
selects it everywhere.

| Shortcut | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl+]` and `Ctrl+[` | Move the selected layers one place up or down the stack. |
| `Ctrl+Shift+]` and `Ctrl+Shift+[` | Move them to the top or the bottom. |
| `Ctrl+Up` and `Ctrl+Down` | Select the layer above or below. |
| `Ctrl+A` and `Ctrl+Shift+A` | Select every layer, and deselect everything. |
| `Alt+Page Up` and `Alt+Page Down` | Move the selected layers a frame earlier or later. With `Shift`, ten frames. |

The first two are also under **Layer ▸ Arrange**, as **Bring forward**, **Send backward**,
**Bring to front** and **Send to back**. Several layers moved together keep their order. A
locked layer stays where it is.

Double-click a layer's name in the Timeline to rename it. On a Precomp layer the
double-click opens its composition, and on a [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) its
clips. `Enter` renames either.

A layer's bar is moved and trimmed in the [Timeline](/panels/timeline/#the-lanes).

## Stagger

The **Stagger** panel starts the selected layers one after another. **Layer ▸ Stagger
layers…** opens it. Locked layers stay put.

- **Offset per layer**: how many frames each layer starts after the one before it.
- **Overlap**: above 0, the layers lie end to end instead, each starting this many frames
  before the one before it ends. The offset is not read.
- **Crossfade**: writes straight Opacity keys across each overlap, on whichever layer is higher
  in the stack.
- **Order**: **Top down**, **Bottom up**, **Order clicked** or **Random**. Order clicked
  follows the order the layers were added to the selection. Random shuffles again each time.
- **Stagger**: the first layer starts on the earliest in point among them, and each one
  after it starts later by the offset, or end to end with an overlap.
- **Line up**: every layer starts on that earliest in point. With Crossfade ticked, each
  also fades in and out over the Overlap at both ends.

Hold `Ctrl+Alt` and drag a selected layer's bar to stagger by hand: `Cmd+Option` on macOS,
`Ctrl+Shift` on Linux. The drag follows the panel's order. The first layer stays put, the
bar under the pointer follows it, and the ones between are spaced evenly. With the first
layer's bar in hand, the last layer moves the whole distance.

## Replace a layer's source

**Replace source**, on the row menu and the **Layer** menu, points the selected layers at
another item of the project, or at a file with **Choose…**. `Alt`+drag an item from the Project
panel onto a layer's row or bar does the same.

Footage, solid and precomp layers take each other's sources. The transform, keys, masks, effects
and markers stay, and the anchor point keeps its place on the picture. A name that was the
old source's follows the new one. A layer that becomes a solid loses its Retime, and the status
line says so.

The menu offers only what the layers can take: a picture for a picture layer, sound for a
sound layer, and never a composition that contains them.

## Copy and paste

**Edit ▸ Cut / Copy / Paste** can copy whole layers if selected and can be pasted into
other compositions as well. A copied layer retains all information: transform, keyframes, 
masks, paint, effects, switches, markers, and retime values.

**Edit ▸ Duplicate** (`Ctrl+D`) duplicates every selected layer.

**Edit ▸ Split layer** (`Ctrl+Shift+D`) splits every selected layer at the playhead. A
Sequence layer gains an edit point there, and any other layer becomes two.

By default a pasted layer starts at the playhead. To paste at the time it was copied from
instead, turn on *Paste layers at their original time* in [Settings ▸
Timeline](/use/settings/). A parent or matte reference is retained only if the target
composition still contains that layer.

| Kind | Colour |

| Null | Coral |

## Switches

<Shot file="layer-switches.png" caption="The switches on a layer's row." />

Each layer has two runs of switches in the Timeline outline.

The **switches** column holds what the Timeline keeps about a layer: visibility, mute, solo,
lock, shy, and guide. The **modes** column holds how the layer is rendered: collapse, flow,
disable effects, motion blur, 3D, and adjustment.

The columns open on visibility, mute, solo and lock, and on disable effects, 3D and motion
blur. Drag a column's seam wider to show the rest.

| Switch | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Visible** | Draws the layer. On a Camera layer it decides which camera is [active](/use/camera/#the-active-camera). With the Timeline active, `X` sets it on the selected layers. |
| **Audible** | Plays the layer's sound. |
| **Solo** | While a layer that draws is soloed, only the soloed layers draw. Sound is soloed apart from the picture. Camera, Light and Null layers work whatever is soloed. |
| **Lock** | Refuses every edit to the layer except its lock, its shy switch and its label colour. |
| **Shy** | Hides the row while the Timeline's shy filter is on. The picture is not changed. |
| **Guide** | See [Guide layers](#guide-layers). |
| **Disable effects** | Off, the layer's whole effect stack is bypassed. |
| **3D** | Places the layer in depth. See [Cameras and 3D](/use/camera/#3d-layers). |
| **Motion blur** | See [Motion blur](#motion-blur). |
| **Adjustment** | See [Adjustment](#adjustment). |
| **Flow** | Footage and Precomp layers. See [Retime](/use/retime/#frames-in-between). |
| **Collapse** | Precomp layers. See [Collapse transformations](#collapse-transformations). |

`Ctrl+L` locks or unlocks the selected layers, and `Ctrl+Shift+L` unlocks every layer.
Both are in the **Layer** menu, as **Lock** and **Unlock all layers**. A locked layer
cannot be clicked in the Viewer; the click goes to the layer behind it.

A row shows only the switches its layer can use. An [Audio layer](/use/audio/) has no
visibility switch, and a layer that can never be heard has no mute switch. A layer with no
effects has no disable effects switch. Camera, Light and Model layers and 3D shapes have no
3D switch. A layer with sound and no picture has no box in the Viewer and cannot be picked
there. Guide is drawn on every row.

- Click a switch on a selected layer and every selected layer takes the same state.
- Press a switch and drag up or down its column, and every row the pointer crosses takes
  that state too. The drag is one undo step.
- `Alt`-click a solo switch to solo that layer alone.

A switch that is off is drawn dimmer than one that is on.

## Motion blur

The **motion blur switch** blurs a layer from its own animated transform. It acts while the
composition's master motion blur is on, which is the motion blur mark on the
[Timeline's](/panels/timeline/) bar.

The shutter is the composition's: **Shutter angle** and **Samples**, in
[Composition settings](/use/compositions/#the-settings). A blurred layer is drawn at more
moments the faster it moves, its parents and the camera included, from **Samples** up to
the count after **to**.

The Motion blur effect is different, see
[Effects](/use/effects/#the-three-kinds-of-motion-blur).

## Collapse transformations

**Collapse** is a switch on a Precomp layer. On, the layers of the nested composition are
drawn straight into this one with the Precomp layer's transform applied to each. The nested
composition is not drawn to a picture of its own first, and its layers are not cut off at
its edges.

The switch does nothing, and the layer draws as an ordinary Precomp layer, while any of
these holds:

- The Precomp layer has a mask, a paint stroke, an enabled effect, Flow on, a blend mode
  other than Normal, or an opacity under 100%.
- Another layer uses it as a [matte](/use/mattes/).
- A visible layer in the nested composition uses a matte, is an adjustment layer with an
  enabled effect, or carries [Smoke and fire](/effects/generate/smoke-and-fire/).
- The nested composition is a [node graph](/use/node-graphs/).

## Adjustment

**Adjustment is a switch**, on any layer that draws something — turn it on and the layer's
effect stack runs on the composite of everything below it. Effects apply only to the area
the layer covers.

**Layer ▸ New ▸ Adjustment** makes a comp-sized layer with the switch already on. Turn the
switch off on one of those and it becomes an ordinary white solid.

Camera, Light, Null and Audio layers cannot be adjustment layers, and the cell is left out
on their rows.

## Guide layers

**Guide** marks a layer *for reference only*. The Viewer draws it like any other layer and
**no exported file contains it**, picture or sound.

Guide beats solo. A locked layer refuses the guide switch.

The export dialogue can be told to write guide layers anyway.

## Groups and precomposing

A **layer group** folds a run of layers under one header row in the Timeline. **Layer ▸
Group layers** (`Ctrl+G`) makes one from the selected layers, and **Layer ▸ Ungroup**
(`Ctrl+Shift+G`) removes it. See [Layer groups](/use/layer-groups/).

**Layer ▸ Precompose…** (`Ctrl+Shift+C`) moves the selected layers into a new composition
and leaves a Precomp layer in their place. See
[Compositions](/use/compositions/#nest-one-composition-in-another).

## Light layers

**Layer ▸ New ▸ Light…** opens **Light settings**, where the light's name, type and options
are set before it is made. A new light stands in front of the composition, aimed at its
centre; an area light starts at a fifth of the composition. **Layer ▸ 3D ▸ Light settings** opens
the window again for the selected light. A keyframed option reads **animated** there and is
left alone. The first 3D model dropped into a composition with no lights brings an
**Ambient light** and a **Key light** with it.

A light is placed by its ordinary transform, so it animates and parents like anything
else. A layer takes light on its front, so a floor lies flat at X rotation -90. Which layers
it shades is per layer: **Accepts lights**, on by default, in a 3D
layer's **Material options** and on the layer's right-click menu in the Timeline.

A light adds to the picture, so a layer no light reaches is left as it is. **Lights start
from black**, under **Lights** in Composition settings, lights a layer from black instead,
with the ambient lights as its base, and leaves 2D layers unlit. It lights the picture as
the screen shows it, as After Effects does without **Linearize working space**. A
composition imported from After Effects has it on, in linear light if the project was
linearised.

**Light options**, inside the light's Transform group, holds its own rows:

- **Light type**: **Point**, **Spot**, **Area**, **Parallel** (every ray runs the same way,
  along the light's aim), **Ambient** (light from everywhere, with no place or direction) or
  **Environment** (light from a picture of everything round the scene).
- **Source**, on an environment light: the layer whose picture surrounds the scene, read as
  a panorama twice as wide as it is tall: footage, a sequence, a solid, a precomp, text or a
  shape. A new one from Light settings starts on the selected footage or precomp, shown in
  the background, and switches the view it was added from to **Final**.
- **Show in background**, on an environment light: draws the picture behind everything, as
  the camera sees it. **Background blur** softens it there.
- **Intensity**: how bright the light is, as a percentage. Below 0 it takes light away,
  except an environment light, which stops at 0.
- **Colour**: the colour of the light.
- **Cone angle** and **Cone feather**, on a spot: the cone's full angle, and how much of its
  edge fades.
- **Size**, on an area light: its width and height.
- **Falloff**: **None**, **Smooth** or **Inverse square clamped**. **Radius** is how far the
  light keeps its full strength, and **Falloff distance** how far past it a smooth falloff
  takes to fade out.
- **Casts shadows**: whether the light casts the shadows of layers that cast them. An ambient
  or environment light casts none.
- **Shadow darkness** and **Shadow diffusion**, while it casts: how dark its shadows are, and
  how soft their edges get with the distance from the layer casting them.
- **Volumetric**: how much the light shows in the air, as a beam or a glow, seen from every
  3D view. At nought it shows none. While it casts shadows, 3D layers that cast cut shafts
  in it, tinted by any light they let through. An ambient or environment light has none.
- **Haze density**, **Haze colour**, **Height falloff**, **Forward scatter** and **Noise**,
  while Volumetric is above nought or keyed: how thick the air is, the colour it tints the
  light, how fast it thins above the ground (the floor the 3D views draw, through the
  comp's middle), how much brighter the light shows looking towards it, and how patchy the
  air is. **Noise size** and **Noise speed**, while Noise is above nought or keyed: how big
  its patches are and how fast they drift.

Every row but the type and the falloff can be keyframed. **Point of interest**, in Light
settings, aims a spot, parallel or area light at a point rather than by its rotation; the
point's row then shows under Transform, and the rotation rows turn the light from there.

An environment light lights 3D layers and models from every side, and its **Orientation**
and **Rotation** turn the picture. It has no place, so it has no Position. Only the topmost
environment light in a composition lights it, and a layer it lights starts from black. A
model's occlusion map darkens the light it takes from one. The Ambient and Key light a first
model brings stay on beside it. The fixed 3D views leave the background out. A view's looks
other than **Final** ignore the light. A layer it reads is never drawn, its row says **source**
and its eye does nothing.

The 3D layers and models in a collapsed precomp take the lights of the composition it sits in.

**Material options**, beside Transform on a 3D layer, sets how it takes the lights:

- **Casts shadows**: **Off**, **On**, or **Only**, which casts the shadow without drawing
  the layer.
- **Accepts shadows**: **Off**, **On**, or **Only**, which draws nothing but the shadows that
  fall on it, on either face, in the colour of any light a layer lets through. In **Normal**
  they multiply what is under the layer, so a coloured shadow tints footage exactly. Where
  lights add, a shadow removes only that light's share, so a white layer shows none: use
  **Only** or a darker Solid.
- **Light transmission**, while it casts: how much of the light passing through the layer
  takes its colour rather than stopping.
- **Holdout**, on a Model layer or a 3D shape: draws what is under its run of 3D layers in
  its place, with any shadows it takes, hiding what is behind it.
- **Accepts lights**: whether the lights shade it. Shadows still fall on it when this is off.
- **Ambient**: how much of the ambient lights it takes.
- **Diffuse**: how much of the other lights it takes.
- **Specular intensity**: how bright the highlight a light makes on it is.
- **Specular shininess**: how small and hard that highlight is.
- **Metal**: how much the highlight takes the layer's own colour rather than the light's.
- **Reflection**: how much of an environment light it reflects, tinted by the layer, as sharp
  as **Specular shininess**. At the default shininess a full reflection is nearly matte.

Under an environment light, **Ambient** is how much of its light the layer takes.

A Null layer has no Material options. A Model layer and a 3D shape have the four choice
rows, and their materials in place of the percentages.

A layer in a project saved before materials existed has no highlight until its **Specular
intensity** is raised.

3D layers separated by a 2D or adjustment layer don't shade each other. Up to 16 layers in
each run of 3D layers cast shadows, those nearest the layers they shade. A Model layer's
shadow reaches every 3D layer and model, and cuts shafts in the haze.

:::note[Not built yet]
Motion blur on a moving layer's shadow. A track matte or a precomp's masks in the shadow it
casts. Shadows from an environment light. An environment light on particles in the 3D scene,
or inside a collapsed precomp.
:::

## 3D shapes

**Layer ▸ New ▸ 3D shape** adds a **Cube**, **Sphere**, **Cylinder**, **Cone**, **Torus**,
**Plane** or **Pyramid** at the composition's centre. The **3D shape** tool (`Shift+D`) on the
toolbar adds the one in hand where the Viewer is clicked. A 3D shape is always 3D, casts
shadows and, in a composition with no lights, brings an **Ambient light** and a **Key light**.

Its **Geometry options** are its size, in pixels, and key like any row:

| Shape | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| Cube | **Width**, **Height**, **Depth**, **Bevel**, **Bevel segments** |
| Sphere | **Radius**, **Segments**, **Slice start**, **Slice end** |
| Cylinder | **Radius**, **Height**, **Segments**, **Bevel**, **Bevel segments**, **Slice start**, **Slice end** |
| Cone, Pyramid | **Top radius**, **Bottom radius**, **Height**, **Segments**, **Bevel**, **Bevel segments**, **Slice start**, **Slice end** |
| Torus | **Radius**, **Pipe radius**, **Segments**, **Pipe segments**, **Slice start**, **Slice end** |
| Plane | **Width**, **Height**, **Corner radius**, **Corner segments** |

**Slice start** and **Slice end** are angles round the shape's upright axis, and the cut faces
are filled. Below 12 segments the sides are flat, so a cone with 4 is a pyramid. **Surface**
has the rows of a [3D model's material](/use/importing/#3d-models), with its presets. A
parametric mesh imported from After Effects becomes a 3D shape.

## Parenting

Parent a layer to another and it inherits the parent's transform and can apply its own
on top of them. Cameras and lights follow their parents too. A two-node camera's point of
interest is read in the parent's space, as its position is. A parent's scale moves a camera
or a light but never stretches or mirrors it. See
[Transforming layers](/use/transform/#parenting).

| **Matte ▸** | Gates the layer by [another layer](/use/mattes/#the-layer-menu). |
| **Layer styles ▸** | Adds a [layer style](/use/layer-styles/). |
| **Reveal ▸** | The Timeline's [reveal keys](/panels/timeline/#reveal-keys) as commands, with **Expand all** and **Collapse all**. |
| **Create ▸** | **Shapes from text**, **Shapes from vector layer**, **Split into shape layers** and **Points from text**. See [Text](/use/text/#convert-it) and [Shapes](/use/shapes/). |
| **3D ▸** | **Camera settings**, **Light settings** and **Model settings** for the selected layer of that kind, then **Project from camera**, **Edit in 3D app** and **Create cameras and lights**, on a [Model layer](/use/importing/#3d-models). |
| **Precompose…** | [Precompose](#groups-and-precomposing). |
| **New composition from selection** | In a composition that holds clips. See [Cutting](/use/cutting/#clips-as-compositions). |
| **Group layers**, **Ungroup** | [Layer groups](/use/layer-groups/). |

A row is greyed while the selection holds nothing it can act on. **Layer settings…**,
**Detach audio**, the two stream rows, **Enable Retime**, the Convert row, **Create** and
the three settings rows under **3D** act on one layer. Every other row acts on each
selected layer that can take it, as one undo step.
