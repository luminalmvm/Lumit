A **multicam comp** holds the cameras and recorders of one shoot, each on a track of its own,
in step. Each track is an **angle**. In the [Cut workspace](/use/cutting/) a multicam comp is
cut as it plays, by picking angles in the **angle viewer**.

<Shot file="multicam.png" caption="The angle viewer beside the composition, and multicam clips on the tracks." eager/>

## Making a multicam comp

Select the footage in the [Project panel](/panels/project/), right-click and choose **New
multicam comp…**. It is also on the **Composition** menu.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The comp's name. |
| **Sync by** | What lines the files up. |
| **Angles** | **Each file** gives every file an angle. **Each folder** makes the files in one folder on disk one camera, one after another on one angle. |
| **Correct drift** | A sound file whose clock runs fast or slow against the cameras plays at the speed that keeps it in step. With **Sound** only. |

| Sync by | What lines up |
| --- | --- |
| **Sound** | The files' sound. |
| **Timecode** | The timecode each file was recorded with. |
| **In marks** | Each file's In mark, set in the source view. |
| **Out marks** | Each file's Out mark, set in the source view. |
| **Markers** | Each file's first marker, set in the source view. |
| **Starts** | Each angle's first file starts with the comp, and its other files follow on. |

The comp takes the frame size and rate most of the picture files have. An angle of another
size is fitted to the frame. Picture angles stand above sound angles, and each angle wears a
label colour of its own. A file with no picture is a **sound angle**, and a new clip's sound
plays the first one.

A file with nothing to line it up by goes in after the other files on its angle, and the
status line names it. A file placed on weak evidence is named too.

**Multicam** on a composition's menu in the Project panel makes any comp a multicam comp,
its Sequence layers its angles, or an ordinary comp again.

## Placing it

Drag a multicam comp onto a track, as footage. Its picture lands on a picture track and its
sound on a sound track, linked. Each is a **multicam clip**, showing the angle's name and
wearing its colour.

## The angle viewer

Double-click a multicam clip, or in the Cut workspace a multicam comp in the Project panel,
to show its angles in the [source view](/use/cutting/#the-source-view). While the playhead is over a multicam
clip, the angle viewer shows the moment under it and plays with it.

The angle the picture shows is outlined. Each angle heard carries a speaker. An angle whose
track is switched off in the multicam comp is left out. **Timecode** on the angle viewer's
menu shows each angle's own timecode.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Picture and sound** | Picking an angle switches both. |
| **Picture** | Picking an angle switches the picture. The sound stays on its angle. |
| **Sound** | Picking an angle switches the sound. |
| **Previous angles**, **Next angles** | Show the next page of angles, sixteen to a page. |

| In the angle viewer | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Click an angle | Cuts the multicam clip at the playhead and switches the piece after the cut. |
| `Alt`+click | Switches the clip under the playhead without a cut. |
| `Shift`+click | Shows the angle across the work area. |
| `Ctrl`+`Alt`+click | Hears the angle's sound under the clip as well, or no longer. |

With no multicam clip under the playhead, the angle viewer stands on its own frame with a
strip and In and Out marks, and a click picks the angles to place. **Insert** and
**Overwrite** place the marked stretch with them.

## Footage in step

With an ordinary clip under the playhead whose file is in a multicam comp, the angle viewer
shows that comp in step with it, the clip's own angle outlined. Clicking another angle, or
pressing its number, puts that angle on the track above, in step, from the playhead to the
Out mark, else to the end of the clip.

## Cutting

Play, and cut to the angles as the shot goes. Each cut is one undo step, and clears the
Cut timeline's selection.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `1` to `9` | Cuts to that angle of the page. |
| `Alt`+`1` to `9` | Switches to that angle without a cut. |
| `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`←`, `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`→` | Switches to the previous or the next angle. |
| `Ctrl`+`Alt`+`1`, `2`, `3` | **Picture and sound**, **Picture**, **Sound**. |
| `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`↑`, `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`↓` | Previous and next page of angles. |

With no multicam clip under the playhead, the digits go to their [markers](/use/markers/).

## Afterwards

- **Angle ▸** on a multicam clip's menu switches the picked clips' picture, and **Sound
  angle ▸** their sound.
- **Add sound ▸** hears another angle's sound under the clip as well. Switching leaves it on
  its angle.
- A cut inside one multicam clip, like any cut that changed nothing, is drawn dotted.
  **Join** on the clip's menu takes it away, and the later piece takes the earlier one's
  angle.
- The **Roll** tool moves a cut between two angles.
- **Flatten** puts the footage the angle plays in the clip's place. Each piece keeps its
  file's own effects and fades, then the angle track's effects, then the clip's effects,
  fades and speed. The angle track's own placement in the multicam comp is not carried.
- **Open multicam comp** opens the comp with its angles as tracks. Moving an angle's clips
  there moves every cut that shows it. Effects on an angle's track show in every cut of it.
- Right-click an angle's track for **Sync to angle ▸**, which lines its files up again by
  their sound against another angle. A file that would land on another stays where it was,
  and the status line names it.
- [Captions](/use/captions/) made from a track of multicam clips follow the angle each clip
  plays.

## Markers on footage

With footage in the source view, `M` puts a marker on it at the frame shown. Its strip shows
them. **Add marker** and **Clear markers** are on the source view's menu, and right-clicking
a marker offers **Delete marker**.
