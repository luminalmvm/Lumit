A **layer style** is drawn from a layer's own shape: a shadow behind it, a glow round it, a
colour over it, a stroke on its edge. A layer holds one of each style at most, and the
styles draw in a fixed order after the layer's [effects](/use/effects/).

## Add a style

- **Layer ▸ Layer styles** adds the chosen style to every selected layer. A style the
  layer already has is greyed.
- **Layer styles ▸** on the [row menu](/use/layers/#the-row-menu) of a Footage, Solid,
  Precomp, Text, Shape or Sequence layer holds the same list.
- Once a layer has a style, the **+** on the **Styles** heading in
  [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/) lists the styles it has not got.

## The styles

In the order they draw, back to front:

| Style | Draws |
| --- | --- |
| **Drop shadow** | The layer's shape, softened, tinted and offset, behind the layer. |
| **Outer glow** | The layer's shape, softened and tinted, behind the layer and in front of its shadow. |
| **Gradient overlay** | A two-colour gradient over the layer, inside its alpha. |
| **Colour overlay** | One colour over the layer, inside its alpha. |
| **Inner glow** | A glow inside the layer, from its edge or from its centre. |
| **Inner shadow** | A shadow inside the layer's edge. |
| **Stroke** | A line along the edge of the layer's alpha. |

The order cannot be changed.

## Edit a style

A layer's styles sit under a **Styles** heading, below its effects, in Effect controls and
in the layer's rows in the Timeline. Every row keyframes as an effect's does.

A style's heading in Effect controls has the tick, **Reset** and the **×** of an effect.
Right-click it for **Bypass**, **Rename**, **Reset** and **Delete**. A style is not
dragged, copied, duplicated or saved as a preset.

Each style ends with **Blend** and **Mix**, as an effect does. On the two overlays the Mix
row is named **Opacity**. A style has no Matte row.

Angles are measured clockwise from straight up. Distances and sizes are in composition
pixels.

## Drop shadow

| Control | Does | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shadow colour** | The shadow's colour. | Black |
| **Opacity** | How dark the shadow is. | 75% |
| **Direction** | The angle the shadow is offset along. | 135° |
| **Distance** | How far the shadow is offset. | 12 |
| **Softness** | How far the shadow's edge is blurred. | 8 |
| **Spread** | Hardens the blurred edge outwards. At 100% the edge is hard. | 0% |
| **Layer knocks out shadow** | Removes the shadow from behind the layer. Off, the shadow shows through a layer that is partly transparent. | On |

## Outer glow

| Control | Does | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Glow colour** | The glow's colour. | A warm white |
| **Opacity** | How strong the glow is. | 75% |
| **Softness** | How far the glow reaches. | 12 |
| **Spread** | Hardens the glow's edge outwards. | 0% |

## Gradient overlay

| Control | Does | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Colour A**, **Colour B** | The colours at the start and the end of the gradient. | White, black |
| **Gradient type** | **Linear** runs the gradient along the angle. **Radial** runs it out from the middle of the layer. | Linear |
| **Angle** | Which way a linear gradient runs. | 180° |
| **Scale** | How far the gradient is stretched across the layer. 100% spans the layer. | 100% |
| **Reverse** | Swaps the two colours. | Off |
| **Opacity** | How much of the overlay is taken. | 100% |

## Colour overlay

| Control | Does | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Colour** | The colour laid over the layer. | White |
| **Opacity** | How much of the overlay is taken. | 100% |

## Inner glow

| Control | Does | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Glow colour** | The glow's colour. | A warm white |
| **Opacity** | How strong the glow is. | 75% |
| **Softness** | How far the glow reaches. | 12 |
| **Choke** | Hardens the glow's edge inwards. | 0% |
| **Source** | **Edge** glows in from the layer's edge. **Centre** glows out from its middle. | Edge |

## Inner shadow

| Control | Does | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shadow colour** | The shadow's colour. | Black |
| **Opacity** | How dark the shadow is. | 75% |
| **Direction** | The angle the shadow is offset along. | 135° |
| **Distance** | How far the shadow is offset inwards. | 8 |
| **Softness** | How far the shadow's edge is blurred. | 8 |
| **Choke** | Hardens the blurred edge inwards. | 0% |

## Stroke

| Control | Does | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stroke colour** | The stroke's colour. | Red |
| **Opacity** | How solid the stroke is. | 100% |
| **Size** | How thick the stroke is. | 3 |
| **Position** | **Outside**, **Inside** or **Centre**: which side of the layer's edge the stroke sits on. | Outside |

The Stroke style follows the layer's alpha. The [Stroke](/effects/generate/stroke/) effect
draws along a mask's path.
