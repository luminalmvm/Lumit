Lumit's **panels** dock and split. An arrangement of panels is a **workspace**, and there
are seven shipped ones plus however many you save.

<Shot file="workspace.png" caption="A workspace: Viewer, Timeline, and Project docked together." eager/>

## The panels

Every panel is under **Window ▸ Panels**, with a tick showing whether it is in the
arrangement. Toggling one adds or drops it.

| Panel | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [**Viewer**](/panels/viewer/) | The picture, and the tools that work on it. |
| [**Project**](/panels/project/) | Assets and folders. |
| [**Timeline**](/panels/timeline/) | Time, layers, keyframes, and the graph editor. |
| **Cut timeline** | The composition's layers as tracks of clips. See [Cutting](/use/cutting/). |
| [**Effect controls**](/panels/effect-controls/) | The selected layer's whole effect stack, parameter by parameter. |
| [**Effects & presets**](/panels/effects-presets/) | Every effect, preset and installed [plugin](/use/plugins/). |
| **Node graph** | Boxes and wires: the selected layer's stack, a Custom shader's inner graph, or a [node graph composition](/use/node-graphs/). See [The node graph](/use/nodes/). |
| **Node** | The parameter rows of whichever box the Node graph has picked, and the form of a Read or Input box. |
| [**Easing**](/panels/easing/) | Shape a curve and apply it to the selected keyframes. |
| **Retime** | Set a layer's speed, stretch, freeze or reverse it, and choose its in-between frames. |
| [**Scopes**](/panels/scopes/) | Waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope and histogram. |
| **Grade** | The selected layer's grade as wheels, primaries, curves and a look. See [Grade a layer](/use/colour-management/#grade-a-layer). |
| **Text** | The font, size, spacing and outline of a [text layer](/use/text/). |
| **Paragraph** | How a text layer's lines are aligned, indented and spaced. |
| **Expressions** | Saved [expressions](/use/expressions/), and an editor that applies one. |
| **Stagger** | Starts the selected layers a set number of frames apart. See [Stagger](/use/layers/#stagger). |
| [**Notes**](/panels/notes/) | One page of text kept in the project file. |
| **Align** | Moves the selected layers' anchor points, or lines the layers up with the composition. See [Align](/panels/align/). |
| [**Hierarchy**](/panels/hierarchy/) | The open composition's layers as a tree, precomps expandable. |
| [**Debug**](/panels/debug-view/) | Engine readouts. Off, and out of the Panels list, until you turn it on from the Help menu. |
| **Mixer** | A strip per audible layer, and the Master strip. See [Mixer](/panels/audio/#mixer). |
| **Audio** | Output levels, beats, and the selected layer's sound. See [Audio panel](/panels/audio/#audio-panel). |
| **Audio timeline** | A row per layer that makes a sound, with its wave on the lane. See [Audio timeline](/panels/audio/#audio-timeline). |

**Window ▸ New Viewer** adds another Viewer panel, and **Window ▸ New 3D viewport** a
[3D viewport](/use/camera/#3d-viewport). The Viewer is the one panel that can be in the
arrangement more than once.

**Window ▸ Extensions** lists the panels of installed [extensions](/use/extensions/), and
**Manage extensions…** opens **Settings ▸ Addons**.

`Ctrl+F6` and `Ctrl+Shift+F6` move the keyboard to the next and the previous panel, and
`Ctrl+F` to the active panel's search box. Six panels have a key that shows or hides them.
See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/#panels).

## Rearrange

Drag a panel's tab. The frame you drag it over shows where it would land:

- **Over the middle** joins that group as another tab.
- **Near an edge** splits the frame and puts the panel on that side. The panels either side
  resize proportionally.

Drag a seam between two panels to change their share. A double-click on a seam, or **Reset
split** on its right-click, gives the two sides the same room. Press `` ` `` (backtick) with
the pointer over a panel to maximise it to the whole window, and again to put it back. A
double-click on a tab does the same, and `Esc` puts a maximised panel back.

A right-click on a panel's tab, on its header in a stack, or on the empty end of its tab
strip offers:

| Row | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Close panel** | Takes the panel out of the arrangement. |
| **Close other panels** | Closes the rest of its group. |
| **Pop out** | On a Viewer only. Moves it into a window of its own that can sit on another monitor. Close that window to put the Viewer back where it was. |
| **Maximise panel** | Fills the window with the panel, or puts it back. |
| **Add panel** | Lists the panels not in the arrangement, and adds the one picked to this group. |
| **Stacked group**, **One open at a time** | See [Stack a group](#stack-a-group). |
| **Reset workspace** | Puts the Edit arrangement back. |

A tab strip too narrow for its tabs lists them at the top of this menu.

## Stack a group

Right-click a tab and tick **Stacked group**. The group's panels are drawn one above
another, each under a header with its name.

- Click a header to twirl its panel open or shut. The open panels share the height.
- Drag the seam between two open panels to change their heights.
- Drag a header to move its panel, the same as a tab. A panel dropped on a header or on an
  open panel joins the stack below it.
- **One open at a time**, in a header's right-click menu, shuts the others when one opens.

A panel ticked in **Window ▸ Panels** joins a stack when the arrangement has one. Unticking
**Stacked group** turns the stack back into tabs.

## The shipped workspaces

The strip at the right-hand end of the toolbar switches between them, and so does **Window ▸
Workspace**. With the toolbar on the left, the strip is on the menu bar's line.
`Alt+Shift+1` to `Alt+Shift+9` reach them by position, counting the presets
first and then your own. Each button's tooltip gives its name and its shortcut.

| Workspace | Arranged for |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | The default. Project, Effect controls and Notes tabbed on the left, Viewer centre, Timeline across the bottom, and a stack on the right: Effects & presets open, with Text, Paragraph, Align and Stagger shut below it. |
| **Effects** | Project and Effect controls tabbed on the left, Viewer centre, the Easing panel beside it, and a stack at the right-hand edge with Effects & presets open and Expressions and Scopes shut. |
| **Nodes** | The [Node graph](/use/nodes/) across most of the window, a short Timeline under it, a small Viewer down the right, and the Node panel open under it with Effects & presets and Expressions shut. |
| **Colour** | Project and Effects & presets tabbed on the left, Viewer centre with Scopes under it, and the Grade panel on the right with Effect controls tabbed behind it. |
| **Audio** | Mixer in front of Project and Effect controls on the left, Viewer centre, the Audio panel open on the right with Effects & presets shut below it, and the Audio timeline across the bottom. |
| **Retiming** | Project, Effect controls and Effects & presets tabbed on the left, the Retime panel open on the right, with Easing, Stagger and Expressions shut below it, and the Timeline on the speed graph. |
| **Cut** | Project, Effect controls, Effects & presets and Notes tabbed on the left, a Viewer showing a source view beside the composition, and the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/) across the bottom with the Mixer at its right. |

Rearranging under a preset moves the panels and leaves the preset alone. A preset moved
from how it shipped carries a dot on the strip.

A right-click on a name on the strip has the rows of **Window ▸ Workspace** for that
workspace. On a preset, **Reset workspace** there puts that preset back to how it shipped.
**Window ▸ Workspace ▸ Reset workspace** puts the Edit arrangement back.

**Change shortcut…**, in either menu, takes the next keys pressed, and **Apply** makes them
switch to that workspace. The shortcut belongs to the workspace's place
on the strip, so renaming a workspace of your own can move it to another. The row is
disabled past the ninth place.

## Save your own

**Window ▸ Workspace ▸ Save as new workspace…** asks for a name and writes the arrangement
out. It joins the strip beside the presets.

The same menu carries **Rename workspace…**, **Delete workspace** and **Export workspace…**,
each live only on one of your own, and **Import workspace…**. Export
writes a `.lumworkspace` file; Import reads one and adds it to the strip. A file that is
not a workspace is refused.

Your workspaces are listed alphabetically. A name is taken only once: saving a second
*Grading* gives you *Grading 2*.

**A workspace of your own keeps what you drag into it.** Move a panel while one is in
force and the change is written back to its file. Deleting one leaves the panels on screen
where they are.

## What the project stores

A project opened on another machine is the same edit under that machine's panel arrangement.

| Yours, per user | The project's |
| --- | --- |
| Which panels are open, where, size | Which compositions are open, and which is in front |
| Timeline column visibility and widths | Per-composition Viewer state: resolution, magnification, channel, guides |
| Interface scale, tooltips, motion | Per-composition Timeline state: twirls, work area, playhead, zoom, horizontal and vertical scroll |
| Where a settings or export window was left | Guides, markers, the region of interest |

Switching workspace never closes, reloads or re-renders anything. It only moves panels.

## How large everything draws

**Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Interface ▸ Scale** runs from 75 % to 200 % in steps of 5, with a
field beside the slider for typing a value. A drag on the slider applies on release.

Three more settings sit under it: **Tooltips**, on or off; **Motion**, how much controls
animate as they change; and **Compact**, which tightens row spacing throughout.

A tooltip gives the control's name and its shortcut, as the keymap has it now.

## The command palette

`Ctrl+Shift+P` opens the command palette, and so do **Window ▸ Command palette…** and the
box at the right-hand end of the menu bar. It finds every menu command, composition, layer,
effect, panel and setting by name, and **Enter** runs or opens the one highlighted.

<Shot file="command-palette.png" caption="The command palette, with a heading over each kind of row." />

Typing narrows the list. One or two letters need only appear in order, so `nc` finds *New
composition*. Three or more have to start a word. `↑` and `↓` move the highlight and `Esc`
closes the palette. Before anything is typed, the palette leads with the last eight commands
run, from it or from the menus.

| Heading | Enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Command** | Runs the menu command. The row carries its menu path, its shortcut, and a tick when it is a switch that is on. |
| **Comp** | Opens the composition in the Viewer and Timeline. |
| **Layer** | Selects that layer in the open composition. |
| **Effect** | Adds the effect to the selected layers. The row reads *Select a layer first* while no layer is selected. |
| **Panel** | Shows the panel, brings it to the front and gives it the keyboard. |
| **Setting** | Opens Settings on that page, at that setting. |

A command its menu shows disabled is listed greyed, after the rest.

The box in the menu bar is hidden in a window under 1,000 pixels wide, and
**Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Command box in the top line** turns it off.

## The menu bar

Nine menus: **File, Edit, Composition, Layer, Effect, Animation, View, Window, Help**. A
menu item that needs something you have not selected appears disabled. A command that is
not built yet is left off its menu. A row shows the shortcut the keymap has for it now. The
headings scroll sideways in a window too narrow for all nine.

On Windows the menu bar is the window's top line, with **Minimise**, **Maximise** (or
**Restore**) and **Close** at its right. Dragging the empty part of the line moves the
window, and a double-click maximises it or puts it back. A right-click there has the same
three commands. Windows 11 opens its snap layouts over **Maximise**. On Linux the same line
replaces
the system's title bar when **Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Lumit's own title bar** is on, from
the next start. macOS keeps its own title bar, and has the menus in the system's menu bar,
with **Settings…** and **About Lumit** in the **Lumit** menu.

The window is at least 840 × 560.

Settings, History and the other windows inside Lumit move when dragged by any part no
control takes. A right-click on a window's title strip has **Reset place**, or **Reset size
and place** on one that resizes, and **Close**.

## Undo and history

**Edit ▸ Undo** and **Redo** name the step they would take. `Ctrl+Z` undoes and
`Ctrl+Shift+Z` redoes. **Edit ▸ History…** lists every step: see
[History](/use/projects/#history).

**Edit ▸ Clear** empties the stores of rendered frames with **Memory cache**, **Disk
cache…** and **All caches…**, and has **Undo history…**. The disk cache asks first.

## The status line

The strip along the bottom of the window. Left to right:

| Part | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | The profile button. See [Profiles](/use/profiles/). |
| **Update** | Where the updater has got to. A press checks, downloads or restarts, and the tooltip lists what the waiting releases change. See [Updating](/start/install/#updating). |
| **Nightly** | A pill, on a nightly build only. |
| **Previews** | Comments nobody has looked at, or a ring while a composition uploads. It opens the Previews window. See [Previews](/use/review-links/). |
| **Project** | The project's name, or **Untitled** before it has a file, with a dot before it while it has unsaved changes. The tooltip reads **Saved**, **Unsaved changes** or **Not saved yet**, and a click saves. A right-click has **Save**, **Save as…**, **Revert to saved…** and **Show in folder**. With no project it reads **No project**. |
| **Backup** | The open project's backup, with Pro. See [Backup](/use/backup/#the-mark-on-the-bottom-strip). |
| **Cache** | How full the RAM, VRAM and disk tiers are. A click opens the three with a **Clear** each, and the disk tier asks first. A right-click has **Clear all caches…** and **Preview and cache settings…**. |
| **Hourglass** | Turns render-time measuring on and off. See [the Timeline](/panels/timeline/#render-times). |
| **Driving** | While another program is connected and can [edit the open project](/use/command-line/#let-another-program-edit-the-project-open-in-lumit), its name, with **Disconnect**. |
| **Shared** | While a project is shared, who is in it. It opens the **Multi-edit** window. See [Shared projects](/use/projects/#shared-projects). |
| **Notice** | The latest message, with **×** to dismiss it. Click it to list the last twenty. |
| **Jobs** | A running export or proxy, with its frame count, a bar and a **Cancel**. An export adds the time left once it has run for five seconds. A finished job reads where the file went, that it was cancelled, or why it failed. A click on a finished export opens its folder. |
| **Output peak** | While playing, what the sound output peaked at, in dB. |
| **Hint** | While `Shift`, `Alt` or `Ctrl` is held, what it does to the armed tool, at the right-hand end. |
