Lumit's **panels** dock and split. An arrangement of panels is a **workspace**, and there
are seven shipped ones plus however many you save.

<Shot file="workspace.png" caption="A workspace: Viewer, Timeline, and Project docked together." eager/>

## The panels

Every panel is in the **Window** menu, with a tick showing whether it is in the
arrangement. Toggling one adds or drops it.

| Panel | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | The picture, and the tools that work on it. |
| **Project** | Assets and folders. |
| **Timeline** | Time, layers, keyframes, and the graph editor. |
| **Cut timeline** | The composition's layers as tracks of clips. See [Cutting](/use/cutting/). |
| **Effect controls** | The selected layer's whole effect stack, parameter by parameter. |
| **Effects & presets** | Every effect, preset and installed [plugin](/use/plugins/). |
| **Node graph** | Boxes and wires: the selected layer's stack, a Custom shader's inner graph, or a [node graph composition](/use/node-graphs/). See [The node graph](/use/nodes/). |
| **Node** | The parameter rows of whichever box the Node graph has picked, and the form of a Read or Input box. |
| **Easing** | Shape a curve and apply it to the selected keyframes. |
| **Scopes** | Waveform and vectorscope. |
| **Text** | The font, size, spacing and outline of a [text layer](/use/text/). |
| **Paragraph** | How a text layer's lines are aligned, indented and spaced. |
| **Expressions** | Saved [expressions](/use/expressions/), and an editor that applies one. |
| **Stagger** | Starts the selected layers a set number of frames apart. See [Stagger](/use/layers/#stagger). |
| **Notes** | One page of text kept in the project file. |
| **Align** | Moves the selected layers' anchor points, or lines the layers up with the composition. See [Align](/panels/align/). |
| **Hierarchy** | The open composition's layers as a tree, precomps expandable. |
| **Debug view** | Engine readouts. Off unless you turn it on from the Help menu. |
| **Mixer** | A strip per audible layer, and the Master strip. See [Mixer](/panels/audio/#mixer). |
| **Audio** | Output levels, beats, and the selected layer's sound. See [Audio panel](/panels/audio/#audio-panel). |
| **Audio timeline** | A row per layer that makes a sound, with its wave on the lane. See [Audio timeline](/panels/audio/#audio-timeline). |

## Rearrange

Drag a panel's tab. The frame you drag it over shows where it would land:

- **Over the middle** joins that group as another tab.
- **Near an edge** splits the frame and puts the panel on that side. The panels either side
  resize proportionally.

Drag a seam between two panels to change their share. Press `` ` `` (backtick) with the
pointer over a panel to maximise it to the whole window, and again to put it back.

Right-clicking a panel's tab offers **Close panel**.

## Stack a group

Right-click a tab and tick **Stacked group**. The group's panels are drawn one above
another, each under a header with its name.

- Click a header to twirl its panel open or shut. The open panels share the height.
- Drag the seam between two open panels to change their heights.
- Drag a header to move its panel, the same as a tab. A panel dropped on a header or on an
  open panel joins the stack below it.
- **One open at a time**, in a header's right-click menu, shuts the others when one opens.

A panel ticked in the **Window** menu joins a stack when the arrangement has one. Unticking
**Stacked group** turns the stack back into tabs.

:::note[Not built yet]
Panels cannot be torn off into their own operating-system window.
:::

## The shipped workspaces

The strip at the right-hand end of the toolbar switches between them, and so does **Window ▸
Workspace**. `Alt+Shift+1` to `Alt+Shift+9` reach them by position, counting the presets
first and then your own. Each button's tooltip gives its name and its shortcut.

| Workspace | Arranged for |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | The default. Project, Effect controls and Notes tabbed on the left, Viewer centre, Timeline across the bottom, and a stack on the right: Effects & presets open, with Text, Paragraph, Align and Stagger shut below it. |
| **Effects** | Project and Effect controls tabbed on the left, Viewer centre, the Easing panel beside it, and a stack at the right-hand edge with Effects & presets open and Expressions and Scopes shut. |
| **Nodes** | The [Node graph](/use/nodes/) across most of the window, a short Timeline under it, a small Viewer down the right, and the Node panel open under it with Effects & presets and Expressions shut. |
| **Colour** | Project and Effect controls tabbed on the left, Viewer dominant, and a wide right-hand column with Scopes open and Effects & presets shut below it. |
| **Audio** | Mixer in front of Project and Effect controls on the left, Viewer centre, the Audio panel open on the right with Effects & presets shut below it, and the Audio timeline across the bottom. |
| **Retiming** | The Easing panel open on the right, with Stagger and Expressions shut below it. |
| **Cut** | Project, Effect controls, Effects & presets and Notes tabbed on the left, a Viewer showing a source view beside the composition, and the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/) across the bottom with the Mixer at its right. |

Rearranging under a preset moves the panels and leaves the preset alone. **Window ▸
Workspace ▸ Reset workspace** puts the preset you are on back to how it shipped.

## Save your own

**Window ▸ Workspace ▸ Save as new workspace…** asks for a name and writes the arrangement
out. It joins the strip beside the presets.

The same menu carries **Rename workspace…**, **Delete workspace**, **Export workspace…**
and **Import workspace…**, each live only on one of your own. Export writes a
`.lumworkspace` file; Import reads one.

Your workspaces are listed alphabetically. A name is taken only once: saving a second
*Grading* gives you *Grading 2*.

**A workspace of your own keeps what you drag into it.** Move a panel while one is in
force and the change is written back to its file. Deleting one leaves the panels on screen
where they are.

## What the project stores

A project opened on another machine is the same edit under that machine's panel arrangement.

| Yours, per user | The project's |
| --- | --- |
| Which panels are open, where, size | Which compositions are open, and which is in front |
| Timeline column visibility and widths | Per-composition Viewer state: resolution, magnification, channel, guides |
| Interface scale, tooltips, motion | Per-composition Timeline state: twirls, work area, playhead, zoom, horizontal and vertical scroll |
| Where a settings or export window was left | Guides, markers, the region of interest |

Switching workspace never closes, reloads or re-renders anything. It only moves panels.

## How large everything draws

**Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Interface ▸ Scale** runs from 75 % to 200 % in steps of 5, with a
field beside the slider for typing value.

Three more settings sit under it: **Tooltips**, on or off; **Motion**, how much controls
animate as they change; and **Compact**, which tightens row spacing throughout.

A tooltip gives the control's name and its shortcut, as the keymap has it now.

## The command palette

`Ctrl+Shift+P` opens the command palette, and so does the box at the right-hand end of the
menu bar. It finds every menu command, composition, layer, effect, panel and setting by
name, and **Enter** runs or opens the one highlighted.

<Shot file="command-palette.png" caption="The command palette, with a badge on each row saying what it is." />

A layer row selects that layer in the open composition, and a setting row opens Settings on
that setting. An effect row reads *Select a layer first* while no layer is selected.

## The menu bar

Nine menus: **File, Edit, Composition, Layer, Effect, Animation, View, Window, Help**. A
menu item that needs something you have not selected appears disabled. A command that is
not built yet is left off its menu.

On Windows the menu bar is the window's top line, with **Minimise**, **Maximise** (or
**Restore**) and **Close** at its right. Dragging the empty part of the line moves the
window, and a double-click maximises it or puts it back. On Linux the same line replaces
the system's title bar when **Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Lumit's own title bar** is on, from
the next start. macOS keeps its own title bar.

The window is at least 840 × 560.

**Edit ▸ Undo** and **Redo** name the step they would take, as **Edit ▸ History** lists it.

## The status line

The strip along the bottom of the window. Left to right:

| Part | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | The profile button. See [Profiles](/use/profiles/). |
| **Update** | Where the updater has got to. A press checks, downloads or restarts. See [Updating](/start/install/#updating). |
| **Nightly** | A pill, on a nightly build only. |
| **Previews** | Comments nobody has looked at, or a ring while a composition uploads. It opens the Previews window. See [Review links](/use/review-links/). |
| **Saved state** | **No project**, **Not saved yet**, **Saved** or **Unsaved changes**. |
| **Backup** | The open project's backup, with Pro. See [Backup](/use/backup/#the-mark-on-the-bottom-strip). |
| **Cache** | How full the RAM, VRAM and disk tiers are. Click it to clear them. |
| **Hourglass** | Turns render-time measuring on and off. See [the Timeline](/panels/timeline/#render-times). |
| **Shared** | While a project is shared, who is in it. It opens the **Multi-edit** window. See [Shared projects](/use/projects/#shared-projects). |
| **Notice** | The latest message. Click it to list the last twenty. |
| **Jobs** | A running export or proxy, with its progress and a **Cancel**. |
| **Output peak** | While playing, what the sound output peaked at, in dB. |
| **Hint** | What the armed tool's modifier keys do, at the right-hand end. |
