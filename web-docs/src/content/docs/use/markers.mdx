A **marker** is a labelled point on a composition or a layer. Comp markers draw as small
flags on the Timeline ruler; layer markers draw on the layer's own bar and move with it.

<Shot file="markers-ruler.png" caption="Comp markers and beat markers on the Timeline ruler." eager/>

Each frame holds one marker at most. Adding a marker where one exists replaces it, and 
dragging a marker on top of another does the same.

## Add, move, edit

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Add at the playhead | **Composition ▸ Add marker at playhead**, or `*`, `Shift+NUMBER`, or `Shift+M` |
| Add under the pointer | Double-click empty ruler, or right-click it: **Add marker here**. *Edit marker…* opens on the new marker. |
| Go to the one before or after | **Composition ▸ Previous marker** or **Next marker**, or `Ctrl+Alt+Left` and `Ctrl+Alt+Right` |
| Go to one | Right-click the flag: **Go to marker** |
| Move | Drag the marker along the ruler |
| Edit | Double-click the flag, or right-click it: *Edit marker…* |
| Delete | Right-click the flag: *Delete marker* |

Pressing or dragging a marker does not move the playhead. `Esc` while a marker is being
dragged puts it back.

**Edit marker…** opens a dialogue with what the marker says, its **Duration** in frames
and its **Colour**. Cancelling the dialogue of a marker made by a double-click leaves the
marker, with nothing written on it.

**Markers…**, on the Timeline's **More commands** menu and on a right-click of empty lane
ground, lists every marker of the composition by frame, an unlabelled one as **(no
label)**. Type a label and press **Add at playhead** to add one, and press the button on a
marker's line to remove it.

Each comp marker draws a line in its colour down through the rows of the Timeline, the Cut
timeline and the Audio timeline. Beat markers draw theirs once the view is zoomed in far
enough to tell them apart. **Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Panels ▸ Marker lines** turns the lines
off.

## Colour

Right-click a flag and pick a chip to set the marker's colour. The first chip is the plain
grey. **Edit marker…** carries the same chips. Layer markers take a colour the same way.
A beat marker takes none.

## Layer markers

**Layer ▸ Markers ▸ Add at playhead** puts a marker on every selected layer, at the
playhead, in place of one already on that frame. A layer marker cannot be dragged along
its bar.

Right-click a layer marker for **Edit marker…**, its colour, **Delete marker** and **Delete
all markers**. **Add at playhead** and **Delete all markers** are also under **Markers** on
the Layer menu and on the right-click menu of a layer's row. A click on a layer marker
selects its layer.

## Regions

A **region** is a comp marker with a duration. It draws as a bar on the ruler, from the flag
to a handle at its end, and shades that stretch of time down through the lanes and the graph
editor in the marker's colour.

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Make a region | Right-click the flag: **Make region**. It starts one second long. |
| Make one over the work area | `R` in the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/) |
| Resize | Drag the handle at the region's end, or set **Duration** in *Edit marker…* |
| Move | Drag the flag |
| Remove | Right-click the flag: **Remove region**, or set **Duration** to 0 |

A region is at least one frame long and stops at the composition's end. Layer markers and
beat markers have no region.

## Snapping

With the **magnet** on, a dragged bar, keyframe, work-area edge or marker snaps to the
markers, the playhead, layer ends, the edit points inside Sequence layers, keyframes and
work-area edges. `Ctrl` suspends it.

On the ruler of the Timeline, the Cut timeline and the Audio timeline, holding `Shift` while
dragging the playhead lands it on the nearest of the same targets. Holding `Ctrl` (`Cmd` on
macOS) [sounds the mix](/use/audio/#scrubbing) as the playhead moves.

## Numbered markers

1. Press `Shift+0`–`Shift+9` to set that numbered marker at the playhead. Pressing the
   shortcut again moves it to the current playhead. The marker's label is its number.
2. Press the bare digit `0`–`9` to jump to that marker. Nothing happens until it has
   been set; if you edit the marker's contents this will stop working for that marker.

## Beat markers

**Beat markers** are markers generated by audio onset detection. On a comp with audio:

- **Composition ▸ Detect beats** analyses the comp's audio and writes beat markers.
- **Composition ▸ Clear beat markers** removes only the generated ones.

Detection runs in the background. Where nothing in the composition is sounding, the status
line says so.

A beat marker stays a beat marker once you drag it or rename it.

The [Audio panel](/panels/audio/#beats)'s **Beats** section generates beat markers with a
chosen source, sensitivity, range and tempo. Once a run has a tempo, the ruler numbers the
bars.
