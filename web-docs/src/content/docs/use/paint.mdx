**Paint** puts strokes straight onto the selected layer. Three tools share one button on
the toolbar. `Ctrl+B` arms it, and pressing again steps through them.

<Shot file="paint.png" caption="Brush strokes on a layer, with the Paint heading open in the Timeline." eager/>

| Tool | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Brush** | Paints the fill colour. |
| **Clone stamp** | Copies pixels from elsewhere on the same layer. `Alt`-click sets the point it copies from. |
| **Eraser** | Makes the layer transparent where the stroke covers it. |

A drag is one stroke, and a click is a single dab. The ring round the pointer is the size
of the mark. `Escape` abandons a stroke in progress, and `Backspace` removes the layer's
last stroke.

With no layer selected nothing is painted, and the status line reads **Select a layer to
paint on**.

## Tool options

These appear on the toolbar while a paint tool is armed, and set the next stroke.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Fill** | The colour the Brush paints. |
| **Size** | The mark's width in the layer's pixels, from 1 to 2,000. Starts at 20. |
| **Shape** | **Round** or **Square**. |
| **Hardness** | How hard the mark's edge is. At 0 it fades all the way from its centre. Starts at 80 %. |
| **Pressure** | Whether a stylus's pressure sets the width. Starts ticked. |
| **Opacity** | How opaque the mark is. Starts at 100 %. |

## The clone stamp

`Alt`-click sets the point copied from, marked on the picture with a cross, and the
status line reads **Clone source set**. The source keeps its distance from the brush
through a stroke. With no source set, a stroke is refused and the status line reads
**Alt-click to set the clone source first**.

A clone copies the layer's picture as it was before any paint. Where the source falls off
the layer, nothing is copied.

## Pressure

With a graphics tablet, pressing harder makes a wider mark. The **Pressure** tick in the
tool options turns it on and off.

Pressure sets the **width** of the mark and nothing else. The stroke carries the pressures
it was drawn with.

## Strokes are paths

Lumit stores a stroke as its path, colour, size, shape, hardness and opacity, and draws it
into the layer at the resolution of each frame. A stroke painted at a quarter-resolution
preview exports at full quality.

Strokes are drawn **before masks and effects**: a mask gates the painted picture, and
effects read the strokes as part of the layer.

## Editing strokes

A layer's strokes are listed under a **Paint** heading in the Timeline when the layer is
twirled open, named after the tool that made them and numbered.

- A stroke's row carries its **opacity**.
- Each stroke picks a **blend mode** from the same list a layer does. An eraser ignores
  it.
- **Start** and **End** under each stroke trim it by a percentage of its own length, and
  both are keyframable.
- Right-click a stroke for **Delete stroke**.

Each stroke is one undo step.

:::note[Limitation]
A stroke's colour, size, shape and hardness cannot be changed after it is painted, and a
stroke cannot be renamed. A keyframed **Start** or **End** draws its diamonds on the
Timeline lane and has no curve in the graph editor.
:::
