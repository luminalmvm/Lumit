A **Sequence layer** holds a run of **clips** cut back-to-back on one row.

<Shot file="sequence-layer.png" caption="A Sequence layer: clips cut back-to-back on one row." eager/>

**clip** is only used in reference to an item within a Sequence layer. Everywhere else, the word
is [layer](/use/layers/).

## Why use one

A Sequence layer suits cutting more than compositing. One row holds a whole run of shots,
so the timeline remains clean.

## Each clip carries its own

- Source.
- Trim, in and out.
- [Retime](/use/retime/).

## Edit clips

To edit a sequence layers clips or retime, double click the sequence layer in the timeline.

- Drag a clip's edge to trim it.
- Drag a clip along the row to move it.
- Use the razor to split a clip at the playhead. A cut between two clips is an
  **edit point**.

Editing a clip's retime never alters it's starting point.

:::note[Known issue]
The razor refuses to cut through a clip's eased speed ramp. Cut first, then ramp.
:::

## What applies to the whole layer

Effects, masks, transforms, and switches on a Sequence layer apply to its **whole
output**, once the clips have been retimed. They are not per-clip.

Apply something to one shot only by splitting it out, or by using a separate layer.
