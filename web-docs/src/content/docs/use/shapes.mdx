A **Shape layer** holds vector shapes with fills, strokes, and path operations.

<Shot file="shape-layer.png" caption="The contents of a shape layer in the Timeline." eager/>

## Draw a shape

1. Deselect all layers (`Ctrl+Shift+A`).
2. Pick a shape tool (`Q`) or the Pen (`G`).
3. Draw in the Viewer.

The new shape layer goes to the top of the composition and is selected. It is named after
its shape, or **Path** when the Pen drew it.

With a shape layer selected the tools add a shape to it. Hold `Alt` to draw a
[mask](/use/masks/) on it instead. With any other layer selected the tools draw a mask.

| Tool | Draws |
| --- | --- |
| **Rectangle** | A rectangle between the two corners of the drag. |
| **Rounded rectangle** | A rectangle with its corners rounded by a quarter of its shorter side. |
| **Ellipse** | An ellipse inside the dragged box. |
| **Polygon** | Five sides, with a point at the top. |
| **Star** | Five points, with one at the top. |

`Q` again steps to the next shape tool. `Shift` keeps the width and height equal. A drag
under four pixels draws nothing.

### The Pen

- **Click** places a corner point.
- **Drag** places a point and pulls out its two handles. `Alt` pulls out the leaving
  handle alone.
- `Backspace` or `Ctrl+Z` removes the last point.
- **Click the first point** to close the path. The point grows a ring when the pointer is
  near enough. A path closes once it has three points.
- `Enter`, `Esc`, a click outside the Viewer or another tool ends the path and leaves it
  open.
- **Right-click** with a path under way for **Close path**, **Finish path**, **Remove last
  point** and **Discard path**.

A path of one point is dropped.

## Open paths

An open path's stroke stops at its ends, and its fill closes with a straight line.
**Layer ▸ Mask and shape path ▸ Closed** opens or closes the path whose row is selected in
the Timeline, and **Close path** on the path's right-click menu in the Viewer does the same.
A rectangle, ellipse, polygon or star is always closed.

With the **Pen**, click either end of the selected layer's open path to carry it on, and its
other end to close it.

Deleting points never deletes the path until its last point goes.

## Rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars

A shape drawn with the **Rectangle**, **Rounded rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Polygon** or
**Star** tool keeps the numbers that make it. In place of a **Path** row it has:

| Shape | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| Rectangle | **Width**, **Height**, **Position x**, **Position y**, **Roundness** |
| Ellipse | **Width**, **Height**, **Position x**, **Position y** |
| Polygon | **Points**, **Position x**, **Position y**, **Rotation**, **Outer radius**, **Outer roundness** |
| Star | **Points**, **Position x**, **Position y**, **Rotation**, **Inner radius**, **Outer radius**, **Inner roundness**, **Outer roundness** |

Every row is animatable. Roundness is in pixels; the roundness of a polygon or star is a
percentage, and 100 makes a polygon a circle. **Points** runs from 3 to 100.

**Convert to path** on the shape's menu turns it into a path, the shape it shows at the
playhead. Dragging one of its points in the Viewer does the same.

## Fills and strokes

A shape takes the **Fill**, **Stroke** and **Width** on the toolbar as it is drawn. Click
the word **Fill** or **Stroke** there for **Solid colour** or **None**. A **Width** of 0
draws no stroke.

Both are editable afterwards from the Timeline, on the shape's own **Fill**, **Stroke** and
**Stroke width** rows, and all three are animatable. **Add** and **Remove** on the shape's
menu give it a fill or a stroke, or take one away. A new fill is white and a new stroke
black. The eye beside a **Fill** or **Stroke** row leaves it out of the picture and keeps
its colour.

## Contents in the Timeline

Twirl the layer open and its shapes, modifiers and groups are listed under **Contents**,
each with a twirl of its own.

| On an entry's row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Click the name** | Picks the entry. `Delete` removes the picked entries, and `Enter` renames one. |
| **Double-click the name** | Renames it. |
| **The eye** | Switches a shape, a modifier or a group off and keeps it. |
| **Add** | On a shape or a group. Lists what can be added to it. |
| **Remove** | On a modifier. Deletes it. |
| **Right-click** | **Rename**, **Add**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete shape**, **Delete modifier** or **Delete group**. A shape also has **Remove** and **Convert to path**, a group **Ungroup**, and a Repeater **Expand copies**. |

Under a shape, in order:

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Combine** | How the shape joins the one above it. On a shape with another directly above it. |
| **Fill** | The colour inside the path. |
| **Gradient** | Flat, Linear or Radial. |
| **Gradient colour**, **Gradient stops** | The colour the ramp ends at, and the ramp's editor. With a ramp. |
| **Stroke**, **Stroke width** | The outline's colour, and its width in pixels. |
| **Opacity** | How opaque the shape is. |
| **Gradient start opacity**, **Gradient end opacity** | How opaque each end of the ramp is. With a ramp. |
| **Stop _n_ position**, **Stop _n_ opacity** | One pair for each stop between the ends. |
| **Path** | The shape itself, or the rows of a rectangle, ellipse, polygon or star. |
| **Gradient start x**, **Gradient start y**, **Gradient end x**, **Gradient end y** | The two points that aim the ramp. With a ramp. |
| **Dash**, **Gap**, **Dash offset** | A dashed outline. On a shape with a stroke. |
| **Taper scope** and the taper's rows | On a shape with a stroke taper. |

## Edit a path

With the Selection tool, a selected layer shows the points of its paths. Selecting a
shape's **Path** row in the Timeline shows that path's points alone, and takes the layer's
box away until another row is selected.

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Click** a point | Selects it and shows its handles. `Shift`-click adds it to the selection or takes it out. |
| **Drag** from empty picture | Selects the points inside the box. With a **Path** row selected, a drag from anywhere off a point does. |
| **Drag** a selected point | Moves every selected point. |
| **Drag** a handle | Moves it. On a smooth point the other handle turns with it, and `Alt` leaves the other where it is. |
| `Ctrl+Alt`-click a point | Turns a smooth point into a corner, or a corner into a smooth point. |
| `Ctrl+Alt`-drag a point | Pulls a pair of handles out of it. |
| `Delete` | Removes the selected points. |
| `Ctrl+Shift+T` | Puts a box round two or more selected points. A drag turns them about its middle, in 15° steps with `Shift`. `Esc`, `Enter` or a click takes the box down. With a **Path** row selected and fewer than two points selected, the box goes round the whole path. |

On a path that is keyframed, an edit sets a keyframe at the playhead.

Right-click a point or the path's line:

| Row | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Delete point** | Removes the selected points. |
| **Corner point**, **Smooth point** | Sets what the selected points are. The one in force is ticked. |
| **Set as first point** | Starts the path at this point. On a closed path with no keyframes. |
| **Add point here** | On the path's line. Adds a point there. |
| **Select all points** | Selects every point of the path. |
| **Close path** | Closes or opens the path. Ticked on a closed one. |
| **Reverse path** | Runs the path the other way. On a path with no keyframes. |
| **Rename…**, **Delete shape** | Rename or delete the shape the path belongs to. |

A rectangle, ellipse, polygon or star has no **Close path** or **Reverse path**.

### The Pen's other tools

`G` again steps through the tools that share the Pen's button: **Add vertex**, **Delete
vertex**, **Convert vertex** and **Mask feather**. They edit a path that is already drawn.
See [what the keys do](/panels/toolbar/#what-the-keys-do).

With the **Pen** itself and a **Path** row selected in the Timeline, a click on that path's
line adds a point, and `Alt` on a point converts it.

## Gradient fills

A shape's first rows are **Fill**, the colour inside its path, and **Gradient**, which is
Flat, Linear or Radial. Choose a ramp and its rows appear: the **Gradient colour** it ends
at, **Gradient stops**, an opacity for each end, and the two points that aim it.

Linear ramps along the line between the points; radial ramps outwards from the first,
with the second on the outer edge. A new ramp runs down the shape's box, or out from its
middle.

**Gradient start opacity** is how opaque the Fill end is and **Gradient end opacity** how
opaque the Gradient colour end is, as a percentage. The ramp fades evenly between them, and
an end at 0 is clear.

The Fill, the points and the opacities are animatable. The Gradient colour and the stops'
colours are not.

### Gradient stops

Click the ramp on the **Gradient stops** row to open its editor. The handles at either end
are the Fill and the Gradient colour.

- Click the ramp to add a stop between them.
- Drag a stop to move it. Drag it away from the ramp, or press **Remove**, to take it off.
- **Colour**, **Opacity** and **Position** set the selected stop.
- Right-click the ramp for **Add stop**, **Remove**, **Reverse** and **Space evenly**.

Each stop also has two rows in the Timeline, **Stop 1 position** and **Stop 1 opacity** for
the first, and both are animatable. Editing a keyed stop in the editor sets a keyframe at the
playhead.

Two stops at the same position make a hard edge. A gradient holds up to 16 stops between
its ends.

## Combine two shapes

A shape with another shape directly above it carries a **Combine** row, saying how it joins
the one **above** it: **Apart**, **Union**, **Subtract**, **Intersect** or **Exclude**. Apart
is the default, and draws it on its own.

A run of shapes all set to something other than Apart folds top to bottom.

**The run wears the first item's paint and modifiers.** The ones after it lend their path
and nothing else, so their own fill, stroke and dash rows are left off the panel.

A shape fills the same way after a combine as before it.

## Animate the shape itself

A shape's **Path** row carries the shape, and its stopwatch animates it. Key one path, move
the playhead, drag a point, and the shape morphs between the two.

**Layer ▸ Mask and shape path ▸ Add or remove keyframe at playhead** does the same for the
shape whose row is selected in the Timeline.

Points are paired by **order**, not by feature; where one path has fewer points, it is
subdivided to match the denser one first. Whether a path is open or closed is held across
the morph.

A **Path** row has no value to plot. In the [graph editor](/panels/graph-editor/) it draws
its rate of change. Every number on a shape has a curve.

## Modifiers

A shape layer's contents is an ordered list: shapes, modifiers and groups. A modifier acts
on shapes above it, and where it is added decides which. Two modifiers of one kind stack: a
repeater under a repeater repeats the copies.

| Added from | Acts on |
| --- | --- |
| **Add** on a shape's row | That shape alone. It moves and is deleted with the shape. |
| **Add** on a group's row | Every shape in the group. It goes at the end of the group. |
| **Global add** on the **Contents** heading | Every shape in the layer. It is listed under **Global modifiers**, below the shapes. |
| **Add** on a modifier's right-click menu | The same shapes as that modifier. It goes under it. |

**Add** lists **Repeater**, **Trim paths**, **Offset paths**, **Twist**, **Deform** and
**Smooth**. On a shape it also lists **Fill**, **Stroke** and **Stroke taper** where the
shape has none, and on a shape or a group, **Group**.

A modifier's rows sit under its own twirl, and every row is animatable. **Move up** and
**Move down** swap an entry with its neighbour, and never move one into or out of the
Global modifiers. **Remove** on a modifier's row deletes it, and its eye switches it off.

### Groups

**Add ▸ Group** puts the row inside a new group. A modifier inside a group acts on the
shapes inside it and on nothing outside. **Ungroup** opens a group out in place.

**Layer ▸ Create ▸ Split into shape layers** makes one shape layer per top-level group,
each where it sat, and switches the original off.

### Smooth

**Smooth** rounds off the whole path, jags and corners alike. Its one row, **Amount**, is
how far along the path each point is averaged with its neighbours, in pixels. 0 leaves the
path alone.

The path never bulges past its own points; a large smooth shrinks it a little instead. An
open path's ends stay where they are.

### Twist

**Twist** turns the shape about an axis and flattens it, as if seen side-on. One end of the
shape stays put and the other turns by the angle, so a circle becomes a figure eight at
180°.

- **Twist angle**: how far the far end turns, in degrees. 0 leaves the shape alone, and a
  new twist starts at 10°.
- **Twist rotation**: how far the twisted shape is spun about the axis, in degrees. A full
  turn loops.
- **Twist axis**: the direction of the axis, in degrees.
- **Twist centre x** and **y**: the point the axis passes through. It starts in the middle of
  the shape.

### Deform

**Deform** moves the outline with a smooth noise field. The **Deform** row picks
**Displace**, which pushes the outline in and out along itself, or **Scale**, which scales
each point's distance from the middle of the shape.

- **Deform amount**: how far, in pixels for Displace and as a percentage for Scale.
- **Deform scale**: the size of the noise's features, in pixels.
- **Deform evolution**: moves through the noise, in degrees. Key it to animate the outline.
- **Deform seed**: picks a different noise.
- **Deform detail**: how often the noise is read along the path, per feature. Lower values
  bend the outline more broadly.

### Stroke taper

**Stroke taper** narrows a shape's outline towards its ends, like a brush stroke. It is a
setting of the shape itself, offered under **Add** and **Remove** on a shape with a stroke.

- **Taper scope**: **Each path** tapers every path of the shape on its own; **Whole shape**
  runs one taper along all of them in order.
- **Start taper** and **End taper**: how much of the width is gone at each end, as a
  percentage. 100 comes to a point.
- **Start length** and **End length**: how far along the path each narrowing runs, as a
  percentage of its length. When the two add up to more than 100 they are shrunk together.
- **Taper ease**: how rounded the narrowing is. 0 is a straight ramp.

The taper follows the trimmed path, and each dash takes the width the outline has where it
falls.

## Trim paths

A **Trim paths** entry carries **Trim start**, **Trim end** and **Trim offset**. Start and
end are a percentage of the path's own length; offset slides that piece along the path in
degrees, and 360 is one full lap of a closed shape.

The fill is trimmed with the outline, and an end below the start draws nothing.

## Dashed outlines

A shape with a stroke carries **Dash**, **Gap** and **Dash offset** rows, in pixels.
Dash is the length of each mark, Gap the space after it, and the pair repeats along
the path. Both start at zero — a solid outline. Key the offset and the dashes move along
the path.

## Offset paths

An **Offset paths** entry carries one **Amount**, in pixels. Positive pushes the outline
outwards; negative pulls it in. Corners come out round. A new one starts at 10.

Pull the outline in by more than the shape is thick somewhere and it can cross itself,
leaving a small loop.

## The repeater

A **Repeater** entry draws the shapes it acts on **Copies** times, each copy one more step
along than the last. Nine rows describe the step: **Repeater position x** and **y**,
**Repeater rotation**, **Repeater scale**, the **Repeater anchor** the turn and the scale
happen about, and **Start opacity** and **End opacity**, which the copies fade evenly
between. A new repeater draws three copies.

**Copy offset** says which copy the original art is, so a negative number puts copies
behind it. A copy is a scaled drawing, not a scaled path: halve the scale and the outline
and the dashes halve with it.

The layer's box grows to hold the copies, and the layer's position stays pinned to that
box's corner. One repeater stops at 100 copies, and stacked repeaters at 1,000 between them.

### Expand copies

Right-click a Repeater for **Expand copies**. **Into this layer** replaces the repeater with
its copies as shapes of their own, named after the original. **Into layers** keeps the first
copy here and makes one shape layer per further copy, below this one. **Layer ▸ Expand all
copies** does the same for every repeater on the selected shape layer. Both read keyed
values at the playhead, and each is one undo step.

## Shapes from other layers

**Layer ▸ Create ▸ Shapes from text** makes a shape layer of a
[text layer's](/use/text/#convert-it) letters, one group per letter, where a letter's
contours are a run joined by **Exclude**. **Shapes from vector layer** does the same for a
layer of an [Illustrator document](/use/importing/#illustrator-documents), one group per
path.

## Give it depth

**Geometry options** make the art a solid in the comp's 3D space, as they do
[for text](/use/text/#give-it-depth). Each fill keeps its colour, and an outline with no fill is
made solid as the shape it paints. Repeater copies are solids of their own.
