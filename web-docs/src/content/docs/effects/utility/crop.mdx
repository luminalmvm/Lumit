## What it does

Takes the picture away past four edges, each set from its own side of the frame. The crop
half of Vegas's Pan/Crop. A clip's **Pan/Crop** button in the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/)
puts a Crop and a [Transform](/effects/utility/transform/) on the clip.

## What each control does

- **Left**, **Top**, **Right** and **Bottom**: how much of the frame goes from that side, in
  per cent.
- **Feather**: how soft the edges are.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
