## What it does

Follows a flat surface, or one or two points, through a shot, and writes the movement onto
another layer as a Corner pin or as transform keys. It analyses a Footage layer's file or
a Precomp layer's nested composition.

Where what is being followed stops being followable, the analysis stops there and keeps the
part that worked.

## What each control does

- **Analyse**: start tracking.
- **Cancel**: stop a track that is running.
- **Create corner pin**: put a keyframed Corner pin on the layer named below.
- **Create transform keys**: keyframe that layer's own Position, Rotation and Scale to the
  movement instead. The keys are added to what the layer already had. Following one point
  writes Position alone.
- **Follow**: a flat surface as four corners, one point, or two points followed separately.
  Two points need not be on one surface.
- **Upper left**, **Upper right**, **Lower left** and **Lower right**: the four corners of
  the surface. Read when **Follow** is *Surface*.
- **Point 1** and **Point 2**: the points to follow. Read when **Follow** is one of the
  point options; **Point 2** only for *Two points*.
- **Region size**: how wide each point's search box is.
- **Pin layer**: which layer the Corner pin, or the transform keys, are put on.
- **Feature density**: how many specks the tracker chases. More is slower but copes better
  with a plain or badly lit surface.
- **Use masks**: masks on the layer are left out of the track.
- **Frames**: which frames Analyse tracks. **All frames** is the whole footage however the
  layer is trimmed, **Layer in to out** is the frames between the layer's in and out
  points, and **Work area** is the frames inside the composition's work area. The corners
  are where the surface is on the first of them.
