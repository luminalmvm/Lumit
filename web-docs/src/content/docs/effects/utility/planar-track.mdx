## What it does

Something flat in the shot — a phone screen, a poster, a sign, a laptop lid — is to have
your own picture put on it. Drop this on the footage, drag the four corner points round the
flat thing, and press **Analyse**. The tracker follows the specks inside that quad and works
out, frame by frame, how the camera is stretching the surface. Then set **Pin layer** and
press **Create corner pin**: a [Corner pin](/effects/distortion/corner-pin/) appears on the
layer you named, with its four corners keyframed to sit on the surface at every frame.

The corners you drag are the surface as it is on the frame you drew them on, and every other
frame is measured against that one rather than against the frame before it. That is what
stops the quad drifting off the phone over a long shot.

The track belongs to this effect and not to the footage, so two Planar tracks on one shot
are two separate answers and neither overwrites the other. That is the difference from
[Camera track](/effects/utility/camera-track/), which solves one camera for a whole source
clip.

Where the surface stops being followable — it turns away, someone walks in front, the shot
whites out — the analysis stops there and keeps the part that worked. A quad drawn over a
blank wall has nothing in it to follow, and is refused rather than guessed at. Mask out
whatever crossed the surface and analyse again.

You can keep working while an analysis runs.

## What each control does

- **Analyse**: start tracking.
- **Cancel**: stop a track that is running.
- **Create corner pin**: put a keyframed Corner pin on the layer named below. It does
  nothing until there is a track to read.
- **Upper left**, **Upper right**, **Lower left** and **Lower right**: the four corners of
  the surface, as it sits on the frame you set them on. Drag them in the Viewer rather than
  typing them.
- **Pin layer**: which layer the Corner pin is put on. This is the layer that ends up on the
  surface, so it is not the footage you are tracking.
- **Feature density**: how many specks the tracker chases inside the quad. More is slower
  but copes better with a plain or badly lit surface.
- **Use masks**: masks on the layer are left out of the track. The quad already says where
  to look; a mask says what to ignore inside it — the hand across the phone, the reflection
  crossing the sign.
