## What this family does

The effects that exist to do a job rather than to create a look.

[Transform](/effects/utility/transform/) is the layer transform properties —
anchor, position, scale, rotation, opacity — available as a stack entry. Its
point is that an effect can sit on an **adjustment layer**, so it can move the
composite of everything beneath it. That is the montage punch-in and whip, done
once rather than on every layer.

[Matte key](/effects/utility/matte-key/) is the keyer: it pulls a green or blue
screen, or any colour you choose, down to transparency, with the strength,
balance, clip and despill controls a colourist expects.

[Set matte](/effects/utility/set-matte/) takes another layer's brightness — or
one of its channels — and makes it this layer's transparency, which is the
track-matte relationship written as an effect so that it can sit anywhere in the
stack.

[Broadcast safe](/effects/utility/broadcast-safe/) is the delivery limiter: it
measures each pixel's composite signal amplitude, pulls the illegal ones back,
and — if you ask it to — shows you which pixels those were.

They are grouped together because none of them is decorative. All four are the
sort of thing you reach for knowing exactly what you need it to do.
