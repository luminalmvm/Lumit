## What it does

Replaces the layer's picture with a pass of the Model layer, or the Precomp with 3D layers in
it, named in **Source**. Models and 3D layers cut through each other as they do in the picture,
a group of 3D layers covers the groups below it, and 2D layers draw nothing.

On any other Source, the picture is left alone.

**Normals**, **Position** and **Part id** have hard edges. An eight-bit project clips Position.

Any effect after it in the stack reads the pass as its picture. A **Custom shader** reads
it beside the picture of another copy of the Precomp in **Second input**.

:::note[Not read here]
Motion blur, a Model layer's own effects, track mattes, blend modes and the gas of Smoke and
fire.
:::

## What each control does

- **Pass**: what to show. **Normals** is which way each pixel faces, nought to one on each
  axis with a half for none. **Position** is where it is, in metres. **Part id** is each part
  of a model and each 3D layer in a flat colour of its own. **Depth** is how far it is from
  the camera, in metres.
- **Space**: what **Normals** and **Position** are measured from. **Comp** is the middle of
  the composition and **Camera** is the camera. X runs right, Y up, and Z towards whoever is
  looking.
- **Source**: the Model layer or Precomp to read.
