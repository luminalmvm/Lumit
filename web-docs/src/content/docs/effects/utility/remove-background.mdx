## What it does

Reads the shot with a trained model and files how much of each pixel is the subject, one
matte a frame. Another layer reads that matte through its Matte row, a **Set matte** or a
track matte.

**Robust Video Matting** reads the shot in order, carrying what it made of one frame into
the next. **BiRefNet** reads each frame on its own, is slower by a long way, and is not
trained on people alone.

The model is an **addon**, installed from **Settings ▸ Addons**. Without it the effect
wears a badge and leaves the picture alone. The badge names the pack the **Model** row
asks for.

## What each control does

- **Analyse**: read the shot and file its matte. Only one analysis runs at a time.
- **Cancel**: stop a run and keep the frames already read. A later Analyse carries on
  from them on BiRefNet, and reads the shot from the top on Robust Video Matting.
- **Model**: which trained model reads the frames.
- **View**: the layer with its background cut away, or the matte itself as a grey
  picture.
- **Invert**: keeps the background and cuts the subject away.
- **Detail**: how much of the frame Robust Video Matting works at. Portrait is head and
  shoulders; Full body is a whole person at a distance.

## Notes

Frames the analysis has not reached render untouched.
