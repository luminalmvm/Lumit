## What it does

Tracks the footage and allows you to recreate the camera path within Lumit.

A track can also cover only part of a shot. Where the shot stops being followable,
the analysis stops there and solves the part that worked.

## What each control does

- **Analyse**: start tracking.
- **Cancel**: stop a track that's running.
- **Feature density**: how many points are tracked. More is slower but copes better
  with hard footage.
- **Focal length**: the lens in millimetres on 35 mm film. At 0 the track works it out.
- **Use masks**: masks on the layer are left out of the track.
- **Frames**: which frames Analyse tracks. **All frames** is the whole footage however the
  layer is trimmed, **Layer in to out** is the frames between the layer's in and out
  points, and **Work area** is the frames inside the composition's work area.
- **Show points**: draws the solved points over the picture.
