---
title: Erosion
description: "Shrinks the layer's shape, or grows it, by a number of pixels."
---

Erosion is a **Utility** effect.

## What it does

Above 0, each pixel takes the most transparent pixel within **Amount** and the shape
shrinks. Below 0 it takes the most opaque one and the shape grows, carrying its edge colours
out. Corners stay square.

:::note[Limits]
- Amount reaches 64 pixels at most.
- Past the layer's edges counts as clear, and the shape cannot grow past the layer's own
  picture.
:::

## Parameters

| Parameter | Control | Range | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Amount** | Slider | -64 to 64 | 8 | Pixels at composition size |

## What each control does

- **Amount**: how far the shape shrinks, or grows below 0.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
