## What it does

Lumit's keyer. It changes a pixel's transparency where a pixel matches a chosen
**screen colour**, so a subject shot against green or blue can be composited
over something else.

:::note[Spatial controls are not here yet]
Screen pre-blur, shrink and grow, screen softness and despot removal are
specified but not yet built, as are the inside and outside garbage masks.
:::

## What each control does

- **View**: what the Viewer shows. Final result is the keyed picture. 
- **Screen colour**: the colour being removed. 
- **Screen gain**: how aggressively the matte falls off. 
- **Screen balance**: how the two non-screen channels are weighted against each
  other when judging a pixel.
- **Despill bias**: shifts the colour the despill pulls the screen channel down
  to.
- **Alpha bias**: shifts what is neutral when judging the matte itself.
- **Despill amount**: how much screen colour is drained out of the subject.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).

The **Screen matte** group cleans up what the key leaves:

- **Clip black**: the matte level at or below which a pixel becomes fully
  transparent.
- **Clip white**: the level at or above which a pixel becomes fully opaque.
- **Clip rollback**: eases both clips back toward the unclipped matte.
- **Replace method**: what colour is put where spill was removed.
- **Replace colour**: the colour those two methods blend in.
