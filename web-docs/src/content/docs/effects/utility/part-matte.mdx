## What it does

Cuts the layer down to the parts picked from the Model layer, or the Precomp with 3D layers
in it, named in **Source**, with soft edges, whichever way the parts move. A part is a node
of a 3D model, each material of a node with more than one, or a whole 3D layer. With nothing
picked, or any other Source, the picture is left alone.

To grade one part, duplicate the layer and put a Part matte and the grade on the top copy.

**Parts** reads **Missing** when none of the parts picked are in the Source any more, and the
picture is left alone. A Part matte on a layer another effect names is not read: a row naming
a Precomp reads its picture without its effects.

Under motion blur a matte keeps the frame's sharp edge, drawn at one moment rather than across
the shutter.

:::note[Not read here]
Motion blur, a Model layer's own effects, track mattes, blend modes and the gas of Smoke and
fire, which it cuts with the rest of the picture.
:::

## What each control does

- **Parts**: the parts it keeps, by name, ticked in its list. The eyedropper picks one in the
  Viewer. Shift adds another or takes one back out, and a pick of nothing lets them all go.
- **Invert**: keep everything but those parts.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Source**: the Model layer or Precomp to read.
