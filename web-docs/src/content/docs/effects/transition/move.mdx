## What it does

Moves the picture out of the frame as **Completion** rises, or into it: pushed, zoomed,
turned or stretched, with a smear along the way. A transition runs one Move on each of its
two pictures and sets **Completion**, **Side** and **Fade** itself.

## What each control does

- **Completion** is how far through the move the pictures are.
- **Side** says which picture this is. Outgoing leaves as Completion rises, and Incoming
  arrives.
- **Moves** chooses which pictures travel: Both, Outgoing or Incoming. The other stays
  still.
- **Direction** is the way they travel. 0° is right and 90° is down.
- **Distance** is how far they travel. At 100 a picture leaves the frame exactly.
- **Zoom** is the scale the outgoing picture reaches. The incoming one starts at the
  inverse.
- **Spin** is the turn the outgoing picture reaches. The incoming one starts as far the
  other way.
- **Stretch** is how much wider along Direction the outgoing picture gets. The incoming one
  starts that wide.
- **Ease** is how the move gathers speed: Linear, Smooth, or Sharp, which is slow at both
  ends and fast in the middle.
- **Smear** is how much of the move is smeared into each frame. It narrows to nothing at
  both ends.
- **Samples** is how many copies of the picture the smear is made of.
- **Split colours** has the smear part red, green and blue.
- **Crossfade** is how much of the move, about its middle, the two pictures fade across.
  At 0 there is no fade.
- **Fade** has this picture fade across the Crossfade.
- **Edges** sets what shows where a picture has moved away: Transparent, Repeat or Mirror.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.

## Presets

On the effect's menu, under **Presets**. The same names are the looks of **Move ▸** in the
**Moves** group of the [Transition menus](/use/cutting/#fades-and-dissolves). A preset
leaves **Completion**, **Side** and **Fade** as they are.

- **Push left**, **Push right**, **Push up** and **Push down**: the incoming picture pushes
  the outgoing one off.
- **Slide left** and **Slide right**: the outgoing picture slides off the incoming one,
  which stays still.
- **Cover left** and **Cover right**: the incoming picture slides in over the outgoing one,
  which stays still.
- **Whip left**, **Whip right**, **Whip up** and **Whip down**: a push on the Sharp ease
  with **Smear** at 60, mirrored edges and a short crossfade.
- **RGB whip** is Whip left with **Split colours** on.
- **Zoom in** and **Zoom out**: the outgoing picture grows to 400%, or shrinks to 25%, and
  the incoming one arrives the same way.
- **Spin** turns the pictures through 180° as they zoom to 150%.
- **Zoom spin** turns them through 90° as they zoom to 300%.
- **Stretch** pulls the pictures to six times their width.
- **Drift** is a dissolve across the whole move, with the pictures travelling 8% of the
  frame.
