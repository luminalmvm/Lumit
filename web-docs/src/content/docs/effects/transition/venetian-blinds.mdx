## What it does

The frame is cut into slats of equal width and every slat begins wiping at the same moment.

## What each control does

- **Completion** is how far the slats have closed, as a percentage.
- **Direction** sets the direction the slats run.
- **Width** is how wide one slat is.
- **Feather** softens each slat's edges.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
