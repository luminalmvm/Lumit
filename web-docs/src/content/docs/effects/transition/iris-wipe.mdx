## What it does

Opening a hole in the layer, which expands until it wipes the entire frame.

- **Inner radius** is editable while **Use inner radius** is on.

## What each control does

- **Iris centre x** and **Iris centre y** select the centre position of the wipe.
- **Iris points** sets how many corners the polygon has.
- **Outer radius** sets how far the corners reach.
- **Use inner radius** turns the polygon into a star by pulling every other
  corner in to **Inner radius**.
- **Inner radius** sets how far the pulled-in corners sit.
- **Rotation** turns the shape.
- **Feather** softens the edge.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
