## What it does

Cuts the picture into strips that slide out of the frame as **Completion** rises, one
after another from the top or the left. A transition runs one Slices on each of its two
pictures, the incoming picture's strips sliding in behind the outgoing ones, and sets
**Completion** and **Side** itself.

## What each control does

- **Completion** is how far through the move the strips are.
- **Side** says which picture this is. Outgoing strips leave as Completion rises, and
  Incoming ones arrive.
- **Count** is how many strips there are.
- **Across** sets which way the strips lie. Horizontal strips travel sideways, and Vertical
  ones up and down.
- **Alternate** sends every other strip the other way.
- **Stagger** is how far apart the strips set off, as a share of the whole move.
- **Random order** has the strips set off in a seeded order.
- **Ease** is how each strip gathers speed: Linear, Smooth, or Sharp, which is slow at both
  ends and fast in the middle.
- **Smear** is how much of each strip's move is smeared into each frame.
- **Seed** chooses the order Random order takes.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.

## Presets

On the effect's menu, under **Presets**. The same names are the looks of **Slices ▸** in
the **Moves** group of the [Transition menus](/use/cutting/#fades-and-dissolves).

- **Slices** is the effect as it starts.
- **Slice glitch** is 24 strips in a random order on the Sharp ease, with **Stagger** at 80
  and **Smear** at 30.
