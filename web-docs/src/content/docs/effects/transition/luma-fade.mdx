## What it does

Takes the layer away in order of brightness as Completion rises, the darkest parts first.
The brightness is the layer's own, a **Pattern** drawn over the frame, or another layer's
when **Matte** is set.

## What each control does

- **Completion** is how far the fade has got as a percentage.
- **Softness** sets how wide the edge between gone and kept is.
- **Pattern** sets where the brightness comes from with no Matte set. Picture is the layer's
  own. Noise goes in cloudy patches, Cells in square blocks in a random order, Diamonds in
  diamonds that grow from their centres, Stripes in upright bars that each go from the left,
  and Centre in a circle that opens from the middle.
- **Scale** is how many of the pattern's cells fit across the picture's shorter side.
- **Map placement** sets where the Matte layer's picture sits on this one.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** sets the layer the brightness is read from.
- **Invert** takes the brightest parts first.

## Presets

On the effect's menu, under **Presets**. Each starts at the playhead.

- **Luma reveal** brings the layer in over a second, with a soft edge.
- **Luma hide** takes the layer away over a second, with a soft edge.
- **Hard hide** takes the layer away over a second, with a hard edge.

## Looks

The looks of **Luma fade ▸** in the **Wipes** group of the
[Transition menus](/use/cutting/#fades-and-dissolves). They are not on the effect's own
**Presets** menu.

- **Luma fade** is the effect as it starts.
- **Noise dissolve** sets **Pattern** to Noise, **Scale** to 14 and **Softness** to 10.
- **Blocks** sets **Pattern** to Cells, **Scale** to 9 and **Softness** to 5.
- **Diamonds** sets **Pattern** to Diamonds, **Scale** to 6 and **Softness** to 5.
- **Stripes** sets **Pattern** to Stripes, **Scale** to 8 and **Softness** to 5.
- **Soft iris** sets **Pattern** to Centre and **Softness** to 40.
