## What it does

Wipes the frame around a centre point, like a clock.

## What each control does

- **Wipe centre x** and **Wipe centre y** positions the wipe's centre.
- **Completion** is how far the sweep is as a precentage.
- **Start angle** is the direction  the sweep begins from.
- **Wipe** chooses which way the wedge opens.
- **Feather** softens the sweeping edge.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
