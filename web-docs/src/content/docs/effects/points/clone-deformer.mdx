## What it does

Changes the copies the [Clone to points](/effects/points/clone-to-points/) above it drew,
as one group. **Align** moves the group so its bounds line up on a point. **Boolean** cuts
the group with another layer. **Round corners** rounds its corners. Several Clone
deformers in a row each work on what the one above left.

**Mode** sets what the group is read as. **Raster** reads the picture, so it works on any
layer and after any effect. **Vector** reads the outlines of Shape layers, and the colours
still come from the picture. It needs a Clone to points of Shape layers directly above it,
with only Clone deformers between. Anywhere else Vector reads the picture, as Raster does.

In Vector, the Boolean layer is read by its outline when it is a Shape layer or has a
mask, and by its picture otherwise. A stroke's width is not part of an outline, so a Shape
layer of strokes alone is filled.

## What each control does

- **Mode**: what the group is read as, its picture or its Shape layers' outlines.
- **Deformer**: what is done to the group.
- **Horizontal**: which side of the group's bounds goes on **Position x**. **None** leaves
  the group where it is across.
- **Vertical**: which side of the group's bounds goes on **Position y**. **None** leaves the
  group where it is down.
- **Position x** and **Position y**: where the group is lined up.
- **Boolean layer**: the layer the group is cut with. With none, nothing is cut.
- **Map placement**: where the Boolean layer sits on this one: as it sits in the
  composition, stretched over the frame, or centred at its own size.
- **Operation**: how the layer cuts the group. **Union** adds the layer. **Subtract** cuts
  it away. **Intersect** keeps only where both are. **Difference** keeps where one is and
  not both. **Subtract from layer** cuts the copies out of the layer, in the layer's
  colours.
- **Radius**: the radius of the circle that fits in each rounded corner.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
