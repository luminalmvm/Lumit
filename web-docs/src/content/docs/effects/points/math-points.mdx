## What it does

Makes **Count** points and places each by a formula. **Waves** moves each point across by
one wave and down by another, by how far along the stream it is. Two waves across one
another draw a Lissajous figure. **Rose** lays the points round a rose curve. **Spiral**
winds them out from the position.

A point's place comes from its number alone, so a [Trail](/effects/points/trail/) below
follows each point from frame to frame.

The points go out on the **Points** socket and are drawn as discs. Each shape shows its own
rows and hides the others'.

## What each control does

- **Shape**: the formula the points are laid out by.
- **Count**: how many points there are.
- **Position x** and **Position y**: the middle of the figure.
- **Rotation**: how far the whole figure is turned about its position, clockwise.
- **Wave x** (Waves): the wave that places a point across. **Linear** is a straight ramp.
- **Amplitude x** (Waves): how far across the wave reaches either side of the position.
- **Frequency x** (Waves): how many times the wave runs from the first point to the last.
- **Phase x** (Waves): where along itself the wave starts. Keyframed, it runs the wave
  through the points.
- **Wave y**, **Amplitude y**, **Frequency y** and **Phase y** (Waves): the same four for
  the wave that places a point down.
- **Radius** (Rose, Spiral): how far the figure reaches from the position.
- **Petals** (Rose): the petals, over **Denominator**. A whole odd number draws that many
  petals and a whole even number twice as many.
- **Denominator** (Rose): what Petals is divided by. At anything but 1 the petals overlap.
- **Turns** (Spiral): how many times it winds round from the first point to the last.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft the disc's edge is.
- **Colour**: the colour a point is drawn in.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the drawing is laid over the picture with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
