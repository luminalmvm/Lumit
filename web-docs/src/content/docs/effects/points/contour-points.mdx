## What it does

Makes a point on the centre of area of each closed piece of a path: one of the layer's
masks, or every path of the Shape layer the effect is on. A Shape layer's paths are read
after its combines, so two shapes cut one from the other give a point on each piece the
cut leaves.

Each point carries its piece's place in the path as its number.

The points go out on the **Points** socket and are drawn as discs.

## What each control does

- **Follow**: whose pieces are read. **Mask** is one of the layer's masks. **Shape** is the
  paths of the Shape layer the effect is on.
- **Mask path**: which of the layer's masks.
- **Size from area**: each point is as big as a circle of its piece's area, instead of
  **Size**.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft the disc's edge is.
- **Colour**: the colour a point is drawn in.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the drawing is laid over the picture with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
