## What it does

Puts evenly spaced points along one of the layer's masks and draws them as discs. A layer
with no mask has no points.

On a Shape layer, **Follow** can take the outline the layer draws in place of a mask.

**Placement** can put them at random places instead, or one on each of the mask's corners.
Each point's number is how far along the path it sits, from 0 to 1. It carries the same
under the name `progress`. See [Names](/effects/points/vary-points/#names).

On a Shape or Text layer, a points effect that is last in the stack draws past the art's
edges into the whole frame. With another effect or a layer style after it, the points are
cut at the art's box.

## What each control does

- **Follow**: what the points sit along. **Mask** is one of the layer's masks. **Shape** is
  the outline the Shape layer the effect is on draws, after any trim, offset or combine,
  and dashes as drawn. Every line of it is followed in the layer's order as one long run,
  repeater copies included, and Count is shared between them by length. A shape with no
  fill or stroke is followed too. On any other layer Shape makes no points.
- **Mask path**: the mask the points follow.
- **Placement**: where along the path the points are put. **Even** spaces them by distance.
  **Random** puts them at random places, the same on every frame. **Corners** puts one on
  each of the mask's own vertices between Start and End, and ignores Count. On a Shape
  layer's outline that is each end of a line and each of the shape's own vertices on it.
  After an offset or a combine it is wherever the line turns, and a curve has a point at
  every joint.
- **Count**: how many points.
- **Start** and **End**: where along the path the first and last points sit, as a per cent
  of its length.
- **Offset**: slides every point along the path. A closed path wraps round and an open one
  stops at its ends, unless **Wrap** is on.
- **Wrap**: on an open path, a point pushed past one end comes back in at the other.
- **Align to path**: turns each point to face the way the path runs.
- **Deform**: moves each point off the path, along the normal where it stands, by a wave
  or by noise measured along the path. **Off** leaves the points on the path.
- **Wave**: the Oscillator's shape.
- **Amplitude**: how far a point moves off the path at most. Below 0 it moves the other way.
- **Frequency**: how many waves, or cells of noise, fit along the whole path.
- **Phase**: slides the wave or the noise along the path. 360 is one wave.
- **Seed**: which random places Random makes, and which noise Value noise reads.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is. At nought it is a hard circle.
- **Colour**: what the discs are drawn in.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  emitted on the socket.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the drawing is laid over the picture with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
