## What it does

Puts points at even intervals: rows, columns, and planes receding into depth. Set **Type**
to **Lines** and it draws a line grid instead, which can be tilted back in perspective.
Lines emits no points.

## What each control does

- **Type**: **Points** draws the lattice as discs. **Lines** draws a grid of lines, with its
  own rows under **Lines** and **Transform**, and hides the rows only Points reads.
- **Layout**: how the points on each plane are arranged. **Rows** is rows and columns.
  **Offset rows** moves every other row across by half a cell. **Rings** makes Rows the
  number of rings and Columns the points on each, with Spacing x and Spacing y the gap
  between rings across and down. **Spiral** winds one evenly dense spiral out from the
  centre, like a sunflower's seeds, and the spacings set how tightly. **Hexagons** packs
  offset rows as a honeycomb, every point the same distance from its six neighbours, and
  works the gap between rows out from Spacing x. **Point** puts every point at Position,
  one on top of another until a Jitter or an effect below moves them apart. **Sphere** makes
  Rows circles of latitude from the top of a ball to the bottom and Columns the points round
  each, with one point at each pole. Its points sit in depth, which only shows once the
  layer is 3D and the composition has a camera. Every layout has Columns times Rows points
  on each plane.
- **Columns**, **Rows** and **Planes**: how many cells across, down, and through the layer's
  plane. Planes at 1 is flat, which is what a 2D layer wants. More than one only shows once
  the layer is 3D and the composition has a camera. See [Cameras and 3D](/use/camera/).
- **Spacing x**, **Spacing y** and **Spacing z**: the gap between cells along each axis. The
  lattice is as big as its spacing makes it, unless **Fit to size** is on.
- **Fit to size**: fits the layout into Width and Height in place of Spacing x and Spacing
  y. It stays that size however many points it holds.
- **Width** and **Height**: how wide and tall the fitted layout is. Rings and Spiral read
  them as their diameter across and down, and Sphere as the ball's size.
- **Position x**, **Position y** and **Position z**: where the middle of the lattice sits.
  Position z is how far in front of or behind the layer's own plane it floats. Nought is on
  the plane.
- **Jitter x**, **Jitter y** and **Jitter z**: how far a cell may wander off its place.
  Each cell gets the same nudge every frame.
- **Seed**: which jitter. Reseed to roll a different one.
- **Start angle**: where round a ring its first point sits. Sphere turns the ball about its
  upright axis by it. It shows for Rings and Sphere, as does the next.
- **Arc**: how much of the circle a ring covers. A whole circle spaces its points evenly
  round it, and anything less puts one at each end.
- **Inner radius**: Rings only. How far out the rings start. With the spacings it is added to every
  ring. Fitted, it is the innermost ring, and at 0 that ring is one point at the centre.
- **Tilt**: how far the ball leans its top towards the camera. It shows for Sphere, as does
  the next.
- **Open top**: how much of the ball is left open from the top, as an angle down from it.
  At 0 the ball is closed, at 90 it is a bowl, and at 180 nothing is left.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is. At nought it is a hard circle.
- **Colour**: what the discs are drawn in. Values above 1 are legal, and useful under a
  [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/).
- **Size from**: how big a cell is. **Grid size** shares the grid's size out between the
  boxes. **Corner point** makes Position and Corner two corners of one cell, and the lines
  fill the layer.
- **Boxes x** and **Boxes y**: how many cells across and down.
- **Grid size x** and **Grid size y**: how wide and tall the whole grid is.
- **Corner x** and **Corner y**: the far corner of the cell that starts at Position.
- **Line width**: how thick a line is.
- **H line rel width** and **V line rel width**: the across and the up and down lines'
  width, times Line width.
- **Major line spacing**: every this many lines from the middle is a major line. At nought
  every line is the same.
- **Major line width**: a major line's width, times a line's.
- **Latitude**: tips the top of the grid away.
- **Swing**: turns the grid on its own plane.
- **Roll**: turns the tipped grid about the view. At Latitude 0, Swing and Roll do the same.
- **Z dist**: how far away the grid is. Above 1 it is smaller.
- **Tele lens width**: a longer lens zooms in with less perspective, a shorter one widens
  the view with more.
- **Max points**: the most points that may exist at once. It does not animate. A lattice
  bigger than the cap is cut off at the end.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  emitted on the socket.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the drawing is laid over the picture with.

After Effects' Grid imports as Lines. Its Feather and Invert Grid are not carried.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
