## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket and hands it on in a new order. The order is
what [Clone to points](/effects/points/clone-to-points/)' **In order**,
[Vary points](/effects/points/vary-points/)' **Index** pattern and
[Connect points](/effects/points/connect-points/)' **In order** mode go by.

Every point keeps its identity, so a [Trail](/effects/points/trail/) below still follows
it. Points that tie keep the order they came in.

The sorted points go out on its own Points socket and are drawn as discs. With nothing
wired in it draws nothing.

## What each control does

- **Sort by**: what decides the order. **Shuffle** mixes the points at random, the same on
  every frame. **Position x** runs left to right and **Position y** top to bottom.
  **Distance** runs from the centre outwards. **Angle** runs clockwise round the centre,
  from the right. **Index** and **Size** run from the smallest.
- **Reverse**: the last point first.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y**: where Distance and Angle are measured from.
- **Seed**: which order Shuffle makes.
- **Feather**: how soft the edge of a drawn disc is.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the drawing is laid over the picture with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
