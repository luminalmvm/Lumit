## What it does

Measures the box round what a layer shows. It has eight sockets: **Width**, **Height**,
**Centre X**, **Centre Y**, **Left**, **Top**, **Right** and **Bottom**.

A Text layer is measured round its letters as they are set, not round its text box, and
follows the words as they change. A Shape layer is measured round its art. A solid and a
precomp are their frame. A points stream wired into **Points** is measured instead of the
layer.

A Null, a camera, a light and an empty layer measure nought.

:::note[Not yet built]
Footage measures the composition's frame, not its own. A layer turned in X or Y measures as
if it lay flat.
:::

## What each control does

- **Layer**: the layer measured. A new Measure measures the layer it is on.
- **Padding x**: room added to the left and right of the box.
- **Padding y**: room added above and below the box.
- **Space**: **Layer** measures in the layer's own pixels. **Comp** measures where the
  layer is drawn, after its transform and its parents'.
