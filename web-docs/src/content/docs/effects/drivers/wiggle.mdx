## What it does

Puts out a number that drifts about instead of holding still. Wire its **Value** socket
into a position, a rotation, a blur radius or anything else that takes a number.

The wobble is worked out from the node's own identity and the layer's time, so a preview
and an export agree, scrubbing in either direction gives the same picture, and two Wiggles
on one layer wobble differently.

The value is centred on nought. To wobble around some other number, take Wiggle into a
[Math](/effects/drivers/math/) set to Add, or into a [Remap](/effects/drivers/remap/).

## What each control does

- **Amount**: how far the value strays either side of nought. It has no unit of its own —
  whatever you wire it into decides what the numbers mean.
- **Frequency**: wobbles per second. Low is a slow drift; high is a jitter.
