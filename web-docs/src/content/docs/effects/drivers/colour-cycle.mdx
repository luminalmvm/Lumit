## What it does

Puts out a colour that keeps changing — red, orange, yellow, and round again — at whatever
rate you set. Wire its **Colour** socket into a [Fill](/effects/generate/fill/), a
[Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/), a [Gradient](/effects/generate/gradient/) stop or a
particle colour and the picture cycles without a single keyframe.

It is the one driver that carries a colour rather than a number, so it only fits sockets
that take one.

## What each control does

- **Phase**: where on the wheel it starts, in whole turns. 0 is red, a third is green, two
  thirds is blue, and 1 is red again. Turns rather than degrees, so a
  [Wiggle](/effects/drivers/wiggle/) of amount 1 wired in here sweeps exactly one
  revolution.
- **Rate**: turns per second, and negative runs the wheel backwards. Nought holds the
  colour still at Phase, which is what you want when something else is driving Phase.
- **Saturation**: how coloured it is. Nought is grey at the brightness asked for.
- **Brightness**: how bright, as a per cent of white. It runs past 100 so a driven colour
  can reach the same headroom a colour parameter has.
