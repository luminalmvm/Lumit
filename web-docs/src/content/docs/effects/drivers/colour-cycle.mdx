## What it does

Puts out a colour whose hue turns through the wheel at a rate you set. Wire its **Colour**
socket into a [Fill](/effects/generate/fill/), a [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/), a
[Gradient](/effects/generate/gradient/) stop or a particle colour.

It is the one driver that carries a colour rather than a number, so it only fits sockets
that take one.

## What each control does

- **Phase**: where on the wheel it starts, in whole turns. 0 is red, a third is green, two
  thirds is blue, and 1 is red again. Turns rather than degrees, so a
  [Wiggle](/effects/drivers/wiggle/) of amount 1 wired in here sweeps exactly one
  revolution.
- **Rate**: turns per second; negative runs the wheel backwards. Nought holds the colour
  still at Phase, for when something else is driving Phase.
- **Saturation**: how coloured it is. Nought is grey.
- **Brightness**: per cent of white. It runs past 100, matching a colour parameter's
  headroom.
