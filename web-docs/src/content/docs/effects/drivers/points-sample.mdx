## What it does

Wire a producer's **Points** socket into this driver and returns two numbers:
**Count**, how many points in this frame, and **Nearest distance**, how far
the closest one is from a place you choose.

It cannot read the points of [Scatter](/effects/generate/scatter/),
[Emit from image](/effects/generate/emit-from-image/) or
[Text to points](/effects/generate/text-to-points/), nor those of a
[Vary points](/effects/generate/vary-points/) or
[Pick points](/effects/generate/pick-points/) that reads a picture. It can read
[Track points](/effects/generate/track-points/).
