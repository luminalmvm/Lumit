## What it does

Wire a producer's teal **Points** socket into this driver and it turns the particles into
two numbers: **Count**, how many are alive at this frame, and **Nearest distance**, how far
the closest one is from a place you choose. Wire those out at a parameter — through a
[Remap](/effects/drivers/remap/), usually — and "the glow brightens when a spark passes the
lamp" is two wires and no new idea.

The particles it counts are the particles the picture draws, so a number here can never
disagree with what you are looking at. Distances are counted in pixels at composition size,
and on a 3D layer they are measured where the composition's camera puts the points, not
where they sit in space.

With nothing wired in, Count is nought and Nearest distance is an enormous number, meaning
"nothing is anywhere near". That is deliberate: nought would read as "a particle is right
here" and fire the whole rig at once.

[Scatter](/effects/generate/scatter/) and
[Emit from image](/effects/generate/emit-from-image/) cannot be sampled this way. Their
points depend on a picture, and at the moment this driver runs there is no picture yet, so
a wire from either reads as an empty stream. [Particulate](/effects/generate/particulate/),
[Grid](/effects/generate/grid/) and
[Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) all answer normally.

## What each control does

- **Position x** and **Position y**: the place Nearest distance is measured from. A fresh
  node puts it in the middle of the composition; drag it to whatever the particles are
  meant to be passing. Count ignores it.
