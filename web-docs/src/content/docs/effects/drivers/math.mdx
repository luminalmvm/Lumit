## What it does

Takes two numbers and does one piece of arithmetic with them, putting the answer out of its
**Value** socket. Wire something into A, something into B, pick Multiply, and out comes the
product. It is what an expression would do, drawn as a box with two wires going in so you
can see at a glance what feeds what.

Either input can be left as a typed number, so scaling one driver by a constant is a single
node. Dividing by nought, and taking the remainder by nought, both answer nought: a frame
has to render, and a number that ran away to infinity would take the picture with it.

## What each control does

- **A**: the left-hand number.
- **B**: the right-hand number.
- **Operation**: what to do with them. **Add**, **Subtract**, **Multiply** and **Divide**
  are the obvious four. **Minimum** and **Maximum** keep the smaller or the larger, which is
  how you put a floor or a ceiling under a driver. **Remainder** is what is left after
  dividing, and is the way to make something repeat. **Power** raises A to B, for bending a
  straight response into a curve.
