## What it does

Returns the loudness at the current frame of the sound the **Source** row picks. It has four
sockets: **Amplitude**, the level of the whole sound, **Low**, the level of the bottom end,
**Peak**, the loudest single sample, and **High**, the level of the top end.

The level is measured over a short stretch of time centred on the frame. A layer or clip
that has been deleted reads 0. An Audio level saved before the Source row existed and set
to a layer reads that layer's file before its volume.

| **Window** | Slider | 0.01 to 1; down to 0.001 and up to 10 | 0.05 | Seconds |

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Clip** is editable while **Source** is set to Clip.
- **Audio** is editable while **Source** is anything but This comp.

## What each control does

- **Source**: which sound is measured. **This comp** is the composition's mix, at the layers'
  own volumes, with muted layers silent and solo honoured. **Layer** is the Audio layer's
  part of that mix, after its volume. **Clip** is one clip of that layer, with its fades.
- **Audio**: the layer Layer and Clip measure.
- **Clip**: the clip Clip measures, from the clips of the Audio layer.
- **Window**: how long a stretch of time the level is measured over.
