## What it does

Returns the loudness of the audio from a given layer at the current frame. It has two sockets:
**Amplitude**, the level of the whole sound, and **Low**, the level of the bottom end.

The level is measured over a short stretch of time centred on the frame.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Control | Range | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Audio** | Layer | Any layer in the composition | None | - |
| **Window** | Slider | 0.01 to 1; down to 0.001 and up to 10 | 0.05 | Seconds |

{/* END GENERATED */}

## What each control does

- **Audio**: the layer whose sound is measured.
- **Window**: how long a stretch of time the level is measured over.
