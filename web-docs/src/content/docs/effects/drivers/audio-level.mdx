## What it does

Gives the loudness of a named layer's sound at the frame being drawn. It has two sockets:
**Amplitude**, the level of the whole sound, and **Low**, the level of the bottom end
alone — the one that follows a kick drum rather than a hi-hat. Wire either into a scale, a
glow or a brightness, usually through a [Remap](/effects/drivers/remap/).

The level is measured over a short stretch of time centred on the frame, not off one
sample. It is one of the two drivers that read times other than their own, and Lumit folds
the stretch into what it caches so a held frame cannot outlive the sound it was measured
from.

With no layer chosen, a layer that has no sound, or a layer somebody deleted, the level
reads nought.

## What each control does

- **Audio**: the layer whose sound is measured. Wires never cross between layers, so this
  is a layer picker rather than a socket.
- **Window**: how long a stretch the level is measured over, centred on the frame. Short
  follows every transient and twitches; long rides the tune and reacts late.
