## What it does

Lets you use points that are contained on another layer, similar to Audio level using
another layers audio.

It reads that layer's points after any effect wired after its first producer that changes
the stream, such as [Vary points](/effects/points/vary-points/) or
[Pick points](/effects/points/pick-points/). It cannot read the points of
[Scatter](/effects/points/scatter/), [Emit from image](/effects/points/emit-from-image/)
or [Text to points](/effects/points/text-to-points/), nor those of a Vary points or Pick
points that reads a picture. A [Bake points](/effects/points/bake-points/) below such an
effect makes its points readable. It can read
[Track points](/effects/points/track-points/).
