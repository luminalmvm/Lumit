## What it does

Wire a number into each channel — an audio level, a wiggle, a remapped distance — and
its **Colour** socket carries the colour they make. Alpha sits at one unless something
is wired into it, so three wires make an opaque colour.

The numbers are scene-linear channels, not per cent, and nothing is clamped: a channel
driven past one stays past one.

## What each control does

Each row is a socket for one channel; the slider is its value when nothing is wired
in. A wire may carry any number — the 0 to 1 range is the slider's, not the socket's.
