## What it does

Wire a colour in and four numbers come out: **Red**, **Green**, **Blue** and
**Alpha** sockets, each carrying that channel as the colour holds it — scene-linear,
unclamped, so a value above one survives. Unwired, the swatch on its row makes the
node a constant four numbers.

[Combine](/effects/drivers/combine/) is the other half; a Split into a Combine gives
the colour back exactly.
