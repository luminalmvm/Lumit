## What it does

Holds a set of named sliders, angles, checkboxes and colours, and draws nothing. An
expression reads one by the effect's name and the control's:
`effect("Shake")("Amplitude")`.

It is what an After Effects pseudo effect arrives as when a preset or a project is
imported. The effect keeps the pseudo effect's name, and each control keeps its name,
its range, its value and its keyframes. A point arrives as two sliders, **x** and **y**.

:::note[Not yet built]
A set cannot be made or edited in Lumit. Menus arrive as a number, counted from one, and
the headings a pseudo effect groups its controls under are not shown.
:::
