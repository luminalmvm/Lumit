## What it does

Merge lays the picture on its **A** socket over the picture on its **B** socket. It is a
box in a [node graph composition](/use/node-graphs/) and is not offered on a layer's
stack. An unwired A gives B; an unwired B gives A over nothing.

## What each control does

- **Mode**: the blend mode A is laid on with.
- **Opacity**: how much of A is laid on.
