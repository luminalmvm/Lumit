## What it does

Combine channels builds one picture from the pictures on its **Red**, **Green**, **Blue**
and **Alpha** sockets. Each channel of the result is one channel of the picture on that
socket, the luminance unless its row picks another. An unwired Red, Green or Blue reads as
nothing, and an unwired Alpha reads as fully opaque, so three pictures make an opaque one.
It is a box in a [node graph composition](/use/node-graphs/) and is not offered on a
layer's stack. It is [Split channels](/effects/compositing/split-channels/) in reverse.

## What each control does

- **Red from**, **Green from**, **Blue from**, **Alpha from**: which channel of the picture
  on that socket makes the channel of the result. **Luminance** reads the brightness, so
  the greyscale pictures from Split channels and any colour picture both work unchanged.
