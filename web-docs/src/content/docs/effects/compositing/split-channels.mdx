## What it does

Split channels takes the picture on its **Input** socket and hands out each channel on a
socket of its own: **Red**, **Green**, **Blue** and **Alpha**. Each is an opaque greyscale
picture of that channel, read before the colour is multiplied by the alpha. It is a box in
a [node graph composition](/use/node-graphs/) and is not offered on a layer's stack. Wire
the four back into [Combine channels](/effects/compositing/combine-channels/) to get the
picture back.
