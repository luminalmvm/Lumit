## What it does

Draws a line through the picture and replacing everything on one side of it
with the reflection of what is on the other.

## What each control does

- **Centre X** and **Centre Y** choose the point the reflection line passes through.
- **Angle** adjusts the angle of the reflection line.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
