## What it does

Analyses how the picture moves through a shot, then moves each frame to steady it. In
**Reverse** it moves a layer with the shake of a stabilised one.

It works on footage layers and Precomp layers. See [Stabilising](/use/stabilising/).

## What each control does

- **Analyse**: analyse the shot again. A new Stabilise analyses by itself.
- **Cancel**: stop an analysis that is running.
- **Mode**: *Stabilise* takes the shake out of this layer. *Reverse* puts another layer's
  shake onto this one.
- **Stabilised layer**: the layer whose shake **Reverse** puts back. A Precomp layer means
  the stabilised layer inside it.
- **Result**: *Smooth motion* keeps the camera's own movement and takes out the shake. *No
  motion* holds the shot still.
- **Smoothness**: how much of the camera's movement is smoothed away. At 0 the shot is
  unchanged. Read for *Smooth motion*.
- **Reference**: the moment of the layer every frame is held to. Read for *No motion*.
- **Use this frame**: sets **Reference** to the frame under the playhead.
- **Method**: what each frame's movement is measured as. *Position* is a slide. *Scale and
  rotation* adds a turn and a change of size. *Perspective* adds the lean of a tilted
  camera.
- **Keep scale**: leaves the picture's change of size alone.
- **Rolling shutter**: *Reduce* takes out the skew of a sensor read row by row, where the
  analysis measured one.
- **Edges**: what the empty edges of a moved frame become. *Show* leaves them empty, *Crop*
  cuts the picture to the part that is always covered, *Crop and scale* scales the picture
  up until they are out of frame, and *Fill* fills them from the frames before and after.
- **Maximum scale**: the most *Crop and scale* scales the picture up.
- **Safe margin**: a share of the frame on each side that *Crop and scale* may leave empty.
- **Steady crop**: how still the crop holds. At 100 one crop serves the whole shot. Lower
  lets it ease with the shot and crop less.
- **Extra scale**: scale added on top.
- **Fill from**: how far before and after a frame *Fill* reaches.
- **Fill feather**: how far in from its edge each frame fades into the fill.
- **Trim left**, **Trim top**, **Trim right** and **Trim bottom**: pixels of each frame's own
  edge that *Fill* never uses.
- **Feature density**: how many points the analysis follows. More is slower but copes
  better with hard footage.
- **Use masks**: masks on the layer are left out of the analysis.
- **Frames**: which frames Analyse reads. **All frames** is the whole footage however the
  layer is trimmed, **Layer in to out** is the frames between the layer's in and out
  points, and **Work area** is the frames inside the composition's work area.
- **Show points**: draws the followed points over the picture.
- **Point size**: how big the points are drawn.
- **Amount**: how much of the correction is used. Keyed to 0 on a frame, it leaves that
  frame where it was shot.

:::note[Limitations]
There is no mesh warp method, and camera gyro data is not read.
:::
