## What it does

Creates a rippling warp across the entire layer.

## What each control does

- **Wave type**: the shape of the wave.
- **Wave height**: how far the picture displaces at a crest.
- **Direction**: the direction the wave travels.
- **Pinning**: which edges of the frame are unaffected.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
