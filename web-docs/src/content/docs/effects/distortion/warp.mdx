## What it does

Multiple bend options which can be used to warp the image in a different way

- **Matte** scales how much of the effect each pixel gets: white applies it in full, black leaves the pixel as it arrived, grey part way. **Invert** reads the matte the other way round, light for dark.

## What each control does

- **Style**: which bend.
  - **Arc** bows the whole picture one way.
  - **Arc upper** and **Arc lower** bow only the top or bottom edge.
  - **Arch** bows top and bottom apart, enlarging the middle.
  - **Bulge** swells the middle on both axes.
  - **Flag** puts one full wave across the width.
  - **Wave** the same as flag with the top and bottom edges out of step.
  - **Fish** bows the sides out and tapers ends.
  - **Rise** lifts the picture diagonally.
  - **Fisheye** magnifies outward from the middle and rounds corners.
  - **Inflate** the same as fisheye with a softer, rounder falloff.
  - **Squeeze** crowds rows toward the middle.
  - **Twist** turns the top one way and the bottom the other.
- **Bend**: the amount to warp by.
- **Horizontal distortion**: tapers the picture between its left and right edges.
- **Vertical distortion**: tapers the picture between its top and bottom edges. 
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
