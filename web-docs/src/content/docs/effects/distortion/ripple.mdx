## What it does

Rings of distortion spread out from a point, fading away with distance.
Animate **Evolution** and the rings travel.

## What each control does

- **Radius**: how far the rings reach.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y**: The point ripples emit from.
- **Type**: **Symmetric** slides each pixel in and out along the radius. 
  **Asymmetric** adds a sideways component a quarter wave out of step.
- **Wave height**: the largest displacement of a pixel.
- **Wave width**: the distance from one crest to the next.
- **Evolution**: Adjust the phase of the ripple.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
