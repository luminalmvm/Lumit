## What it does

Spinning a circular patch of the picture around its own centre so that straight
lines become spirals.

## What each control does

- **Angle**: the amount to rotate around the centre.
- **Radius**: how wide the circle is.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y**: the centre point of the twirl.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
