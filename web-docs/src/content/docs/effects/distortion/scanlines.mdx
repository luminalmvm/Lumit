## What it does

Darkens every other band of the picture, the way a cathode-ray display would.

## What each control does

- **Intensity**: how dark the scanlines get.
- **Line period**: the height of one light-and-dark pair.
- **Roll speed**: how fast the pattern travels up or down the frame.
- **Interlace offset**: alternates which half of each period is darkened.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
