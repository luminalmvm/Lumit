## What it does

Hits the layer with sudden jolts that ease back to rest. Each kind of hit has its own
switch and amount, and they land together as one jolt.

## What each control does

- **Amount**: how hard every kind of hit lands.
- **Trigger**: what fires a jolt. Random fires them at the **Rate**. Markers fires one on
  each of the layer's markers and the composition's markers that are not beats. Beats fires
  one on each of the composition's beat markers. On Markers and Beats every kind of hit
  fires at once.
- **Rate** (Random): how many jolts a second.
- **Separate timing** (Random): each kind of hit keeps its own time instead of landing
  together.
- **Ease in**: how many frames a jolt takes to reach its peak. At 0 it hits at once.
- **Ease out**: how many frames a jolt takes to settle.
- **Seed**: which random jolts occur.
- **Edges**: what fills the border a scale or a slide reveals. Mirror reflects the picture
  back. Repeat holds the edge pixel. Transparent leaves it clear.
- **Jolts only**: the layer shows only while a jolt is on it.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).

Each group starts with its own switch. Each hit's strength is random, up to the group's
**Amount**.

- **Blur**: **Amount** is the radius. **Aspect** at 0 blurs evenly; towards 100 only
  sideways, towards −100 only up and down.
- **Colour**: **Amount** is how far the picture takes on **Tint**, over its own light. **Random hue**
  is how far each hit's hue wanders from it.
- **Light**: **Amount** is in stops. **Direction** is Brighter, Darker, or Both at random.
- **Scale**: **Amount** is how much a hit scales the picture, up or down at random, about
  **Origin x** and **Origin y**. **Random origin** is how far each hit's origin wanders.
- **Slide**: **Amount** is how far a hit slides the picture. **Direction** is the way it
  goes, and **Spread** how far either side of it a hit may go; at 180 any way at all.
  **RGB split** parts red and blue along the slide.
- **Time**: **Amount** is how many frames a hit jumps the layer, back to the frame itself as
  it settles. **Direction** is Both at random, Forward or Backward. The layer jumps with the
  effects above Jolt applied.

## Presets

On the effect's menu, under **Presets**.

- **Camera kick** knocks the picture down about once a second, with a blur along the knock.
- **Glitch hit** slides the picture sideways with the colour channels parted and a random
  colour.
- **Light flicker** flickers the light brighter and darker.
- **Time stutter** jumps the layer a few frames ahead or back three times a second.
- **Beat pulse** hits scale, blur and light on each beat marker.

:::note[Not built]
- With Time on, only the first of Jolt and Timewarp in a stack moves the layer in time. The
  other shows the frame as it is.
:::
