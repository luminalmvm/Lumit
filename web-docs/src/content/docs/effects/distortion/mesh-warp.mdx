## What it does

Lays a grid over the layer, **Rows** by **Columns** cells, and bends the picture as the
grid's points are moved. Each point has two handles, one along its row and one along its
column, that curve the picture between it and its neighbours. It works on any kind of
layer.

A new effect starts with a flat grid and changes nothing.

## In the Viewer

With the effect selected in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), the grid is drawn
over the layer. **Guides ▸ Layer controls** hides it.

- Only the Selection tool drags the grid.
- **Drag** a point to move it. Its handles move with it.
- **Click** a point to show its handles, then drag the end of one. The handle on the
  other side of the point mirrors it.
- **Shift** keeps a drag to one axis.
- A drag is one undo step.

The points' own rows are not listed in Effect controls or the Timeline.

## What each control does

- **Rows**: how many cells the grid has down the layer.
- **Columns**: how many cells it has across. A change to Rows or Columns starts a flat
  grid. The points of the grid before are kept, and come back when its Rows and Columns do.
- **Quality**: how many steps each pixel takes to find where it came from.
- **Animate mesh**: on, a drag in the Viewer sets a keyframe on every point and handle at
  the playhead. Off, a drag sets a keyframe only on what it moved, and only where that is
  keyframed already.
- **Reset mesh**: puts every point and handle of the grid back where a flat grid has it,
  and removes their keyframes. It is one undo step.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
