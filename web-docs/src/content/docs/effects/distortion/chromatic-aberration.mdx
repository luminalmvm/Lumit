## What it does

Splits the colour channels based on their distance from the centre of the image.

Can be split by simple rgb, or calculate split amount based on a colours physical wavelength.

## What each control does

- **Amount** is the amount to scale the offset at the frame's edge.
- **Colour 1**, **Colour 2** and **Colour 3** tint the inward, centred and
  outward taps.
- **Wavelength** changes the rgb stepped aberration for a smooth wavelength-based dispersion.
- **Samples** is how many taps fill the offset while Wavelength is on. 
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
