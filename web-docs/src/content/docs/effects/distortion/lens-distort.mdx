## What it does

Bends the edges of a layer, supporting both concave and convex warping.

## What each control does

- **Field of view** changes how wide the lens is.
- **Reverse**, when off, adds the bow. On, it removes the bow
  instead, giving a pincushion effect.
- **Orientation** picks which side of the frame the angle spans.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y** place the optical centre to bend around.
- **Edges** adjusts what to fill resulting transparent pixels with. 
  **Transparent** is the default. **Repeat** smears the border pixel outward. 
  **Mirror** folds the picture back on itself.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
