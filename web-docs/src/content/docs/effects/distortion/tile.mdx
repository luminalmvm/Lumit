## What it does

Tile cuts a rectangle out of the layer and repeatedly stamps it side by side until the frame is filled.

Push **Output width** or **Output height** past the frame's own size and the
stamping carries on past the layer's edges, and the layer grows to hold it.

## What each control does

- **Tile centre x** and **Tile centre y**: the centre point to begin repeating from.
- **Tile width** and **Tile height**: how large the tile rectangle is.
- **Output width** and **Output height**: how much gets stamped, in pixels.
- **Mirror edges**: flips alternate stamps, so copies meet without a seam.
- **Phase**: slides every row of tiles sideways by a fraction of a tile.
- **Horizontal phase shift**: decides if Phase slides vertically or horizontally.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
