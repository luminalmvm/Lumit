Sphere is a **Distortion** effect.

## What it does

Wraps the layer round a ball and lights it with one light, as After Effects' CC Sphere
does. The layer runs left to right round the ball and top to bottom from pole to pole.
Outside the ball the result is clear.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Control | Range | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reflection map** | Layer | Any layer in the composition | None | - |
| **Rotation X**, **Rotation Y**, **Rotation Z** | Dial | Any angle | 0° | Degrees |
| **Rotation order** | Dropdown | XYZ · XZY · YXZ · YZX · ZXY · ZYX | XYZ | - |
| **Radius** | Slider | 0 to 4000; any value by typing | 200 | Pixels at composition size |
| **Offset X (%)**, **Offset Y (%)** | Slider | -50 to 150 | 50 | - |
| **Render** | Dropdown | Full · Outside · Inside | Full | - |
| **Light intensity** | Slider | 0 to 400; any value by typing | 100 | - |
| **Light colour** | Colour | Each channel 0 to 1 | White | - |
| **Light height** | Slider | -100 to 100; any value by typing | 35 | - |
| **Light direction** | Dial | Any angle | -60° | Degrees |
| **Ambient** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 25 | - |
| **Diffuse** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 75 | - |
| **Specular** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 30 | - |
| **Roughness** | Slider | 1 to 100 | 20 | - |
| **Metal** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 100 | - |
| **Reflective** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 0 | - |
| **Internal shadows** | Checkbox | On or off | Off | - |
| **Transparency falloff** | Checkbox | On or off | Off | - |

## What each control does

- **Reflection map**: the layer mirrored in the ball's surface while **Reflective** is
  above 0. With none picked, the layer itself is mirrored.
- **Rotation X**, **Rotation Y** and **Rotation Z**: turn the ball about each axis.
- **Rotation order**: the order the three turns are made in.
- **Radius**: the ball's size.
- **Offset X (%)** and **Offset Y (%)**: where the ball's centre sits, as a share of the
  layer.
- **Render**: **Outside** shows the near side of the ball and **Inside** the far side, seen
  from within. **Full** shows both, the inside through the clear parts of the outside.
- **Light intensity**: how bright the light is.
- **Light colour**: the colour of the light.
- **Light height**: how far the light is lifted towards the viewer. At 0 it shines from the
  side, at 100 from straight in front and below 0 from behind.
- **Light direction**: the side the light shines from, as a turn clockwise from straight
  up.
- **Ambient**: an even light on the whole ball.
- **Diffuse**: how much the light brightens the surface that faces it.
- **Specular**: how strong the highlight is.
- **Roughness**: how wide the highlight spreads.
- **Metal**: how much of the layer's own colour the highlight takes. At 0 it is the colour
  of the light.
- **Reflective**: how much of the Reflection map is mirrored in the surface.
- **Internal shadows**: on, the opaque parts of the ball the light passes through shade
  the inside.
- **Transparency falloff**: on, the outside fades towards the ball's rim.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
