title: DisplaceX
description: "Moves, turns and scales each pixel by a map: the layer itself, another layer or a ramp."

DisplaceX is a **Distortion** effect.

## What it does

Applies a move, a turn and a scale to the layer, and lets a map say how much of them each
pixel takes. The map is the layer itself, the layer picked in **Map layer**, or a ramp the
effect draws. With the defaults it changes nothing.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Channel** is editable while **Map** is anything but Gradient ramp.
- **Map mix input** is editable while **Map** is anything but Layer itself.
- **Ramp shape**, **Ramp origin X (%)**, **Ramp origin Y (%)**, **Ramp scale** and **Link
  anchor to origin** are editable while **Map** is set to Gradient ramp.
- **Ramp angle** is editable while **Map** is set to Gradient ramp and **Ramp shape** to
  Linear.
- **Scale (%)** is editable while **Uniform scale** is on, and **Scale X (%)** and **Scale Y
  (%)** while it is off.

## What each control does

- **Map layer**: the layer read as the map while **Map** is set to Map layer. With none
  picked, the layer itself is read.
- **Map**: where the map comes from. **Gradient ramp** draws one from the Ramp rows.
- **Channel**: what of the map's picture is read.
- **Ramp shape**: **Radial** runs the ramp outward from its origin, and **Linear** along
  **Ramp angle**.
- **Ramp origin X (%)** and **Ramp origin Y (%)**: where the ramp starts, as a share of the
  layer.
- **Ramp scale**: how far the ramp runs from black to white.
- **Ramp angle**: the direction a Linear ramp runs.
- **Map interpretation**: **Standard** reads grey as none of the transform, white as all of
  it and black as all of it backwards. **Legacy** reads black as none and white as all.
- **Invert map**: reads the map inverted.
- **Map mix input**: how much of the layer's own picture is mixed into the Map layer's.
- **Map softness**: blurs the map before it is read.
- **Map gamma**: a gamma curve on the map.
- **Displacement offset (%)**: an amount added to what the map gives, at every pixel.
- **Displacement easing**: a curve on the map's value from black to white.
- **Translate X** and **Translate Y**: how far a pixel that takes all of the transform
  moves.
- **Rotate**: how far it turns round the anchor.
- **Uniform scale**: on, **Scale (%)** scales both ways. Off, **Scale X (%)** and **Scale Y
  (%)** scale each way.
- **Link anchor to origin**: puts the anchor on the ramp's origin.
- **Anchor X (%)** and **Anchor Y (%)**: the point the turn and the scale are made round,
  as a share of the layer.
- **Clamp negative scale**: leaves a pixel clear where its scale would fall to 0 or below.
- **Translate multiplier**, **Rotate multiplier** and **Scale multiplier**: multiply each
  part of the transform.
- **Iterations**: how many copies are averaged, from the full amount back toward none.
- **Shutter duration (%)**: how far back toward none the iterations reach.
- **Red**, **Green** and **Blue**: each colour channel's share of the transform.
- **Edge behaviour**: what is read past the picture's edges. **Transparent** reads nothing,
  **Clamp** holds the edge pixels, **Repeat** starts the picture again and **Mirror** runs
  it back.
- **Left**, **Top**, **Right** and **Bottom**: move each of those edges in.
- **Anti-aliasing**: **2x** and **4x** read each pixel at 4 or 16 places and average them.
- **View**: **Map** shows the map after softness, gamma and easing, in grey.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**, beside Matte: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
