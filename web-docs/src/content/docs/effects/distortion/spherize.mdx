## What it does

Warps the layer as if a magnifying glass was warping the contents. Inverted this
acts like a fisheye lens.

## What each control does

- **Radius**: adjust the area to warp.
- **Bulge**: which direction the image distorts in.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y**: the centre point of the warp area.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
