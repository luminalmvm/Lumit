## What it does

Offset the colour channels from one another separately. Or use the wavelength based
approach to achieve a smooth color split.

## What each control does

- **Amount**: The amount to offset each channel by, or overall wavelength split amount.
- **Angle**: What direction the split should occur.
- **Red**, **Green** and **Blue**: Adjust split scaling per channel.
- **Colour 1**, **Colour 2** and **Colour 3**: the tints on the channels.
- **Wavelength**: switches from the three colour channels to a smooth dispersion.
- **Samples**: how many taps fill the offset while Wavelength is on. 
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).

## Notes

For a fringe that grows radially out of the frame's centre, use
[Chromatic aberration](/effects/distortion/chromatic-aberration/), the
always-radial sibling of this effect.
