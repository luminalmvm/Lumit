Cylinder is a **Distortion** effect.

## What it does

Wraps the layer round an open tube: left to right round it, and top to bottom along it. The
tube is drawn without perspective and lit by one light. A tube seen straight down its length
draws nothing.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Control | Range | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rotation X** | Dial | Any angle | 0° | Degrees |
| **Rotation Y** | Dial | Any angle | 0° | Degrees |
| **Rotation Z** | Dial | Any angle | 0° | Degrees |
| **Rotation order** | Dropdown | XYZ · XZY · YXZ · YZX · ZXY · ZYX | XYZ | - |
| **Radius** | Slider | 0 to 4000; any value by typing | 200 | Pixels at composition size |
| **Height** | Slider | 0 to 8000; any value by typing | 600 | Pixels at composition size |
| **Offset X (%)**, **Offset Y (%)** | Slider | -50 to 150 | 50 | - |
| **Render** | Dropdown | Full · Outside · Inside | Full | - |
| **Light intensity** | Slider | 0 to 400; any value by typing | 100 | - |
| **Light colour** | Colour | Each channel 0 to 1 | White | - |
| **Light height** | Slider | -100 to 100; any value by typing | 35 | - |
| **Light direction** | Dial | Any angle | -60° | Degrees |
| **Ambient** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 25 | - |
| **Diffuse** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 75 | - |
| **Specular** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 30 | - |
| **Roughness** | Slider | 1 to 100 | 20 | - |

| **Transparency falloff** | Checkbox | On or off | Off | - |

## What each control does

- **Rotation X**, **Rotation Y** and **Rotation Z**: turn the tube about each axis. The
  tube's length runs along Y.
- **Rotation order**: the order the three turns are made in.
- **Radius**: the radius of the tube.
- **Height**: the length of the tube.
- **Offset X (%)** and **Offset Y (%)**: where the middle of the tube sits, as a share of
  the layer.
- **Render**: **Outside** draws the outside of the tube, **Inside** draws the inside, and
  **Full** draws both, the inside showing through the clear parts of the outside.
- **Light intensity**: how bright the light is, as a per cent.
- **Light colour**: the colour of the light.
- **Light height**: how far the light is lifted toward the viewer. At 0 it shines from the
  side, and below 0 from behind.
- **Light direction**: the side the light comes from. 0° is above.
- **Ambient**: light that reaches every part of the tube alike.
- **Diffuse**: how much the light brightens the parts that face it.
- **Specular**: how strong the highlight is.
- **Roughness**: how wide the highlight is.
- **Metal**: how much of the layer's own colour the highlight takes. At 0 it is the colour
  of the light.
- **Transparency falloff**: fades the outside of the tube toward its two edges.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
