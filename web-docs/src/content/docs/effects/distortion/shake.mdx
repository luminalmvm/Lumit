## What it does

Wobbles the layer as though the camera were handheld.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Matte** scales the shake's displacement per pixel, read where the pixel lands: white moves it the full Amplitude and Rotation amount, grey less, black leaves it still, so a soft matte turns the shove into a warp. **Invert** inverts the mattes for calculating strength.

## What each control does

- **Amplitude**: the overall scaler for the intensity of the shake.
- **Frequency**: how fast wobbles happen, in cycles per second.
- **Rotation amount**: how much the frame rotates as it moves.
- **Edges**: what fills the border. Mirror reflects the
  picture back. Repeat holds the edge pixel. Transparent leaves it clear.
- **Seed**: generates which random shake occurs.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).

The **Per-axis wobble** group biases the movement rather than adding to it:

- **X amount** / **Y amount**: multipliers on the amplitude for each axis.
- **X frequency** / **Y frequency**: multipliers on the frequency per axis.
- **Z amount** and **Z frequency**: depth shake.

The **Motion blur** group adds the shake's own smear:

- **Motion blur**: samples the wobble several times across the shutter and
  averages the results.
- **Shutter**: how far across the shutter window those samples spread.
