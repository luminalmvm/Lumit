## What it does

Wobbles the layer as though the camera were handheld.

## What each control does

- **Amplitude**: the overall scale for the intensity of the shake, rotation and depth
  included. At 0 the layer is left as it came in, with **Scale**, **Crop** and **Blend**
  fading out over the last 4.
- **Frequency**: how fast wobbles happen, in cycles per second.
- **Phase**: how far the whole shake runs behind, in seconds. Below 0 it runs ahead.
- **Style**: how the shake moves. Normal wobbles smoothly. Twitchy holds still between bursts
  of shaking. Jumpy jumps to a new place **Frequency** times a second.
- **Stillness** (Twitchy): about how much of the time the picture is held still.
- **Twitch frequency** (Twitchy): how often a burst can start or stop, per second.
- **Drift** (Jumpy): how far the picture moves towards its next place before it jumps. At 0
  it holds still between jumps.
- **Centre bias** (Jumpy): the chance a jump goes back to the centre. At 1 every jump does.
- **Rotation amount**: how much the frame rotates as it moves, at the default amplitude.
- **Rotation frequency**: a multiplier on **Frequency** for the rotation alone.
- **Scale**: a steady zoom on top of the shake. 100% is natural size.
- **Edges**: what fills the border. Mirror reflects the
  picture back. Repeat holds the edge pixel. Transparent leaves it clear.
- **Y edges**: the top and bottom border on its own. Same follows **Edges**.
- **Seed**: generates which random shake occurs.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.

The **Per-axis wobble** group biases the movement rather than adding to it:

- **X amount** / **Y amount**: multipliers on the amplitude for each axis.
- **X frequency** / **Y frequency**: multipliers on the frequency per axis.
- **Z amount** and **Z frequency**: depth shake.

The **Wave** group adds a steady swing on top of the random shake, per axis:

- **X wave**, **Y wave**, **Z wave**, **Rotation wave**: how far it swings. All four
  follow the amplitude.
- **X wave frequency** and the others: multipliers on the frequency, like **X frequency**.
- **X wave phase** and the others: where in the swing it starts, in degrees. X and Y at 90
  degrees apart move in a circle.

The **Channels** group shakes the red, green and blue channels apart:

- **Red amount**, **Green amount**, **Blue amount**: multipliers on the whole shake for each
  channel. Below 0 the channel goes the other way.
- **Red phase**, **Green phase**, **Blue phase**: how far each channel's shake runs behind,
  in seconds, like **Phase**.
- **Randomness**: a wobble of each channel's own on top, as big as the shake at 1.
- **Random rate**: a multiplier on the frequency for that wobble.

The alpha is the average of the three channels.

The **Crop** group cuts the layer before it shakes:

- **Crop left**, **Crop right**, **Crop top**, **Crop bottom**: how much is cut from each
  side, as a percentage of the width or height. **Edges** fills past the cut as it fills
  past the frame.

The **Motion blur** group adds the shake's own smear:

- **Motion blur**: samples the wobble several times across the shutter and
  averages the results.
- **Shutter**: how far across the shutter window those samples spread. Up to 4 by typing.
- **Samples**: how many of them there are. A big amplitude at few samples
  shows each one as a ghost; more samples make a smooth smear at more cost.
