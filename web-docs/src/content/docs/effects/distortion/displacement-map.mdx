## What it does

Warping/displacement of one layer scaled with the contents of another.

## What each control does

- **Matte** is the map, and is required for the effect to change anything.
- **Horizontal channel** and **Vertical channel** choose the channel used to
  scale each direction.
- **Horizontal amount** and **Vertical amount** adjust the scaling multiplier.
- **Edges** adjusts what resulting transparent pixels are filled with. **Repeat** 
  smears the border pixel and keeps the frame full. **Transparent** fills with 
  nothing. **Mirror** reflects the picture back.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Invert** reads the map the other way round.
