## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket, keeps some of its points and drops the rest. A
**Pattern** gives each point a number from 0 to 1, and the point is kept while that number
lies between **From** and **To**.

The kept points go out on its own Points socket and are drawn as discs. They keep their
identity, and a [Trail](/effects/generate/trail/) below still follows them.

A point can be marked picked, and it carries a number. The effects below can act on only
the picked points, through their **Apply to** row, or read the number to choose a clone
layer, a time offset or an order. Every point is picked until a Pick points says otherwise,
and its number is its place in the stream until an effect sets it. **Result** can mark the
kept points as picked and drop nothing.

With the **Image** pattern its points depend on a picture. The points effects below it in
the same layer's stack can read them. [Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/)
cannot, and neither can [Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) from another layer.

Two Pick points reading one producer, with **Every nth** at 2 and **Offset** at 0 and 1,
split its points between two [Clone to points](/effects/generate/clone-to-points/).

## What each control does

- **Result**: what becomes of the points the rows below keep. **Drop the rest** removes the
  others. **Pick** removes nothing and marks the kept points as picked. **Add to picked**,
  **Remove from picked** and **Keep in picked** remove nothing either. They add the kept
  points to what was picked already, take them from it, or keep only them in it.
- **Pattern**: where a point's number comes from. **Index** is its place in the stream, 0
  for the first point and 1 for the last. **Random** is a roll of its own, the same every
  frame. **Noise** is a smooth noise read at its position. **Distance** is how far it is
  from the centre, reaching 1 at **Radius**. **Age** is how far through its life it is. The
  points of [Particulate](/effects/generate/particulate/) and
  [Track points](/effects/generate/track-points/) age, and the rest do not. **Constant** is
  always 1. **Across** runs from 0 at Radius left of the centre to 1 at Radius right of it,
  and **Down** does the same from top to bottom. **Image** is how bright the picture under
  the point is. **Picked** is 1 for a picked point and 0 for the rest. **Number** is the
  number the point carries over Number range, held to 0 to 1. **Stripes** is 1 inside a
  band and 0 between. **Checker** is 1 and 0 in alternate squares. **Waves** is a wave that
  repeats over space and can travel over time.
- **From** and **To**: the range of the pattern's number that is kept. On Random, To is the
  share of the points kept. On Distance they keep a disc or a ring round the centre. On
  Index, To rising from nought brings the points on one at a time.
- **Seed**: which roll, or which noise. It shows for Random and Noise.
- **Noise scale**: how far apart two points must be to differ. It shows for Noise.
- **Noise speed**: how fast the noise changes over time, and how many waves pass a point
  each second. At nought they hold still. It shows for Noise and Waves.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y**: the place Distance, Across and Down are measured from, and
  where the stripes, squares and waves start.
- **Radius**: how far from the centre the number reaches 1. It shows for Distance, Across
  and Down.
- **Number range**: the number that reads as 1. A point's number is divided by it, and with
  word numbers 0 to 4 a range of 4 spreads the words from 0 to 1. It shows for Number.
- **Spacing**: how far it is from one stripe, square or wave to the next. It shows for
  Stripes, Checker and Waves.
- **Angle**: how far the stripes and squares are turned about the centre, and the way the
  waves travel. At 0 the stripes lie across and the waves travel to the right. Rings take
  no notice of it.
- **Width**: how much of each Spacing a stripe fills. It shows for Stripes.
- **Wave**: the shape of each wave. It shows for Waves.
- **Rings**: the waves run out from the centre as rings, not across as bands.
- **Image layer**: the layer whose picture the Image pattern reads. Unset reads this
  effect's own input picture. It shows for Image.
- **Every nth**: keeps one point in that many, counted by id. A particle stays in or out
  for its whole life.
- **Offset**: which point of each run is kept.
- **Invert**, under **Count**: swaps the kept points and the dropped ones.
- **Feather**: how soft the edge of a drawn disc is.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
