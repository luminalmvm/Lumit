## What it does

[Scatter](/effects/generate/scatter/) keeps the points that land on something *opaque*;
this one keeps the points that land on something *bright*. Point it at a layer and its
highlights become a cloud of points in the shape of themselves.

A point that lands between the threshold and white stands part of the time, so a gradient
comes out as a crowd that thins rather than a hard cut. Brightness is measured on the light
in the picture, not on alpha coverage, so a soft edge does not read as grey and thin out
twice.

The same points are handed out on a teal **Points** socket, and Mix at nought emits them
while drawing nothing.

One limit, the same one Scatter has: because these points depend on a picture, neither a
[Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) driver nor a
[Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) tap can read the stream. Wiring one of this
layer's own consumers to it works.

## What each control does

- **Source layer**: the layer whose brightness decides where the points go. Nothing is
  emitted until you set it.
- **Threshold**: where "bright" starts. Below it nothing is kept; at white everything is.
- **Density**: how many points are thrown per hundred-pixel square of the composition, so
  the same number means the same spacing whatever size the composition is.
- **Seed**: which points. Reseeding moves the crowd and changes which of it survives.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is. At nought it is a hard circle.
- **Colour**: what the points are drawn in. Values above 1 are legal, and useful under a
  [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/).
- **Max points**: the most points that may be thrown. A ceiling on the work rather than on
  the look, since only some of them stand. It does not animate.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  emitted on the socket.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
