## What it does

Stroke draws a round stroke along a mask's path.

## What each control does

- **Mask**: which of the layer's masks to draw.
- **Colour**: the brush's colour. 
- **Brush size**: how wide the brush stroke is.
- **Brush hardness**: how crisp its edge is.
- **Spacing**: how far apart brush stamps are.
- **Start** and **End**: beginning and end points for the brush stroke.
- **Paint style**: what the stroke lands on. **On original** paints over the layer; 
  **On transparent** keeps the stroke and makes the rest of the layer transparent; 
  **Reveal original** keeps the picture *only* where the brush appears and hides the rest.
- **Opacity**: how strong the brush is.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
