## What it does

Sparks, dust, snow, streaks, embers. Many small things are born from an emitter, carried
about by gravity, wind and turbulence, and fade as they age. Particulate draws them over
whatever is underneath it, and hands the same particles out on a teal **Points** socket for
the rest of the family to build on.

Nothing is remembered from frame to frame. Where a particle is at a given moment is worked
out from its birth and its age rather than from the frame before it, so scrubbing is
instant, an export can render frames in any order, and two renders of the same frame agree.
The practical consequence is the one worth knowing: the picture depends on the **Emit rate**
you keyframed, not on which frames you happened to play, so a rate you animate rewrites the
whole history of births rather than only the frames after the change.

Particles live in three axes. **Position z**, **Depth**, **Direction z**, **Spread z** and
**Wind z** all default to flat, so a 2D layer draws exactly what it always drew. Turn the
layer's 3D switch on and give the composition a camera and the depth becomes visible:
particles pass in front of and behind other layers, and foreshorten as they recede. See
[Cameras and 3D](/use/camera/).

**Where particles are born.** Point emits them all from one place. Line spreads them along a
segment. Ellipse and Rectangle fill their interior evenly, and **Ellipse outline** and
**Rectangle outline** put them only on the edge, which is how you get a ring or a frame of
sparks rather than a filled disc. Mask path emits along one of the layer's own masks, evenly
by length, so an emitter can follow any shape you can draw — see [Masks](/use/masks/). With
**Depth** above nought a Point becomes a line receding from the camera, an Ellipse a
cylinder and a Rectangle a box; Line and Mask path stay flat.

**How a particle is drawn.** **Disc** is a feathered dot, and is the cheapest. **Sprite**
stamps a layer of your choosing at each particle, turned and scaled per particle — a
snowflake, a leaf, a piece of debris. **Streak** draws a short capsule from where the
particle was a moment ago to where it is now, which is what makes a spark read as fast.
Each mode has one control of its own, and the other two are greyed while it is not chosen.
A Sprite mode with no layer set draws discs rather than nothing, because a render mode has
to draw something.

**The points wire.** Everything drawn here is also available as data. Run a wire from the
Points socket into [Clone to points](/effects/generate/clone-to-points/) to stamp a layer at
each particle, [Connect points](/effects/generate/connect-points/) to web them together,
[Trail](/effects/generate/trail/) to draw where they have been, or
[Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) to turn them into numbers that drive
something else. A [Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) node carries the same
stream to another layer.

## What each control does

The **Emitter** group — where particles come from and how they leave:

- **Shape**: which emitter. Point, Line, Ellipse, Rectangle, Mask path, Ellipse outline or
  Rectangle outline, as above.
- **Position x**, **Position y** and **Position z**: where the emitter sits. Position z is
  how far in front of or behind the layer's own plane it floats; nought is on the plane.
- **Width** and **Height**: how big the emitter is. Line reads only Width; Point and Mask
  path read neither.
- **Depth**: how far the emitter reaches through the plane, which turns a flat emitter into
  a solid one.
- **Emitter angle**: turns Line, Ellipse and Rectangle about Position.
- **Mask path**: which of the layer's masks particles are born along, in Mask path mode. An
  empty path emits nothing.
- **Emit rate**: births per second. Keyframe it for a burst; at nought nothing new is born
  and what is already alive carries on.
- **Direction**: which way particles leave, in the layer's plane. Minus ninety is up.
- **Direction z**: how far they lean out of that plane. Positive throws them away from the
  camera, negative towards it.
- **Spread**: the cone about Direction. At 360 they leave every way at once.
- **Spread z**: the lean's own cone. At nought every particle leaves in the plane, so a full
  Spread stays a disc of directions rather than quietly becoming a sphere.
- **Initial speed**: how fast a particle leaves.
- **Speed jitter**: how much particles differ from each other in that speed.

The **Particle** group — what one is and how it changes as it ages:

- **Life** and **Life jitter**: how long a particle lasts, and how much that varies.
- **Size** and **Size jitter**: how big it is at birth, and how much that varies.
- **Size over life**: multiplies Size against the particle's age, from birth on the left to
  death on the right. Flat by default, so a particle stays the size it was born.
- **Opacity over life**: the same curve for opacity. It starts solid and ends invisible,
  which is most of why the default look reads as motes rather than dots.
- **Colour** and **End colour**: the colour at birth and at death, blended across the
  particle's life. Values above 1 are legal and useful under a
  [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/).
- **Rotation**: how far round a particle is drawn. It shows on Sprite and Streak, and not on
  a disc.
- **Rotation jitter**: how much particles differ from one another in that rotation. A whole
  turn by default, because a hundred identical stamps all facing the same way reads as a
  mistake. Set it to nought and Rotation means exactly what it says.
- **Spin**: how fast that rotation turns.
- **Align to motion**: turns each particle to face the way it is travelling, with Spin added
  on top.

The **Forces** group — what pushes them about:

- **Gravity**: a constant pull, positive downwards.
- **Wind x**, **Wind y** and **Wind z**: the air's own speed. Wind acts through Drag, so with
  Drag at nought it does nothing at all.
- **Drag**: how quickly a particle's speed comes round to the wind's. This is the control
  that makes Wind mean anything.
- **Turbulence amount**: how far a rolling noise pushes a particle off its path.
- **Turbulence scale**: how far apart two particles have to be before the noise pushes them
  different ways. Small is a boil, large is a drift.
- **Turbulence speed**: how fast that noise evolves as a particle ages.

The **Render** group — how they are drawn:

- **Mode**: Disc, Sprite or Streak.
- **Feather**: how soft a disc's edge is. Disc mode only.
- **Sprite layer**: the layer stamped at each particle. Sprite mode only, and discs are
  drawn until you set it.
- **Streak length**: how long the tail is, in seconds. Streak mode only.
- **Max particles**: the most particles that may be alive at once. It is a capacity
  declaration rather than a look control, so it does not animate; over the budget the newest
  survive.
- **Seed**: which particles. Reseed to roll a different crowd.

The remaining properties:

- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Blend**: how the particles composite with the layer they are drawn over.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
