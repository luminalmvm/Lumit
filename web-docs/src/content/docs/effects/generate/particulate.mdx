## What each control does

The **Emitter** group, where particles come from and travel:

- **Shape**: which emitter.
- **Position x**, **Position y** and **Position z**: where the emitter sits.
- **Width** and **Height**: how big the emitter is.
- **Depth**: how far the emitter reaches through the plane.
- **Emitter angle**: turns Line, Ellipse and Rectangle about Position.
- **Follow**: what a Mask path emitter walks. **Mask** is one of the layer's masks. **Shape**
  is the outline the Shape layer the effect is on draws, after any trim, offset or combine,
  and dashes as drawn, one line after another, repeater copies included. On any other layer
  Shape emits nothing.
- **Mask path**: which of the layer's masks particles are emitted from.
- **Density layer**: a layer whose brightness weights where particles are born inside the
  emitter. A birth with nowhere bright enough to go is skipped. While it is set, the
  points effects below Particulate on the same layer can read its particles, and
  [Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) and
  [Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) cannot.
- **Map placement**: where the Density layer's picture sits on this layer. **As placed in
  comp** reads it where that layer sits in the composition. **Stretch to fit** stretches
  it over this layer. **Centre** centres it at its own size.
  An effect saved before this row existed stays on Stretch to fit.
- **Emit rate**: new particles per second.
- **Direction**: which way particles travel.
- **Direction z**: how far they lean out of that plane.
- **Spread**: the cone about Direction.
- **Spread z**: the lean's own cone.
- **Initial speed**: how fast a particle leaves.
- **Speed jitter**: how much particles differ from each other in that speed.

The **Bursts** group, particles thrown out all at once beside the steady flow:

- **Burst**: **Off**, **Once at start** on the layer's first frame, **Every N frames**, or
  **On trigger**, once each time **Trigger** rises past 0.5.
- **Burst count**: how many particles one burst throws out.
- **Burst interval**: how many frames apart the bursts are.
- **Trigger**: the number On trigger watches. Wire a beat driver to it, or keyframe it.

The **Particle** group, how a particle looks:

- **Life** and **Life jitter**: how long a particle lasts, and how much that varies.
- **Size** and **Size jitter**: how big it is at birth, and how much that varies.
- **Size over life**: multiplies Size against the particle's age, from birth on the left to
  death on the right. Flat by default, so a particle stays the size it was born.
- **Opacity over life**: the same curve for opacity. It starts solid and ends invisible.
- **Colour** and **End colour**: the colour at birth and at death, blended across the
  particle's life.
- **Rotation**: the rotation of particles.
- **Rotation jitter**: how much particles differ from one another in that rotation.
- **Spin**: how fast that rotation turns.
- **Align to motion**: turns each particle to face the way it is travelling, with Spin added
  on top.

The **Variation** group, what runs those changes:

- **Size by**, **Opacity by** and **Colour by**: what runs Size over life, Opacity over life
  and the blend to End colour. **Age** runs from birth to death. **Speed** runs from Speed
  min to Speed max. **Random** is a number of the particle's own that stays the same all
  its life.
- **Speed min** and **Speed max**: the speeds, in pixels per second, at the left and right
  ends of the curve or blend.

The **Forces** group, what moves them:

- **Gravity**: a constant pull.
- **Wind x**, **Wind y** and **Wind z**: the air's speed.
- **Drag**: how quickly a particle's speed comes round to the wind's.
- **Turbulence amount**: how far a rolling noise pushes a particle off its path.
- **Turbulence scale**: how far apart two particles have to be before the noise pushes them
  different ways.
- **Turbulence speed**: how fast that noise evolves as a particle ages.

The **Edges** group, what happens at the edge of the layer's frame:

- **Edge**: **None** lets particles carry on out of sight. **Kill** ends a particle the first
  time it leaves the frame, and it does not come back. **Wrap** brings it back in on the
  opposite side.
- **Edge margin**: how far past the frame a particle goes before Edge acts.

The **Collide** group, what particles hit:

- **Collide with**: a layer whose shapes and masks particles hit. A layer with neither is
  hit at its rectangle. While it is set, each frame's particles are worked out from the
  frame before. A particle keeps the place it was born when the emitter moves, and a
  change of force acts from the frame it happens on. Density layer and Particle blend are
  not read, and at most 100,000 particles are alive at once.
- **On contact**: **Bounce** sends a particle off the other way. **Slide** carries it on
  along the surface. **Stick** holds it where it hit, moving with that layer. **Kill**
  ends it.
- **Bounciness**: how much of its speed a particle keeps as it bounces.
- **Friction**: how much a particle slows along the surface.

Particles hit shapes across and down. Depth does not change what they hit.

The **Render** group, how they are drawn:

- **Mode**: Disc, Sprite or Streak.
- **Feather**: how soft a disc's edge is. Disc mode only.
- **Sprite layer**: the layer stamped at each particle. Sprite mode only, and discs are
  drawn until you set it.
- **Streak length**: how long the tail is, in seconds.
- **Particle blend**: how particles combine with each other where they overlap, by the layer
  blend modes. They then go over the picture together, as Normal particles do.
- **Max particles**: the most particles that may be alive at once.

The remaining properties:

- **Seed**: changes the randomness.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Blend**: how the particles composite with the layer they are drawn over.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
