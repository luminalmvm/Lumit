## What it does

Wire a producer's **Points** socket into this effect and every pair of points closer than **Max distance** creates a line joining them.

Nearness is measured on the frame rather than in 3D space.

Each line takes the average of the colours of its two points. **Colour** multiplies this.

## What each control does

- **Max distance**: how far apart two points may be and still be joined.
- **Max connections**: the most lines that may meet at any one point.
- **Taper**: how much a line thins as it lengthens.
- **Fade**: how much a line fades as it lengthens.
- **Width**: the thickness of a line.
- **Feather**: how soft a line's edge is.
- **Colour**: multiplies the colour each line inherits from its two ends.
- **Max points**: the most points that this effect operates on, newest points first.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
