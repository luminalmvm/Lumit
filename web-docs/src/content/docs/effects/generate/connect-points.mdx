## What it does

Wire a producer's **Points** socket into this effect and it joins the points with lines.
**Mode** chooses which points are joined.

Distance is measured on the frame, or in 3D space with **Depth** on.

Each line takes the average of the colours of its two points. **Colour** multiplies this.

## What each control does

- **Mode**: which points are joined. **Nearby** joins every pair closer than Max distance,
  up to Max connections at each point. **In order** joins each point to the next, in the
  order of the number each carries. **Triangles** draws a mesh of triangles over the points.
  **Outline** draws only the lines round the outside. **Nearest** joins each point to its
  closest few, however far off they are, and runs Taper and Fade from its shortest line to
  its longest.
- **Order by**: the name of the number In order sorts the points by. Left empty, it is the
  number each point carries. See [Names](/effects/generate/vary-points/#names).
- **Between**: which points a line may run between. **Picked** joins picked points only.
  **Picked and the rest** joins a picked point to one that is not. See
  [Pick points](/effects/generate/pick-points/).
- **From group**: the group Picked reads, and the first of the two that Picked and the rest
  joins, as a name. Left empty, it is the picked points. A point is in a named group while
  the name reads above 0.5.
- **To group**: the second of the two groups that Picked and the rest joins. Left empty, it
  is the points that are not picked. The same name in both joins that group to itself.
- **Max distance**: how far apart two points may be and still be joined. A longer line is
  dropped in every mode but Nearest.
- **Max connections**: the most lines that may meet at any one point, in Nearby. In Nearest
  it is how many neighbours each point reaches for.
- **Closed**: joins the last point of In order back to the first.
- **Depth**: measures Max distance between the points themselves in all three axes, rather
  than between where the camera puts them on the frame. The two differ only on a 3D layer
  with a camera.
- **Taper**: how much a line thins as it lengthens.
- **Fade**: how much a line fades as it lengthens.
- **Width**: the thickness of a line.
- **Feather**: how soft a line's edge is.
- **Colour**: multiplies the colour each line inherits from its two ends.
- **Max points**: the most points that this effect operates on, newest points first.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
