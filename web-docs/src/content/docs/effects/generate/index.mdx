## What this family does

Every other family changes the picture that reaches it. These make one.

Some of them ignore the incoming picture entirely — [Gradient](/effects/generate/gradient/)
and [Fractal noise](/effects/generate/fractal-noise/) paint over the whole frame, and
[Fill](/effects/generate/fill/) keeps only the layer's shape and throws away its
colour — so what you put them on is usually a solid, a shape or an adjustment
layer whose job is to be a surface for them.

The rest add something *to* what arrived rather than replacing it.
[Noise](/effects/generate/noise/) and [Add grain](/effects/generate/add-grain/)
lay grain over the picture, plainly and as film does it. [Beam](/effects/generate/beam/),
[Lightning](/effects/generate/lightning/) and [Radio waves](/effects/generate/radio-waves/)
draw over it — a shaft of light, a forked bolt, a set of expanding rings — and
each can be told to keep the layer underneath or to be the only thing there is.
[Vegas](/effects/generate/vegas/) is the odd one: it *reads* the picture to find
its contours, and then draws along them.

Generated pictures earn their keep twice. Once as themselves: a background ramp,
a wash of cloud, film grain over a flat sky, a bolt of lightning across a title.
And once as *input to something else* — point another effect's **Matte** row at a
layer carrying a Fractal noise and that effect now varies across the frame in a
way no slider could describe. That second use is the reason this family lands
before the distortion effects that will read it.
