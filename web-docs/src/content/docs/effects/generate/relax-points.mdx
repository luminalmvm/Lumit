## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket and gives each point a round space of its own.
Where two spaces overlap, both points are pushed apart across the layer's plane, and that
is done **Iterations** times.

Nothing is remembered between frames. Every frame starts from the places the wire brings.

The moved points go out on its own Points socket and are drawn as discs.

## What each control does

- **Radius**: the space each point wants round itself. Two points end up at least twice
  this apart.
- **Use point size**: each point's space is its own size instead.
- **Spacing**: how much of its own size a point wants. Over 100 leaves gaps between points
  and under it lets them overlap.
- **Iterations**: how many times the points are pushed apart. More settles further.
- **Strength**: how much of an overlap one pass removes. At 100 the points can jitter from
  frame to frame.
- **Bounds**: **Free** lets the points drift anywhere. **Keep inside** holds them in the
  box they started in.
- **Apply to**: which points move. The rest stay where they are and still push the ones
  that move. See [Pick points](/effects/generate/pick-points/).
- **Max points**: the most points it works on. A longer stream is trimmed to its newest.
- **Feather**: how soft the edge of a drawn disc is.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
