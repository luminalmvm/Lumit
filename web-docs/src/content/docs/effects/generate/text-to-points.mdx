## What it does

Makes one point for each character, word or line of a Text layer and draws them as discs.
On the Text layer itself each point sits on its piece of the text, and moves when an
animator pushes a letter.

A point's number is its count along the text, or the count **Number** asks for. On a path
it turns to face the way the path runs.

Every point carries four names as well. `character`, `word` and `line` are the counts of
the character, word and line its piece starts with, and `width` is the width of its piece
in pixels at composition size. See [Names](/effects/generate/vary-points/#names).

The points effects below it in the same layer's stack can read its points.
[Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) cannot, and neither can
[Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) from another layer. A
[Bake points](/effects/generate/bake-points/) below it makes its points readable by both.

On the Text layer itself its points are cut at the text's box unless it is at the end of the
stack. See [Points along path](/effects/generate/points-along-path/).

## What each control does

- **Text layer**: the Text layer the points are made from. Unset is the layer this effect
  is on. A layer that is not text makes no points.
- **Per**: whether each character, each word or each line gets a point.
- **Include spaces**: a space gets a point too. A line break never gets one.
- **Place**: where on its piece a point sits. **Origin** is where the piece starts on the
  baseline. **Centre** is the middle of it.
- **Number**: which count a point's number carries. **Same as Per** counts the points
  themselves. **Character**, **Word** and **Line** count the character, word or line a
  point's piece starts with. With Per on Character and Number on Word, every letter of a
  word carries one number.
- **Size from width**: a point's size is the width of its piece in place of Size. For a
  character that is how far it moves the pen on.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is.
- **Colour**: what the discs are drawn in.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  emitted on the socket.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
