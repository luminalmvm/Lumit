## What it does

Energy line draws a white-hot core with a coloured glow along a path: a line between two
points, the layer's masks, a Shape layer's paths, or the letters of a Text layer. The lit
stretch can be trimmed and slid along the path, and noise can push the core and the glow
about. With motion blur on for the layer, a moving line and a moving stretch blur across
the shutter.

:::note
Text draws its letters as the layer sets them. Text animators don't move the light.
:::

## What each control does

- **Preset**: a look to start from. Picking one sets the core, glow, flicker and
  distortion controls. Editing any of them sets **Preset** back to **Custom**.
- **Draw along**: **Line** runs between **From** and **To**. **Mask** follows the
  layer's masks. **Shape** follows a Shape layer's paths. **Text** follows the outlines of
  a Text layer's letters.
- **From x**, **From y**, **To x** and **To y**: the two ends of the line.
- **Mask**: which mask to follow when **Every mask** is off.
- **Every mask**: on, every mask on the layer is drawn.
- **Paths**: with several masks, a shape's items or the letters, **One after another**
  lights them in turn along **Start** and **End**. **All at once** lights each on its own,
  together.
- **Start** and **End**: where the light starts and stops, as a share of the whole path.
  Keyframe **End** from **Start** to draw the line on. A stretch shorter than the core is
  wide is dimmer.
- **Offset**: slides the lit stretch along the path. On a closed path it comes round
  again.
- **Core size**: the core's width.
- **Core softness**: how much of the core's width its edge fades over.
- **Core colour**: the colour in the middle of the core. The edge of the core takes the
  glow colour.
- **Halo**: a tight glow hugging the core, in the glow colour.
- **Start size** and **End size**: the core's width at each end of the lit stretch, as a
  share of **Core size**.
- **Glow colour**: the glow's colour.
- **Glow intensity**: how bright the glow is.
- **Glow size**: how far the glow reaches.
- **Glow falloff**: how much of the glow is sent far out rather than kept near the core.
- **Flicker**: how far the light dips.
- **Flicker speed**: how many times a second it dips.
- **Type**: the distortion noise. **Smooth** swells, **Smoke** curls, **Crackle** is fast
  and jagged.
- **Core distortion** and **Glow distortion**: how far the noise pushes the core and the
  glow, in pixels.
- **Noise size**: the size of the noise's swirls.
- **Detail**: how many layers of finer noise ride on the first.
- **Evolution speed**: how fast the noise changes.
- **Wind direction** and **Wind speed**: which way the noise is carried, and how many
  pixels a second. 0° is up.
- **Seed**: a different arrangement of the noise and the flicker.
- **Composite on original**: on, the layer stays under the light. Off, the light is all
  there is.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
