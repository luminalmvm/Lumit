## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket and changes each point's size, opacity, rotation,
colour and position. A **Pattern** gives each point a number from 0 to 1, and that number
sets how much of each change the point takes.

The changed points go out on its own Points socket and are drawn as discs. The producer
above still draws its own unchanged points while its **Mix** is above nought.

Two Vary points in a row have a seed each. On Random, one on **Offset x** and one on
**Offset y** scatter the points both ways.

**Apply to** changes only the picked points, and **Set number** writes the number each
point carries. See [Pick points](/effects/generate/pick-points/) for both.

With the **Image** pattern or **Colour from image** on, its points depend on a picture. The
points effects below it in the same layer's stack can read them.
[Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) cannot, and neither can
[Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) from another layer.

## What each control does

- **Pattern**: where a point's number comes from. **Index** is its place in the stream, 0
  for the first point and 1 for the last. **Random** is a roll of its own, the same every
  frame. **Noise** is a smooth noise read at its position. **Distance** is how far it is
  from the centre, reaching 1 at **Radius**. **Age** is how far through its life it is. The
  points of [Particulate](/effects/generate/particulate/) and
  [Track points](/effects/generate/track-points/) age, and the rest do not. **Constant** is
  always 1. **Across** runs from 0 at Radius left of the centre to 1 at Radius right of it,
  and **Down** does the same from top to bottom. **Image** is how bright the picture under
  the point is. **Picked** is 1 for a picked point and 0 for the rest. **Number** is the
  number the point carries over Number range, held to 0 to 1. **Stripes** is 1 inside a
  band and 0 between. **Checker** is 1 and 0 in alternate squares. **Waves** is a wave that
  repeats over space and can travel over time.
- **Curve**: reshapes the pattern's number before the changes read it.
- **Seed**: which roll, or which noise. It shows for Random and Noise.
- **Noise scale**: how far apart two points must be to differ. It shows for Noise.
- **Noise speed**: how fast the noise changes over time, and how many waves pass a point
  each second. At nought they hold still. It shows for Noise and Waves.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y**: the place Distance, Across and Down are measured from, and
  where the stripes, squares and waves start.
- **Radius**: how far from the centre the number reaches 1. It shows for Distance, Across
  and Down.
- **Number range**: the number that reads as 1. A point's number is divided by it, and with
  word numbers 0 to 4 a range of 4 spreads the words from 0 to 1. It shows for Number.
- **Spacing**: how far it is from one stripe, square or wave to the next. It shows for
  Stripes, Checker and Waves.
- **Angle**: how far the stripes and squares are turned about the centre, and the way the
  waves travel. At 0 the stripes lie across and the waves travel to the right. Rings take
  no notice of it.
- **Width**: how much of each Spacing a stripe fills. It shows for Stripes.
- **Wave**: the shape of each wave. It shows for Waves.
- **Rings**: the waves run out from the centre as rings, not across as bands.
- **Image layer**: the layer whose picture the Image pattern and Colour from image read.
  Unset reads this effect's own input picture.
- **Colour from image**: the point takes the colour of the picture under it. Its own alpha
  still multiplies it.
- **Apply to**: which points are changed. The rest pass through as they came.
- **Size**: the size a point takes where the pattern reads 1, as a per cent of its own.
- **Opacity**: the opacity a point takes where the pattern reads 1, as a per cent of its
  own.
- **Stretch x** and **Stretch y**: how far a point is stretched across and down where the
  pattern reads 1, in its own turned frame.
- **Start colour**: multiplies the point's colour where the pattern reads 0.
- **Use middle colour**: runs the colours from Start colour through Middle colour to
  Colour.
- **Middle colour**: multiplies the point's colour where the pattern reads a half.
- **Colour**: multiplies the point's colour where the pattern reads 1.
- **Both ways**: Rotation and the Offsets read the pattern as -1 to 1 in place of 0 to 1. A
  Random or Noise pattern rolls again for each Offset.
- **Rotation**: the turn added where the pattern reads 1.
- **Offset x**, **Offset y** and **Offset z**: how far the point is moved where the pattern
  reads 1.
- **Offset forward** and **Offset sideways**: how far the point is moved along the way it
  faces, and at right angles to it, where the pattern reads 1. The way it faces is its
  rotation, with Rotation above added.
- **Set number**: writes the number each point carries.
- **Number from** and **Number to**: the number a point gets where the pattern reads 0, and
  where it reads 1.
- **Feather**: how soft the edge of a drawn disc is.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
