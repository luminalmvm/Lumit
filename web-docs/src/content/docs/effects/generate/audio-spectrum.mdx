## What it does

Measures the sound around the frame into a row of bands, spaced evenly in pitch, and draws one
bar for each along a line, a circle or a mask. **Line** draws one line through the bars' tips
and **Waveform** draws the wave itself. A sound that has been deleted reads as silence.

The **Points** socket hands on one point at each band's tip, for
[Particulate](/effects/generate/particulate/) to emit from.

## What each control does

- **Source**: which sound is measured: **This comp** is the composition's mix, **Layer** one
  layer of it, **Clip** one clip of that layer.
- **Audio**: the layer **Layer** and **Clip** measure.
- **Clip**: the clip **Clip** measures.
- **Shape**: **Bars**, **Line**, **Ring** or **Waveform**. **Ring** is bars round a circle half
  as wide as **From** is from **To**, centred between them.
- **Bands**: how many bars, or points along a line.
- **Start frequency** and **End frequency**: the lowest and highest pitch drawn, in hertz.
- **Draw along**: **Line** lays the bands between **From** and **To**, **Mask** along a mask.
- **From x**, **From y**, **To x** and **To y**: where the line runs.
- **Path**: which mask to follow. **First mask** is the layer's first.
- **Height**: how far a band at full level reaches.
- **Thickness**: how wide a bar or the line is.
- **Softness**: how much of the width its edge fades over.
- **Inside colour**: the colour where a bar starts.
- **Outside colour**: the colour a band at full level reaches.
- **Hue spread**: how far the colours turn round the wheel from the first band to the last.
- **Mirror**: each band reaches both ways from the line.
- **Smoothing**: how many frames before this one are averaged in. On **Waveform**, how much
  sound one sweep shows.
- **Composite on original**: on, the layer this effect is applied to is drawn underneath.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
