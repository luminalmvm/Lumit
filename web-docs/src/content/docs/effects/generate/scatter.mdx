## What it does

[Grid](/effects/generate/grid/) puts points where the arithmetic says. Scatter throws them
at random and keeps the ones that land on something — "something" being the layer's own
alpha, so a silhouette, a piece of text or a keyed subject becomes a cloud of points in the
shape of itself.

A point stands or falls on the alpha under it rather than on a hard test: where the layer is
solid every point stands, where it is half transparent half of them do, and where there is
nothing none of them do. That is why a soft edge comes out as a thinning crowd rather than
as a cut line.

Like Grid, it hands the same points out on a teal **Points** socket for
[Clone to points](/effects/generate/clone-to-points/),
[Connect points](/effects/generate/connect-points/) and
[Trail](/effects/generate/trail/) to build on, and Mix at nought emits the stream while
drawing nothing.

Two limits worth knowing. Where a point lands is fixed by the seed, so it is the same place
at every frame and at every preview resolution — but whether a point on a *soft edge* is
kept is decided from the picture at the resolution being drawn, so a half-resolution
preview can keep a slightly different fringe from the export. And a
[Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) driver cannot read this stream at all: it
depends on a picture, and a driver is worked out before there is one.

## What each control does

- **Density**: how many points are thrown per hundred-pixel square of the composition. It is
  a count per area, so the same number means the same spacing whatever size the composition
  is.
- **Seed**: which points. Reseeding moves the crowd and changes which of it survives.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is. At nought it is a hard circle.
- **Colour**: what the points are drawn in. Values above 1 are legal, and useful under a
  [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/).
- **Max points**: the most points that may be thrown. A ceiling on the work rather than on
  the look, since only some of them stand. It does not animate.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  emitted on the socket.
- **Matte**: unusually, this is not a strength. It chooses whose alpha the points land
  inside — set it and the points take that layer's shape instead of this one's. **Invert**
  reads that matte the other way round, which fills everything the shape does not cover.
  There is no Channel row here: Scatter reads alpha and only alpha, so turn a brightness
  into an alpha with [Set matte](/effects/utility/set-matte/) or
  [Matte key](/effects/utility/matte-key/) first.
