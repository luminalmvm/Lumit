## What it does

Throws points at random and keeps the ones that land inside the layer.

## What each control does

- **Density**: how many points are thrown per hundred-pixel square of the composition.
- **Seed**: randomises where points are thrown.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is.
- **Colour**: what the points are drawn in.
- **Max points**: the most points.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture.
- **Matte**: this is not a strength. It chooses the alpha the points land
  inside. Points take that layer's shape instead of this one's. **Invert**
  reads that matte the other way round, which fills everything the shape does not cover.
