Woodgrain is a **Textures** effect.

## What it does

Draws a plank cut along a tree trunk. The growth rings show as long stripes, which open into
arches where the trunk wobbles, and fine grain runs along the plank.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Control | Range | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| **Dark wood** | Colour | Each channel 0 to 1 | 0.16, 0.065, 0.02 | - |
| **Ring spacing** | Slider | 2 to 400; any value by typing | 24 | Pixels at composition size |

| **Ring intensity** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 80 | - |
| **Ring unevenness** | Slider | 0 to 100 | 30 | - |

## What each control does

- **Light wood** and **Dark wood**: the two colours. Each ring runs from the light one to
  the dark one.
- **Ring spacing**: how far apart the rings are.
- **Ring sharpness**: how late and how suddenly each ring turns dark.
- **Ring intensity**: how dark the rings get.
- **Ring unevenness**: how much the rings differ in width.
- **Ring noise**: how far noise roughens the rings.
- **Ring noise size**: how big that noise is.
- **Graininess**: how strong the grain is.
- **Grain size**: how wide the streaks of grain are. Each is forty times as long.
- **Plank depth**: how far from the middle of the trunk the plank is cut.
- **Trunk wobble**: how far the trunk strays from a straight line.
- **Trunk wobble length**: the distance along the plank over which it strays.
- **Angular wobble**: how far the rings are from round.
- **Rotation**: turns the plank.
- **Trunk centre**: a point on the line the trunk runs along, and the point the plank turns
  about.
- **Seed**: which wobble and noise the plank gets.
- **Paint**: **Inside layer** paints only where the layer draws, and **Whole frame** fills
  the whole of the layer's picture.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
