## What it does

Wire a producer's teal **Points** socket into this effect, pick a layer, and that layer's
picture is stamped once per point — at the point's place, turned by the point's own
rotation, sized by the point's own size and tinted by its colour. A hundred snowflakes from
[Particulate](/effects/generate/particulate/), a lattice of thumbnails from
[Grid](/effects/generate/grid/), a logo scattered inside a silhouette from
[Scatter](/effects/generate/scatter/).

The points can come from an effect in this layer's own stack, or from another layer entirely
through a [Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) tap.

Stamps are laid down oldest first, so a newer point covers an older one, and that order is
the same on every machine and from either scrub direction.

With nothing wired in, or with no Clone layer set, the picture passes straight through. The
box in the graph carries the no-stream mark, so you can see which of the two it is.

## What each control does

- **Clone layer**: the layer whose picture is stamped. Nothing is drawn until you set it.
- **Scale**: multiplies each point's own size. At 100 a stamp is as wide as the point would
  have been as a disc, so most rigs want this well above 100.
- **Rotation**: added to each point's own rotation, so a producer's spin still reads.
- **Tint**: how much of the point's colour the stamp takes on. A point's colour carries its
  fading as well as its hue, so at nought a stamp is opaque wherever the layer is, and at
  100 it follows the point all the way out.
- **Max clones**: the most points that may be stamped. A stream longer than this is trimmed
  to its newest. A capacity declaration, so it does not animate.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
