## What it does

Wire a producer's **Points** socket into this effect, pick a layer, and that layer's
picture is stamped once per point at the point's position, turned by its rotation, sized by
its size and tinted by its colour.

## What each control does

- **Clone layer**: the layer whose picture is stamped.
- **Scale**: multiplies each point's own size.
- **Rotation**: added to each point's own rotation.
- **Tint**: how much of the point's colour the stamp takes on.
- **Max clones**: the most points that may be stamped.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
