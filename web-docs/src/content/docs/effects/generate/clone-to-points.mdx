## What it does

Wire a producer's **Points** socket into this effect, pick a layer, and that layer's
picture is stamped once per point at the point's position, turned by its rotation, sized by
its size and tinted by its colour.

Up to four layers can be set, and **Choose by** says which of them a point takes. **Time
offset** shows each stamp its layer at another moment. A
[Clone index](/effects/drivers/clone-index/) driver anywhere in what a clone layer shows
makes that layer render once per stamp, each copy reading its own number.

A clone layer that is outside its in and out points at a stamp's moment draws nothing for
the points that take it.

One Clone to points asks for at most 32 pictures of its clone layers: layers times moments,
or one a clone. With several layers set, **Time samples** gives way, and four layers get
eight moments each.

## What each control does

- **Clone layer**: the layer whose picture is stamped.
- **Clone layer 2**, **Clone layer 3** and **Clone layer 4**: more layers to stamp. With
  more than one set, each point takes one of them.
- **Choose by**: how a point takes its layer when more than one is set. **In order** takes
  each in turn down the stream. **Random** rolls for it, the same every frame. **Number**
  reads the number the point carries. **Every layer** stamps them all at every point, the
  first row at the bottom. See [Pick points](/effects/generate/pick-points/) for the number.
- **Seed**: which roll a Random choice of layer and a Random time offset give.
- **Time offset**: shows each stamp its layer at another moment. **In order** steps back
  once a stamp. **Random** shuffles the same steps by the Seed. **Number** steps back by
  the number the point carries. **Point age** plays the layer from its start as the point
  ages.
- **Time step**: how far apart two steps are.
- **Time samples**: the most moments a layer is rendered at. Steps past the last wrap round
  to the first, and a point older than the last holds it. Each moment is another render of
  the layer.
- **Per clone**: **Auto** renders a layer once per stamp when a
  [Clone index](/effects/drivers/clone-index/) driver is anywhere in what it shows. **Off**
  renders every copy alike. Under Point age the copies are alike too.
- **Max renders**: the most copies rendered with a number of their own. Stamps past it
  share them in turn. Each is another render of the layer.
- **Apply to**: which points get a stamp.
- **Size**: what a stamp's size is. **Point size** is a square of the point's own size with
  the whole layer squeezed into it. **Layer size** is the layer at its own size and shape.
  **Fit cell** and **Fill cell** scale the layer into a cell and keep its shape. Fit leaves
  the rest of the cell empty and Fill cuts off what overhangs. For the last three a point's
  size is a per cent, and a point of size 100 is the layer's or the cell's own size.
- **Cell width** and **Cell height**: the cell's size. They show for Fit cell and Fill
  cell.
- **Scale**: multiplies each point's own size.
- **Rotation**: added to each point's own rotation.
- **Anchor x** and **Anchor y**: which place on the stamp sits on the point, as a per cent
  across and down it. The stamp turns about this place.
- **Corner radius**: rounds the stamp's corners. It is measured on the stamp of a point
  whose size is 100, and turns and scales with the stamp.
- **Opacity**: multiplies every stamp's opacity.
- **Tint**: how much of the point's colour the stamp takes on.
- **Depth sort**: draws the stamps furthest from the camera first, and near ones cover far
  ones. Off draws them in the stream's own order. It shows only on a 3D layer with a camera.
- **Max clones**: the most stamps that may be drawn. Under Every layer a point counts once
  for each layer.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
