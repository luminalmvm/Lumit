## What it does

Drawing a shaft of light across the frame, created between two points and has a taper.

## What each control does

- **Start x** and **Start y**: the start position of the beam.
- **End x** and **End y**: the end position of the beam.
- **Length**: how much of the length the beam occupies, as a percentage.
- **Time**: where the beam's **head** has reached along the length.
- **Start thickness**: how wide the beam is at its base.
- **End thickness**: how wide it is at the head.
- **Softness**: how much of the beam's half-width the core and rim colours blend.
- **Inside colour**: the core colour.
- **Outside colour**: the rim colour.
- **Composite on original**: When enabled the layer that beam is applied to appears underneath.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
