## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket and hands it on from its own Points socket, drawn
as discs. Press **Bake** and the stream is written to disk for every frame of the layer.
**Mode** says which is handed on afterwards, the baked stream or the live one.

A baked stream can be read anywhere a stream can.
[Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) and
[Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/) read it even where they cannot read the live
one: the points of [Scatter](/effects/generate/scatter/),
[Emit from image](/effects/generate/emit-from-image/) and
[Text to points](/effects/generate/text-to-points/), and those of a
[Vary points](/effects/generate/vary-points/) or
[Pick points](/effects/generate/pick-points/) that reads a picture.

A bake records what it was made from: the effects above it on the layer and their keyframes,
the wires and drivers that feed them, the layer's masks, its text or its source, every
layer those effects name, and the composition's size, frame rate and length. After a change
to any of these the bake is stale, and **Auto** hands on the live stream until it is baked
again. Moving the layer keeps a bake.

The bake is kept in Lumit's cache on this machine and not in the project file. On another
machine it has to be baked again.

:::note[What a bake does not hold]
- A change to a media file on disk, to a composition nested deeper than the layer's own
  source, to the clips of a Sequence layer, to the source of a layer the effects name, to a
  layer an expression reads, or to what an adjustment layer sees beneath it does not make a
  bake stale.
- A bake holds up to 20,000 frames, 100,000 points in a frame and 160 MB over all its
  frames. Past any of these it stops early, and the frames after it are not baked.
- Baked points hold still within a frame, and accumulation motion blur does not smear them.
:::

## What each control does

- **Mode**: which stream is handed on. **Auto** is the bake while it matches what it was
  made from, and the live stream otherwise. **Read** is the bake whatever has changed since,
  and no points on a frame that is not baked. **Bypass** is always the live stream.
- **Bake**: write the stream to disk for every frame of the layer. It replaces the bake
  before.
- **Cancel**: stop a bake that is running. The bake before it is kept.
- **Feather**: how soft the edge of a drawn disc is.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
