## What it does

Wire a producer's teal **Points** socket into this effect and every point grows a tail: a
line of dots, or one connected ribbon, running back through the places it was a moment ago
and fading as it goes.

Nothing is remembered between frames: Trail asks the producer again at each of a handful
of earlier moments and reads each point's older self out of the answer. A tail is the same
however you arrived at the frame, and a point that had not been born yet at one of those
moments simply has a shorter tail.

With nothing wired in, the picture passes straight through and the box carries the
no-stream mark.

## What each control does

- **Samples**: how many earlier moments are looked at, and so how many dabs long a tail is.
  Each one costs another evaluation of the producer, so this is what the effect costs.
- **Spacing**: how far apart in time those moments are. The default is close to one
  composition frame. Wider spacing buys a longer tail for the same number of samples, at
  the price of a gappier one.
- **Style**: **Dots** draws one dab per sample. **Segments** joins each dab to the one
  before it into a continuous ribbon.
- **Scale**: multiplies each point's own size — the diameter of a dot, or the thickness of a
  segment.
- **Feather**: how soft a dab's edge is.
- **Fade**: how far the far end of a tail fades out. At 100 the oldest sample is invisible
  and the tail dies away; at nought the whole tail is as solid as the point itself.
- **Max trails**: the most points that may grow a tail. A stream longer than this is trimmed
  to its newest. A capacity declaration, so it does not animate.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
