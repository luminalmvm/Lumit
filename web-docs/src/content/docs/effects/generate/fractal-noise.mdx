## What it does

Making a repeatable pattern of fractal noise, with options for animation and complexity. 
Normally used as a base for more complex effects or textures.

## What each control does

- **Noise type**: **Perlin** is the smooth, cloud-like pattern. **Value** is cheaper and 
  built from corner values where Perlin uses corner slopes, which gives a squarer look.
- **Fractal type**: **Basic** adds layers as they are. **Turbulent** folds each layer about its middle.
- **Invert noise**: inverts the finished pattern.
- **Contrast**: Increase the difference between white and dark areas of the noise. 
- **Brightness**: lifts or darkens the noise after Contrast.
- **Rotation**: turns the noise pattern.
- **Uniform scaling**: when off, **Scale width** and **Scale height** scale separately.
- **Offset x** and **Offset y**: offset the noise patterns position. 
- **Complexity**: how many layers of detail are contained in the fractal noise.
- **Sub influence**: amount to scale down how much each layer of complexity adds to the noise 
  relative to the last.
- **Sub scaling**: amount to scale down the size of each layer of complexity relative to the last.
- **Evolution**: the current phase of the fractal noise
- **Cycle evolution** and **Cycle**: make Evolution loop. With Cycle at 3, the evolution at 0
  revolutions and the evolution at 3 full revolutions are the same, this allows for seamless looping.
- **Seed**: which random pattern is drawn.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
