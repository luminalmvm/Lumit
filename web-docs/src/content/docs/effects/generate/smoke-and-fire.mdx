## What it does

Turns a 3D layer into a box of gas. The layer's transform is the box, as wide and tall as its
picture and as deep as the shorter of the two, or the comp's size for a layer with no picture.
The layer's own picture does not show.

Adding it turns the layer's 3D switch on. The gas is drawn with the comp's volumetric light, in
every 3D view. It is lit by the comp's lights, cut by the shadows of 3D layers and models, stops
at whatever is in front of it, and shadows what is under it where a light reaches. With no lights
in the comp it is lit from above.

Each frame is worked out from the frame before, from the layer's in point. Moving the box or
changing the **Look** rows draws again without working it out again. The box's floor is solid. Gas
fades out at its other faces.

Moving the box carries the gas with it. With **Trail** on the gas stays where it is in the comp
and the box moves through it. Rotating the box tilts up with it. Flames light their own smoke
and no other layer, which wants a Point light. An extruded layer gives off no gas, and gas meets
it as a flat card.

**Bake** writes the walk to the project's cache. The Bake line says **Not enough video memory**
and no smoke is drawn when the grid does not fit on the graphics card.

A `.vdb` file imports as a Model layer with no faces and this effect with **Simulate** off, and
draws its own density and heat through the **Look** rows, with **Density** thickening it. A
numbered file reads the numbered files after it as its next frames. Its **Up axis** starts on Z,
and it and **Units** are set in Model settings. On a Model layer with faces the effect draws
nothing.

**Fire**, **Candle**, **Torch**, **Explosion** and **Smoke** are in the effect's presets. The effect starts as a small fire.

## What each control does

- **Simulate**: work each frame out from the frame before. Off, only what a bake or the card
  already holds is shown.
- **Sub-steps**: how many times a frame the gas moves.
- **Bake**: write the simulation to the project's cache.
- **Cancel**: stop a bake and keep the frames written.
- **Resolution**: cells along the box's longest side.
- **Emit from**: a patch on the box's floor, the particles of the Particulate on **Emit layer**,
  or the surface of the Model layer on **Emit layer**.
- **Emit layer**: the layer the gas comes from.
- **Emitter size**: how wide the patch is, as a share of the floor.
- **Density**: how much smoke it gives off.
- **Temperature**: how hot the gas is where it starts. Hot gas rises, and glows past a third.
- **Fuel**: how much there is to burn where the gas starts. Fuel that is hot burns, which heats
  the gas, swells it and gives off smoke.
- **Expansion**: how hard burning swells the gas.
- **Buoyancy**: how hard heat pushes the gas up. Below nought it sinks.
- **Turbulence**: how much the gas is broken up into small swirls.
- **Vorticity**: how much curls are kept alive.
- **Dissipation**: how fast smoke thins and heat fades.
- **Wind x**, **Wind y** and **Wind z**: the speed the air moves at along the box's sides, in
  comp pixels a second.
- **Trail**: moving the box leaves the gas behind in the comp.
- **Collide with**: a layer the gas goes round.
- **All 3D layers**: the gas goes round every 3D layer and Model layer in the comp too.
- **Smoke colour**: the smoke's colour, as light falling on it shows it.
- **Fire brightness**: how much light hot gas gives.
- **Fire colour**: the fire at its coolest.
- **Fire core**: the fire at its hottest.
- **Shadow strength**: how dark the smoke's shadows are, on itself and on what is under it.
