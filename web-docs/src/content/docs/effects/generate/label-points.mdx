## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket and writes a label beside every point. The look
comes from a Text layer: its font, size, colour and style. The wording is a template, typed
into the **Text** row, or the Text layer's own text while that row is empty. The Text layer
can be hidden and still be read.

| In the template | Written as |
| --- | --- |
| `{index}` | The point's place in the stream. |
| `{number}` | The number it carries. |
| `{x}` and `{y}` | Where it is seen on the frame, in pixels at composition size. |
| `{id}` | Its id. |
| `{size}` | Its size. |
| `{age}` | Its age. |
| `{attr:NAME}` | The number it carries under the name NAME. |
| `{@NAME}` | One of the `@` names. `{@index}`, `{@id}` and `{@n}` are written whole. |
| `{pick:a\|b\|c}` | One of the words between the bars, chosen per point. |
| `{chars:N}` | N random letters and digits (up to 64). |
| `{hex:N}` | N random hexadecimal digits (up to 64). |

Any other text is written as it is, at every point. So is a token whose name reads nothing.
See [Names](/effects/generate/vary-points/#names).

The random tokens give each point its own text, the same on every frame and whatever order the
points come in; **Seed** rolls a new set. A list of words longer than the Text row holds can be
the Text layer's own text, with the Text row left empty.

Labels follow where the points are seen and do not turn with them; Label rotation turns them. A point behind the
camera has no label.

## What each control does

- **Apply to**: which points get a label.
- **Group**: the group Picked and Not picked go by, as a name. Left empty, they go by the
  points a Pick points picked. See [Names](/effects/generate/vary-points/#names).
- **Threshold**: what a point's Group must read above to be in it. It is read only with a
  Group named.
- **Text layer**: the Text layer whose look the labels take. Unset draws nothing, unless
  this effect is on a Text layer.
- **Text**: the template. The Text layer then gives only the look. Left empty, the Text
  layer's own text is the template.
- **Decimals**: how many decimal places the numbers in a label are written to.
- **Seed**: which words and characters the random tokens choose.
- **Offset x** and **Offset y**: how far across and down from its point a label sits.
- **Anchor**: which part of the label sits at that offset. **Left** runs the text away to
  the right of it, and **Right** to the left.
- **Vertical anchor**: which part of the label's height sits at that offset, and what it turns
  and grows about. **Top** hangs it from its point.
- **Max labels**: the most labels drawn. Past it the newest points keep theirs.
- **Change**: how much a wave is at a point (Siceliot). Wire a driver into it, an Oscillator
  with Phase from on Position say, and each point reads its own value.
- **Change above**: above it a point is in the wave: its random tokens scramble at **Scramble
  rate** and its colour flashes as **Flash colour** says, Colour A and Colour B for Two colours.
  Below it the label rests on its own text and the Text layer's colour.
- **Text step**: each whole number gives the random tokens a new roll, which stays. Wire Ring
  wave's Passes into it and a label keeps the text the last ring left it with.
- **Flash colour** on **By strength**: the label's own colour multiplied by a colour that grows
  with Change, from none at 0 to **Hue start** plus **Hue range**, **Saturation** and **Darken**
  at 100, smoothly and whatever Change above says. Past 100 the hue keeps turning round.
- **Flash colour** on **Gradient**: the label's own colour multiplied by a gradient of your
  colours, **Gradient 1** at Change 0 to the last of **Gradient stops** at 100, evenly spaced.
- **Label scale**: how big a label is drawn, per cent of the Text layer's size; wired, each
  label takes its own. **Grow** adds to it with Change: at 100 a label is that much bigger.
- **Label rotation**: how far a label turns about its anchor, clockwise; wired, each label
  turns its own way. Labels are drawn between pixels, at any size and turn.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
