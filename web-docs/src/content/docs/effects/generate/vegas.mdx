## What it does

Vegas finds a contour where the picture's brightness or transparency crosses a level,
and draws a stroke along it.

## What each control does

- **Source**: **Luminance** finds the contour from brightness. **Alpha** finds
  it based on alpha, and **Mask/Path** draws the stroke round one of the layer's masks.
- **Mask**: which mask, while Source is Mask/Path.
- **Threshold**: thresholds what counts as a contour. 
- **Width**: how thick the stroke is.
- **Hardness**: how crisp edges are.
- **Segment length**: the length of one dash and gap.
- **Length**: how much of one segment is lit, as a percentage.
- **Rotation**: offsets the dashes along the contour.
- **Colour**: the stroke's colour. 
- **Opacity**: how strong the stroke is.
- **Composite on original**: on, the layer the effect is on draws under the stroke.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
