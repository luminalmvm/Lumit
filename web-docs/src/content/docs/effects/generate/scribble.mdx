## What it does

Scribble fills a mask with parallel pencil strokes at whatever angle you choose.

## What each control does

- **Mask**: which of the layer's masks to fill.
- **Colour**: the pencil's colour. 
- **Angle**: direction strokes run.
- **Stroke width**: how thick a stroke is.
- **Spacing**: how far apart strokes are laid.
- **Path overlap**: how far each stroke runs over the mask's edge.
- **Start** and **End**: where the pen begins and stops.
- **Wiggle type**: how the stroke wobbles.
- **Wiggles per second**: how frequency of wiggles. Disabled while the type is Static.
- **Seed**: randomises the wiggles.
- **Opacity**: how strong the pencil is.
- **Composite on original**: on, the layer the effect on draws under the scribble. 
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
