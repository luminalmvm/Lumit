## What it does

Follows things in a Footage layer's picture and hands them out as points, drawn as discs.
**Features** follows corners, with the tracker
[Camera track](/effects/utility/camera-track/) uses. **Blobs** follows bright or dark
regions by their centres.

Press **Analyse** and the footage is read once. Until then the effect makes no points and
draws nothing. After a change to a row the mode reads, or to the footage, it makes none
until it is analysed again.

The analysis is kept in Lumit's cache on this machine and not in the project file. On
another machine it has to be run again.

Every point keeps one identity for as long as it is followed, and a
[Trail](/effects/generate/trail/) or a
[Clone to points](/effects/generate/clone-to-points/) below stays attached to it. A
feature's number is the tracker's confidence in it, from 0 to 1. On a retimed layer the
points stay on the picture.

Any points effect can read these points, and so can
[Points sample](/effects/drivers/points-sample/) and
[Layer points](/effects/drivers/layer-points/).

## What each control does

- **Mode**: what is followed. **Features** is corners in the picture. **Blobs** is whole
  bright regions.
- **Feature density**: how many features are followed. It shows for Features, as do the
  next three.
- **Quality**: how distinct a corner has to be, as a share of the frame's best one. Lower
  follows more and weaker points.
- **Spacing**: the least distance between two features, in the footage's own pixels.
- **Window**: how wide the patch followed round each feature is, in the footage's own
  pixels. Bigger holds on better and is less exact on small detail.
- **Threshold**: how bright a pixel has to be to count as part of a blob, as a per cent of
  full white. It shows for Blobs, as do the next four.
- **Invert**, below **Threshold**: follows dark blobs on a light picture instead.
- **Min area**: the smallest blob kept, in pixels of the footage.
- **Max area**: the largest blob kept, in pixels of the footage.
- **Max distance**: how far a blob may move between two frames and still be the same blob,
  in the footage's own pixels.
- **Analyse**: start tracking.
- **Cancel**: stop a track that is running.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as. A blob is drawn at its own size
  instead.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is.
- **Colour**: what the discs are drawn in.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  emitted on the socket.
- **Matte** and its **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness. See [Effects](/use/effects/).
