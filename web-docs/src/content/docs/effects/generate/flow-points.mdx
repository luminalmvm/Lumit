## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket and carries each point along a field, across the
layer's plane. The field says which way a point at any place is moving, and how fast.

A point's place is found by walking it through the field in fixed steps, from where the
wire has it, for as long as **Clock** says. A frame is the same however it is reached. Each
point's speed becomes the way the field is carrying it.

A frame walks at most a million steps over all its points. Past that each walk is cut into
fewer, longer steps.

A frame that follows the one before is quick, as the walk so far is kept. A walk past its
step limit, a stream whose input moves, and the Point age clock are walked in full each
frame.

The moved points go out on its own Points socket and are drawn as discs.

## What each control does

- **Apply to**: which points are carried. The rest stay where they are.
- **Group**: the group Picked and Not picked go by, as a name. Left empty, they go by the
  points a Pick points picked. See [Names](/effects/generate/vary-points/#names).
- **Threshold**: what a point's Group must read above to be in it. It is read only with a
  Group named.
- **Field**: what carries the points. **Curl noise** swirls them without bunching them up.
  **Vortex** turns them round the centre. **Pull** draws them to it. **Wind** blows them
  one way. **Combined** adds the four together, each by its own share.
- **Speed**: how fast the field carries a point where it is strongest, in pixels a second.
  A negative number runs it backwards. A Vortex then turns anticlockwise and a Pull pushes
  away.
- **Curl noise**, **Vortex**, **Pull** and **Wind**: each field's share of Speed. 0 leaves
  that field out and a negative number runs it backwards. They show for Combined.
- **Noise scale**: how big the swirls are. It shows for Curl noise and Combined, as do the
  next two.
- **Noise speed**: how fast the swirls themselves change. At nought they hold still.
- **Seed**: which noise.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y**: the middle of a Vortex or a Pull. They show for Vortex,
  Pull and Combined, as do the next two.
- **Radius**: how far from the centre the field reaches.
- **Falloff**: how strong the field is from the centre, at the left of the curve, out to
  Radius, at the right. Past Radius it stays as strong as it is there.
- **Direction**: the way the Wind blows. 0 is to the right and 90 is down. It shows for
  Wind and Combined.
- **Clock**: how long each point has been carried for. **Layer time** is the layer's own
  time. **Point age** is each point's own age, and a newborn particle starts where it was
  born.
- **Steps per second**: how finely the walk is cut. More follows a tight field better and
  costs more.
- **Feather**: how soft the edge of a drawn disc is.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
