## What it does

Puts a point at every cell of a regular lattice — rows, columns, and planes receding into
depth — and draws each one as a soft disc. There is no time in it: the grid at frame 500 is
the grid at frame 3.

The discs are only half of what it is for. Grid also hands the same points out on a teal
**Points** socket, so the family that reads a stream can build on it: a copy of a layer at
every cell with [Clone to points](/effects/generate/clone-to-points/), a mesh over the
lattice with [Connect points](/effects/generate/connect-points/), a tail behind each cell
with [Trail](/effects/generate/trail/).

Turn Mix down to nought and the stream is still handed out while nothing is drawn. That is
how you use Grid purely as a source of points.

## What each control does

- **Columns**, **Rows** and **Planes**: how many cells across, down, and through the layer's
  plane. Planes at 1 is flat, which is what a 2D layer wants; more than one only shows once
  the layer is 3D and the composition has a camera. See [Cameras and 3D](/use/camera/).
- **Spacing x**, **Spacing y** and **Spacing z**: the gap between cells along each axis. The
  lattice is as big as its spacing makes it — it is not fitted to the frame.
- **Position x**, **Position y** and **Position z**: where the middle of the lattice sits.
  Position z is how far in front of or behind the layer's own plane it floats; nought is on
  the plane.
- **Jitter x**, **Jitter y** and **Jitter z**: how far a cell may wander off its place, so a
  lattice can be nudged out of its own perfection. Each cell gets the same nudge every
  frame.
- **Seed**: which jitter. Reseed to roll a different one.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is. At nought it is a hard circle.
- **Colour**: what the discs are drawn in. Values above 1 are legal, and useful under a
  [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/).
- **Max points**: the most points that may exist at once. It is a capacity declaration
  rather than a look control, so it does not animate; a lattice bigger than the cap is cut
  off at the end.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  emitted on the socket.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
