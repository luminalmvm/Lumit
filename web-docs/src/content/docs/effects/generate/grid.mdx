## What it does

Puts points at even intervals: rows, columns, and planes receding into depth.

## What each control does

- **Layout**: how the points on each plane are arranged. **Rows** is rows and columns.
  **Offset rows** moves every other row across by half a cell. **Rings** makes Rows the
  number of rings and Columns the points on each, with Spacing x and Spacing y the gap
  between rings across and down. **Spiral** winds one evenly dense spiral out from the
  centre, like a sunflower's seeds, and the spacings set how tightly. **Hexagons** packs
  offset rows as a honeycomb, every point the same distance from its six neighbours, and
  works the gap between rows out from Spacing x. **Point** puts every point at Position,
  one on top of another until a Jitter or an effect below moves them apart. Every layout has
  Columns times Rows points on each plane.
- **Columns**, **Rows** and **Planes**: how many cells across, down, and through the layer's
  plane. Planes at 1 is flat, which is what a 2D layer wants. More than one only shows once
  the layer is 3D and the composition has a camera. See [Cameras and 3D](/use/camera/).
- **Spacing x**, **Spacing y** and **Spacing z**: the gap between cells along each axis. The
  lattice is as big as its spacing makes it, unless **Fit to size** is on.
- **Fit to size**: fits the layout into Width and Height in place of Spacing x and Spacing
  y. It stays that size however many points it holds.
- **Width** and **Height**: how wide and tall the fitted layout is. Rings and Spiral read
  them as their diameter across and down.
- **Position x**, **Position y** and **Position z**: where the middle of the lattice sits.
  Position z is how far in front of or behind the layer's own plane it floats. Nought is on
  the plane.
- **Jitter x**, **Jitter y** and **Jitter z**: how far a cell may wander off its place.
  Each cell gets the same nudge every frame.
- **Seed**: which jitter. Reseed to roll a different one.
- **Start angle**: where round a ring its first point sits. It shows for Rings, as do the
  next two.
- **Arc**: how much of the circle a ring covers. A whole circle spaces its points evenly
  round it, and anything less puts one at each end.
- **Inner radius**: how far out the rings start. With the spacings it is added to every
  ring. Fitted, it is the innermost ring, and at 0 that ring is one point at the centre.
- **Size**: the diameter of the disc a point is drawn as.
- **Feather**: how soft that disc's edge is. At nought it is a hard circle.
- **Colour**: what the discs are drawn in. Values above 1 are legal, and useful under a
  [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/).
- **Max points**: the most points that may exist at once. It does not animate. A lattice
  bigger than the cap is cut off at the end.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  emitted on the socket.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
