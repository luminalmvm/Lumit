## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket and moves it as one. Every point is scaled away
from an anchor, turned round it, then placed as **Place** says. The points keep their own
sizes and rotations unless **Scale sizes** and **Turn points** are on.

A share of the frame and a pinned place are measured on the composition's size, not the
layer's.

The changed points go out on its own Points socket and are drawn as discs.

## What each control does

- **Apply to**: which points are moved. The rest pass through as they came.
- **Group**: the group Picked and Not picked go by, as a name. Left empty, they go by the
  points a Pick points picked. See [Names](/effects/generate/vary-points/#names).
- **Threshold**: what a point's Group must read above to be in it. It is read only with a
  Group named.
- **Anchor to points**: puts the anchor on the box round the points being moved, in place
  of a place in the frame.
- **Anchor in**: how an anchor in the frame is given. **Pixels** is a place in pixels.
  **Share of frame** is a per cent of the composition's size, and stays put when the
  composition is resized.
- **Anchor x** and **Anchor y**: the place the stream is scaled from and turned round. As a
  share of the frame, 0 is the composition's left or top edge and 100 its right or bottom.
- **Anchor on points x** and **Anchor on points y**: where on that box the anchor sits. 0
  is its left or top edge and 100 its right or bottom.
- **Place**: how the stream is placed. **Offset** moves it by Position from where it came.
  **Share of frame** moves it until the anchor lands on a share of the composition.
  **Pinned** moves it until the anchor lands Position away from the corner, edge or centre
  of the composition that Pin to names.
- **Place x** and **Place y**: how far across and down the composition the anchor lands, as
  a per cent of its size. They show for Share of frame.
- **Pin to**: the place in the composition that Position is measured from. It shows for
  Pinned. Bottom right, with the anchor at the points' own bottom right and Position at -32
  and -32, sits the points 32 pixels in from that corner.
- **Position x** and **Position y**: how far the stream is moved across and down. Pinned,
  they are how far from the Pin to place the anchor lands. They show for Offset and Pinned.
- **Position z**: how far the stream is moved through the layer's plane, however it is
  placed.
- **Scale x %** and **Scale y %**: how far the stream is spread from the anchor, across and
  down. A negative number flips it.
- **Rotation**: how far the stream is turned round the anchor.
- **Scale sizes**: the points grow and shrink with the stream.
- **Turn points**: the points turn with the stream.
- **Feather**: how soft the edge of a drawn disc is.
- **Mix**: how much of the drawing lands on the picture. At nought the points are still
  handed on.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness.
  See [Effects](/use/effects/).
