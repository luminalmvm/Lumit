## What it does

Reads the stream on its **Points** socket and draws a picture of where the points are.
Every pixel finds the point nearest to it on the frame, and **Output** says what it draws
from that point.

Other effects read the picture through their **Matte** row or a layer row. The **Image**
pattern of [Vary points](/effects/generate/vary-points/) and
[Pick points](/effects/generate/pick-points/) reads it the same way.

## What each control does

- **Output**: what each pixel draws from its nearest point. **Distance** is a grey, 1 at a
  point and falling to 0 at Radius away. **Direction** is which way the point lies, written
  the way [Displacement map](/effects/distortion/displacement-map/) reads a map: red steers
  the sideways push, green the up-and-down one, and mid-grey is no push. **Colour** is the
  point's own colour. **Number** is the number the point carries, as a grey.
- **Radius**: how far from a point Distance takes to fall to 0, measured on the frame.
- **Limit to radius**: leaves Colour and Number transparent further than Radius from every
  point. Off fills the frame, each point owning the pixels nearest it.
- **Invert**: turns Distance over, 0 at a point and rising to 1 at Radius away.
- **Number range**: the number that draws as white when Output is Number.
- **Name**: which of a point's numbers Number draws. Left empty, it is the number the point
  carries. See [Names](/effects/generate/vary-points/#names).
- **Colour name**: the name Colour draws, where the points carry a colour under one. A
  number or an offset under the name draws as a grey. Left empty, it is the point's own
  colour.
- **Max points**: the most points the field is made from. A longer stream is trimmed to its
  newest.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture underneath.
- **Matte** and its **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness. See [Effects](/use/effects/).
