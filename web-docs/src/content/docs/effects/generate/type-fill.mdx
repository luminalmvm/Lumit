## What it does

Sets the **Words**, typed between bars (`Flood|the|Grid`), in rows from top to bottom. Each row
is cut into columns across the whole width, one word each, the columns as wide as the words
naturally are. A **Region layer** says where the words may go: along each row a column keeps
only the stretches the region leaves free. The column's word is stretched across and down from
its first stretch to its last, each stretch showing its own slice of it: a shape that cuts a
column in two cuts the word, "t" on one side and "he" on the other, and never shows it twice.

The region can be any layer: a shape, a letter, a precomp of shapes put together with blend
modes. Animate it and the words flood the room it leaves. The look (font, style, colour) comes
from a **Text layer**, which can be hidden.

## What each control does

- **Text layer**: the Text layer whose look the words take. Unset draws nothing.
- **Words**: the words between bars, left to right in every row.
- **Repeat words**: how many times Words is set one after another in every row.
- **Direction**: **Rows** runs the words left to right in rows, one under another.
  **Columns** stands them one under another in columns side by side, still upright. Rows,
  Grid height and Row gap then count across the frame, and Row shift moves up and down.
- **Column widths**: how wide each word's column is, as numbers in the order of Words,
  between bars, dashes or spaces: `46|8|40`. They are shares of the width. A shorter list
  goes round again. Left empty, each column is as wide as its word naturally is.
- **Column mix**: how far the columns go from the words' own widths, at 0, to Column
  widths, at 100.
- **Region layer**: where the words may go. Unset, the whole frame is theirs.
- **Region placement**: where the Region layer's picture sits on this one.
- **Region from** and **Threshold**: a place is in the region where the Region layer is that
  opaque (Alpha) or that bright (Luminance).
- **Invert region**: fill where the region is not.
- **Cut words**: keep every word whole at its column's width and let the region cut it pixel by
  pixel, instead of stretching it into the room it has. Word gap no longer insets the edges.
  Either way the region cuts the words pixel by pixel.
- **Fit piece**: give every piece the region leaves in a column its own whole word, fitted to
  the piece's box across and down. Nothing is cut, and Cut words does nothing beside it.
- **Piece scale**: how much of its piece's box a word fills, with Fit piece.
- **Clip to region**: with Fit piece, cut the words where the region stops, pixel by pixel.
- **Rows**: how many rows fill the frame from top to bottom.
- **Grid height**: how tall the rows stand together, centred in the frame. 0 is the frame's
  height.
- **Row gap**: the room between one row and the next.
- **Row open**: how open the rows are. Each row grows out of a flat line through its middle.
- **Row stagger**: how far apart the rows open. At 0 they open together. Higher, the middle
  row opens first and the outer rows last.
- **Row shift**: how far every row slides sideways. The words wrap round.
- **Alternate rows**: every other row slides the other way.
- **Word gap**: the room between the words of a row, and at the region's edges.
- **Min width**: a column whose word would be narrower than this is left empty.
- **Order**: each row's words in the same order, moved on one word a row, or shuffled per row by
  the **Seed**.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.

Two Type fills with the same Region layer, one with Invert region on and its words in the other
colour, fill both sides of a shape: black on white outside, white on black inside. With
**Cut words** on in both, they share each word across the shape's edge.
