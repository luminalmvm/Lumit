## What it does

An ASC Color Decision List. Each channel is multiplied by **Slope**, has **Offset** added,
is held to 0 to 1 and raised to **Power**. **Saturation** then scales colour about Rec. 709
luma, and the result is held to 0 to 1 again. It is worked in its **Space**. A CDL with
every row at rest passes the picture on untouched, with nothing held to 0 to 1.

**Import CDL…** makes a CDL node from a `.cdl`, `.cc` or `.ccc` file; see
[CDL](/use/grading/#cdl).

## What each control does

- **Space**: the signal the numbers were made on. **Video** is the working linear under a
  2.4 gamma, **Log** is ACEScct, and **Linear** is the working linear as it is.
- **Slope** multiplies each channel.
- **Offset** adds to each channel, after Slope.
- **Power**: the exponent each channel is raised to, last.
- **Saturation**: colourfulness about Rec. 709 luma.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
