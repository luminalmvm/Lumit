## What it does

Adjust how colourful the output is. At zero it's greyscale.

Every pixel's colourfulness is multiplied by the same amount, regardless of its
input saturation.

- **Matte** pulls Saturation toward 100 per pixel: white applies the full Saturation, black leaves the colour as it was. **Invert** reads that matte inverted.

## What each control does

- **Saturation**: the multiplier on colourfulness, as a percentage.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
