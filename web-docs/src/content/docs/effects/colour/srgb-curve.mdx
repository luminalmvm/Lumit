## What it does

Lumit works in linear light. **Linear to sRGB** puts the picture onto the sRGB curve, and
**sRGB to linear** takes it back off. A grade between the two works on the encoded
picture. An adjustment layer imported from a Photoshop document comes in between the two
where that gives Photoshop's picture.

## What each control does

- **Direction**: onto the sRGB curve, or back off it.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
