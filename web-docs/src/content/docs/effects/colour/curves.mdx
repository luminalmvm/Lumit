## What it does

Adjust the brightness against brightness values. You can view this as inputting 
brightness into a function which outputs a different brightness based on the curve
you set.

## What each control does

The five curves share one graph. The buttons beside it pick which curve it shows.

- Click the graph to add a point, up to 32 on a curve. Drag a point to move it. Drag it
  well clear of the graph to remove it. The two end points cannot be removed.
- **Reset curve**, under the buttons once the curve showing is bent, straightens that
  curve.
- A right-click on the graph offers **Add point**, **Delete point**, **Reset curve**,
  **Copy curve** and **Paste curve**, and the graph's size: **Small**, **Medium** or
  **Large**.
- **Master** applies to red, green and blue together, after their own curves.
- **Red**, **Green** and **Blue** apply to individual channels.
- **Alpha** applies to the layer's transparency.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
