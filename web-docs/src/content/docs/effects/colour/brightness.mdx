## What it does

Adds or subtracts light to every pixel.

- **Matte** pulls Brightness toward 0 and Contrast toward 0 per pixel: white applies both in full, black neither. **Invert** reads that matte inverted.

## What each control does

- **Brightness** is the light added to every channel. At full deflection either
  way it is a whole unit of scene-linear light, which is a lot; small numbers
  are the usual ones.
- **Contrast** is the spread about mid-grey. Zero passes the picture through and
  −100 flattens the frame to grey. At +100 the spread doubles, and there is no
  ceiling above.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the brightness amount from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).

## Notes

Brightness is addition, so it lifts the shadows as much as highlights. That makes 
it flatter than [Exposure](/effects/colour/exposure/), which multiplies instead.
