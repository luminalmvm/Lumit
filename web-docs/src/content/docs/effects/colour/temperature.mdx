Temperature is a [Colour](/effects/colour/) effect.

## What it is for

The warmth lever. Positive values lift red and drop blue, which reads as warmer
and more like tungsten light; negative does the reverse for a cooler, bluer
picture. Green is held where it is, because a warm-cool shift is a red-blue
question and moving green as well is what makes a white balance go strange.

Use it to take a cast off footage that was captured under the wrong balance, to
match two shots that disagree about what white is, or — animated — to warm a
montage into a sunset and back.

It is deliberately a single control rather than a full white balance. Full
deflection is a decisive orange or blue rather than a subtle nudge, so most work
happens in the middle of the range.

For a cast that is not on the red-to-blue axis — a green cast from strip
lighting, for instance — use [Colour balance](/effects/colour/colour-balance/),
which can move each channel independently.

## What each control does

- **Temperature** — positive is warmer, negative cooler. It is a plain number
  rather than a figure in kelvin, because it shifts the picture from wherever it
  already is instead of declaring what the light was.
- **Mix** — how much of the shifted picture replaces the original.
