## What each control does

- **Amount** — how intense the vignette is.
- **Radius** — how far from the middle the darkening begins.
- **Softness** — the width of the fade between transparent and full black.
- **Roundness** — At zero it's an ellipse that reaches every edge equally. 
  One is a circle.
- **Ramp** — the shape of the falloff curve.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
