## What it does

If a pixel is brighter than the threshold value it becomes white, otherwise it becomes black.

## What each control does

- **Level** — the cutoff point.
- **Softness** — how wide the crossing between black and white is. **0** is a
  hard cut. Greater than this creates a gradient either side of the level value. 
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).

## Notes

**Transparency is not affected.** The alpha channel is not included in the threshold

**For a colour version of the same idea**, use
[Posterize](/effects/colour/posterize/) at a low Levels count, or
[Tritone](/effects/colour/tritone/) to map the two tones onto colours of your
choosing.
