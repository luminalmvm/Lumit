## What it does

A mesh over the vectorscope's colour wheel: a spoke at each of six or eight even hues, three
rings out to full colour, and the centre. A moved point takes the colours under it where it
went, hue and saturation together, and the colours round it follow, fading out by the next
spoke and ring. It is worked in video gamma. A mesh at rest changes nothing. The mesh is
dragged in the [Grade panel](/panels/grade/#colour-warper).

## What each control does

- **Resolution**: **Six hues** or **Eight hues**, the spokes round the three rings.
- **Points**: how far each mesh point has moved, the centre first. A point not listed has
  not moved.
- **Keep luma**: on, colours move without their brightness changing. Off, each pixel's
  brightest channel stays where it was.
- **Strength**: how far the colours follow the points.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the picture.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
