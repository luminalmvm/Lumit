## What it does

Converts the layer to black and white, with additional controls for the brightness of
different colors.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Tint colour** is editable while **Tint** is on.
- **Matte** scales how much of the effect each pixel gets: white applies it in full, black leaves the pixel as it arrived, grey part way. **Invert** reads the matte the other way round, light for dark.

## What each control does

- **Reds**, **Yellows**, **Greens**, **Cyans**, **Blues** and **Magentas** set
  how bright each colour becomes.
- **Tint** and **Tint colour** tint the greyscale output.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the greyscale amount from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
