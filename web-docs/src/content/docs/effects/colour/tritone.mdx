## What it does

Similar to [Tint](/effects/colour/tint/), but with three colours, one for blacks, mid-tones, and whites.

## What each control does

- **Highlights**: the colour white maps to.
- **Midtones**: the colour mid-grey maps to.
- **Shadows**: the colour black maps to.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
