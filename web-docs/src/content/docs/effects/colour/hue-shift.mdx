## What it does

Similar to Hue and saturation, but only provies options to adjust the hue of the entire input.

- **Matte** scales Angle toward 0 per pixel: white turns the hue by the full Angle, grey part of the way, black not at all. **Invert** inverts the mattes for calculating strength.

## What each control does

- **Angle** is how far round the hue wheel to rotate colours.
- **Preserve luminance** toggles whether brightness is held as the hue turns.
  This is based on the percieved brightness of colours and different hues.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
