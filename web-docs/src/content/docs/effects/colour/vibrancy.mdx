## What it does

A saturation lift that raises the saturation of greyer pixels more than vivid ones.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Matte** scales Amount per pixel: white applies the full Amount, grey a fraction of it, black none. **Invert** inverts the mattes for calculating strength.

## What each control does

- **Amount** — the amount to increase vibrance by.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
