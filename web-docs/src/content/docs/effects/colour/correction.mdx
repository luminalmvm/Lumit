## What it does

One node of a grade: wheels, log wheels, adjustments, an RGB mixer, curves and hue curves,
keyed by a qualifier, four windows and the Matte. The grade is worked in its **Space** and
lands where the key is white. A Correction with every row at rest passes the picture on
untouched. The [Grade panel](/panels/grade/) edits it page by page; see
[Grading](/use/grading/).

Exposure, Temperature and Tint act on the linear picture, then the HDR zones, before it is
put into the Space. The rest runs in this order: Offset, Lift, Gamma, Gain, the log wheels,
Lum mix, Contrast, Shadows and Highlights, the mixer, Hue with Saturation and Colour boost,
Midtone detail, the curves, the soft clip, then the hue curves.

## What each control does

- **Space**: where the grade is worked. **Video** is the working linear under a 2.4 gamma,
  **Log** is ACEScct, and **Linear** is the working linear as it is.
- **Combine**: how the node meets the one above it. **Serial** grades what the node above
  made. **Parallel** grades the same picture as the node above and adds its change to theirs.
  **Layer** grades the same picture and lays its result over theirs through its **Blend**, by
  its key and Mix. A Correction with nothing above it, or under another effect, is Serial.
- **Lift** raises or lowers the blacks and leaves white where it is.
- **Gamma** bends the mid-tones.
- **Gain** multiplies every level.
- **Offset** adds the same amount at every level, before the other three wheels.
- **Shadow**, **Midtone** and **Highlight** add colour below **Low range**, between the two
  ranges and above **High range**.
- **Low range** and **High range**: the luma, in the grading space, where shadows end and
  where highlights start.
- **Exposure**: stops of light, on the linear picture.
- **Temperature** and **Tint**: blue to orange and green to magenta, the
  [Temperature](/effects/colour/temperature/) effect's maths.
- **Contrast** spreads the levels away from the **Pivot**, or draws them in below 100.
- **Pivot**: the level contrast turns about, in the grading space.
- **Saturation** scales colour about luma.
- **Hue** turns every colour round the colour wheel. Luma stays.
- **Lum mix** at 100 lets the wheels change brightness. At 0 they change colour only.
- **Colour boost** raises or lowers saturation, the weak colours more than the strong ones.
- **Shadows** and **Highlights** brighten or darken the dark half and the bright half of the
  picture.
- **Midtone detail** raises or lowers local contrast in the mid-tones.
- **Global balance** leans the whole picture's colour, in stops a channel, its brightness
  kept.
- **Black offset** raises or lowers the darkest levels of the linear picture.
- **Falloff**: how many stops each HDR zone fades in over.
- **Black**, **Dark**, **Shadow**, **Light**, **Highlight** and **Specular** are the HDR
  zones, by brightness in stops from mid grey. Black, Dark and Shadow reach down from their
  **range**, the other three up from it. Each has a **balance**, in stops a channel with its
  brightness kept, an **exposure** in stops and a **saturation**. Where zones overlap, their
  changes add up.
- **Red output**, **Green output** and **Blue output**: how much red, green and blue make
  each output channel.
- **Keep luma** puts back the luma the mixer moved.
- **Luma**, **Red**, **Green** and **Blue**: tone curves. Each colour curve acts on its own
  channel, then Luma on all three.
- **High soft clip** eases the brightest levels towards white, and **Low soft clip** the
  darkest towards black.
- **Hue v hue** turns one hue towards another.
- **Hue v sat** saturates or desaturates by hue.
- **Hue v lum** brightens or darkens by hue.
- **Lum v sat** saturates or desaturates by brightness.
- **Sat v sat** saturates or desaturates by saturation.
- **Sat v lum** brightens or darkens by saturation.
- **Qualifier**: what the node is keyed on, **HSL**, **RGB**, **Luma** or **3D**. **Off**
  keys everything.
- **Use hue**, **Use saturation** and **Use luma** leave that range out of an HSL key while
  off.
- **Hue** is the middle of the hue range, red at 0°. **Hue width** is how wide it is, **Hue
  softness** how far the key fades past each edge, and **Hue symmetry** moves the width to
  one side of the middle.
- **Saturation low**, **Saturation high** and **Saturation softness**, and **Luma low**,
  **Luma high** and **Luma softness**: each range's edges, and how far the key fades past
  them.
- **Red low** to **Blue high**: each channel's range for an RGB key. **Softness** is the fade
  past all of them.
- **Keep samples** and **Drop samples**: the colours a 3D key keeps and leaves out, up to 32
  each, sampled by strokes on the [Viewer](/use/grading/#the-3d-qualifier). **Keep sample
  luma** and **Drop sample luma** hold each sample's luma.
- **Size**: how far round each sample a 3D key reaches. **Softness**, under **3D**, is how
  far past Size it fades.
- **Denoise** softens the picture the key is judged on.
- **Clean black** pushes the key's weakest greys to black, and **Clean white** its strongest
  to white.
- **Black clip** and **White clip**: the least and the most the key can be.
- **Blur radius** blurs the key.
- **Grow** widens the key, or narrows it below 0.
- **Invert**, under **Matte finesse**, keys everything the qualifier left out.
- **Window 1** to **Window 4**: the slot's shape. **Ellipse**, **Rectangle**, **Polygon** or
  **Gradient**, or **Off**.
- **Centre X**, **Centre Y**, **Width**, **Height** and **Angle** place the shape. At angle 0
  a gradient keys the part above its centre line.
- **Roundness** rounds a rectangle's corners.
- **Outer softness** fades the window past its edge, and **Inner softness** inside it.
- **Opacity**: how much the window keys.
- **Invert**, in a window's group, keys outside the shape.
- **Subtract** cuts the window out of the slots before it. Otherwise it joins them.
- **Points**: a polygon's points, drawn on the [Viewer](/panels/viewer/). A polygon of fewer
  than three points is off.
- **Smooth** draws a polygon as a curve through its points.
- **Mix**: how much of the grade replaces the picture.
- **Matte** and **Invert** join another layer's brightness to the node's key; see
  [Effects](/use/effects/). A key wire from a Correction above can feed it instead; see
  [The node graph](/use/nodes/#key-wires).
