## What it does

Exposure multiplies lightness light rather than being additive, so blacks remain black.

## What each control does

- **Stops** is the amount to increase or decrease exposure by. This is equivalent to doublings and halvings.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
