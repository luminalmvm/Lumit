Colorama is a **Colour** effect.

## What it does

Reads a phase from one channel of each pixel and looks it up on a wheel of colours, as
After Effects' Colorama does. A phase of zero is the top of the wheel, and a rising phase
runs round it anticlockwise. The colour found replaces what **Modify** names.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Add phase from** and **Add mode** are editable while **Add phase** names a layer.
- **Stops** and the **Colour** and **Position** rows are editable while **Use preset
  palette** is set to None.
- **Matching colour**, **Matching tolerance** and **Matching softness** are editable while
  **Matching mode** is anything but Off.

## What each control does

- **Add phase**: a layer whose phase is combined with this layer's.
- **Get phase from**: the channel of the layer the phase is read from. **Zero** reads 0.
- **Add phase from**: the channel read from the Add phase layer.
- **Add mode**: how the two phases combine. **Wrap** adds them and carries on round the
  wheel, **Clamp** adds them and stops at the end, **Average** takes their mean and
  **Screen** screens them.
- **Phase shift**: turns every phase round the wheel.
- **Use preset palette**: a ready-made wheel. **None** uses the Colour and Position rows.
- **Stops**: how many colours are used, from Colour 1.
- **Colour 1** to **Colour 8** and **Position 1** to **Position 8**: each colour, and where
  it sits on the wheel, in per cent clockwise from the top. Stops are sorted by Position.
- **Cycle repetitions**: how many times the phase runs round the wheel.
- **Interpolate palette**: on, each colour blends into the next. Off, each holds until the
  next.
- **Modify**: what of the pixel the colour found replaces.
- **Modify alpha**: multiplies the pixel's alpha by the alpha of the colour found.
- **Change empty pixels**: on, clear pixels are repainted too.
- **Matching mode**: limits the change to pixels near **Matching colour**, compared by
  **RGB**, **Hue** or **Chroma**.
- **Matching colour**: the colour pixels are compared with.
- **Matching tolerance**: how far from Matching colour a pixel may be and change in full.
- **Matching softness**: how far past the tolerance the change fades out.
- **Composite over layer**: lays the result over the layer as it was.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
