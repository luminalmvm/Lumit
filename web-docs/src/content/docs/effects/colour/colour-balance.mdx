## What it does

**Lift** adjusts shadows.

**Gamma** adjusts mid-tones.

**Gain** adjusts highlights.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Matte** scales how much of the effect each pixel gets: white applies it in full, black leaves the pixel as it arrived, grey part way. **Invert** reads the matte the other way round, light for dark.

## What each control does

- **Lift** adjusts the tint and brightness of shadows.
- **Gamma** adjusts the tint and brightness of mid-tones.
- **Gain** adjusts the tint and brightness of highlights.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).

Each of the three opens the colour picker, and the picker carries a screen
dropper, so a cast can be sampled straight off the picture with no guesswork.
See [Effect Controls](/panels/effect-controls/).
