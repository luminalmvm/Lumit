## What it does

Marks where a [grade group's](/use/grading/#groups) grade runs in a member shot's stack.
A shot that joins a group gets two: a **Pre** at the start of its own grade and a **Post**
after it. The render puts the group's effects in each one's place. One whose group has gone
passes the picture on untouched.

A [shared node](/use/grading/#shared-nodes) is a Group grade too, its Part **Shared**.

## What each control does

- **Part**: **Pre** runs the group's grade before the shot's own, **Post** the one after it.
  **Shared** runs a shared node where the effect stands.
