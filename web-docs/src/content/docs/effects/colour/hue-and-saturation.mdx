## What it does

Allows you to adjust hue, brightness values, or saturation. This can be done
across the entire input, or adjusted on the colour of a pixel.

## What each control does

- **Hue** counts degrees around the colour wheel.
- **Saturation** adjusts saturation, -100 is greyscale, and +100 doubles the saturation.
- **Lightness** adjusts brightness.
- **Master** applies to every pixel. **Reds**, **Yellows**, **Greens**,
  **Cyans**, **Blues** and **Magentas** apply to that range, and by how saturated the pixel already is.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
