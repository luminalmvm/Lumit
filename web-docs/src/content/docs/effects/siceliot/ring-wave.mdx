## What it does

Starts a ring every **Interval** and grows it outwards across the frame. Each point reads
how strong the rings are where it stands, so Ring wave goes on the layer that holds
[Clone to points](/effects/generate/clone-to-points/) or
[Label points](/effects/generate/label-points/), wired into that effect's own rows: Clone to
points' **Scale**, **Rotation** or **Opacity**, or Label points' **Change**, **Label
scale**, **Text step** or **Label rotation**. Anywhere else it reads at the top left corner
of the composition.

It has four sockets:

- **Value**: Minimum where no ring is, Maximum on a ring's middle line.
- **Rotation**: the turn a ring gives what it crosses, in degrees clockwise, strongest on
  its middle and following the way the ring travels.
- **Passes**: how many rings have crossed the point. It goes up by one as each ring's
  middle reaches the point, and never goes back.
- **Grow**: how much bigger the rings make the point, per cent.

The same frame always reads the same values.

## What each control does

- **Minimum**: the value where no ring is.
- **Maximum**: the value on a ring's middle line.
- **Interval**: seconds between one ring and the next.
- **Speed**: how fast a ring grows, in pixels a second.
- **Speed variance**: how much faster or slower each ring may be.
- **Thickness min** and **Thickness max**: how far from its middle line a ring fades to
  nothing. Each ring is somewhere between the two.
- **Origin**: where the rings start. **Anywhere** on the frame, on its **Edges**, in its
  **Centre**, or **Away from points**, at least Clearance from every point it is wired to.
- **Clearance**: how far from every point a ring starts, on Away from points.
- **Add up**: rings that overlap add up past Maximum. Off, they stop at Maximum.
- **Grow min** and **Grow max**: the least and the most a ring makes what it crosses grow,
  on the Grow socket. Each ring has its own amount between the two.
- **Spin**: how much the rings turn what they cross, on the Rotation socket.
- **Seed**: where each ring starts, how fast it grows and how thick it is.

## With Label points

On the layer that holds Label points, wire **Value** into Label points' **Change** and
**Passes** into **Text step**. A label scrambles as a ring crosses it and keeps the text the
ring leaves it with. **Grow**, plus 100 through a [Math](/effects/drivers/math/) set to
Add, into **Label scale** swells each label as a ring passes.
