## What it does

Swings a value between **Minimum** and **Maximum** in a wave. Each copy
[Clone to points](/effects/generate/clone-to-points/) stamps sits at its own place on the
wave, set by its number or by where it stands.

Wired into Clone to points' own **Scale**, **Rotation** or **Opacity**, or into
[Label points](/effects/generate/label-points/)' **Change**, **Label scale**, **Text step**
or **Label rotation**, it gives each stamp or label its own value, for up to 4096 points.
In what a clone layer shows, it renders that layer once per copy, as
[Clone index](/effects/drivers/clone-index/) does. Anywhere else it reads as the first copy
of one.

Where a copy stands is known only when the driver is wired into those rows. Anywhere else
it reads as the top left corner of the composition.

## What each control does

- **Shape**: the wave's shape. A sine starts halfway between Minimum and Maximum, on its
  way up.
- **Minimum**: the bottom of the wave.
- **Maximum**: the top of the wave.
- **Frequency**: waves a second.
- **Phase**: where in the wave it starts.
- **Phase per copy**: how much further along the wave each copy is than the one before.
- **Phase from**: what sets each copy's place on the wave. **Copy** goes by its number;
  **Position** goes by where it stands, so the wave crosses the points whatever order they
  come in.
- **Wave angle**: the direction the wave travels across the points. 0° runs left to
  right, 90° top to bottom.
- **Wavelength**: the distance from one crest to the next.

## With Clone to points

On the layer that holds Clone to points, an Oscillator from 100 to 900 with **Phase from**
on Position, wired into Clone to points' **Scale**, sends a wave of size across a grid of
copies. With Phase from on Copy and **Phase per copy** at 15°, the copies pulse one after
another instead.
