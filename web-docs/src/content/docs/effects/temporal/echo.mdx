## What it does

Lays a run of the layer at other moments over or behind the present one. Each echo is the
layer as it was then: where its transform and its parents had it, with the effects above
Echo. The controls are those of After Effects' Echo.

On an adjustment layer the echoes are of everything below it, one frame apart.

## What each control does

- **Echo time (seconds)** sets how far apart the echoes are. Below 0 they are earlier
  moments, above 0 later ones.
- **Number of echoes** sets how many echoes are shown.
- **Starting intensity** sets how strong the layer itself is. Every echo is scaled by it too.
- **Decay** sets how much of the intensity each echo keeps from the one before.
- **Echo operator** says how the echoes are combined. **Composite in back** puts each echo
  behind the ones before it, and **Composite in front** lays it over them. **Maximum** and
  **Minimum** keep the lighter or the darker. **Blend** averages the layer and its echoes.
  The rest are the [blend modes](/use/blend-modes/) of the same names.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
