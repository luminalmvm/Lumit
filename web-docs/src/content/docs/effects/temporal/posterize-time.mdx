## What it does

Makes time step, in practice acting as a way to reduce a layers framerate.

## What each control does

- **Frame rate** sets how many times a second the picture updates.
- **Phase** shifts where the steps land.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
