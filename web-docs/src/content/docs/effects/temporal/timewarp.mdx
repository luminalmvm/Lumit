## What it does

Shows the layer as it was at another moment, set by a **Speed** or a keyframed **Source
frame**. Between two frames it holds the first, mixes the two, or makes the frame between
them from the motion it measures. On a precomp it retimes the comp inside, and on an
adjustment layer everything below it.

The effects above Timewarp in the stack are retimed with the layer. The effects below it
are not. A layer's own transform keeps its own time, so on a text or shape layer the
motion of the layer itself is not retimed; precompose it to retime that too.

## What each control does

- **Adjust time by** picks whether **Speed** or **Source frame** sets the time shown.
- **Speed** is a percentage of real time, counted from the start of the layer. A negative
  speed runs backwards.
- **Source frame** is the frame shown now, at the composition's rate. Keyframed, it remaps
  time.
- **Method** sets how a time between two frames is shown. **Whole frames** holds the
  earlier frame, **Frame mix** crossfades the two and **Pixel motion** makes the frame
  between them from the motion.
- **Quality** sets how closely the motion is measured.
- **Smoothness** sets how smooth the measured motion is kept. Higher tears less; lower
  keeps the edges between moving things sharper.
- **Motion blur** smears the picture along the measured motion.
- **Shutter angle** sets how long the shutter is open, in degrees of one frame.
- **Samples** is a maximum on the number of samples along each streak.
- **Input rate** is the frame rate the layer is counted in. At 0 it is the composition's.
  Footage shot faster than the composition shows every frame it has at its own rate.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.

## Presets

On the effect's menu, under **Presets**. Keyframes start at the playhead.

- **Half speed** plays at 50%.
- **Quarter speed** plays at 25%, at High quality.
- **Slow motion with blur** plays at 30% with motion blur.
- **Speed ramp** slows to 20% for a second and back.
- **Fast forward blur** plays at 400% in whole frames, with motion blur.

:::note[Not built]
- One effect a stack moves the layer in time: the first Timewarp, or Jolt with Time on. The
  others show the frame as it is.
- Above Timewarp, effects that read another layer, a mask path or neighbouring frames read
  nothing at its other moments.
- An expression on Speed or Source frame is read as one value, not over time.
- As a clip effect in Cut, Timewarp shows the frame as it is.
:::
