## What it does

The expensive, physically correct motion blur. It renders the composition beneath 
it multiple times *within* a frame, then averages the
results to generate the motion blur.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Matte** scales Shutter angle per pixel: the open shutter shrinks toward the frame's own moment where the matte is dark, so part of the picture is blurred over half a frame and part is not blurred at all. **Invert** reads that matte inverted.

## What each control does

- **Samples** is how many times the scene below is rendered per frame at equal offsets.
- **Shutter angle** says how much of the frame the shutter is open
  for.
- **Shutter phase** places the open window relative to the frame's own moment.
- **Force on all layers** enables every layer's own transform motion blur for
  the duration of each sample render, using this effect's shutter settings.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
