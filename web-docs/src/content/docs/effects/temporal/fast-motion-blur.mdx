## What it does

This effect measures where every pixel moved between one frame and the next, and
smears each one along its own path.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Vector scale** is editable while **Motion vectors** names a layer.
- **Matte** scales Shutter angle per pixel: the streak is genuinely shorter where the matte is dark, gathering from nearer along the motion, rather than a long streak faded back over a sharp picture. **Invert** reads that matte inverted.

## What each control does

- **Shutter angle** sets how long the imaginary shutter is open.
- **Samples** is a *maximum* on the number of samples the blur combines.
- **Quality**: increasing gives higher quality blur, for increased render time.
- **View** is a diagnostic display. Motion vectors show measured movement
  as colour. Confidence shows where the measurement is likely correct in white, and
  Dominant motion shows the neighbourhood motion an uncertain pixel borrows.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
