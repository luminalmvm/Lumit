## What this family does

Every other effect works on the frame in front of it. These four work on
*time* — they read frames either side of the current one, or change which moment
gets drawn at all.

- [Echo](/effects/temporal/echo/) composites a run of earlier frames behind or
  over the present one, for trails and speed lines.
- [Fast motion blur](/effects/temporal/fast-motion-blur/) measures the motion
  between frames and smears each pixel along it, in one pass.
- [Motion blur](/effects/temporal/motion-blur/) re-renders the whole scene
  beneath it at several moments inside one frame and averages the results. It is
  the correct, expensive one.
- [Posterize time](/effects/temporal/posterize-time/) holds time on a coarser
  grid, so the picture steps rather than flows — the stop-motion look.

## The three kinds of motion blur

They are easy to confuse, and they are genuinely different things.

| What | Where it lives | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The motion blur **switch** | A layer switch | Smears one layer along its own transform. |
| **Motion blur** effect | The effect stack | Re-renders the scene below at sub-frame moments and averages them. |
| **Fast motion blur** effect | The effect stack | Smears the motion already inside the footage, in one pass. |

The switch is free and only knows about the layer's own animated position, scale
and rotation. The effect below it is the correct one — it catches footage motion,
animated effects, the camera, everything — and costs several full renders per
frame. Fast motion blur is the cheap middle: it can smear motion that is inside
the picture, which the switch cannot, without re-rendering anything.

## What to expect of them

Two of these read the layer's own **source** frames, which has consequences worth
knowing. Echo and Fast motion blur work on footage layers; on a solid, a shape or
an adjustment layer there is nothing beneath them to read and they step aside.
And where one of the re-rendering effects — Motion blur or Posterize time — has a
temporal effect inside the scene it is sampling, that inner effect holds still
rather than being sampled through, which is a documented boundary rather than a
fault.

All four resolve time through [Retime](/use/retime/), so a slowed layer's echoes
and blurs stretch with it correctly.
