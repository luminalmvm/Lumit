## What it does

The sound is played from a delay line whose length an oscillator moves, with none of the
untouched sound kept. A slow, deep setting stops deepening at the longest sweep the line
holds.

## What each control does

- **Rate**: cycles a second.
- **Depth**: how far the pitch moves either way, in cents. 100 is a semitone.
- **Shape**: 0 sine, 1 triangle, 2 square.
