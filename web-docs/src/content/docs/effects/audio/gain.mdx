## What it does

Gain moves both channels together and the two trims move one channel each. At the bottom of
the Gain row the sound is silent.

## What each control does

- **Gain**: the level of both channels, in dB.
- **Left trim**: the left channel's level on top of Gain, in dB.
- **Right trim**: the right channel's level on top of Gain, in dB.
- **Invert**: 1 flips the polarity of both channels, 0 leaves it.
