## What it does

A chain of all-pass filters swept by an oscillator and mixed with the untouched sound. Two
stages make one notch.

## What each control does

- **Stages**: how many all-pass filters the chain holds.
- **Rate**: cycles a second.
- **Depth**: how far the sweep travels. At 100 it moves two octaves either side of Centre.
- **Centre**: the middle of the sweep, in Hz.
- **Feedback**: how much of the last stage goes back into the first. Below 0 it goes back
  the other way up.
- **Stereo phase**: how far the right channel's cycle sits behind the left's.
- **Wet**: how much of the phased sound is heard. The notches are deepest at 50.
