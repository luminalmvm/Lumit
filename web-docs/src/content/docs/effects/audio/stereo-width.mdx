## What it does

Width scales the difference between the two channels. At 0 both channels carry the same
sound.

| **Width** | Slider | 0 to 200 | 100 | Per cent |
| **Balance** | Slider | -100 to 100 | 0 | Per cent |

{/* END GENERATED */}

## What each control does

- **Width**: how much of the difference between the channels is kept. 0 is mono, 100 is the
  sound as it is, 200 is twice the difference.
- **Balance**: moves the sound towards the left, below 0, or the right, above 0, at constant
  loudness.
- **Mono below**: a frequency in Hz. Everything under it is folded into the middle. 0 is
  off.
