## What it does

The sound comes back a **Time** later, and each repeat goes round again through a low pass
until it falls silent. The repeats ring on past the end of the layer's sound.

## What each control does

- **Time**: how far behind the sound the first repeat lands, in milliseconds.
- **Note**: the beat division the repeat would land on. It does nothing yet.
- **Sync**: 1 would follow the beat grid instead of Time. It does nothing yet.
- **Feedback**: how much of each repeat goes round again.
- **Damping**: where the low pass in the feedback sits, in Hz.
- **Ping-pong**: how much of one channel's return crosses into the other. At 100 the repeats
  walk left, right, left.
- **Wet**: how much of the output is repeats.
