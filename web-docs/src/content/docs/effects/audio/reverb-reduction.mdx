## What it does

Turns down the echo a room adds to a recording. From the room's reverb time it works out
how much of the sound at each moment is the room still ringing, and turns that down.

With **Find reverb time** on, the reverb time is measured in the whole of the layer's own
sound, from how it dies away in its pauses. Off, **Reverb time** is used.

**Best quality** first takes away the reverb that can be predicted from the sound just
before it, worked out over the whole of the layer's sound, then turns down what is left.

The display above the controls shows the sound arriving around the playhead, the reverb
found in it and what comes out. Under it is the reverb time found in the three seconds
around the playhead, where there is one.

## What each control does

- **Reduction**: the most the reverb is turned down, in dB.
- **Find reverb time**: measures the room's reverb time in the sound.
- **Reverb time**: how long the room rings, in seconds, while Find reverb time is off.
- **Smoothing**: how slowly the reduction closes after a sound. Low leaves a watery residue.
  High leaves reverb behind.
- **Best quality**: takes out the predictable reverb first, then turns down the rest.
- **Reverb only**: plays what is taken away.

**Effect ▸ Only what is removed** (`Alt+N`) switches **Reverb only**.
