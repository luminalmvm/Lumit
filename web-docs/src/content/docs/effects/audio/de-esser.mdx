## What it does

Turns down the hiss of S and SH sounds in a voice. It holds the level of the sound above
**Frequency** against the level of the whole sound. When the difference passes
**Threshold**, the sound above Frequency is turned down until the S has passed. Threshold is
against the voice's own level, so it holds as the voice gets louder or quieter.

Sound quieter than -60 dB is left alone.

The display above the controls marks Frequency on the sound arriving around the playhead,
with the level the sound above it has to reach and what comes out.

## What each control does

- **Threshold**: how loud the sound above Frequency has to be against the whole sound, in
  dB, before it is turned down.
- **Frequency**: where the S sounds start, in hertz.
- **Range**: the most an S is turned down, in dB.
- **Speed**: how quickly the sound comes back up after an S, in milliseconds.
- **Whole sound**: turns the whole sound down during an S, and not only the part above
  Frequency.
- **Sibilance only**: plays what is taken away.

**Effect ▸ Only what is removed** (`Alt+N`) switches **Sibilance only**.
