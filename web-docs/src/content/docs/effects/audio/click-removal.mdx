## What it does

Finds the short bursts a recording should not have, such as digital clicks, crackle and the
clicks of a mouth, and rebuilds each one from the sound either side of it. A burst longer
than **Maximum length**, like a drum or a consonant, is left alone.

The display above the controls draws the two seconds around the playhead and marks the
clicks it finds there. Under it is how many.

## What each control does

- **Type**: what is looked for. **Clicks** is single clicks. **Crackle** looks a second time
  for the small clicks the large ones hid. **Mouth clicks** looks at the sound above 2 kHz,
  with Maximum length at 3 milliseconds or more.
- **Sensitivity**: how readily a burst counts as a click.
- **Maximum length**: the longest a burst can be and still be a click, in milliseconds.
- **Widen**: how far either side of a click the repair reaches, in milliseconds.
- **Clicks only**: plays what is taken away.

**Effect ▸ Only what is removed** (`Alt+N`) switches **Clicks only**.
