## What it does

Five filters in a row, per channel. Band 1 starts as a high pass and band 5 as a low pass,
both switched off; bands 2 to 4 start as bells at no gain.

## What each control does

- **Band 1 on** to **Band 5 on**: 1 puts the band in the path, 0 takes it out.
- **Band 1 type** to **Band 5 type**: the band's shape: 0 bell, 1 low shelf, 2 high shelf, 3
  high pass, 4 low pass, 5 notch.
- **Band 1 frequency** to **Band 5 frequency**: the band's centre or corner, in Hz.
- **Band 1 gain** to **Band 5 gain**: the band's gain, in dB. A high pass, a low pass and a
  notch ignore it.
- **Band 1 Q** to **Band 5 Q**: how narrow the band's shape is.
- **Output**: a trim on the way out, in dB.
