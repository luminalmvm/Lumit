## What it does

Reshapes the waveform in one of five ways. Soft clip rounds the peaks over, hard clip cuts
them off and foldback turns them back down. Bit crush lowers the bit depth and rate reduce
lowers the sample rate. The shaping runs at four times the sample rate. Tone tilts the shaped
sound darker or brighter.

## What each control does

- **Drive**: how hard the sound is pushed into the shaper, in dB. On bit crush it lowers the
  bit depth instead, from 16 bits at 0 to 2 bits at the top. On rate reduce it lowers the
  sample rate, to 3,000 Hz at the top.
- **Shape**: 0 soft clip, 1 hard clip, 2 foldback, 3 bit crush, 4 rate reduce.
- **Tone**: a tilt over the shaped sound, in dB. Below 0 is darker, above 0 brighter.
- **Output**: the level on the way out, in dB.
- **Wet**: how much of the output is the shaped sound.
