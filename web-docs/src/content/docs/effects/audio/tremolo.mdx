## What it does

One gain moved by an oscillator. The top of the cycle is the sound's own level. At a Stereo
phase of 180 the two channels move opposite ways, which is an auto-pan.

## What each control does

- **Rate**: cycles a second.
- **Depth**: how far the level falls at the bottom of the cycle. At 100 the bottom is
  silence.
- **Shape**: 0 sine, 1 triangle, 2 square.
- **Stereo phase**: how far the right channel's cycle sits behind the left's.
