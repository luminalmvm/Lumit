## What it does

A resonant band-pass filter. Mode picks what moves it: an oscillator, or the level of the
sound, which is an auto-wah.

## What each control does

- **Mode**: 0 sweeps with an oscillator, 1 with the level of the sound.
- **Rate**: cycles a second, with Mode at 0.
- **Depth**: how far the sweep travels. At 100 the band moves two octaves either side of
  Frequency.
- **Frequency**: where the band rests, in Hz. The oscillator sweeps either side of it; the
  level sweeps up from it.
- **Resonance**: how narrow the band is.
- **Sensitivity**: how hard the level pushes the sweep, with Mode at 1.
- **Wet**: how much of the filtered sound is heard.
