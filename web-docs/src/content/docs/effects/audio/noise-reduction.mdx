## What it does

Turns down steady noise under a recording: hiss, a fan, the sound of a room. It works from a
**noise profile**, the level of the noise at every frequency, and turns each frequency down
by how much of it the profile accounts for.

With **Adaptive** on, or with nothing learned, the profile is found in the whole of the
layer's own sound, from its quietest moments at each frequency.

**Learn** measures the profile from a stretch of the sound and turns Adaptive off, as one
undo step:

- With a work area that covers only part of the layer, it reads the work area, up to 30
  seconds.
- Otherwise it reads the quietest second and a half in the 30 seconds around the playhead.

Effects above Noise reduction in the stack are run on the stretch first. The profile is kept
with the effect and saved in the project.

The display above the controls shows the sound arriving around the playhead, the profile,
the profile raised by **Threshold**, and what comes out. It reads **Measuring** while Learn
runs, **Learned** when the profile drawn is a learned one, and **Nothing to measure** when
the layer has no sound there.

## What each control does

- **Learn**: measure the noise over the work area, or the quietest stretch near the playhead.
- **Adaptive**: find the noise in the layer's own sound and not from a learned profile.
- **Threshold**: how far the profile is raised before the sound is held against it, in dB.
- **Reduction**: the most any frequency is turned down, in dB.
- **Smoothing**: how slowly the reduction follows the sound. Low leaves a watery, chirping
  residue. High leaves noise after a sound stops.
- **Tonal reduction**: how much further steady tones in the profile, such as a whine, are
  turned down, in dB.
- **Noise only**: plays what is taken away.

**Effect ▸ Learn noise** (`Alt+L`) presses **Learn**, and **Effect ▸ Only what is removed**
(`Alt+N`) switches **Noise only**.
