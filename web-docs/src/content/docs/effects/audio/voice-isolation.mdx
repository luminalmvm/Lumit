## What it does

A trained model hears the whole of the layer's sound and finds the speech in it. The speech
and everything else each get a level of their own.

The model is an **addon**, **DeepFilterNet3**, installed from **Settings ▸ Addons** with the
**Model runtime**. Without them the effect wears a badge naming what to install and leaves
the sound alone, in an export too. Installing them takes effect at once, sound already
loaded included.

More than an hour of stereo sound at 48 kHz, or as many samples at another rate, is
refused. The sound plays unchanged, and the badge reads **Too long for Voice isolation.
Split the layer.**

Moving a control does not run the model again.

## What each control does

- **Voice**: the level of the speech, in dB.
- **Background**: the level of everything else, in dB. At -60 it is taken out altogether.
- **Wet**: how much of the result is in the output. Below 100 the untouched sound is mixed
  in beside it.
- **Background only**: plays what is taken away.

**Effect ▸ Only what is removed** (`Alt+N`) switches **Background only**.
