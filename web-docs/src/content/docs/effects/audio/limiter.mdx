## What it does

Nothing comes out louder than the Ceiling. The detector hears the sound a Lookahead ahead of
the output, so the gain is down before a peak arrives, and the layer's sound is placed that
much earlier.

## What each control does

- **Ceiling**: the loudest the output may be, in dB.
- **Input**: gain into the limiter, in dB.
- **Lookahead**: how far ahead it hears, in milliseconds.
- **Release**: how fast it lets go once the loud part has gone, in milliseconds.
- **True peak**: 1 measures between the samples, at four times the rate. 0 measures the
  samples.
