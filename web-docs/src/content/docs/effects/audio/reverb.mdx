## What each control does

- **Room size**: how long the room holds on to the sound.
- **Damping**: how much top end each reflection loses.
- **Width**: how far apart the two channels' rooms are heard. At 0 the tail is mono.
- **Pre-delay**: the gap between the sound and the room answering it, in milliseconds.
- **High cut**: a low pass over the tail alone, in Hz.
- **Dry**: how much of the sound itself reaches the output.
- **Wet**: how much of the room reaches it.
