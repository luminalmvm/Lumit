## What it does

Turns the sound down once it is quieter than the **Threshold**. The gate shuts once the level
has fallen **Hysteresis** under the threshold and the **Hold** has run out. At the top of
**Ratio** the gate shuts; lower down it is an expander that leans on the quiet parts.

## What each control does

- **Threshold**: the level the gate opens at, in dB.
- **Ratio**: how steeply the quiet part is turned down, as n to 1. 1 is off.
- **Hysteresis**: how far under the threshold, in dB, the level falls before the gate may
  shut.
- **Attack**: how fast it opens once the sound arrives, in milliseconds.
- **Hold**: how long it stays open after the level drops, in milliseconds.
- **Release**: how fast it shuts once the hold has run out, in milliseconds.
- **Range**: the lowest a shut gate goes, in dB. 0 lets everything through and -100 is
  silence.
