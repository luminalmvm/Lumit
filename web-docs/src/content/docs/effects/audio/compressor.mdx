## What it does

Turns the sound down once it is louder than the **Threshold**. The detector hears the louder
of the two channels, so both are turned down together. **Lookahead** adds no delay: the
layer's sound is placed that much earlier.

## What each control does

- **Threshold**: where compression starts, in dB.
- **Ratio**: how much of what goes past the threshold comes back, as n to 1.
- **Knee**: the dB either side of the threshold the curve bends over.
- **Attack**: how fast the reduction deepens, in milliseconds.
- **Release**: how fast it lets go again, in milliseconds.
- **Makeup**: gain put back after the compression, in dB.
- **Detector**: what the detector listens to: 0 peak, 1 RMS.
- **Lookahead**: how far ahead the detector hears, in milliseconds.
- **Wet**: how much of the compressed sound is in the output. Below 100 the untouched sound
  is mixed in beside it.
