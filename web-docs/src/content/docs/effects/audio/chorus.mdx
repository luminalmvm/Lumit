## What it does

Copies of the sound are read from a delay line whose length an oscillator moves, and mixed
back in. Mode sets how far the sweep travels: far for a chorus, short for a flanger.

## What each control does

- **Mode**: 0 chorus, 1 flanger.
- **Rate**: cycles a second.
- **Depth**: how much of the mode's travel the sweep uses.
- **Delay**: the middle of the sweep, in milliseconds.
- **Feedback**: how much of the wet sound goes back into the line. Below 0 it goes back the
  other way up.
- **Voices**: how many copies each channel reads.
- **Spread**: how far apart in the cycle the copies sit.
- **Wet**: how much of the copies is heard. At 0 the sound comes through untouched.
