## What it does

Draws a flare from a design: a list of elements such as glows, rays, rings and ghosts, kept
in the effect and made in the **Flare** window. The rows below animate the whole flare. The
design itself does not animate.

Ghosts and other elements on the axis line up on the line from the light through the
**Centre**, so they swing across the frame as the light moves.

## What each control does

- **Edit flare…** opens the Flare window. See [The Flare window](#the-flare-window).
- **Light**: where the light is.
- **Light from**: **Point** is the Light row. **3D point** places the light at **Light x**,
  **Light y** and **Light z** in the composition's 3D space, seen through its camera. A
  light behind the camera draws nothing. **Lights** draws a flare for each Light layer in
  the composition, up to 16, and skips ambient lights. **Bright spots** draws a flare for
  each bright spot in the layer's own picture, up to 16. **Mask points** draws a flare on
  each vertex of the mask picked in **Mask**, up to 16, and follows the mask as it moves.
  The mask also cuts the layer unless its mode is **None**.
  **Points** draws a flare on each point of a points stream wired into the effect, up to
  16, in the point's colour and at its opacity.
- **Threshold**: how bright a spot must be to flare, in **Bright spots**.
- **Softness**: how far above **Threshold** a spot flares fully.
- **Mask**: the mask whose vertices flare, in **Mask points**. Unset is the layer's first
  mask.
- **Brightness**: the gain on everything the flare draws.
- **Size**: the size of the whole flare, in per cent.
- **Rotation**: turns the whole flare.
- **Colour**: multiplies the light's colour.
- **Evolution**: moves the shimmer of rays and the drift of orbs.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/).

The **Centre** group:

- **Centre X** and **Centre Y**: the point the axis runs through. A new Sprite flare puts it
  in the middle of the composition.

The **Flicker** group:

- **Amount**: how far the brightness dips at random. 0 is no flicker.
- **Speed**: dips a second.
- **Seed**: picks a different flicker.

The **Lights** group:

- **Use light colour**: each flare takes its light's colour.
- **Use light intensity**: each flare takes its light's intensity.
- **Size with distance**: a flare shrinks as its light moves away from the camera.

The **Hiding** group:

- **Hide behind**: a layer the light can go behind.
- **Channel**: reads that layer's **Alpha** or **Luma**.
- **Light size**: the size of the light, in pixels. A light half covered flares at about
  half.
- **Depth**: a layer read as distance. The light hides where it is nearer than **Light
  distance (m)**.
- **Light distance (m)**: how far the light is from the camera, in metres.
- **Edge fade**: how far past the frame's edge the light fades out. 0 never fades.

The **Textures** group:

- **Texture layer**: a layer whose picture elements can use as their texture.

When the layer and the composition have motion blur on, the flare blurs along the light's
path. **Bright spots** does not.

## The Flare window

**Edit flare…** opens the **Flare** window. On the left are the flare's elements, drawn
from the top of the list down. In the middle is a preview with the light, which can be
dragged, and a mark at the centre. Under it are the flares to start from, **Built in** and,
once any are saved, **Yours**. On the right are the controls of the selected element.

- **Add** adds an element.
- Drag an element to move it in the list. The eye shows or hides it. Alt-click the eye to
  show only that element in the preview.
- Double-click an element's name to rename it. Right-click for **Duplicate** and **Delete**.
- **Black** shows the flare on black. **Frame** shows it over the composition at the
  playhead.
- **Moving light** moves the light round the preview. **Still** stops it.
- **Fit** shows the flare at the preview's size. **100%** shows it at composition size.
- Click a flare under the preview to replace the elements with it.
- **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** undo and redo inside the window.

While the window is open the Viewer shows the flare as it is being edited.

| Element | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Glow** | A soft light round the source. |
| **Rays** | Many fine rays of varied length. |
| **Star** | A few bold points. |
| **Streak** | Anamorphic lines through the light. **End width** narrows them towards their ends. |
| **Ring** | A circle round the light. |
| **Halo** | An arc round the light that faces the centre and grows as the light moves out. |
| **Ghost** | One iris shape on the axis. **Notches** cuts a dent at each corner. |
| **Ghosts** | A train of iris shapes on the axis. |
| **Orbs** | Soft discs across the frame, lit by how near the light is. |
| **Dirt** | A texture across the frame, lit near the light. |
| **Picture** | One texture at a place on the axis. |

Every element has:

- **Size**, **Brightness** and **Rotation**.
- **Colour**: **Light** takes the light's colour. **Solid** is one colour. **Mixed** picks
  one of several for each copy. **Gradient** runs from the first colour in the middle to the
  second at the edge. **Rainbow** is a spectrum across the element.
- **Place**, on axis elements: 0 is the light, 1 is the centre, 2 is the light mirrored
  through the centre.
- **Response**: **Brighter near** makes the element brighter as the light nears **The
  centre** or **The edges**. **Amount** is how much, **Range** how far the change spans.
- **Ignore**: the element keeps its own size, brightness or rotation when the effect's
  rows change them, or doesn't fade at the frame's edge.

**Textures** are **Dust**, **Smudge**, **Caustic**, **Rings**, **Specks**, **Hex bokeh**,
**Scratches**, **Rainbow**, **Water drops**, **Grain** and **Layer**, the picture of the
**Texture layer**. The preview draws nothing for **Layer**.

- **Import…** opens a `.flare` file. A file that is not one is refused: "Not a flare file".
- **Share…** offers **Export to a file…**, which writes a `.flare` file, and **Copy as text**.
- **Cancel** closes the window and changes nothing.
- **Save to your flares** saves it to your own flares, offered in every project.
- **Use flare** puts the flare on the effect. It is kept in the effect and saved with the
  project.
