## What it does

A very simple lens flare effect. Generally recommended to use the physically based lens flare for high quality flares.

## What each control does

- **Light x** and **Light y**: where the light is positioned.
- **Intensity**: the gain on the lens flare.
- **Tint**: the colour of the flare.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).

The **Glow** group:

- **Glow size**: its radius, in pixels.
- **Glow intensity**: its gain.

The **Ghosts** group:

- **Ghosts**: how many discs march along the axis.
- **Ghost spacing**: how far apart they sit.
- **Ghost size**: their base radius.
- **Ghost intensity**: their gain. 

The **Streak** group:

- **Streak length**: how far it reaches from the light.
- **Streak intensity**: its gain.
- **Streak angle**: direction it runs in.
