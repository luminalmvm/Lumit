## What it does

The outline of a shape gets warped by fractal noise .

## What each control does

- **Edge type**: **Roughen** for a soft irregular outline, **Cut** for hard edges, 
  **Spiky** for sharp points.
- **Border**: intensity of how much is removed from the edges.
- **Edge sharpness**: how crisp the edge is.
- **Fractal influence**: how far the noise moves the outline.
- **Scale**: the scale of the noise.
- **Offset x** and **Offset y**: adjusts the position of the noise.
- **Complexity**: how many octaves of detail are summed in the noise.
- **Evolution**: changes the phase of the noise.
- **Cycle evolution** and **Cycle**: make Evolution loop after a whole number.
- **Colour edge** and **Edge colour**: paint the roughened edge in one colour.
- **Seed**: randomises the noise pattern used.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
