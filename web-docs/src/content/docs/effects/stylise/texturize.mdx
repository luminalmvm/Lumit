## What it does

Uses another layer as a texture to look as if the source layer has been printed on it.

## What each control does

- **Texture**: the layer to be pressed into this one.
- **Light direction**: direction from where the light falls across the relief.
- **Relief**: how far apart two points being compared are.
- **Texture contrast**: how strongly relief shows.
- **Placement**: what happens *outside* a copy of the texture. **Stretch** holds
  its edge outward, **Tile** repeats the texture, **Centre** leaves the rest of the frame
  untextured.
- **Scale**: large a copy of the texture is.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
