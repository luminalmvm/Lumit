## What it does

The frame is cut into a grid of rectangles and every one is painted a single
colour. It is the pixelation effect.

## What each control does

- **Horizontal blocks** and **Vertical blocks**: how many blocks span the frame
  across and down.
- **Sharp colours**: off, each block is the average of what is under it; on, each
  block is its centre pixel's colour.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
