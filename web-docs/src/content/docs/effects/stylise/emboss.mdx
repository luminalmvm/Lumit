## What it does

The picture is treated as a surface with height and lit from one direction.
Resulting in a stamped-metal look.

## What each control does

- **Direction**: Direction light applied from.
- **Relief**: distance between neighbours pixels compare again.
- **Contrast**: the gain on the relief.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
