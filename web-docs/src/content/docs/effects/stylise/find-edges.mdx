title: Find edges
description: "Draws the picture as a line drawing of its own edges — dark lines on white, or glowing lines on black."

Find edges is a **Stylise** effect.

## What it is for

Everywhere the picture changes quickly — an outline, a rim, a hard shadow — a
line is drawn. Everywhere it is flat, nothing is. What comes back looks like a
pencil drawing of the shot: a white page with the edges inked on it.

Turn **Invert edges** on and it flips: black page, glowing coloured lines. That
is where the neon and the blueprint looks start, and it is what you screen or add
back over the original frame for a rimlit, electrified version of it.

## What each control does

- **Invert edges** — off (the default, and After Effects') the frame is white
  with dark lines on it; on, black with bright ones.
- **Mix** — how much of the drawing replaces the original. Halfway is a sketched
  overlay on the real picture.
- **Matte** and **Invert** — drive the effect from another layer's brightness;
  see [Effects](/use/effects/). Note that the Matte row's **Invert** and the
  effect's own **Invert edges** are two different switches.

## Notes

**It has no threshold and no radius, on purpose.** Find edges draws every edge it
can see, at full strength. If the result is too busy, the fix is upstream: put a
[Median](/effects/stylise/median/) or a [Blur](/effects/blur-sharpen/blur/)
before it to take the fine detail out, and the lines that survive are the ones
that matter. That reads better than any threshold this effect could offer,
because you can see what you are removing.

**Each colour channel gets its own lines.** An edge between two colours of the
same brightness — red against green, say — still draws, and it draws in the
colour of the difference. That is why the inverted version comes back with
coloured lines rather than white ones.

**The lines land where a person would draw them.** Lumit works in scene-linear
light, where the step from a bright sky to a brighter one is a bigger number than
the step from a shadow to a slightly-lit shadow, though the eye sees the second
and not the first. The differences are therefore measured on a perceptual scale,
which is what stops the effect drawing a map of the highlights and nothing else.

**Transparency is untouched.** A drawing of a title comes back as a drawing of a
title, not as a full white frame.

**For a cartoon look**, stack it: [Posterize](/effects/colour/posterize/) for the
flat areas, Find edges inverted for the outlines, and combine the two with a
[blend mode](/use/blend-modes/).
