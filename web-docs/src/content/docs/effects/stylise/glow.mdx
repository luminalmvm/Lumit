## What it does

Parts of the image brighter than the threshold emit a glow.

## What each control does

- **Threshold**: how bright a pixel must be before it blooms.
- **Softness**: how abruptly a pixel starts to bloom as it crosses the threshold.
- **Radius**: how far the bloom spreads.
- **Falloff**: how hard the bloom gathers into a bright core. At 0 it spreads evenly.
- **Amount**: how far the bloom's colours are pulled apart.
- **Angle**: the direction they are pulled apart in.
- **Wavelength** spreads the bloom's colours as one gradient instead of three taps.
- **Samples** is how many taps fill the gradient while Wavelength is on.
- **Colour 1**, **Colour 2** and **Colour 3** tint the three taps, the first one way along
  Angle and the third the other.
- **Intensity**: how intense the bloom is.
- **Tint**: the colour of the bloom. Neutral keeps each highlight's own colour.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
