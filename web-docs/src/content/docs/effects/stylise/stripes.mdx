title: Stripes

Stripes is a **Stylise** effect.

## What it does

Reads a band of pixels through **Centre** and repeats it along **Angle**. At a **Width** of
1 every pixel of the line is pulled out into a streak.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Control | Range | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Centre** | Point | Any position | 960, 540 | Pixels at composition size |
| **Angle** | Dial | Any angle | 0° | Degrees |
| **Width** | Slider | 1 to 1000; any value by typing | 1 | Pixels at composition size |
| **Side** | Dropdown | Both sides · Front · Back | Both sides | - |

## What each control does

- **Centre**: a point on the band that is read.
- **Angle**: the direction of the streaks. 0° runs left and right.
- **Width**: how wide the repeated band is.
- **Side**: which side of the line is drawn over. **Front** is the side Angle points to.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
