## What it does

Turns the layer into a sheet of holographic foil. The layer's alpha is the sheet's outline,
and its colour is printed on the foil as **Print** says.

The foil is modelled as it is made: fine grooves pressed into aluminium under a clear
lacquer. Each light splits into a rainbow at the angle the grooves send it, the flat metal
mirrors the lights and a soft grey studio, and the lacquer adds its own gloss. The lights
stay fixed to the viewer and the sheet tips under them: as **Tilt x** and **Tilt y**
change, each rainbow moves across the sheet.

<Shot file="holographic-foil-tilt.webp" caption="The effect as it starts, with Tilt y at -40, -32, -25, -18, -11, -4 and 3." />

The sheet has a real width and is seen from a real distance. The result is scene-linear
light, brighter than white in the glints.

The model of diffraction foil follows the open-source holospot kit by Dmitry Kurash (MIT
licensed), reimplemented for Lumit. It also uses Wyman, Sloan and Shirley's colour-matching
fit, Brian Karis's environment BRDF fit, Sergei Winitzki's inverse error function, Inigo
Quilez's hexagon and Voronoi edge distances, Jarzynski and Olano's pcg3d hash, the Khronos
PBR Neutral curve and AgX as published for three.js.

## What each control does

### Material

**Material** is what the foil is made of. **Auto** on **Lighting** and on **Tint** follows
it.

| Material | The foil | Auto lighting | Auto tint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Prism** | One grating across the whole sheet, as on a card or a compact disc. | Studio | Clear |
| **Shatter** | Cracked shards, each with its own grooves and its own tilt. They flash one by one. | One spot and a broad tungsten panel | Gold |
| **Glitter** | Flakes in a binder over a dark ink, with larger stars among them. | Two spots over a dim studio | Blue |
| **Sparkle** | Dots that share one grating, on matte metal. | Two wide spots over a pale studio | Sky blue |
| **Vortex** | A spiral of grooves, crossed by bands of plainer foil. | Studio's three spots and a broad tungsten panel | Pale gold |

### Print

**Print** is what the layer's own colour does. The layer's alpha is the sheet's outline
whichever is chosen.

| Print | The layer's colour |
| --- | --- |
| **Ink over foil** | Ink on the foil. White leaves the foil bare, a colour tints it and black hides it. |
| **Foil only** | Ignored. The whole sheet is foil. |
| **Opaque print** | Printed on white paper that covers the foil. Foil shows where the **Foil mask** is white. With no Foil mask it shows where the picture itself is bright. |

<Shot file="holographic-foil-print.webp" caption="A printed card on Ink over foil, Foil only, Opaque print, and Opaque print with a Foil mask that is white round the border." />

### Tilt

- **Tilt x** tips the top edge toward the viewer.
- **Tilt y** turns the face toward the right.
- **Spin** turns the grooves within the sheet.
- **Auto tilt** is how far the sheet wobbles by itself. Straight grooves swing mostly about
  their own axis. Every other pattern traces a slow ellipse.
- **Auto tilt speed (Hz)** is how many times a second the wobble swings. It runs on the
  layer's own time.
- **Follow layer rotation** adds a 3D layer's own rotation, its parents' and the active
  camera's view to the tilt. A 2D layer's rotation leaves the tilt alone.
- **Motion blur** is how much of each frame the colours blur over while the sheet or the
  lights move. 0 is off. It follows this row alone, not the layer's motion blur switch.

Turned past edge-on, by **Follow layer rotation** or by a tilt with **Auto tilt**, the
sheet shows its plain back: satin grey metal, with no rainbow and no print. Its alpha is
unchanged.

### Sheet

- **Sheet width (mm)** is how wide the layer's picture is in the world. Shards, flakes,
  dots, relief and the bevel are measured on it.
- **View distance (m)** is how far the eye is from the sheet. Nearer gives more bands.

### Grating

- **Pitch (nm)** is the groove spacing. Wider packs the colours nearer the glint.
- **Groove angle** is which way straight grooves run, from the sheet's x axis.
- **Pattern**: **Linear** is straight grooves, **Rings** grooves in circles like a compact
  disc, **Sunburst** grooves that run out from the centre. **From map** follows the
  **Direction map**, and is Rings until one is named.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y** place the centre of the rings, the sunburst and the vortex.
- **Depth (nm)** is how deep the grooves are. Deeper moves light from the mirror into the
  rainbow.
- **Coverage** is how much of the sheet carries the grating. The rest is plain mirror.
- **Fan** is how far each band spreads along the grooves.
- **Softness** is how rough the metal is. It softens the bands and the glints.
- **Orientation jitter** is how far the grooves stray from their direction.
- **Pitch jitter** is how far the groove spacing strays. It softens the hues.
- **Lacquer index** is the refractive index of the lacquer over the grooves.

### Tint

**Tint** is a dye in the lacquer: **Clear**, **Gold**, **Blue**, a **Custom** dye, or
**Auto** for the dye of the Material.

- **Passes** is which side of its edge a custom dye lets through: **Long waves** or
  **Short waves**.
- **Edge (nm)** is where a custom dye turns.
- **Edge width (nm)** is how gradually it turns.
- **Floor** is how much it lets through on its blocked side.

### Lights

Three spots, a panel and a soft grey studio stand round the viewer and stay there. The
sheet tips under them.

**Lighting** picks the rig.

| Lighting | The rig |
| --- | --- |
| **Studio** | Three spots and a long panel over a dim studio. |
| **Spot** | One spot in a dark room. |
| **Softbox** | One square panel. |
| **Strip** | One long, thin panel. |
| **Dark** | No lamps. The studio's rims and fill alone. |
| **Desk** | A tungsten lamp and a daylight window. |
| **Office** | One fluorescent strip overhead. |
| **Raking** | One spot far to the side, at a grazing angle. |
| **Custom** | The rows of **Spot A**, **Spot B**, **Spot C**, **Panel** and **Surroundings**. They start as the Studio rig. |
| **Auto** | The rig of the Material. |

<Shot file="holographic-foil-lights.webp" caption="Prism under Studio, Spot, Softbox and Strip, then Dark, Desk, Office and Raking." />

**Rig rotation** turns every light and the studio about the view axis. Keyframe it to
swing the whole rig. A single light keyframes under **Custom** alone, by its **Direction
x** and **Direction y**.

Each of **Spot A**, **Spot B**, **Spot C** and **Panel**:

- **On** lights the sheet with it.
- **Direction x** and **Direction y** are where the light is as seen from the sheet, from
  -1 to 1 across and up. At 0, 0 it is where the viewer is.
- **Size** is how wide a spot looks from the sheet.
- **Width** is how wide the panel looks along the line out from the view axis, and
  **Length** how long it looks across that line.
- **Intensity** is how bright it is.
- **Distance (m)** is how far it is from the sheet.
- **Spectrum** is the colour of its light: **Equal**, **Daylight**, **Tungsten**, **LED**
  or **Fluorescent**.

The **Surroundings** group is the studio:

- **Rims** is two thin strips low at the sides, for the edges to catch.
- **Fill** is a broad soft light overhead.
- **Scrim** is a pale sheet above the viewer. Bare foil reads grey under it.
- **Room** is the faint light everywhere else in front.

### Surface

- **Coat roughness** is how rough the clear coat is. Low is a sharp gloss.
- **Relief** is the fine relief pressed into the surface: **Scratches**, **Brushed**,
  **Hammered**, **Crinkled**, **Linen**, **Fingerprint**, **Sandblast** or **None**.
  **Bevel from alpha** presses the sheet's own outline into it instead.
- **Strength** is how strongly the relief bends the surface.
- **Scale** is how many times the relief repeats across the sheet.
- **Relief on** is which layer the relief bends: the **Foil** under the lacquer, or the
  **Varnish** on top alone.
- **Invert** turns grooves into ridges.

<Shot file="holographic-foil-relief.webp" caption="The reliefs under the Spot rig: Scratches, Brushed, Hammered and Crinkled, then Linen, Fingerprint, Sandblast and None." />

- **Bevel width (mm)** is how far in from the outline the bevel's wall reaches.
- **Bevel height (µm)** is how high the bevel stands.
- **Profile** is the wall's shape: **Round**, a flat **Bevel**, a **Dome** that rises from
  the edge, or **Deboss**, pressed in.
- **Softness** is how much the outline is rounded before the wall is shaped.

The bevel bends the foil and the varnish together. A stroke thinner than the wall stays
level along its middle. It looks the same at every preview resolution and export size.

A very wide wall is narrowed until its blur is a quarter of the sheet's longer side. On a
sheet 90 mm on its longer side that is about 41 mm at **Softness** 20 and 25 mm at 100,
and half as much for **Dome**.

<Shot file="holographic-foil-bevel.webp" caption="Bevel from alpha on a text layer: Round, Bevel, Dome and Deboss." />

- **Waviness** is how far broad waves in the sheet tip it.
- **Orange peel (µm)** is how rough the varnish's fine ripple is.
- **Lines** embosses fine lines across the sheet, on the layer **Relief on** names:
  **Lines** one way, **Crosshatch** both.
- **Line pitch (mm)** is the spacing of the lines, and **Line height (µm)** their depth.
- **Bow (mm)** is how far the sheet bows in its middle. **Bow axis** bows it **Across** its
  width or **Along** its height.
- **Wear** adds handling marks: smudges, hairline scratches round the highlights, dust and
  patchy foil.

### Maps

Each of the five rows names a layer, and Lumit renders that layer on its own. The layer
can be hidden.

- **Height map**: the layer's brightness is the height of the surface. It takes the place
  of **Relief**, **Bevel from alpha** included. **Strength**, **Relief on** and **Invert**
  still apply. White letters on black emboss the letters into the foil.
- **Repeats** is how many times the Height map repeats across the sheet.
- **Wrap** is how the Height map meets itself where it repeats: **Repeat** as it is,
  **Mirror** turned over at each join, **Clamp** held at its edges.
- **Foil mask**: white shows foil and black shows print. Over an **Opaque print** of a
  photograph, a text layer sets its words in foil. Under **Ink over foil** black is ink on
  white paper, and under **Foil only** white paper alone.
- **Direction map** steers the grooves of Prism and Sparkle while **Pattern** is **From
  map**. A logo's outlines stand out as bands of their own.
- **Reading**: **Angle** takes the Direction map's brightness as the groove angle, black 0
  degrees and white 180. **Along edges** runs the grooves along the edges in it, and
  leaves rings where it is flat.
- **Pitch map** sets the groove spacing: black keeps **Pitch (nm)** and white gives
  **Pitch at white (nm)**. A text layer draws its letters in another colour of the same
  rainbow.
- **Environment** is a layer the foil mirrors in place of the studio. A photograph shows
  in the plain mirror between the bands.
- **Amount** is how much of the mirror the Environment takes. At 100 it takes the studio's
  place. Above 100 it is brighter.
- **Placement**, on the first four rows, is where the layer's picture sits on this one.
  **As placed in comp** reads it where the layer sits in the composition. **Stretch to
  fit** stretches it over this layer. **Centre** centres it at its own size.

The Direction map, its **Placement** and **Reading** are greyed unless **Pattern** is From
map and **Material** is Prism or Sparkle. The Pitch map, its **Placement** and **Pitch at
white (nm)** are greyed on Shatter and Glitter.

<Shot file="holographic-foil-maps.webp" caption="Top: one text layer as the Height map, as the Foil mask of an Opaque print, and as the Direction map. Bottom: a logo as the Direction map, the text layer as the Pitch map, and a photograph as the Environment." />

### Output

- **Exposure** is a gain on the light, in stops.
- **Highlights** is how light above white is rolled off: **Neutral** and **AgX** bring it
  under white, **Off** keeps all of it.
- **Quality**: **Draft** draws two orders of rainbow in place of three, fewer cells of the
  panel, and one flake or dot lit on its own in each pixel in place of two. **High** adds
  cells to the panel and a third order to each flake.
- **Seed** picks another layout of shards, flakes, dots, relief and wear.

Glare, grain and focus are not part of the effect. [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/),
[Add grain](/effects/generate/add-grain/) and
[Depth of field](/effects/blur-sharpen/depth-of-field/) after it on the layer, in that
order, add them. With **Highlights** on **Off**, Glow reads the glints at their full
brightness.

The rows every effect ends with:

- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** scales **Coverage** for each pixel: white keeps the grating, black leaves plain
  mirror foil. **Invert** reads it the other way round.
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.

## Speed and colour depth

The effect is worked out on the graphics card. The times here were measured on an RTX 5080
on Windows.

- The first frame of each Material waits while its shader is built: about 4 seconds for
  Prism and Vortex, 5 for Shatter and 9 for Glitter and Sparkle. The other Materials are
  then built in the background, for about a minute.
- **Wear**, **Bevel from alpha** and a Height map, Direction map, Pitch map or Environment
  layer use a larger shader, built the same way. One that a frame needs before the
  background reaches it takes up to 15 seconds.
- At **Normal** a 1920×1080 frame takes 3 to 9 ms and a 3840×2160 frame 12 to 32 ms.
  **Draft** is 1.3 to 1.9 times as fast.
- A still sheet is drawn once and cached for the layer's span. **Auto tilt** above 0 draws
  every frame. So does **Motion blur** with **Follow layer rotation** on, or with keyframes
  on a tilt, **Spin**, **Rig rotation** or a light's direction.
- Glitter whose flakes are smaller than a pixel reads up to 15 per cent brighter or darker
  at a lower preview resolution.
- In a project at 8 bpc [colour depth](/use/colour-management/#colour-depth) nothing is
  brighter than white, the glints included.
