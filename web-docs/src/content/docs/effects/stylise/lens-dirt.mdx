## What it does

Dirt on the glass of a lens: dust, grit, hairs, smudges, haze, scratches and water. The dirt
only shows where light from the bright parts of the picture reaches it. The glass is always out
of focus by the **F-stop**: wide open a speck of dust is a large faint disc in the iris's
shape, stopped down a small bright one. Droplets bend the picture behind them.

The pattern comes from the **Seed**, sized against the frame, so every preview resolution
shows the same dirt.

## What each control does

- **Intensity**: how bright the lit dirt is.
- **F-stop**: how far out of focus the glass is. In a [node graph](/use/node-graphs/), wire
  Depth of field pro's **F-stop** here to follow it.
- **Focus distance (m)**: where the lens is focused. Focusing close brings the dirt a little
  sharper. Wire Depth of field pro's **Focus distance (m)** here to follow it.
- **Dirt scale**: the size of the pattern.
- **Glass curvature**: how far the glass bulges. The dirt crowds towards the edges of the frame
  as the glass turns away, and spreads out on dished glass below 0. Type up to 2 for the hard
  bend of a fisheye's front glass. Wire Depth of field pro's
  **Glass curvature** here to follow its lens.

The **Dirt** group:

- **Dust**, **Grit**, **Hairs**, **Smudges**, **Haze**, **Scratches** and **Droplets**: how
  much of each is on the glass. Dust sits on several surfaces, so its discs come in many
  sizes and turn into cat's eyes towards the frame's edge. Grit and hairs sit near the
  sensor and stay sharp. Fingerprint ridges in the smudges show only when stopped down.
- **Refraction**: how hard droplets bend the picture behind them.

The **Iris** group shapes the out-of-focus dust:

- **Blades** is how many straight edges the iris has.
- **Roundness** blends the blades towards a circle.
- **Rotation** rotates the iris.

The **Light** group:

- **Threshold**: the brightness above which the picture lights the dirt.
- **Spread**: how far across the glass a light reaches.
- **Ambient**: lights all of the glass, whatever the picture.

The **Colour** group:

- **Colour**: **Single** gives all the dirt the **Tint**. **Gradient** gives each speck a
  colour picked at random between **Tint** and **Second tint**.
- **Colour variation**: how far each speck's hue and brightness stray either side of its
  colour. Smudges and haze vary slowly across the glass instead.

Additional properties:

- **Display**: **Final** is the picture with its dirt, **Dirt alone** the lit dirt over
  black, to lay over something else. In a node graph each has an output socket of its own;
  see [node graph compositions](/use/node-graphs/).
- **Seed**: picks a different pattern.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
