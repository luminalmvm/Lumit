## What it does

Lays a soft band of light through the middle of the picture, broken up by noise that drifts
with time. The light is screened on, and nothing is added where the layer is transparent.

## What each control does

- **Amount** is how strong the light is.
- **Colour** is the light at the heart of the band.
- **Edge colour** is the light at the band's fringe.
- **Angle** sets which way the band lies. At 0° it is upright.
- **Size** is how wide the band is, as a percentage of the picture's height.
- **Drift** is how fast the light changes.
- **Seed** chooses which pattern of light is drawn.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.

## Presets

On the effect's menu, under **Presets**. The same names are the looks of **Light leak ▸**
in the **Hits** group of the [Transition menus](/use/cutting/#fades-and-dissolves).

- **Light leak** is the effect as it starts.
- **Film burn** sets **Amount** to 200, **Size** to 110 and **Drift** to 2.5.
