## What it does

Soft pools of light are laid over the picture, warm where a pool lands and cool
between them, and the contrast is lifted under them. The pools are a noise field,
repeatable from a seed. **Drift** moves through the field rather than redrawing
it. **Style** sets the shape of the pools.

## What each control does

- **Style**: **Amorphous** is soft round pools. **Digital** breaks them into square
  blocks an eighth of **Scale** across. **Streaks** stretches them into horizontal
  bands four times **Scale** long and a third of it tall.
- **Intensity**: how far the light pulls the picture either side of neutral. At 0
  only **Contrast** is left.
- **Light colour**: the colour of the light inside a pool. A mid grey changes
  nothing.
- **Shade colour**: the colour of the light between the pools. A mid grey changes
  nothing.
- **Scale**: the size of one pool.
- **Drift**: one full turn moves the pools on by one of their own widths.
- **Contrast**: a scale about mid-grey, as [Contrast](/effects/colour/contrast/)
  applies it.
- **Seed**: which arrangement of pools this instance lights with.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
