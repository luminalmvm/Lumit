## What it does

Light streams out of the picture's bright parts, away from **Source point**, as through haze or
dust: a logo with light behind it, sun through trees, a sweep across a title. Each pixel gathers
the picture along the line back towards the source point, so whatever is bright throws a streak
outward that fades as it goes.

The rays are gathered in four passes, each reaching eight times as far as the one before, so
**Ray length** costs nothing: long rays take the same time to draw as short ones. The source point
can sit off the layer, and it moves smoothly by fractions of a pixel.

## What each control does

### Pre-process

- **Threshold** keeps only the parts brighter than it, by how much brighter they are, so a picture
  throws rays from its highlights alone. It reads the lightness as a picture shows it.
- **Use mask** lets only a circle around the source point throw rays: **Mask radius** is its size
  and **Mask feather** how far beyond the radius it fades out.

### Source point and Ray length

- **Source point** is where the rays start. Drag it in the Viewer. It can lie off the picture, as
  far beyond each edge as the picture is wide or tall. The picture is the layer, and with
  **Extend beyond layer** on the room round it too.
- **Ray length** is how far the rays reach, in their own distance from the source: at 4, something
  bright throws its ray out to five times as far from the source as it is. It does not change how
  long a frame takes.
- **Extend beyond layer** gives the layer room around it for the rays to reach into, so the rays
  of a text or shape layer are not cut off at its bounding box. The room is as far as the longest
  ray reaches, up to the picture's diagonal on each side; turn it off on a full-frame layer, where
  the frame cuts the rays anyway.

### Shimmer

The shimmer changes the ray length around the source, so the rays seem to move in and out and
sideways.

- **Amount** is how much the length changes, and **Detail** how fine the changes are.
- **Phase** moves the shimmer. Each full turn is a new shimmer; animate it to make the light play.
- **Source point affects shimmer** moves the shimmer as the source point moves, as light through real
  haze would. **Radius** is how far the source point travels for the shimmer to change right
  through: low is fast, high is slow. If a logo flickers, **Reduce flicker** keeps only the
  coarse shimmer and moves it half as fast.
- **Use loop** makes the shimmer repeat every **Revolutions in loop** turns of Phase. For a loop
  four seconds long with two turns, key Phase at 0° at the start and 720° four seconds later, set
  Revolutions in loop to 2, and leave the last frame out of the loop.

### Boost light

**Boost light** brightens the rays. High enough, it burns the picture out to white, which makes a
transition.

### Colourise

- **None** keeps the picture's own colours, for light through stained glass or out of a coloured
  logo.
- **One colour** colours the rays **Colour**, brighter where they are brighter.
- **3-colour gradient** colours the rays by how bright they are, from **Shadows** through
  **Midtones** to **Highlights**, fading to black below and to white above.
  **5-colour gradient** adds **Mid-low** and **Mid-high** between them.
- Only the colour rows a choice uses show: **Colour** for One colour; **Highlights**,
  **Midtones** and **Shadows** for the gradients and the colourings; **Mid-high** and
  **Mid-low** as well for 5-colour gradient, **Dawn**, **Isotope** and **Thermal**.
- The colourings below them fill the colours in for you: **Ember**, **Rust**, **Acid**, **Lagoon**,
  **Arc**, **Orchid**, **Plum**, **Dusk**, **Rose** and **Twilight** are three-colour; **Dawn**,
  **Isotope** and **Thermal** are five-colour. Editing any colour after picking one puts the menu
  back to the plain gradient, since the colours are no longer that colouring's. A colour with
  keyframes keeps them.
- **Based on** is what the colours are picked by: **Lightness**, **Luminance**, **Alpha**, one of
  **Red**, **Green** or **Blue**, or **Alpha edges**, the edges of the layer's alpha, as wide as
  **Edge thickness**, so a solid logo throws rays only from its outline.

### Compositing

- **Source opacity** is how much of the picture shows under the rays, and **Rays opacity** how
  strong the rays are.
- **Blend mode** is how the rays go onto the picture. **None** shows the rays alone, **Normal** lays
  them over it, and the rest work as the layer modes of the same name.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.
