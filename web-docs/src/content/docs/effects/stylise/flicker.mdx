## What it does

Raises and lowers the layer's brightness at random, frame by frame. The same flicker can move
the contrast and the opacity with it, and wander each colour channel on its own. The pattern
comes from the seed and the layer's time, and a frame draws the same flicker every time.

## What each control does

- **Amount** is how far the light moves. The four rows below it each take a share of it.
- **Speed** is how many flickers there are a second.
- **Seed** chooses which pattern of flicker is drawn.
- **Motion**: **Smooth** eases from one flicker to the next. **Hold** keeps each one until
  the next, then jumps.
- **Time offset** moves the pattern earlier or later, in seconds.
- **Bias** below 0 makes the flicker mostly dip below the layer's own light, and above 0 mostly
  flash above it. At 0 it goes both ways evenly.
- **Light** is how much the brightness flickers.
- **Colour** is how much each colour channel flickers on its own.
- **Contrast** is how much the contrast flickers, about a mid grey.
- **Opacity** is how much the layer fades out on a dip. It never goes above the layer's own
  opacity.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Blend**: the blend mode the result is laid over the original with.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
- **Channel**: the channel of the Matte layer that is read.

Position and blur do not flicker.

## Presets

On the effect's menu, under **Presets**.

- **Neon tube** sets **Amount** to 70, **Speed** to 20, **Motion** to **Hold** and **Bias**
  to -70.
- **Failing light** sets **Amount** to 100, **Speed** to 4 and **Bias** to -90.
- **Film projector** sets **Amount** to 12, **Speed** to 24 and **Contrast** to 40.
- **CRT** sets **Amount** to 10, **Speed** to 50, **Motion** to **Hold** and **Colour** to 30.
