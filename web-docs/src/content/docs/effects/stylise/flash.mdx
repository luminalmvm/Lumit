## What it does

Puts a burst of colour over the picture and clears it again. Can be automated based
on beat markers.

## What each control does

- **Mode**: Manual for keyframed hits, Trigger for one flash per beat marker,
  Strobe for one every so many beats.
- **Trigger**: Manual mode's hits. 
- **Duration**: how long a beat-driven flash lasts.
- **Shape**: change how the flash should or shouldn't fade.
- **Every Nth beat**: in Strobe mode, how many beats to skip between flashes.
- **Phase offset**: shifts beat-driven flashes earlier or later.
- **Intensity**: scales the intensity of the flash.
- **Colour**: what colour the flash is. 
- **Decay**: how quickly a Manual hit fades.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
