Flash is a [Stylise](/effects/stylise/) effect.

## What it is for

Puts a burst of colour over the picture and takes it away again. The white flash
on a kick drum is half the genre, and this is the effect that does it.

It works three ways, chosen by **Mode**. **Manual** is keyframed: you place hits
yourself on the Trigger control and each one decays away. **Trigger** takes its
hits from the composition's beat markers, so a whole sequence flashes in time
with the music without a single keyframe. **Strobe** does the same but fires only
every so many beats, for a slower pulse over a fast tempo.

The beat modes need beat markers to exist. Detect them from the audio first — see
[Markers](/use/markers/) — and with none present the effect simply stays dark
rather than complaining.

The flash is added in linear light, so a bright flash genuinely blows the
picture out rather than washing it toward grey, and anything downstream that
reads highlights — a [Glow](/effects/stylise/glow/), for instance — will see it.

## What each control does

- **Mode** — Manual for keyframed hits, Trigger for one flash per beat marker,
  Strobe for one every so many beats.
- **Trigger** — Manual mode's hits. Each keyframe on it is a hit at that
  strength, and the flash falls away from there. This control does nothing in the
  two beat-driven modes.
- **Duration** — how long a beat-driven flash lasts, in frames. Short is a snap;
  long enough and the flashes start to overlap into a constant wash.
- **Shape** — Hard holds the flash at full strength for its whole duration and
  then cuts, which is the aggressive, strobing option. Fade ramps it down across
  the same span, which reads as a bloom of light rather than as a switch.
- **Every Nth beat** — in Strobe mode, how many beats to skip between flashes. Two
  halves the rate, four quarters it. It has no effect in the other modes.
- **Phase offset** — shifts beat-driven flashes earlier or later, in frames.
  Use it when the detected beat sits fractionally off where the hit actually
  feels, which is common where the snare sits lazily behind the beat.
- **Intensity** — a scale on the whole envelope, whichever mode produced it. This
  is the master strength control, and it can be pushed past full for a flash that
  blows well past white.
- **Colour** — what colour the flash is. White is the default hit; a colour is how
  you tie a flash to a palette, and since it is scene-linear it can be set
  brighter than white.
- **Decay** — how quickly a Manual hit falls away, in milliseconds. Short is a
  snap, long is a lingering pulse. It applies to Manual mode only; the beat modes
  use Duration and Shape instead.
- **Mix** — how much of the flashed picture replaces the original.
