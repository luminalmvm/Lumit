## What it does

A blur that extends around a centre point, or blurs directed outwards from the 
centre point.

## What each control does

- **Amount** is the peak blur. Pixels nearer the centre get proportionally less.
- **Centre X** and **Centre Y** place where the spin or zoom originates.
- **Type**: Spin revolves the centre. Zoom extends out from the centre point.
- **Edges**. Similar to Repeat edge pixels from other effects with one more option.
  Mirror reflects the picture at the edges to blur with.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
