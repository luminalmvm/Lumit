Sharpen is a [Blur & sharpen](/effects/blur-sharpen/) effect.

## What it is for

The blunt one. It exaggerates the difference between each pixel and its four
neighbours, which crisps the picture without any of the judgement
[Unsharp mask](/effects/blur-sharpen/unsharp-mask/) applies.

Use it when you want a quick crispening and you are watching the result — a
touch on a graphic, a lift on a layer that has been scaled down, a last nudge at
the top of a stack. When the footage is noisy or heavily compressed, use Unsharp
mask instead: this effect has no threshold, so it sharpens grain as
enthusiastically as it sharpens detail.

It leaves transparency alone and never invents dark detail at the frame's
border.

## What each control does

- **Amount** — how far the difference from the neighbours is exaggerated. At zero
  the picture passes through untouched, and the useful range is short: this
  control climbs into crunch quickly, which is the honest character of a plain
  sharpen.
- **Radius** — how far out the four neighbours are taken, in whole pixels. One
  pixel sharpens the finest detail there is. Larger distances skip past fine
  texture and lift coarser structure instead, which is a way to crisp a picture
  that has already been scaled up without emphasising its softness.
- **Mix** — how much of the sharpened picture replaces the original.
