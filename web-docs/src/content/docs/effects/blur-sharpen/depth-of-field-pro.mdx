## What it does

A depth of field blur traced through a real lens prescription. The out-of-focus shape
changes across the frame and through focus as that lens's does: cat's eyes towards the
corners, bright or soft edges either side of focus, and coloured fringes from the glass.
Something near and blurred goes soft at its edges over what is behind it, which shows
through, the way a real lens sees a little round it.
Bright pixels are drawn as whole bokeh shapes.

**Depth** reads distance in metres from a Precomp with 3D layers in it, and any other
layer as a depth pass from 0 to 1. Without it, the whole frame sits at **Farthest (m)**.

## What each control does

- **Depth** is how far away each pixel is. A Precomp with 3D layers in it gives distance
  in metres. Any other layer is a depth pass, 0 at **Nearest (m)** and 1 at **Farthest
  (m)**.
- **Depth channel** selects the channel read as depth.
- **Invert** reads the depth pass inverted.
- **Lens** picks a bundled lens. A lens chosen in the lens window replaces it.
- **Edit lens…** opens the lens window. See [The lens window](#the-lens-window).
- **F-stop** is the working aperture. It cannot open wider than the lens.
- **Focus distance (m)** is where the lens is focused, in metres.
- **Use focus point** with **Focus point x** and **Focus point y** focus on the depth
  under that spot.
- **Sensor** is the sensor size the footage was shot on. A smaller sensor sees less of
  the lens's edge and blurs less.
- **Blur size** scales every blur after the optics.
- **Colour fringing** scales how much the glass splits colour.
- **Glass dirt** smears and specks the front glass. It shows as streaks inside the
  out-of-focus highlights.

The **Iris** group shapes the aperture:

- **Blades** is how many straight edges the iris has.
- **Roundness** blends the blades towards a circle.
- **Rotation** rotates the iris.

The **Highlights** group:

- **Highlight threshold** is the brightness above which a pixel is drawn as its own bokeh
  shape.
- **Highlight boost** multiplies what is above the threshold.

The **Depth map** group:

- **Nearest (m)** is the distance a depth of 0 means.
- **Farthest (m)** is the distance a depth of 1 means.

Additional properties:

- **Display**: **Final** is the blur, **Depth** the distance in metres it used.
- **Zebra**: stripes over what blurs less than a pixel. They are part of the picture, in
  exports too.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte**, **Invert** and **Channel** limit the effect to where another layer is bright.

In a [node graph](/use/node-graphs/), **Depth** is a socket that takes a depth or a
picture, the **Depth** view hands on the distance, **Focus distance (m)** hands out where
the lens is focused, for Lens flare's **Focus distance (m)** to follow, **F-stop** its
aperture, for Lens dirt's, and **Glass curvature** how far the lens's front glass bulges,
for Lens dirt's too.

## The lens window

**Edit lens…** opens the same window on Depth of field pro and on Lens flare. It lists the
bundled lenses, your own lenses, and the lens kept in the effect. **Import…** opens a
`.lens` file.

Every surface of the open lens can be changed: radius, thickness, index, Abbe number,
diameter, coating, and which surface is the stop.

- **Use lens** puts the lens on the effect. An edited or imported lens is kept in the
  effect and saved with the project.
- **Save to your lenses** saves it to your own lenses, offered in every project. Bundled
  lenses are never changed.
