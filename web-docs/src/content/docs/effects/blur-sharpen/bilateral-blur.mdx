---
title: Bilateral blur
description: "Smooths areas of similar colour and keeps the edges between them sharp."
---

Bilateral blur is a **Blur & sharpen** effect.

## What it does

Blurs each pixel with its neighbours, each neighbour counting less the more its colour
differs. It is drawn across, then down.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Control | Range | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Radius** | Slider | 0 to 100; any value by typing | 8 | Pixels at composition size |
| **Tolerance** | Slider | 1 to 100 | 20 | - |

## What each control does

- **Radius**: how far the blur reaches.
- **Tolerance**: how much a colour can differ and still count as similar.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the strength of the effect from another layer's
  brightness; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
