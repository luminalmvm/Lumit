## What it does

A blur along a single line. 

For a smear that follows visual motion in the footage, use
[Fast motion blur](/effects/temporal/fast-motion-blur/).

- **Matte** is an input to an effect, scaling the strength. **Invert** inverts the mattes for calculating strength.

## What each control does

- **Length** is the length of the blur.
- **Angle** adjusts the angle of the blur.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the blur amount from another layer's brightness, 
  scaled by the blur amounts you set; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
