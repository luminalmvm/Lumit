## What it does

A blur that streaks each pixel along a direction read from a map. The map is the
**Matte** layer, or this layer's own picture when **Matte** is unset.

## What each control does

- **Vectors from**: **Map slope** streaks along the contours of one channel of the map,
  longest where it changes fastest. **Map channels** reads two channels as a push on each
  axis, with mid-grey still.
- **Slope channel**: the channel whose contours the streaks follow.
- **Horizontal channel** and **Vertical channel**: the channels that push sideways and up
  and down.
- **Length** is the longest streak.
- **Angle offset** turns every streak. 90° streaks across the contours instead of along
  them.
- **Constant length** gives every streak the full **Length**.
- **Follow the vectors** bends each streak along the map. Off keeps it straight.
- **Direction**: **Both ways** centres the streak on its pixel. **Forwards** trails it
  along the vectors, **Backwards** against them.
- **Falloff** fades the streak towards its end.
- **Map softness** blurs the map before its vectors are read.
- **Map placement**: where the map sits on this layer. **As placed in comp** reads it
  where the Matte layer sits in the composition. **Stretch to fit** stretches it over
  this layer. **Centre** centres it at its own size.
- **Edges**: what a streak reads past the frame.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Invert** reads the map the other way round.
