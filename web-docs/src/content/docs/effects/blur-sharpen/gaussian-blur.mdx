## What it does

Applies an even blur across the whole image.

In the Effect Controls panel:

- **Matte** scales the blur radius per pixel: white blurs at the full Radius, grey blurs narrowly, black not at all — a blur whose width varies across the frame, which a strength dissolve cannot produce. **Invert** inverts the mattes for calculating strength.

## What each control does

- **Radius** adjusts the size of the blur.
- **Mix**: how much of the result replaces the original.
- **Matte** and **Invert** drive the blur amount from another layer's brightness, 
  scaled by the blur amounts you set; see [Effects](/use/effects/).
