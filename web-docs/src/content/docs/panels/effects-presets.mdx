Every available effect can be found in this panel grouped by category. Within a category
the effects are in alphabetical order, here and in the **Effect** menu.
Saved presets are listed above the categories.

Open it from **Window ▸ Panels ▸ Effects & presets**, or press `Ctrl+5`.

| Group | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Favourites** | The starred effects and presets. Drawn once something is starred. |
| **Saved presets** | The presets in the preset library, in their groups, and the presets that come with Lumit under **Film**. See [Effect presets](/use/presets/). |
| **Graphics templates** | The library of [graphics templates](/use/graphics-templates/#the-library), as tiles. |
| The categories | The effects that ship with Lumit, and the effects of installed [extensions](/use/extensions/). [Drivers](/use/nodes/#drivers) are under **Controls**. |
| A plugin's own heading | [OpenFX plugins](/use/plugins/), under the grouping each declares. One that declares none is under **Plugins**, and [audio plugins](/use/audio-plugins/) are under **Audio plugins**. |

A click on a heading folds it. Right-click any heading for **Collapse** or **Expand**,
**Expand all** and **Collapse all**.

## Search

The box at the top filters effects by their name or their heading's, and presets by their
name or their group's. It also finds an effect by the After Effects or plugin name it is
imported from, such as Deep Glow or S_Glow for Glow. While it holds text every heading
with a match is open. `Ctrl+F` puts the cursor in it while the panel is focused. With
nothing found the panel reads **No effects match**.

## Apply one

Drag an effect or a saved preset onto a layer in the Timeline, onto
[Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), onto the [node graph](/use/nodes/) or onto a
clip in the Cut timeline.

A double-click, or **Add to selected** on the right-click menu, applies it to the clip
selected in the Cut timeline, or else to every selected layer as one undo step.

## Save and load a preset

The two marks beside the search box are **Load preset…**, which applies a preset file
from anywhere on disk to the primary selected layer, and **Save preset…**, which saves
that layer as one. Both are greyed with no layer selected. The right-click menus of
**Saved presets** and of the empty panel hold the same two. See
[Effect presets](/use/presets/).

## An effect's menu

Right-click an effect:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add to selected** | Applies the effect. Greyed with no layer or clip selected. |
| **Favourite** | Stars the effect, or takes the star off. |
| **Manual page** | Opens the effect's page in this manual. |
| **From an OpenFX plugin**, **From an audio plugin**, **From an extension** | A line saying where an effect that is not Lumit's own came from. |
| [**Switch this plugin off**](/use/plugins/#switch-one-off) | On a plugin. |

## Groups

Saved presets are grouped by the folders they sit in inside the preset library. Right-click
**Saved presets** or a group for **New group**, **Rename…**, **Show in folder** and **Delete**.
Deleting a group deletes the presets in it, and asks first when there are any. Drag a preset
onto a group to move it there, or right-click it and choose **Move to**. A preset's own menu
also has **Add to selected**, **Favourite**, **Rename…**, **Show in folder** and **Delete**,
which asks first. The presets that come with Lumit can't be renamed, moved or deleted.

A preset that carries transform keys or text animators wears a key mark.

## Favourites

The star beside an effect or a preset makes it a favourite. A row that is not a favourite
shows its star while the pointer is over it. The star by the search box lists the
favourites alone, and is greyed until there are some. Favourites are kept between launches.

## After Effects presets

**Settings ▸ General ▸ After Effects presets ▸ Import** turns every `.ffx` in a folder,
and the folders inside it, into a Lumit preset listed here, grouped by those folders. A
preset with no effects, or named like one already in its group, is skipped. The same preset
left at the top by an earlier import moves into its group. **Load preset…** and
**Animation ▸ Apply animation preset…** also open an `.ffx` directly.

| In the preset | Arrives as |
| --- | --- |
| An effect Lumit has | That effect, with its values and keyframes |
| A pseudo effect | [Custom controls](/effects/controls/custom-controls/), under the same name |
| Any other effect | A placeholder that keeps its values and draws nothing |
| An expression | The same [expression](/use/expressions/#what-javascript-can-read) |
| An expression over keyframes | The keyframes |

A layer's own transform, masks and text animators in a preset are not read.
