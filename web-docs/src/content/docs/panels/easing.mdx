The Easing panel holds one curve shape and applies it to the selected keyframes.
**Window ▸ Panels ▸ Easing** opens it, as does **Easing…** on the bottom bar of the
Timeline's [Graph mode](/panels/graph-editor/).

## The box

The box draws the travel between two keyframes, from the first at the bottom left to the
second at the top right. The diagonal is a straight move.

- Drag either handle to bend the curve. `Shift` snaps the handle to twentieths of the box,
  and `Alt` mirrors the other handle round the middle.
- A handle stays inside the box sideways. It can go half a box above the top or below the
  bottom, which makes the move overshoot.

The strip under the box plays the shape. Pointing at it plays it again.

The four numbers under it are the two handles' places in the box, to type or drag: how far
across and how far up for the handle leaving the first keyframe, then the same for the
handle arriving at the second.

Under the numbers:

| Button | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Read from keyframes** | Loads the shape of the first selected span into the box. |
| **Reverse** | Plays the shape backwards: a slow start becomes a slow finish. |
| **Apply as you shape** | While on, letting go of a handle, typing a number or pressing Reverse applies the shape at once. |
| **Save…** | Keeps the shape in the box under a name. |
| **More** | **Import easings…** and **Export easings…**. |
| **Apply** | Puts the shape on the selection. |

Right-click the box for **Apply**, **Apply in only**, **Apply out only**, **Copy as
cubic-bezier**, **Paste**, **Reverse**, **Save…** and **Reset**. **Paste** takes any four
numbers on the clipboard, written as `cubic-bezier()` or bare. **Reset** puts the box back
to Easy ease.

## The shapes

Each tile draws its curve, and its name shows beside **Apply** while the pointer is over
it. Click a tile to load its shape into the box and apply it to the selection.
`Shift`+click eases only the side coming in to each span's second keyframe, and
`Ctrl`+click only the side going out of its first. The other side keeps what it had.

| Shapes | |
| --- | --- |
| **Easy ease** | Slow at both ends. The shape `F9` gives. |
| **Slow start**, **Slow finish** | Slow at one end and straight at the other. |
| **Heavy ease** | Slow at both ends, more strongly. |
| **Snap** | Away fast, then a long glide in. |
| **Sine**, **Quad**, **Cubic**, **Quart**, **Expo** | Each as **in**, **out** and **in out**. In is a slow start, out a slow finish. |
| **Back in**, **Back out**, **Back in out** | Pulls back before it sets off, overshoots its end, or both. |

Right-click a tile for **Apply**, **Apply in only**, **Apply out only**, **Copy as
cubic-bezier** and **Save…**.

## Apply

**Apply** puts the shape in the box on the selection. A span between two keyframes takes
the shape when both of its ends are selected, so a run of selected keyframes is eased from
end to end, and one selected keyframe on its own takes nothing. One press is one undo
step, however many layers the selection crosses.

The selection is the Timeline's, in Layers mode or Graph mode. The panel keeps its shape
while the selection changes.

Apply is greyed while the graph editor shows the **Speed** or the **Acceleration** graph,
and with no Timeline in the arrangement. A click on a tile then only loads its shape.

## Save a shape

**Save…** asks for a name and keeps the shape in the box. A name already taken gets a
number after it. Once a shape is saved, the shapes sit in two tabs, **Built in** and
**Yours**.

The menu of one of your own tiles has **Rename** and **Delete** in place of **Save…**.

Saved shapes are kept with your settings, not in the project. **Export easings…** writes
them to a `.json` file, and **Import easings…** adds a file's shapes after yours, numbering
a name already taken.

## One keyframe's numbers

While exactly one keyframe is selected, the panel shows the frame it sits on and its
eases, to type or drag:

- **Speed in** and **Speed out**: how fast the value is changing as it arrives and as it
  leaves, in the property's units per second.
- **Influence in** and **Influence out**: how far each handle reaches towards the
  neighbouring keyframe, from 0.1% to 1000%.
- **Continuous** keeps the speed out equal to the speed in. Typing either speed sets both.

The first or last keyframe of a curve shows only the side it has.

## As a popup

With **Shape eases in a popup** on in **Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Panels**, **Easing…** opens
the same editor as a popup over the bottom bar. It stays open across **Apply**, and closes
on **Close** or a click outside it.
