The **Audio** workspace puts the **Mixer** on the left, the **Audio** panel on the right and
the **Audio timeline** across the bottom. All three show the active composition.

## Audio panel

The Audio panel holds the output's levels, the selected layer's sound and **Beats**, down a
column.

### Levels

Two bars meter the composition's output, left and right, over a scale in dB. Each draws its
average level, its peak behind it, and a hold line that rests on the loudest peak. The number
beside a bar is its peak in dB.

A lamp lights under the numbers when the limiter has held the mix back. Clicking it puts it
out.

Under the scale, the output's loudness:

- **S**: short-term loudness, the last three seconds, in LUFS.
- **I**: integrated loudness, everything played since it was reset, in LUFS. Clicking it
  resets it.
- **TP**: the loudest true peak since then, in dB.

They follow ITU-R BS.1770 and EBU R128. Loading another composition starts them again.
Played slowed down, nothing is measured.

Under them, a graph draws the short-term loudness of the last 30 seconds of playing, with the
[loudness target](/use/mixing/#loudness) as a line across it. **Reset loudness** clears it.

Right-click the bars, or any meter in the Mixer:

- **Peak hold ▸**: **Off**, **3 s**, **10 s** or **Until reset**. 3 s by default.
- **Reset peaks**: clears the hold lines and puts the limiter's lamps out.
- **Reset loudness**: starts the integrated loudness and true peak again.

### Selected layer

The first selected layer's name, then its sound. With no layer selected the section is gone.
Right-click the name for the [same menu as a track head](#audio-timeline).

- **Volume**: from −60 to +12 dB, with a stopwatch.
- **Pan**: from −100 (left) to 100 (right), with a stopwatch and a dial.
- **Fade in** and **Fade out**: the length of the fade at each end, in seconds, and its shape
  as three curves: **Ease**, **Linear** or **Exponential**. A fade is two Volume keyframes. A
  curve on an end with no fade makes one half a second long. A length of 0 removes it.
- **Drive with audio…**: lists the free number parameters on the layer's effects. Picking one
  wires [Audio level](/effects/drivers/audio-level/), Remap and Smooth into it, in the
  [node graph](/use/nodes/), as one undo step.
- **Lower behind…**: a popover listing the composition's other layers that make a sound, and
  its buses, then **Amount**, **Threshold**, **Attack**, **Release** and **Remove** once one
  is picked. See [Lower behind](/use/mixing/#lower-behind). While it is set, the source and
  the amount stand under the buttons, and a click on them opens the popover.

**Drive with audio…** with no free parameter says *No free number parameters on this layer's
effects.* A chain the layer refuses says *The audio chain could not be built on this layer.*
**Lower behind…** with no other layer says *No other layer to lower this one behind.*

A layer with no sound keeps only the two buttons.

### Beats

**Generate beat markers** on the heading places a [beat marker](/use/markers/) on each beat
it finds, replacing the composition's beat markers with a new set. The twirl opens what it
uses.

- **Source**: **This comp** listens to the composition's mix. The list also names each layer
  that makes a sound.
- **Sensitivity**: from 0 to 100, 50 by default. Higher finds more beats.
- **Range**: **Whole comp**, or **Work area** for only the beats inside the work area.
- **Min spacing**: a beat closer than this to a louder one is dropped, in milliseconds.
  120 by default; 0 keeps every beat.
- **BPM**: the tempo the beats are snapped to. At 0 the detection estimates it and the well
  reads **Auto**. After a run the well shows the tempo used.
- **Tap**: each press is a beat. The tempo is the median gap of the last nine presses, held
  between 30 and 300 BPM. A pause of two seconds starts the count again.
- **‹** and **›**: with a tempo set, move the markers the next run places 10 ms earlier or
  later. The total shows beside them.
- **Clear generated**: removes the beat markers and keeps the ones placed by hand. Shown
  while there are any.

The status line says how many markers were placed, and the tempo when there is one. A run
with nothing sounding in the source is refused, and the status line says so.

## Mixer

The Mixer has a strip for each layer of the composition that makes a sound, in layer order,
then the **buses** after a hairline, and the **Master** strip at the right. Pressing anywhere
on a strip selects its layer, and Ctrl or Shift adds it to the selection. The mouse wheel
scrolls the strips sideways. Routing, solo, Gain and automation are on
[Mixing](/use/mixing/).

With no layer that makes a sound the panel reads **No sound**, with **Insert audio layer** and
**Insert bus** under it.

Each strip carries, top to bottom:

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Underlined in the layer's label colour, with the bus glyph before a bus's. Double-click renames the layer. |
| **Gain** pot | Drag up or down to set Gain, from −24 to +24 dB. Double-click returns it to 0 dB. Not on a bus. |
| **Pan** pot | Drag up or down to pan. Double-click centres it. |
| Inserts | The audio effects, a slot each. A click brings Effect controls to the front on the effect. The empty slot adds one. |
| Sends | A slot per send: the bus's name, **Pre** on a pre-fader send, and a bar dragged sideways to set its level. Double-click the bar for 0 dB. The empty slot lists the buses to send to. Shown while the composition has a bus. |
| Automation mode | A chip beside the clip lamp naming the strip's mode. A click picks another. |
| Fader | Drag up or down to set Volume. Double-click returns it to 0 dB. While playing it follows the keys. |
| Meters | Left and right, on the fader's scale, green up to −18 dB, amber to −6 dB and red above. |
| Clip lamp | Over the meters. Lights when the strip's sound reaches full scale, until clicked. |
| dB well | The fader's value. Click to type one. |
| Mute, **S**, **fx** | The layer's audible, solo and effects switches. **fx** shows when audio effects are switched on in the layer's stack. |
| Output | A chip naming where the sound goes: **Master** or a bus. A click lists the buses, and **New bus**. |

Each pot's caption reads its value while it turns. `Ctrl` gives finer steps on a pot, the fader and a send bar, and `Esc` during a drag puts the
value back. A thin bar beside the meters shows how far Lower behind, or on the Master the limiter,
holds the sound down.
When the panel is short, the inserts and the sends fold to one row each that counts them and
opens them in a popover.

The fader's scale runs from silence at the bottom to +12 dB at the top, with 0 dB two thirds
of the way up. A fader or pot on an
animated Volume or Pan sets the keyframe at the playhead.

The Master strip has a **LIM** lamp, which lights when the limiter has held the mix back and
goes out when clicked, its own automation mode chip, and a fader that sets the gain of the
whole mix and can be keyed. A slim bar beside its meter draws the short-term loudness, with
the [loudness target](/use/mixing/#loudness) as a mark across it. Its loudness numbers stand
beside the meter while the Mixer has room:
**M**, **S**, **I**, **TP** and **LRA**, with the target beside **I**. When it has no room, the integrated loudness stands alone under the dB well.

Right-click:

| On | Commands |
| --- | --- |
| Strip | The [track head's menu](#audio-timeline). It acts on every selected strip when the strip is one of them. |
| Fader | **Reset volume**, **Set level…**, and **Add keyframe** or **Remove keyframe** at the playhead. |
| Pan pot | **Reset pan**, **Set pan…**, **Full left**, **Full right**, and **Add keyframe** or **Remove keyframe**. |
| Insert | **On**, **Remove**, **Move up**, **Move down**. |
| Send | **Pre-fader**, **Post-fader**, **Mute send**, **Remove send**, and **Show bus**, which brings the bus's strip into view. |
| Meters | **Peak hold ▸**, **Reset peaks**, **Reset loudness**. |
| Master | **Reset volume**, **Set level…**, **Loudness target ▸**, **Normalise mix…**, **Peak hold ▸**, **Reset peaks**, **Reset loudness**. |
| Empty ground | **Insert audio layer**, **Insert bus**, **Unmute all**, **Clear solos**, **Reset all faders**, and **Automation ▸**, which sets the mode of every strip. |

With the Mixer active, these keys act on the selected strips:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Left`, `Right` | Selects the previous or next strip. |
| `Up`, `Down` | Raises or lowers the fader by 0.5 dB. With `Shift`, by 0.1 dB. |
| `Home` | Returns the fader to 0 dB. |
| `M` | Mutes or unmutes. |
| `S` | Solos or unsolos. |
| `Alt`+`S` | Solos the strip alone. |
| `A` | The next automation mode. |

## Audio timeline

The Audio timeline lists the layers of the composition that make a sound, as **tracks**,
over the same time as the [Timeline](/panels/timeline/). It keeps its own zoom, scroll and
twirls.

Each track carries mute, solo and effects switches, its
[automation mode](/use/mixing/#automation), a twirl, its number, its label colour and its
name. A click on the mode picks another. A bus's track carries the bus glyph before its name,
takes no clips, and draws the sound routed into it, through each source's fader and send
level, before its own effects and fader.

- The twirl opens the track's lanes, **Volume**, **Pan** and a **Send to** lane for each
  send, then its **Effects**.
- **Search tracks** lists only the tracks whose names contain what is typed.
- `Enter` renames the selected track while the panel is active.
- The switch at the right of a track's head, shown with the pointer on the head, changes its
  lane between **Wave** and **Spectral**.
- Drag the lower edge of a track to make it two to eight rows tall. Double-click the edge to
  put it back to two.

Pressing a head selects its track, and Ctrl or Shift adds it to the selection. Right-click a
head for the commands below. They act on every selected track when the track is one of them.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename** | Turns the name into a field. |
| **Mute**, **Solo**, **Solo safe**, **Bypass effects**, **Lock** | The track's switches. See [Solo and mute](/use/mixing/#solo-and-mute). |
| **Reset volume**, **Reset pan** | Back to 0 dB, and to the centre. On an animated row, at the playhead. |
| **Reset gain** | Gain back to 0 dB, when it is off 0 dB. |
| **Normalise**, **Normalise loudness ▸** | As on a clip, for every clip on the track. **Normalise** sets the Gain of a layer that is one file. |
| **Normalise together**, **Normalise loudness together ▸** | With several tracks selected, one change for all of them. See [Gain and Volume](/use/mixing/#gain-and-volume). |
| **Detect beats** | Places beat markers from this track alone. |
| **Volume envelope**, **Pan envelope** | Shows or hides the line across the track, as in the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/#envelopes). `Shift`+`V` and `Shift`+`P` do the same on the selected tracks. |
| **Lane ▸** | **Wave** or **Spectral**. |
| **Select layer** | Selects every clip on the track. |
| **Move up**, **Move down** | Moves the track past its neighbour. |
| **Minimise all tracks** | Back to two rows, every track. |
| **Reset height** | Back to two rows, when a track's edge has been dragged. |
| **Output ▸**, **Send to ▸**, **Mute send ▸** | Where the sound goes. See [Routing](/use/mixing/#routing). |
| **Automation ▸** | The track's automation mode. |
| **Lower behind ▸** | The layer or bus this track is lowered behind. See [Lower behind](/use/mixing/#lower-behind). |
| **Detach audio** | Moves the sound of a layer with a picture to an Audio layer of its own. |
| **Add audio effect ▸** | Adds to the track's stack. |
| **Duplicate**, **Label colour ▸**, **Delete** | As on any layer. |

Right-click the column header for **Lane ▸** on every track and **Reset all heights**.

Right-click the empty ground under the tracks for **Insert audio layer**, **Insert bus**,
**Paste**, **Select all** and **Add marker here**. With no tracks, **Insert audio layer** is
on the lane. A new track goes above the selected one, or at the bottom.

A layer with a picture as well as sound is drawn faded. Its menu has **Rename**, **Detach
audio**, **Lock** and **Delete**. Its **Detach audio** switch moves its sound to an Audio layer
of its own and mutes the original, which leaves the list.

### Lanes

A lane draws its value as a line across time, over the track's wave, between the layer's in
and out points. It is two rows tall until its lower edge is dragged. Its head has the
stopwatch, the name and **Pre** or **Post** on a send's lane on the first row, and the keyframe
navigator and the value at the playhead on the second. The line takes the gestures of an
[envelope](/use/cutting/#envelopes), and a box dragged on the lane picks keys. A lane's
Volume runs on the fader's scale. A send's lane has **Mute send** on its right-click, and is
dimmed while the send is muted.

While the composition plays, a lane's line and its field record on a track whose mode is not
**Read**, and the stretch being written is drawn in the error colour. See
[Automation](/use/mixing/#automation).

### Master

The **Master** track is pinned at the foot of the list. Its lane draws the composition's mix,
and its twirl opens a **Volume** lane for the master fader, keyed in composition time. Its
head carries the master's automation mode. Right-click it for **Automation ▸**, **Reset
volume**, **Loudness target ▸** and **Normalise mix…**. See [Loudness](/use/mixing/#loudness).

### Clips

An Audio layer is a track of one clip until the first edit on it turns it into a
[Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) of clips.
Clicking a clip selects it and its track, and Ctrl or Shift adds it to the selection. The body
of a clip drags it along its track or to another, and either edge trims it.

With the pointer on a clip, its effects switch, add-effect button and twirl show beside its
name, with its gain line when it is at 0 dB.

With the pointer on a track, each clip shows a handle at its top corners. Dragging one sets
the length of the fade at that end, and dragging it back to the corner removes the fade.

Right-click a clip. The commands act on every selected clip when the clip is one of them.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Split** | Cuts the clip at the playhead. `Ctrl`+`K` does it too. Disabled with the playhead off the clip. |
| **Trim to playhead ▸** | Brings the clip's start or end to the playhead, with or without closing the room. |
| **Transition ▸** | **Fade in ▸** or **Fade out ▸** sets the shape of the fade: **Linear**, **Fast**, **Slow**, **Smooth** or **Sharp**. |
| **Normalise** | Sets each clip's gain so its loudest sample lands at **Normalise to** in [Settings](/use/settings/#audio), −1 dB unless changed. Each by its own loudest sample, as one undo step. On a track's head, every clip on it. A layer that is one file sets its [Gain](/use/mixing/#gain-and-volume). |
| **Normalise loudness ▸** | Sets each clip's gain so its measured loudness lands at −14, −16 or −23 LUFS. On a track's head, every clip on it. |
| **Normalise together**, **Normalise loudness together ▸** | With several clips selected, moves them all by one change, so the loudest lands on the target and the rest keep their balance. |
| **Reset gain** | Puts each clip's gain back at 0 dB. Disabled while none is off 0 dB. |
| **Keep pitch** | Whether the track's sound keeps its pitch when it plays faster or slower. |
| **Lane ▸** | **Wave** or **Spectral**. |
| **Bypass effects** | Switches off the clip's effects, when it has any. |
| **Add audio effect ▸** | Adds to the clip's stack. |
| **Take ▸** | Picks a take, or deletes the one showing, when the clip has several. |
| **Audio streams ▸**, **Split audio streams**, **Merge audio streams** | As on a layer, when the clip's file has more than one [audio stream](/use/audio/#audio-streams). |
| **New composition from clip** | Makes a composition of the clip and opens it. |
| **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Duplicate** | **Paste** places at the playhead, on this track. **Duplicate** places a copy after the clip, or past the end of the track where something is in the way. |
| **Paste attributes** | Puts the copied clip's effects, levels, fades and speed on the selected clips. |
| **Group**, **Ungroup** | Joins two or more selected clips, or lets one go. |
| **Select to end** | Selects this clip and those after it on the track. |
| **Select layer** | Selects every clip on the track. |
| **Clip colour ▸** | The track's colour, or one of the label colours. |
| **Delete clip** | Removes the clip and leaves a gap. |
| **Ripple delete** | Removes the clip and moves what follows back. `Shift`+`Delete` does it too. |

An Audio layer not yet cut has **Split**, **Transition ▸**, **Keep pitch**, **Lane ▸**,
**Clip colour ▸** and **Delete**.

Right-click a gap on a track for **Paste**, **Select all**, **Close gap** and **Add marker
here**.

Right-click a fade, or an overlap between two clips, for the same five shapes and
**Custom…**, which opens the fade editor. A shape picked on an overlap goes on both sides of
it. **Remove fade in** or **Remove fade out** takes away a fade with no overlap.

Right-click a keyframe for the [menu every keyframe has](/use/keyframes/), less **Ease…**.

`Delete` removes the selected keyframes, or the selected clips when no keyframe is selected.
`Ctrl+X`, `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+V` cut, copy and paste them.
