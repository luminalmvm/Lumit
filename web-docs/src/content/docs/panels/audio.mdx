The **Audio** workspace puts the **Mixer** on the left, the **Audio** panel on the right and
the **Audio timeline** across the bottom. All three show the active composition. Each is also
under **Window ▸ Panels**, and `Ctrl+4` shows and hides the Audio panel.

## Audio panel

The Audio panel holds the output's levels, the selected layer's sound and **Beats**, down a
column.

### Levels

Two bars meter the composition's output, left and right, over a scale in dB. Each draws its
average level, its peak behind it, and a hold line that rests on the loudest peak. The number
beside a bar is its peak in dB.

A lamp lights under the numbers when the limiter has held the mix back. Clicking it puts it
out.

Under the scale, the output's loudness:

- **S**: short-term loudness, the last three seconds, in LUFS.
- **I**: integrated loudness, everything played since it was reset, in LUFS. Clicking it
  resets it.
- **TP**: the loudest true peak since then, in dB.

They follow ITU-R BS.1770 and EBU R128. Loading another composition starts them again.
Played slowed down, nothing is measured.

Under them, a graph draws the short-term loudness of the last 30 seconds of playing, with the
[loudness target](/use/mixing/#loudness) as a line across it. **Reset loudness** clears it.

Right-click the bars, or any meter in the Mixer:

- **Peak hold ▸**: **Off**, **3 s**, **10 s** or **Until reset**. 3 s by default.
- **Reset peaks**: clears the hold lines and puts the limiter's lamps out.
- **Reset loudness**: starts the integrated loudness and true peak again.

### Selected layer

The first selected layer's name, then its sound. With no layer selected the section is gone.
Right-click the name for the [menu a track has](#a-tracks-menu).

- **Volume**: from −60 to +12 dB, with a stopwatch.
- **Pan**: from −100 (left) to 100 (right), with a stopwatch and a dial.
- **Fade in** and **Fade out**: the length of the fade at each end, up to 60 seconds, and its
  shape as three curves: **Ease**, **Linear** or **Exponential**. A fade is two Volume
  keyframes. A curve on an end with no fade makes one half a second long. A length of 0
  removes it.
- **Drive with audio…**: lists the free number parameters on the layer's effects. Picking one
  wires [Audio level](/effects/drivers/audio-level/), Remap and Smooth into it, in the
  [node graph](/use/nodes/), as one undo step.
- **Lower behind…**: a popover listing the composition's other layers that make a sound, and
  its buses, then **Amount**, **Threshold**, **Attack**, **Release** and **Remove** once one
  is picked. See [Lower behind](/use/mixing/#lower-behind). While it is set, the source and
  the amount stand under the buttons, and a click on them opens the popover.

Right-click the **Volume** or the **Pan** row for the menu a
[property row](/panels/timeline/#right-click-menus) has in the Timeline.

**Drive with audio…** with no free parameter says *No free number parameters on this layer's
effects.* A chain the layer refuses says *The audio chain could not be built on this layer.*
**Lower behind…** with no other layer says *No other layer to lower this one behind.*

A layer with no sound keeps only the two buttons.

### Beats

**Generate beat markers** on the heading places a [beat marker](/use/markers/) on each beat
it finds, replacing the composition's beat markers with a new set. The twirl opens what it
uses.

- **Source**: **This comp** listens to the composition's mix. The list also names each layer
  that makes a sound. A layer named here is heard whatever its audible and solo switches say.
- **Sensitivity**: from 0 to 100, 50 by default. Higher finds more beats. Right-click it for
  **Reset**, **Copy value** and **Paste value**.
- **Range**: **Whole comp**, or **Work area** for only the beats inside the work area.
- **Min spacing**: a beat closer than this to a louder one is dropped, from 0 to 1000 ms.
  120 by default; 0 keeps every beat.
- **BPM**: the tempo the beats are snapped to, up to 300. At 0 the detection estimates it and
  the well reads **Auto**. After a run the well shows the tempo used.
- **Tap**: each press is a beat. The tempo is the median gap of the last nine presses, held
  between 30 and 300 BPM. A pause of two seconds starts the count again.
- **‹** and **›**: with a tempo set, move the markers the next run places 10 ms earlier or
  later. The total shows beside them.
- **Clear generated**: removes the beat markers and keeps the ones placed by hand. Shown
  while there are any.

The status line says how many markers were placed, and the tempo when there is one. A run
with nothing sounding in the source is **refused**, and the status line says so.

**Composition ▸ Detect beats** runs the same detection on the whole mix, at sensitivity 50,
over the whole composition, with no minimum spacing and the estimated tempo. See
[beat markers](/use/audio/#beat-markers).

## Mixer

The Mixer has a strip for each layer of the composition that makes a sound, in layer order,
then the **buses** after a hairline, and the **Master** strip at the right. It is also beside
the Cut timeline in the **Cut** workspace. Pressing anywhere on a strip selects its layer, and
`Ctrl` or `Shift` adds it to the selection. The mouse wheel scrolls the strips sideways.
Routing, solo, Gain and automation are on [Mixing](/use/mixing/).

With no layer that makes a sound the panel reads **No sound**, with **Insert audio layer** and
**Insert bus** under it.

### A layer's strip

Each strip carries, top to bottom:

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Underlined in the layer's label colour, with the bus glyph before a bus's. Double-click renames the layer. |
| **Gain** pot | Drag up or down to set Gain, from −24 to +24 dB. Double-click returns it to 0 dB. Not on a bus. |
| **Pan** pot | Drag up or down to pan. Double-click centres it. Its tooltip reads **C**, **L** or **R**. |
| Inserts | The audio effects, a slot each. A click brings Effect controls to the front on the effect. The empty slot adds one. |
| Sends | A slot per send: the bus's name, **Pre** on a pre-fader send, and a bar dragged sideways to set its level. Double-click the bar for 0 dB. The empty slot lists the buses to send to. Shown while the composition has a bus. |
| Automation mode | A chip beside the clip lamp naming the strip's mode. A click picks another. |
| Fader | Drag up or down to set Volume. Double-click returns it to 0 dB. While playing it follows the keys. |
| Meters | Left and right, on the fader's scale, green up to −18 dB, amber to −6 dB and red above. |
| Clip lamp | Over the meters. Lights when the strip's sound reaches full scale, until clicked. |
| dB well | The fader's value. Click to type one. It reads **-inf** with the fader at the bottom. |
| Mute, **S**, **fx** | The layer's audible, solo and effects switches. **fx** shows when audio effects are switched on in the layer's stack, and its tooltip counts them. |
| Output | A chip naming where the sound goes: **Master** or a [bus](#buses-and-sends). A click lists the buses, and **New bus**. |

Each pot's caption reads its value while it turns. `Ctrl` gives finer steps on a pot, the fader and a send bar, and `Esc` during a drag puts the
value back. A thin bar beside the meters shows how far Lower behind, or on the Master the limiter,
holds the sound down.
When the panel is short, the inserts and the sends fold to one row each that counts them and
opens them in a popover.

The fader's scale runs from silence at the bottom to +12 dB at the top, with 0 dB two thirds
of the way up. A fader or pot on an
animated Volume or Pan sets the keyframe at the playhead. A drag is one undo step.

### Meters

Each meter draws its average level, its peak behind it, and a hold line that rests on the
loudest peak. **Peak hold ▸** on a meter's right-click menu sets how long the line rests,
as in the [Audio panel](#levels).

The first 32 strips are metered.

### The Master strip

The Master strip's fader sets the gain of the whole mix, ahead of the limiter, and can be
keyed. The limiter holds the output at −0.3 dBFS, or at the true peak of the composition's
[loudness target](/use/mixing/#loudness), in preview and in export. The **LIM** lamp
lights when the limiter has held the mix back, and clicking it puts it out. The strip has its
own automation mode chip. A slim bar beside its meter draws the short-term loudness, with
the [loudness target](/use/mixing/#loudness) as a mark across it. Its loudness numbers are
**M**, **S**, **I**, **TP** and **LRA**, with the target beside **I**.

The [loudness](#levels) stands beside the Master's meter while the Mixer has room, and the
integrated loudness alone under its dB well when it does not.

### Mixer menus

| On | Commands |
| --- | --- |
| Strip | The [track's menu](#a-tracks-menu). It acts on every selected strip when the strip is one of them. |
| Fader | **Reset volume**, **Set level…**, and **Add keyframe** or **Remove keyframe** at the playhead. |
| Pan pot | **Reset pan**, **Set pan…**, **Full left**, **Full right**, and **Add keyframe** or **Remove keyframe**. |
| Insert | **On**, **Remove**, **Move up**, **Move down**. |
| Send | **Pre-fader**, **Post-fader**, **Mute send**, **Remove send**, and **Show bus**, which brings the bus's strip into view. |
| Meters | **Peak hold ▸**, **Reset peaks**, **Reset loudness**. |
| Master | **Reset volume**, **Set level…**, **Loudness target ▸**, **Normalise mix…**, **Peak hold ▸**, **Reset peaks**, **Reset loudness**. |
| Empty ground | **Insert audio layer**, **Insert bus**, **Unmute all**, **Clear solos**, **Reset all faders**, and **Automation ▸**, which sets the mode of every strip. |

With the Mixer active, these keys act on the selected strips:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Left`, `Right` | Selects the previous or next strip. |
| `Up`, `Down` | Raises or lowers the fader by 0.5 dB. With `Shift`, by 0.1 dB. |
| `Home` | Returns the fader to 0 dB. |
| `M` | Mutes or unmutes. |
| `S` | Solos or unsolos. |
| `Alt`+`S` | Solos the strip alone. |
| `A` | The next automation mode. |

## Audio timeline

The Audio timeline lists the layers of the composition that make a sound, as **tracks**,
over the same time as the [Timeline](/panels/timeline/). It keeps its own zoom, scroll and
twirls.

The first edit made here gives the composition a
[Sound mix](/panels/timeline/#sound-mix) row in the Timeline.

### Tracks

An [Audio layer](/use/audio/#audio-layers) is a track, and so are an audio-only
[Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) and a [bus](#buses-and-sends). A muted one stays
listed.

Each track carries mute, solo and effects switches, its
[automation mode](/use/mixing/#automation), a twirl, its number, its label colour and its
name. A click on the mode picks another. A bus's track carries the bus glyph before its name,
takes no clips, and draws the sound routed into it, through each source's fader and send
level, before its own effects and fader.

- The twirl opens the track's lanes, **Volume**, **Pan** and a **Send to** lane for each
  send, then its **Effects**.
- **Search tracks** lists only the tracks whose names contain what is typed. The time and the
  frame number beside it can be typed into.
- `Enter` renames the selected track while the panel is active.
- The switch at the right of a track's head, shown with the pointer on the head, changes its
  lane between **Wave** and **Spectral**.
- Drag the lower edge of a track to make it two to eight rows tall. Double-click the edge to
  put it back to two.

Pressing a head selects its track, and `Ctrl` or `Shift` adds it to the selection.

A layer with a picture as well as sound is listed while its audible switch is on, drawn
faded. Its menu has **Rename**, **Detach audio**, **Lock** and **Delete**. Its **Detach
audio** switch moves its sound to an Audio layer of its own and mutes the original, which
leaves the list.

### A track's menu

Right-click a head for the commands below. They act on every selected track when the track
is one of them. A sound track's header in the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/#tracks) and a
strip in the [Mixer](#mixer) have the same menu.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename** | Turns the name into a field. |
| **Mute**, **Solo**, **Solo safe**, **Bypass effects**, **Lock** | The track's switches. See [Solo and mute](/use/mixing/#solo-and-mute). |
| **Reset volume**, **Reset pan** | Back to 0 dB, and to the centre. On an animated row, at the playhead. |
| **Reset gain** | Gain back to 0 dB, when it is off 0 dB. |
| **Normalise** | Sets the gain of every clip on the track so its loudest sample lands at **Normalise to** in [Settings](/use/settings/#audio). A layer that is one file sets its [Gain](/use/mixing/#gain-and-volume). |
| **Normalise loudness ▸** | Sets the gain of every clip on the track so its measured loudness lands at −14, −16 or −23 LUFS. On a track that holds clips. |
| **Normalise together**, **Normalise loudness together ▸** | With several tracks selected, one change for all of them. See [Gain and Volume](/use/mixing/#gain-and-volume). |
| **Detect beats** | Places [beat markers](/use/audio/#beat-markers) from this track's sound alone. |
| **Volume envelope**, **Pan envelope** | Shows or hides the line across the track, as in the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/#envelopes). `Shift`+`V` and `Shift`+`P` do the same on the selected tracks, or on every track with none selected. |
| **Lane ▸** | **Wave** or **Spectral**. |
| **Select layer** | Selects every clip on the track. |
| **Move up**, **Move down** | Moves the track past its neighbour. |
| **Minimise all tracks** | Back to two rows, every track. |
| **Reset height** | Back to two rows, when a track's edge has been dragged. |
| **Output ▸**, **Send to ▸**, **Mute send ▸** | Where the sound goes. See [Routing](/use/mixing/#routing). |
| **Automation ▸** | The track's automation mode. |
| **Lower behind ▸** | The layer or bus this track is lowered behind. See [Lower behind](/use/mixing/#lower-behind). |
| **Detach audio** | Moves the sound of a layer with a picture to an Audio layer of its own. |
| **Add audio effect ▸** | Adds to the track's stack. |
| **Duplicate**, **Label colour ▸**, **Delete** | As on any layer. |

Right-click the column header for **Lane ▸** on every track and **Reset all heights**.

Right-click the empty ground under the tracks for **Paste**, **Insert audio layer**, **Insert
bus**, **Select all** and **Add marker here**. With no tracks, **Insert audio layer** is on
the lane. A new track goes above the selected one, or at the bottom.

### Lanes

A lane draws its value as a line across time, over the track's wave, between the layer's in
and out points. It is two rows tall until its lower edge is dragged. Its head has the
stopwatch, the name and **Pre** or **Post** on a send's lane on the first row, and the keyframe
navigator and the value at the playhead on the second. The line takes the gestures of an
[envelope](/use/cutting/#envelopes), and a box dragged on the lane picks keys. A lane's
Volume runs on the fader's scale. A send's lane has **Mute send** on its right-click, and is
dimmed while the send is muted.

While the composition plays, a lane's line and its field record on a track whose mode is not
**Read**, and the stretch being written is drawn in the error colour. See
[Automation](/use/mixing/#automation).

### Master

The **Master** track is pinned at the foot of the list. Its lane draws the composition's mix,
and its twirl opens a **Volume** lane for the master fader, keyed in composition time. Its
head carries the master's automation mode. Right-click it for **Automation ▸**, **Reset
volume**, **Loudness target ▸** and **Normalise mix…**. See [Loudness](/use/mixing/#loudness).

### Clips

An Audio layer is a track of one clip until the first edit on it turns it into a
[Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) of clips. Until then it draws as one bar. A retimed
layer takes no clips. A clip draws its waveform or its spectrogram, and a stereo waveform
draws the left channel over the right.

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Click a clip | Selects it and its track. `Ctrl` or `Shift` adds it to the selection, and `Esc` deselects. |
| Drag a clip | Moves it along its track, or onto another. Dropped below the last track, it gets a track of its own. |
| Drag an edge | Trims the clip. With `Shift`, stretches it. |
| Drag footage from the Project panel onto a track | Makes a clip at the pointer. |
| Drag footage onto empty ground | Makes an Audio layer. |

A composition dragged from the Project panel is not taken.

A clip moved or dropped onto another keeps both, and the overlap is a
[crossfade](#fades-and-crossfades). A track whose last clip is moved away or deleted is
removed with it, in the same undo step.

A dragged clip or edge snaps to edit points, layer ends, markers, keyframes, the playhead
and the work area. `Ctrl` suspends the snap, and `Esc` abandons the drag. With
**Settings ▸ Timeline ▸ Sound moves in whole frames** off, a moved clip lands where the
pointer is, between frames.

A start dragged back past the beginning of the clip's file plays silence up to the point
where the sound begins. A mark shows that point, and the edge snaps to it.

With the [Razor](/panels/toolbar/) armed, a click cuts the clip under the pointer, and
`Shift`+click cuts every track at that frame.

### A clip's header

With the pointer on a clip, three controls show beside its name:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Effects** | Turns the clip's own effects off and on. |
| **Add effect** | Lists the [audio effects](/effects/#audio) and adds one to the clip. |
| **Twirl** | Opens the clip's effects as rows under the track. |

A narrow clip draws no header.

### A clip's level

The line across a clip is its gain. Drag it to turn the clip up or down, from +24 dB at
the top of the clip to **-inf** at the foot. A readout gives the level in dB during the
drag. It is separate from the track's Volume. A clip at 0 dB shows its line with the pointer
on it.

### A clip's menu

Right-click a clip. The commands act on every selected clip when the clip is one of them.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Split** | Cuts the clip at the playhead. `Ctrl`+`K` does it too. Disabled with the playhead off the clip. |
| **Trim to playhead ▸** | Brings the clip's start or end to the playhead, with or without closing the room: **Trim start**, **Trim end**, **Ripple trim start**, **Ripple trim end**. `Alt`+`Q`, `Alt`+`W`, `Q` and `W` do the same on the selected clips. |
| **Transition ▸** | **Fade in ▸** or **Fade out ▸** sets the shape of the fade: **Linear**, **Fast**, **Slow**, **Smooth** or **Sharp**. |
| **Freeze frame…** | Asks how long, and holds the sound at the playhead as silence of that length. What follows moves later. |
| **Keep pitch** | Whether the track's sound keeps its pitch when it plays faster or slower. |
| **Normalise** | Sets each clip's gain so its loudest sample lands at **Normalise to** in [Settings](/use/settings/#audio), −1 dB unless changed. Each by its own loudest sample, as one undo step. On a track's head, every clip on it. A layer that is one file sets its [Gain](/use/mixing/#gain-and-volume). |
| **Normalise loudness ▸** | Sets each clip's gain so its measured loudness lands at −14, −16 or −23 LUFS. On a track's head, every clip on it. |
| **Normalise together**, **Normalise loudness together ▸** | With several clips selected, moves them all by one change, so the loudest lands on the target and the rest keep their balance. |
| **Reset gain** | Puts each clip's gain back at 0 dB. Disabled while none is off 0 dB. |
| **Take ▸** | Picks a [take](/use/cutting/#takes), or deletes the one showing, when the clip has several. |
| **Audio streams ▸**, **Split audio streams**, **Merge audio streams** | As on a layer, when the clip's file has more than one [audio stream](/use/audio/#audio-streams). |
| **Add audio effect ▸** | Adds to the clip's stack. |
| **Lane ▸** | **Wave** or **Spectral**. |
| **Open in Source** | Loads the clip's footage into the [source view](/use/cutting/#the-source-view). |
| **Effect controls** | Shows the clip's stack in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/). |
| **New composition from clip** | Makes a composition of the clip and opens it. On a clip that already plays one it reads **Open composition**, and with several clips selected **New composition from selection**. |
| **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Duplicate** | **Paste** places at the playhead, on this track. **Duplicate**, `Ctrl`+`D`, places a copy after the clip, or past the end of the track where something is in the way. |
| **Paste attributes** | Puts the copied clip's effects, levels, fades and speed on the selected clips. `Ctrl`+`Alt`+`V` does it too. |
| **Group**, **Ungroup** | Joins two or more selected clips, or lets one go. `Ctrl`+`G` and `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`G`. |
| **Select to end** | Selects this clip and those after it on the track. |
| **Select layer** | Selects every clip on the track. |
| **Clip colour ▸** | The track's colour, or one of the label colours. |
| **Bypass effects** | Switches off the clip's effects, when it has any. |
| **Delete clip** | Removes the clip and leaves a gap. |
| **Ripple delete** | Removes the clip and moves what follows back. `Shift`+`Delete` does it too. |

An Audio layer not yet cut has **Split**, **Transition ▸**, **Keep pitch**, **Normalise**,
**Lane ▸**, **Clip colour ▸** and **Delete**.

Right-click a gap on a track for **Paste**, **Close gap**, **Select all** and **Add marker
here**.

`Delete` removes the selected keyframes, or the selected clips when no keyframe is selected.
`Ctrl+X`, `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+V` cut, copy and paste them.

### Fades and crossfades

A clip fades in from its start and out to its end. Where two clips overlap, the overlap is
a crossfade: the earlier clip fades out across it as the later one fades in.

- With the pointer on a track, each clip shows a handle at its top corners. Drag one to set
  the length of the fade at that end. A readout gives the length in seconds. Dragged back to
  the corner, the fade is removed.
- Right-click a fade, or an overlap between two clips, for its shape: **Linear**, **Fast**,
  **Slow**, **Smooth**, **Sharp** or **Custom…**. A shape picked on an overlap goes on both
  sides of it.
- **Remove fade in** or **Remove fade out** on that menu takes away a fade with no overlap.

A fade is **Fast** until it is given another shape.

**Custom…** opens the fade editor, which draws the fade's curve, or both curves of a
crossfade, with handles to drag. **Apply** writes the result. With **Keep level** ticked,
dragging one curve of a crossfade reshapes the other to hold the level across the join.

### Effects and keyframes

A track's twirl opens its [lanes](#lanes), then **Effects**, with the rows of each audio
effect on the track. A clip's twirl opens the same rows for the clip's own effects. A
clip's effects run before its track's.

Keyframes on these rows draw in the lanes. A box dragged over empty lane selects the
keyframes it crosses, a drag moves them, `Ctrl+C` copies them and `Ctrl+V` pastes at the
playhead. A shape sent from the [Easing panel](/panels/easing/) applies to the selected
keyframes. Right-click a keyframe for the [menu every keyframe has](/use/keyframes/), less
**Ease…**.

A selected track shows its effects in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/).

### Buses and sends

A **bus** is a track with no sound of its own. Its effects and its Volume work on the sound
routed to it, and what comes out goes to the master or to another bus.

- **Insert bus**, on the right-click menu of the empty ground here and in the Mixer, adds a
  bus.
- **Output ▸** on a track's menu sends the track's sound to **Master** or to one bus. In the
  Mixer the strip's **Output** chip names it.
- **Send to ▸** ticks the buses that take a copy of the track's sound, as well as where its
  output goes. It is there once the composition has a bus.
- A track with a send has a **Send to** lane for each bus under its twirl. The lane sets
  how much is sent, in dB, and takes keyframes. A new send is at 0 dB.
- **Post** on a send's lane takes the copy after the track's Volume and Pan. Click it for
  **Pre**, which takes the copy before them.

A track whose bus is deleted goes to the master.

### Moving about

The ruler carries the playhead, the work area and [markers](/use/markers/) as the
[Timeline's](/panels/timeline/#moving-about) does, and the strip above it shows the whole
composition with a window on the part in view. The slider on the bottom bar zooms.

- `Shift`+wheel scrolls sideways.
- `Ctrl`+wheel zooms time, anchored on the pointer.
- A middle-button drag pans both ways at once.
