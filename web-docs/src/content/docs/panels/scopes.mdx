Scopes read the frame the Viewer previews. Open the panel with **Window ▸ Panels ▸ Scopes**.

The picker at the top chooses one of four scopes.

| Scope | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Waveform** | Brightness against horizontal position, as luma or as the three channels over each other. |
| **RGB parade** | The red, green and blue waveforms side by side. |
| **Vectorscope** | Hue and saturation on a colour wheel. |
| **Histogram** | How many pixels sit at each level, as luma or per channel. |

**Luma** and **RGB** beside the picker choose the reading of the Waveform and the
Histogram.

Right-click the trace to change the scope, its reading, the graticule or the colours.

| Row | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Waveform**, **RGB parade**, **Vectorscope**, **Histogram** | Picks the scope. |
| **Luma**, **RGB** | Picks the reading. Greyed on the RGB parade and the Vectorscope. |
| **Graticule** | Shows or hides the scale. |
| **Theme colours** | Draws the scope in the theme's colours. Off, it is drawn in its own fixed colours. |

The menu stays open while rows are picked.

## The scales

- **Waveform** and **RGB parade**: lines at 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100. 0 is black and 100 is
  white. The parade has a line between each channel's third of the width.
- **Vectorscope**: rings at a quarter, a half, three quarters and the whole of the radius,
  a cross through the centre, targets for red, green, blue, magenta, yellow and cyan, and
  the skin-tone line.
- **Histogram**: levels 0, 64, 128, 192 and 255 across. Its height is scaled to the
  tallest column.

## What is read

The scope reads the open composition at the playhead, at the preview resolution the Viewer
is using and through the active view's [3D view](/use/camera/#3d-views). It is drawn again
when the playhead moves and when the picture changes, a value being dragged included.

With a [3D viewport](/use/camera/#3d-viewport) active, the scope reads another view of the
same composition.

With no composition open the panel reads *No composition selected*.

**Theme colours** is the same switch as **Edit ▸ Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Viewer ▸ Scopes
use theme colour**.
