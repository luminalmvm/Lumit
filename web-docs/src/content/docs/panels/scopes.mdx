Scopes read the frame the Viewer previews. Open the panel with **Window ▸ Panels ▸ Scopes**.

The picker at the top chooses one of five scopes.

| Scope | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Waveform** | Brightness against horizontal position, as luma or as the three channels over each other. |
| **RGB parade** | The red, green and blue waveforms side by side. |
| **Vectorscope** | Hue and saturation on a colour wheel. |
| **Histogram** | How many pixels sit at each level, as luma or per channel. |
| **CIE chromaticity** | Each pixel's colour on the CIE 1931 xy diagram. Pixels near black are left out. |

**Luma** and **RGB** beside the picker choose the reading of the Waveform and the
Histogram. **2x** beside it draws the Vectorscope at twice the size.

## Layouts

The picker at the panel's top right chooses **One scope**, **Two scopes** or **Four
scopes**. Two sit side by side, and four in two rows of two. Each scope has its own header,
with its picker and options. A second Scopes panel keeps its own layout and scopes.

## The scale

The graticule is drawn over each trace:

- **Waveform** and **RGB parade**: five lines, numbered 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 in
  **Percent**, or 0, 256, 512, 768 and 1023 in **10-bit**. The lowest is black and the
  highest is white. The parade has a line between each channel's third of the width.
- **Vectorscope**: rings at a quarter, a half, three quarters and the whole of the radius,
  a cross through the centre, targets where 75% red, magenta, blue, cyan, green and yellow
  bars land, and the skin tone line.
- **Histogram**: five levels across, from 0 to 255 in **Percent**, or 0 to 1023 in
  **10-bit**. Its height is scaled to the tallest column.
- **CIE chromaticity**: the spectral locus, the Rec. 709, P3 and Rec. 2020 gamuts, and the
  D65 white point.

## The menu

Right-click a scope for its menu. The menu stays open while rows are picked.

| Row | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Waveform**, **RGB parade**, **Vectorscope**, **Histogram**, **CIE chromaticity** | Picks the scope. |
| **Luma**, **RGB** | Picks the reading. Greyed on the RGB parade, the Vectorscope and CIE chromaticity. |
| **2x** | Draws the Vectorscope at twice the size. Greyed on the other scopes. |
| **One scope**, **Two scopes**, **Four scopes** | Picks the layout. |
| **Graticule** | Shows or hides the scale. |
| **Percent**, **10-bit** | Picks how the scale is numbered. |
| **Theme colours** | Draws the scopes in the theme's colours. Off, they are drawn in their own fixed colours. |
| **Brightness** | Draws the traces fainter or stronger, from 25% to 400%. |

The scope, its reading and **2x** belong to the scope that was right-clicked. The layout,
the scale and the brightness belong to the panel.

**Theme colours** is the same switch as **Edit ▸ Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Viewer ▸ Scopes
use theme colour**.

## What is read

The scopes read the open composition at the playhead, at the preview resolution the Viewer
is using and through the active view's [3D view](/use/camera/#3d-views). They are drawn
again when the playhead moves and when the picture changes, a value being dragged included.

With a [3D viewport](/use/camera/#3d-viewport) active, the scopes read another view of the
same composition.

With no composition open the panel reads *No composition selected*.
