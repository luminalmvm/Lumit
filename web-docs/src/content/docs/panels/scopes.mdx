Scopes read the frame the Viewer previews.

| Scope | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Waveform** | Brightness against horizontal position, as luma or as the three channels over each other. |
| **RGB parade** | The red, green and blue waveforms side by side. |
| **Vectorscope** | Hue and saturation on a colour wheel. **2x** draws it at twice the size. |
| **Histogram** | How many pixels sit at each level, as luma or per channel. |
| **CIE chromaticity** | Each pixel's colour on the CIE 1931 xy diagram. |

## Layouts

**One scope**, **Two scopes** or **Four scopes** sit side by side, from the picker at the
panel's top right. Each has its own header, with the scope and its options. A second Scopes
panel keeps its own layout and scopes.

## The scale

The graticule is drawn over each trace:

- The waveform and parade are marked in **Percent**, 0 to 100, or **10-bit**, 0 to 1023.
- The vectorscope marks where 75% colour bars land, and the skin tone line.
- The CIE diagram draws the spectral locus, the Rec. 709, P3 and Rec. 2020 gamuts, and the
  D65 white point.

## The menu

Right-click a scope for its instrument, **Luma** or **RGB**, and **2x**, then the layout,
**Graticule**, **Percent** or **10-bit**, and **Theme colours**. **Brightness**, at the
foot, draws the traces fainter or stronger, from 25% to 400%.
