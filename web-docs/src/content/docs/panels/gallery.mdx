The Gallery keeps **stills**: frames as the Viewer showed them, each with the grade of the
shot it was grabbed from. See [Stills and the Gallery](/use/grading/#stills-and-the-gallery).

<Shot file="gallery.png" caption="The Gallery's Stills album, a still selected." eager/>

Stills are kept in albums in the library's **Stills** folder, where every project sees
them. The first album is called **Stills**.

## The header

- The album's name lists the albums, ticked on the one shown, then **New album…**, **Rename
  album…** and **Delete album**. Deleting an album deletes its stills, after asking.
- **Grab still** keeps the composition's frame at the playhead, through the Viewer's colour
  view, with the current shot's grade, in the album shown. `Ctrl+Alt+G` does the same.

## Memories

A strip of eight numbered slots sits above the stills, each a grade kept in the project.
A filled slot shows a picture of the shot it was kept from.

- Click a filled slot to put its grade in place of the grade on every picked shot.
- Right-click a slot for **Apply memory**, **Keep the current grade here** and **Clear
  memory**.

See [Memories](/use/grading/#memories).

## The stills

Each still shows its picture and its name, after a dot in its shot's label colour where the
shot had one. A new still is named by its shot and the Viewer's clock, as
`faces-warm.mp4 1:16`, with a number after it where the album has that name already. A line
under the name gives the shot and timecode the name leaves out.

- Click a still to select it. `Ctrl`+click adds a still to the selection, or takes it out,
  and `Shift`+click selects every still from the last one clicked.
- Double-click a still to make it the Viewer's [reference](/use/grading/#comparing-against-a-reference).
- Hold `Alt` over a still to see its grade on the current shot. Nothing is written.
- Drag a still onto a tile in the [Shots panel](/panels/shots/) to put its grade in place of
  that shot's.

A selected still has an accent border, and the Viewer's reference a stronger hairline.

Right-click a still:

| Row | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Apply grade** | Puts the still's grade in place of the current shot's. |
| **Apply grade to picked shots** | The same, on every picked shot. With more than one picked. |
| **Compare** | Makes the still the Viewer's reference. |
| **Stills in a grid** | Shows the selected stills in the Viewer's [grid](/use/grading/#the-grid). With more than one selected. |
| **Export still…** | Writes the still's picture as a PNG. |
| **Export grade as LUT…** | Writes the still's grade as a 33-point `.cube` for Video, titled with the still's name. |
| **Rename…** | Renames the still. |
| **Move to** | Moves the still to another album, one row an album. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the still, after asking. |

A still renamed or moved stays the reference. A still deleted stops being it.
