The Timeline has two halves: the **outline** on the left, naming layers and their properties,
and on the right either the **lanes** or the **graph**.

<Shot file="timeline.png" caption="The Timeline: outline on the left, lanes on the right." eager/>

## Layers and Graph

Two mode tabs sit at the far right of the panel's header, with the playhead's time and frame
number at its far left. The outline is **the same in both modes**; switching mode changes
what is drawn against time, and nothing else.

<Shot file="layers-shut.png" caption="Layers mode with everything shut: a summary diamond on the bar for each keyframed moment." />

**Layers** draws each layer as a bar, with a lane per property once the layer is twirled open.
Shut, a layer's keys are summarised as small diamonds on its own bar.

<Shot file="layers-open.png" caption="Layers mode, a layer twirled open: keyframes on their own lanes, shaped by their interpolation." />

<Shot file="graph-mode.png" caption="Graph mode: the curve of the selected property, running edge to edge." />

**Graph** draws the curves. A property's curves are on the pane while its row is **selected**
in the outline — there is no tick and no separate list. The curves share one value scale, so
a rotation in degrees and an opacity in per cent are one flat line under another.

## The Animated filter

**ANIMATED**, on the bottom bar, lists only properties that have keyframes, across every
layer. `U` is the shortcut, and it works in both modes.

Beside it, the **shy filter** hides every layer with its shy switch on, and a master
**motion blur** toggle governs the whole composition.

## The ruler

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Work area** | The stretch that plays and exports. Drag either end; it defaults to the whole composition. |
| **Markers ribbon** | Composition markers. |
| **Layer markers** | Markers sitting on a layer. |
| **Beat markers** | Markers placed by **Composition ▸ Detect beats**. |
| **Cache bar** | Which frames are cached, coloured by which tier they are in. |

## The outline columns

<Shot file="timeline-outline.png" caption="The outline columns." />

Left to right: **Switches**, **Layer**, **Modes**, **Compose**, **Parent**, and the render
times.

| Column | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Switches** | Visibility, mute, solo, lock, shy, guide. |
| **Layer** | Twirl, label colour, layer number, name. The one column that stretches. |
| **Modes** | Collapse, flow, disable effects, motion blur, 3D, adjustment. |
| **Compose** | Matte and blend mode. |
| **Parent** | The parent picker. |

Drag a column heading to reorder the groups, and drag a seam to resize one. Toggles on the
bottom bar show and hide **Switches**, **Modes** and **Parent**; Compose answers to no
toggle.

**Switches** is what the Timeline keeps about a layer, and **Modes** is how the layer is
rendered. See [Layers](/use/layers/#switches).

## The lanes

<Shot file="timeline-lanes.png" caption="A layer twirled open, showing its properties and keyframes." />

Each layer has a bar showing when it draws. Drag the bar to move it, and either end to trim
it. A trimmed layer keeps a faint outline of its full source extent, so you can see what you
cut off.

Twirl a layer open and its transform, masks, effects, flow options, paint strokes and shape
contents appear beneath it, each on a lane of its own.

A [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) draws its clips on one row and behaves differently
from everything else here.

## Editing keyframes in bulk

<Shot file="layers-selected.png" caption="A block of keyframes selected: the box, its stretch handles, and the count of what is inside." />

Sweep a box over the lanes and every keyframe inside it is selected, across as many
properties and layers as the box crosses. The selection is drawn as a **block** with a badge
reading how many keys it holds and how many frames it spans.

- Drag the block's **body** to move every key together.
- Drag an **end handle** to stretch the whole block — the keys inside keep their proportions.
- The **magnet** on the bottom bar decides whether a dragged key lands on a whole frame or
  may sit between two.

The strip along the bottom bar acts on whatever keys are selected, in whichever mode you are
in: **Interpolation** — Linear, Hold, Ease, Bezier — then **Reverse**, **Copy** and **Paste
at playhead**.

## Render times

The stopwatch on the bottom strip turns on render-time measuring. Each layer's row shows how
long that layer took on the last measured frame in milliseconds, each effect's heading shows
its own, and the column heading carries the whole frame's. A Precomp layer's number includes
everything inside it.
