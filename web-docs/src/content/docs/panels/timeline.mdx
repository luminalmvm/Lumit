The Timeline has two halves: the **outline** on the left, naming layers and their properties,
and on the right either the **lanes** or the **graph**.

<Shot file="timeline.png" caption="The Timeline: outline on the left, lanes on the right." eager/>

## The header

The top line carries one tab per open composition and **Export**, which opens the export
dialogue for the composition in front.

- Click a tab to bring its composition to the front.
- Drag a tab along the line to reorder the tabs.
- A tab's close button shuts it. The tab to its left comes to the front, or the next one
  when the first tab shuts.

Under the tabs, above the outline:

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Playhead time** | The playhead as a timecode, counted from the composition's start timecode. Click it and type a time to move the playhead. A time with fewer than four fields reads as seconds, minutes and seconds, or hours, minutes and seconds. |
| **Frame number** | The same moment as a frame count from 0, with the composition's length in frames after it. Click it and type a frame. |
| **Search layers** | Lists only the layers whose name contains what is typed. `Ctrl+F` does not reach it. |
| **Layers** and **Graph** | The two modes. `Shift+F3` switches between them. The Lantern shape draws the pair on the top line. |

A typed time or frame outside the composition lands on its nearest end.

## Layers and Graph

The outline is **the same in both modes**; switching mode changes what is drawn against
time, and nothing else.

<Shot file="layers-shut.png" caption="Layers mode with everything shut: a summary diamond on the bar for each keyframe." />

**Layers** draws each layer as a bar, with a lane per property once the layer is twirled open.
Shut, a layer's keys are summarised as diamonds within its own bar.

<Shot file="layers-open.png" caption="Layers mode, a layer twirled open: keyframes on their own lanes, shaped by their interpolation." />

<Shot file="graph-mode.png" caption="Graph mode: the curve of the selected property." />

**Graph** draws the curves. A property's curves are on the pane while its row is
**selected** in the outline. The curves share one value scale. See
[the graph editor](/panels/graph-editor/).

## The Animated filter

**Animated**, on the bottom bar, lists only the rows that have keyframes, across every
layer.

Beside it, the **shy filter** hides every layer with its shy switch on, and a master
**motion blur** toggles motion blur for all layers in the composition with the switch on.

## Reveal keys

A reveal key opens the selected layers showing only the rows it names. Pressing it again
shuts them.

| Key | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| `P`, `S`, `R`, `T`, `A` | Position, Scale, Rotation, Opacity, Anchor point. On a 3D layer `R` shows all three rotation rows. |
| `E` | Effects. |
| `M` | Masks. |
| `L` | The Audio group. Twice adds its Waveform lane, and three times shuts the layers. See [Audio](/use/audio/). |
| `U` | The rows that have keyframes. |
| `U` twice | Transform, each effect, each text animator and Audio, where one holds something a new layer would not have. Each opens whole. |
| `U` three times | Shuts the layers. |
| `Shift+U` | The rows changed from what a new layer has, and nothing else. |

`U` and `L` count presses made within half a second of each other. With no layer selected,
`U` and `Shift+U` act on every layer in the composition.

**Layer ▸ Reveal** carries the single reveals as commands. The **Animation** menu carries
these, on the selected layers or on every layer when none is selected:

| Command | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Reveal properties with keyframes** | The rows that have keyframes. |
| **Reveal properties with animation** | Those, and the rows driven by an expression or by a wire from the node graph. |
| **Reveal all modified properties** | Those, and everything a new layer would not have: a typed value, an effect, a mask. |
| **Reveal selected properties** | The selected property rows, on their layers, with the rest folded. |

**Expand all** and **Collapse all**, on the lane area's menu, the column headings' menu and
under **Layer ▸ Reveal**, open or shut the selected layers, or every layer with none
selected. `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+`` ` and `Ctrl+Alt+`` ` are the shortcuts. Expand all opens each
layer's Transform, Effects and Masks headings and leaves each effect shut.

A twirl pressed on a selected layer twirls every selected layer with it.

## The ruler

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Work area** | The stretch that plays and exports. Drag either end; it defaults to the whole composition. |
| **Markers ribbon** | Composition markers. |
| **Layer markers** | Markers sitting on a layer. |
| **Beat markers** | Markers placed by **Composition ▸ Detect beats**. |
| **Cache bar** | Which frames are cached, coloured by which tier they are in. |

- Click or drag on the ruler to move the playhead. Dragging it past either end of the lanes
  scrolls them.
- Hold `Ctrl` while dragging to hear the composition at the speed of the drag. See
  [Scrubbing](/use/audio/#scrubbing).
- Hold `Shift` while dragging and the playhead lands on the nearest marker, layer end, edit
  point, keyframe or work-area edge.
- Double-click the work area's band, in the lower half of the ruler, to give the work area
  back the whole composition.
- Double-click anywhere else on the ruler to make a [marker](/use/markers/) there.
- `B` and `N` set the work area's start and end to the playhead.
- `Esc` while a work-area edge or a marker is being dragged puts it back.

## The outline columns

<Shot file="timeline-outline.png" caption="The outline columns." />

Left to right: **Switches**, **Layer**, **Modes**, **Compose**, **Parent**, and the render
times.

| Column | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Switches** | Visibility, mute, solo, lock, shy, guide. |
| **Layer** | Twirl, layer number, label colour, name. The one column that stretches. |
| **Modes** | Disable effects, 3D, motion blur, adjustment, flow, collapse. |
| **Compose** | Matte and blend mode. |
| **Parent** | The parent picker. |

Drag a column heading to reorder the groups, and drag a seam to resize one. Toggles on the
bottom bar show and hide **Modes**, **Matte and blend**, **Parent** and the render times,
as does a right-click on the headings or that bar.

**Switches** opens on visibility, mute, solo and lock, and **Modes** on disable effects, 3D
and motion blur. Drag the seam wider to show the rest. The order, the widths and the hidden
columns are kept between launches.

**Switches** is what the Timeline keeps about a layer, and **Modes** is how the layer is
rendered. See [Layers](/use/layers/#switches).

A matte, blend or parent picker still at its default reads as muted text. It draws as a
picker once it is changed, or while the pointer is over its row.

A matte or parent picker shows the number and name of the layer it points at, and its list
numbers every layer the same way. A layer's number is its place in the composition, and it
keeps that number while a search or the shy filter hides other layers.

## The outline rows

- Click a layer's row to select it. `Ctrl`+click adds or drops one, and `Shift`+click
  selects the run between.
- Double-click a layer's name to open a Precomp layer's composition or a Sequence layer's
  clips. On any other layer it renames the layer, as `Enter` does.
- Click the label colour to pick one of nine colours for every selected layer.
- Drag a layer by its name to move it up or down the stack.
- Footage, a composition or a solid dragged from the Project panel lands in the stack where
  it is dropped. Several files lie end to end, and with `Shift` held they all start
  together. With `Alt` held, the layer under the pointer takes the item as its source.
- Click a property's name to select the row. Its keyframes are selected with it, and its
  curve is drawn in Graph mode. `Ctrl`+click and `Shift`+click add rows the same way they
  add layers.
- `X` turns the visibility of every selected layer on or off.
- Click empty ground in either half to select nothing.

A property row carries a stopwatch, a previous-keyframe and a next-keyframe arrow, a
keyframe button, and its value. See [Keyframes](/use/keyframes/).

[Layer groups](/use/layer-groups/) draw as a header row over the layers they hold.

## The lanes

<Shot file="timeline-lanes.png" caption="A layer twirled open, showing its properties and keyframes." />

Each layer has a bar showing when it draws. Drag the bar to move it, and either end to
trim it. A trimmed layer keeps a faint outline of its full source extent. While the bar is
moved or trimmed, a readout beside the pointer gives its in frame, its out frame and its
length. A still placed in a composition lasts the whole composition, and both ends of its bar
drag freely.

- Dragging a selected bar moves every selected layer with it. A locked layer stays where it
  is.
- `Ctrl+Alt` held as the drag begins [staggers](/use/layers/#stagger) the selected layers
  instead.
- `Shift` held while dragging an end [stretches](/use/retime/#stretch) the layer.
- A Footage, Precomp or Audio layer's end stops where its source runs out, and a small
  triangle marks that corner of the bar. A still image, and a layer with
  [Retime](/use/retime/) on, have no such stop.
- `[` and `]` move the selected layers so their in point or out point lands on the
  playhead. `Alt+[` and `Alt+]` trim that end to the playhead instead.
- Double-click a Precomp layer's bar to open its composition, and a Sequence layer's bar to
  open its clips.
- `Esc` while a bar is being dragged puts it back.

Twirl a layer open and its transform, masks, effects, flow options, paint strokes and shape
contents appear beneath it, each on a lane of its own. An effect's parameter rows show the
value box without its slider. A click on an effect's name selects the effect, and the arrow
beside it twirls it open.

A text layer with [animators](/use/text/#animators) has an **Animators** heading, and a
layer with [Puppet](/use/puppet/#pins-in-the-timeline) pins a **Puppet** heading.

A layer that makes a sound has an **Audio** group: **Volume**, **Pan**, **Gain** from −24 to
+24 dB, a **Sends** fold with a keyable row and a **Pre** or **Post** switch for each send, and
the **Waveform**. A send's row has **Mute send** on its right-click, and is dimmed while the
send is muted. A bus has no Gain and no Waveform. See [Mixing](/use/mixing/) and
[Audio](/use/audio/#waveforms).

A [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) draws its clips on one row. A
[caption layer](/use/captions/#edit-captions) twirls open to its captions as blocks.

On a [node graph composition](/use/node-graphs/) the Timeline draws the ruler and, under
it, a lane for each keyed parameter of the box selected on the canvas, or of every box with
none selected. With no Node graph panel open, the canvas is drawn below them.

### Keyframes on a lane

A keyframe's mark is drawn in two halves, one for each side's interpolation: a diamond half
for linear, an hourglass half for bezier or automatic, a square half for hold.

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Select a keyframe | Click it. `Shift`+click or `Ctrl`+click adds it to the selection, or drops it. |
| Select several | Drag a box over empty lane ground. `Shift` or `Ctrl` held as the drag begins adds to the selection. |
| Move | Drag a selected keyframe. Every selected keyframe moves with it, and a readout gives the frame and value. |
| Stagger | Hold `Ctrl+Alt` as the drag begins. See [Keyframes](/use/keyframes/#move-and-delete). |
| Add a keyframe | `Ctrl`+click a lane that already has keyframes. The new one holds the value the property reads there. |
| Delete | `Delete` or `Backspace`, or right-click a keyframe: **Delete keyframe**. |
| Abandon a drag | `Esc` while the button is down. |

### Sound mix

Once a composition has been edited in the [Audio timeline](/panels/audio/#audio-timeline),
its Audio and Bus layers fold into a **Sound mix** row pinned at the foot of the Timeline.
The row draws the composition's mix, and its dB well sets the master fader. Its twirl opens
the fold. A double-click opens the **Audio** workspace, and the right-click menu offers
**Open Audio workspace** and **Convert to precomp**, which moves the folded layers into a
nested composition and takes the row away.

## Snapping

The **magnet** on the bar under the lanes turns snapping on and off.

With it on, a dragged bar, keyframe, work-area edge or marker lands on the nearest of these
within twelve pixels on screen: an edit point, a layer's in or out point, a keyframe, a
marker, the playhead, or a work-area edge. With none near, it lands on a whole frame. The
thing it has landed on is marked with a line while it holds the drag.

With it off, nothing snaps and a keyframe can sit between frames.

`Ctrl` held during a drag suspends snapping.

## Moving about

- **The wheel** scrolls the rows.
- **Shift+wheel** scrolls sideways.
- **Ctrl+wheel** zooms time, anchored on the pointer. The After Effects shortcuts use
  **Alt+wheel**.
- **A middle-button drag** pans both ways at once, and so does a drag with the **Hand**
  tool. The outline moves with the lanes.
- `=` and `-` zoom time in and out about the playhead. `\` shows the whole composition, and
  pressed again goes back to the zoom before.
- **`F`** in Layers mode zooms time to the keyframes of the selected layers, or of every
  layer with none selected.
- **Dragging a key** near either end of the lanes scrolls them.
- **The navigator** is the strip above the ruler. It stands for the whole composition, with
  the part in view drawn as a window and the playhead as a line. Drag the window to pan,
  drag either end of it to zoom, and press outside it to bring the window there.
- **Minimap under the lanes**, in **Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Panels**, draws every layer's
  bar as a thin line in a strip under the lanes. The part in view is a window on it, and
  dragging the window scrolls. It is on by default in the Lantern shape.

During playback the lanes jump on a page when the playhead leaves the view.

Each composition keeps its own playhead, zoom and scroll, across and down, between launches
too.

| **Modes**, **Matte and blend**, **Parent**, **Time** | Shows and hides that [column](#the-outline-columns). |
| **Animated**, **Hide shy**, **Motion blur** | See [The Animated filter](#the-animated-filter). |
| **More commands** | The three dots. See [Right-click menus](#right-click-menus). |

Under the lanes:

| Item | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Zoom** | A slider from the whole composition at the left to twenty frames across the lanes at the right. The mark at each end steps the zoom, and pointing at the slider gives the zoom in per cent. The Desk shape draws a dial in its place, with the number of frames one ruler tick spans beside it. |
| **Magnet** | Turns [snapping](#snapping) on and off. |
| **Scope**, **Auto fit**, **Fit selection** | In Graph mode. See [the graph editor](/panels/graph-editor/#controls). |

not fit go into **⋯** at the end of their bar.

## Editing keyframes in bulk

<Shot file="layers-selected.png" caption="A block of keyframes selected: the box, its stretch handles, and the count of what is inside." />

Sweep a box over the lanes and every keyframe inside it is selected, across as many
properties and layers as the box crosses. The selection is drawn as a **block** with a
badge reading how many keys it holds and how many frames it spans.

- Drag the block's **body** to move every key together.
- Drag an **end handle** to stretch the whole block — the keys inside keep their
  proportions.
- The **magnet** on the bottom bar decides whether a dragged key snaps to whole frames.
- Click the **badge** to open the Ease popover on the block.
- A block whose keys all sit on one frame has no end handles. Drag its keys to move them.

See [Editing many keyframes at once](/tutorials/many-keyframes/).

## Render times

The hourglass on the bottom strip turns render-time measuring on and off. It starts on.
Each layer's row shows how long that layer took on the last measured frame in
milliseconds, each effect's heading shows its own, and the column heading carries the whole
frame's. A Precomp layer's number includes everything inside it.
