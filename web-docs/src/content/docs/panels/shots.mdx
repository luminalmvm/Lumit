The Shots panel lists every [shot](/use/grading/#shots) of the composition in time order,
each as a tile with its graded picture.

<Shot file="shots.png" caption="The Shots panel, the current shot outlined in the accent." eager/>

## The tiles

- The picture is the shot alone, with its grade, at its middle frame. It is made again after
  the shot's stack changes.
- The number sits top left, outlined in the accent while the shot has a grade switched on.
- The shot's name sits under the picture, and its version's name when it has more than one.
- A dot, top right, marks a shot in a [grade group](/use/grading/#groups), in the group's
  colour.
- The current shot has an accent border, and the other picked shots a stronger hairline.

The tiles run across the panel, or down it when the panel is taller than wide.

## The header

How many shots there are, **All**, **Graded** and **Ungraded**, which filter the tiles,
**Clip colour** and **Lightbox**.

**Clip colour** shows only the shots of one label colour: the clip's own, else its layer's.
Its menu offers **Every colour** and the Timeline's label chips.

## The Lightbox

**Lightbox**, in the header, on a tile's menu and `Ctrl+Alt+L`, fills the window with the
Shots panel as a grid of large tiles in time order, about four across a full window and
never wider than the composition. Its tiles, filters, menus and still drops work as the
panel's do. Press it again, or `Escape`, to put the panel back.

## Picking shots

- Click a tile to make it the current shot. Its layer is selected, a clip is picked as the
  [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/) picks it, and the playhead moves to the shot's first frame if
  it was outside.
- `Ctrl`+click adds a shot to the pick, or takes it out. The current shot stays current.
- `Shift`+click picks every shot between the last one clicked and this one, and makes this
  one current.
- Double-click a clip's tile to bring up the Cut timeline, where it is in the workspace.
  Any other double-click brings up the Timeline.

Clips are picked with clips, and layers with layers.

`↑` and `↓` move to the previous and the next shot, and `Home` and `End` to the first and
the last, while a Colour panel is active. See
[The current shot](/use/grading/#the-current-shot).

## The menu

Right-click a tile. The current shot stays as it is. On a tile that is picked or current,
**Paste grade**, the two **Apply grade** rows, **Reset grade** and **Group ▸** act on every
picked shot. The other rows act on the tile's shot alone.

| Row | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy grade** | Puts the shot's grade on the clipboard. |
| **Paste grade** | Puts the clipboard's grade in place of the shot's. |
| **Apply grade from previous shot** | Puts the grade of the shot before it in time in place of its own. |
| **Apply grade from two shots back** | The same, from the shot two before it. |
| **Reset grade** | Puts every node of the grade back to its defaults, keys dropped. |
| **Versions ▸** | The shot's versions, ticked on the live one, then **New version**, **Duplicate version**, **Rename…** and **Delete version**. See [Versions](/use/grading/#versions). |
| **Group ▸** | **New group from selection**, then each group, ticked on the one the shots share, to join. **Rename group…** and **Delete group** while the shots share one group, and **Leave group**. See [Groups](/use/grading/#groups). |
| **Match to current shot** | Matches the shot, read at its middle frame, to the current one, read at the playhead when the playhead is in it, else at its middle frame. |
| **Grab still** | Keeps the composition's frame at the playhead in the Gallery with the shot's grade, or the shot's middle frame when the playhead is outside it. |
| **Export LUT…** | Writes the shot's grade as a 33-point `.cube` for Video. |
| **Shots in a grid** | Shows the picked shots in the Viewer's [grid](/use/grading/#the-grid). With more than one picked. |
| **Lightbox** | Shows or hides the Lightbox. |

Drop a still from the [Gallery](/panels/gallery/) on a tile to put its grade in place of the
shot's, as one undo step.
