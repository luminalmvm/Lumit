The Grade panel edits the current node of the current shot. See [Grading](/use/grading/).

<Shot file="grade-panel.png" caption="The Grade panel on the Wheels page, with Adjust beside it." eager/>

## The header

| Control | Does |
| --- | --- |
| The name | The current shot's name. With more than one layer or clip picked, how many an edit lands on. **Group pre** or **Group post** while a group's grade is in view. |
| The node chips | A numbered chip for each Colour effect and shared node in the stack, in stack order, named on hover. A click makes it the current node, and a right-click makes it current and opens the panel's menu. A bypassed node is dimmed. **+** adds a serial node after the current one. |
| The version | The live version's name. A click lists the versions, then **New version**, **Duplicate version**, **Rename…** and **Delete version**, then **Versions in a grid**, greyed with one version. Not shown while a group's grade is in view. |
| **Before** | Held, shows the shot with its grade, its group's grades and its shared nodes off. |
| **Reset grade** | Puts every node of the grade back to its defaults, leaving **Space** and **Combine**. A keyed row takes its default at the playhead. |

**Before** and **Reset grade** show while the shot has a grade.

Right-click the panel for **Reset node**, **Bypass node**, **Reveal in Effect controls** and
**Delete node**, then **Add serial node**, **Add parallel node**, **Add layer node** and
**Add outside node**, then **Reset grade**, **Bypass grade**, **Copy grade**, **Paste
grade** and **Remove grade**. **Reset node** leaves **Space** and **Combine**, and a keyed
row takes its default at the playhead.

## The pages

The pages sit under the header, as buttons or, where the buttons do not fit, a list. A dot
marks each page whose rows the current node has changed. A reset beside them puts the
shown page's rows back.

A panel at least 1000 pixels wide shows Adjust beside Wheels, except in HDR, and Look
beside Match. A panel under 520 pixels wide and more than twice as tall as it is wide shows
every page, each under a heading that twirls it open.

A number scrubs on a drag and types on a click. `Shift` scrubs ten times as fast, `Ctrl` a
tenth and `Alt` a hundredth. A drag on a slider's track takes the value to the pointer, and
a double-click on the track puts the default back. Right-click a row for **Reset**, its
value to copy and paste, its keys and its expression, then the panel's menu. A keyed row
shows in the animated colour and takes a key at the playhead. A row an expression drives is
greyed.

With more than one layer or clip picked, an edit lands on each of them, on its node of the
same effect counted the same way: its second Correction for the second Correction. A picked
shot with no Correction gets one, and one with too few is left as it is. Keys, expressions,
the Match page, a Colour warper's mesh and the rows of an effect with no page of its own
land on the current shot alone.

While **Group pre** or **Group post** is in view in the [Grade nodes](/panels/grade-nodes/)
panel, every control edits that group's grade. The first touch adds a Correction at the end
of it. On a [shared node](/use/grading/#shared-nodes), every control edits the shared node.

### Wheels

**Wheels**, **Bars**, **Log** and **HDR** show the wheels four ways, each marked with a dot
while its rows are changed.

- **Wheels**: **Lift**, **Gamma**, **Gain** and **Offset**. A drag inside a wheel moves its
  colour, and a drag on the bar beside it its master, at half the pointer's speed, or a
  tenth with `Shift`. A double-click puts either back. Under each wheel, its name and four
  numbers: the master, then red, green and blue, each over a tick in its colour.
- **Bars**: each wheel as four upright bars, master, red, green and blue. They drag as a
  wheel does, and a double-click puts one bar back.
- **Log**: **Shadow**, **Midtone**, **Highlight** and **Offset**, with **Low range** and
  **High range** first in the row of numbers under them.
- **HDR**: the zone strip, then a cell for each zone, **Black**, **Dark**, **Shadow**,
  **Light**, **Highlight** and **Specular**, and **Global**. See [HDR](#hdr).

Under the wheels, except in HDR, a row of numbers: **Temperature**, **Tint**,
**Contrast**, **Pivot**, **Saturation**, **Hue** and **Lum mix**.

#### HDR

The zone strip runs from 8 stops under mid grey to 8 over. Each zone is a line showing how
much of it reaches each stop, with a handle at its range. Drag a handle to move the range,
a tenth as fast with `Shift`, and double-click it to put it back.

Each zone's cell has a balance wheel with no master, the zone's name, then **Exp**, **Sat**
and **Range**. **Global** has the wheel for **Global balance**, then **Black offset** and
**Falloff**. The cells stand seven across where each has room, else in rows.

### Adjust

A slider each for **Exposure**, **Temperature**, **Tint**, **Contrast**, **Pivot**,
**Saturation**, **Hue**, **Lum mix**, **Colour boost**, **Shadows**, **Highlights** and
**Midtone detail**. **Pick a neutral**, beside Temperature, arms the dropper: what it picks
on the picture is made neutral, through Temperature and Tint, as one undo step.

**Mixer** is three rows of three numbers: how much red, green and blue make each output
channel. **Keep luma** puts back the luma the mixer moved.

### Curves

**Custom** is the tone curves, **Luma**, **Red**, **Green** and **Blue**, picked by the
buttons beside the plot, with **High soft clip** and **Low soft clip**. The other six are
the hue curves: **Hue v hue**, **Hue v sat**, **Hue v lum**, **Lum v sat**, **Sat v sat**
and **Sat v lum**. A panel wide enough shows Custom beside the hue curves.

On a hue curve:

- The flat line across the middle changes nothing.
- A click on the line adds a point. Drag a point to move it, or off the plot to remove it.
- Double-click a point to put it back on the middle line. Right-click for **Delete point** and
  **Reset curve**.
- A curve whose input is hue wraps round, off the right edge and in at the left.
- Under **Hue v hue**, **Hue v sat** and **Hue v lum**, a swatch for each of red, yellow,
  green, cyan, blue and magenta drops three points on that hue.
- **Pick on the picture** arms the dropper, and drops three points where the picked colour
  sits on the curve.

### Qualifier

**Off**, **HSL**, **RGB**, **Luma** or **3D**, then **Pick**, **Add to pick** and
**Subtract from pick**, which arm the picture; see
[The qualifier](/use/grading/#the-qualifier).

Each range is a bar with a box over it. The box's top corners move its edges, the handles
under it set the softness, and a drag inside the box moves the whole range. The hue bar
wraps. HSL's bars each have a tick, and a bar with its tick off is left out of the key. The
numbers sit beside each bar, or under it.

**3D** shows a **Keep** row and a **Drop** row: how many samples there are, a swatch for
each, and **Clear**, which empties them and forgets the strokes that made them. **Size**
and **Softness** sit under them. See [The 3D qualifier](/use/grading/#the-3d-qualifier).

**Finesse** has **Denoise**, **Clean black**, **Clean white**, **Black clip**, **White
clip**, **Blur radius** and **Grow**, then **Invert**.

**Highlight**, beside the page's name, turns the matte view to Highlight, and off again. A
right-click offers **Off**, **Highlight** and **Key**.

### Windows

A line for each of the four windows: its name, then **Ellipse**, **Rectangle**,
**Polygon** and **Gradient**. A shape's button turns the window on as that shape and picks
it, and pressed again turns it off. A click on a line picks that window, or lets it go, and
the Viewer shows the picked window's handles. A reset on the line puts the window's rows
back.

The picked window's rows show while it is on: **Outer softness**, **Inner softness**,
**Opacity**, **Roundness** on a rectangle, **Invert**, **Subtract**, and **Smooth** on a
polygon.

**Track back**, **Stop** and **Track forwards** track the picked window, with a bar for how
far the track has got. See [Tracking a window](/use/grading/#tracking-a-window).

### Match

- **Balance**: **Auto balance**.
- **Match to**: the shot or still to match to, the Viewer's reference first, then
  **Match**, and **Compare**, which makes it the Viewer's reference.
- **Chart**: **Show chart**, then **Match chart**, and the mean ΔE before and after.

See [Matching](/use/grading/#matching).

### Look

- **Looks**: the look picked for the shot, until its grade changes, else **None**. It lists
  the library's looks, with **Save look…** at the foot.
- **LUT**: **Choose…** picks a `.cube` file for the shot's first LUT node. With a file
  chosen, it shows the file's name, a clear button and the node's **Mix**.
- **Export LUT**: the size, 17, 33 or 65, the pictures it is for, **Video**, **Log** or
  **sRGB**, and **Export…**.
- **CDL**: **Import…** adds a CDL node after the current node, and **Export…** writes the
  current Correction or CDL node.

See [Looks and LUTs](/use/grading/#looks-and-luts) and [CDL](/use/grading/#cdl).

## Other nodes

A node that is not a Correction shows its own rows in place of the pages, under its name,
as [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/) draws them. A reset beside the name puts
them back.

### CDL

**Space**, then **Slope**, **Offset** and **Power**, a number a channel, then
**Saturation** and **Mix**. See [CDL](/effects/colour/cdl/).

### Colour warper

The mesh is drawn inside a rim of hues, over the picture's own colours, plotted faintly as
the vectorscope plots them. The mesh at rest stays in hairline under the moved one.

- Drag a point to move it, a tenth as fast with `Shift`. Each drag is one undo step.
- Double-click a point to put it back.
- Right-click the mesh to put every point back.

**Resolution**, **Keep luma**, **Strength** and **Mix** sit beside the mesh, or under it in
a panel under 640 pixels wide. See [Colour warper](/effects/colour/colour-warper/).
