The Grade panel edits the current node of the current shot. See [Grading](/use/grading/).

<Shot file="grade-panel.png" caption="The Grade panel on the Wheels page, with Adjust beside it." eager/>

## The header

| Control | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Shot** | The current shot's name, how many layers or clips an edit lands on, or **Group pre** or **Group post** while a group's grade is in view. |
| **Nodes** | A chip a node, numbered in stack order and named on hover. A click makes it current, and a right-click opens the node's menu. A bypassed node is dimmed. **+** adds a serial node after the current one. |
| **Version** | The live version's name. A click lists the versions, with **New version**, **Duplicate version**, **Rename…** and **Delete version**, then **Versions in a grid** with more than one. Hidden while a group's grade is in view. |
| **Before** | Held, shows the shot without its grade. |
| **Reset grade** | Puts every node of the grade back to its defaults. A keyed row takes its default at the playhead. |

Right-click the panel for **Reset node**, **Bypass node**, **Reveal in Effect controls** and
**Delete node**, then **Add serial node**, **Add parallel node**, **Add layer node** and
**Add outside node**, then **Reset grade**, **Bypass grade**, **Copy grade**, **Paste
grade** and **Remove grade**.

## The pages

The pages sit under the header, as buttons or, in a narrow panel, a list. A dot marks each
page whose rows the current node has changed. Beside them, a reset puts the page's rows
back. A panel at least 1000 pixels wide shows Adjust beside Wheels, except in HDR, and
Look beside Match.
A panel under 520 pixels wide and more than twice as tall shows every page, each under a
heading that twirls it open.

Every number scrubs, types and goes back to its default on a double-click. `Shift` moves
it finely. A right-click on a row offers its reset, its keys and its expression. A keyed row
shows in the animated colour, and a row an expression drives stands down.

While **Group pre** or **Group post** is in view in the [Grade nodes](/panels/grade-nodes/)
panel, every control edits that group's grade. The first touch adds a Correction at the end
of it. On a [shared node](/use/grading/#shared-nodes), every control edits the shared node.

### Wheels

**Wheels**, **Bars**, **Log** and **HDR** show the wheels four ways, each marked with a dot
while its rows are changed.

- **Wheels**: **Lift**, **Gamma**, **Gain** and **Offset**. Drag inside a wheel to move its
  colour, and its bar to move its master. Double-click a wheel to reset its colour. Under
  it, its name and four numbers: **Y**, the master, then red, green and blue, each with a
  tick in its colour.
- **Bars**: each wheel as four upright bars, master, red, green and blue. A drag is relative,
  and a double-click puts one bar back.
- **Log**: **Shadow**, **Midtone**, **Highlight** and **Offset**, with **Low range** and
  **High range** first in the row of numbers under them.
- **HDR**: the zone strip, then a cell for each zone, **Black**, **Dark**, **Shadow**,
  **Light**, **Highlight** and **Specular**, and **Global**. See [HDR](#hdr).

Under the wheels, except in HDR, a row of numbers: **Temperature**, **Tint**,
**Contrast**, **Pivot**, **Saturation**, **Hue** and **Lum mix**.

#### HDR

The zone strip runs from 8 stops under mid grey to 8 over. Each zone is a line showing how
much of it reaches each stop, with a notch at its range. Drag a notch to move the range,
`Shift` for finer, and double-click it to reset it.

Each zone's cell has a balance wheel with no master, the zone's name, then **Exp**, **Sat**
and **Range**. **Global** has the **Global balance** wheel, **Black offset** and
**Falloff**. The cells sit seven across where each has room, else in rows.

### Adjust

A slider each for **Exposure**, **Temperature**, **Tint**, **Contrast**, **Pivot**,
**Saturation**, **Hue**, **Lum mix**, **Colour boost**, **Shadows**, **Highlights** and
**Midtone detail**. **Pick a neutral**, beside Temperature, arms the dropper: what it picks
on the picture is made neutral, through Temperature and Tint, as one undo step.

**Mixer** is three rows of three: how much red, green and blue make each output channel.
**Keep luma** puts back the luma the mixer moved.

### Curves

**Custom** is the tone curves, **Luma**, **Red**, **Green** and **Blue**, with **High soft
clip** and **Low soft clip**. The other six are the hue curves: **Hue v hue**, **Hue v
sat**, **Hue v lum**, **Lum v sat**, **Sat v sat** and **Sat v lum**.

On a hue curve:

- The flat line across the middle changes nothing.
- A click on the line adds a point. Drag a point to move it, or off the plot to remove it.
- Double-click a point to put it back on the middle line. Right-click for **Delete point** and
  **Reset curve**.
- A curve whose input is hue wraps round, off the right edge and in at the left.
- Under a hue curve, a swatch for each of red, yellow, green, cyan, blue and magenta drops
  three points on that hue.
- **Pick on the picture** arms the dropper, and drops three points where the picked colour
  sits on the curve.

### Qualifier

**Off**, **HSL**, **RGB**, **Luma** or **3D**, then **Pick**, **Add to pick** and
**Subtract from pick**, which arm the picture; see
[The qualifier](/use/grading/#the-qualifier).

Each range is a bar with a box over it. The box's top corners move its edges, the handles
under it set the softness, and a drag inside the box moves the whole range. The hue bar
wraps. HSL's bars each have a tick that leaves that range out. The numbers sit under each
bar.

**3D** shows a **Keep** row and a **Drop** row: how many samples there are, a swatch for
each, and **Clear**, which empties them and forgets the strokes that made them. **Size**
and **Softness** sit under them. See [The 3D qualifier](/use/grading/#the-3d-qualifier).

**Finesse** has **Denoise**, **Clean black**, **Clean white**, **Black clip**, **White
clip**, **Blur radius** and **Grow**, then **Invert**.

**Highlight**, beside the page's name, turns the matte view to Highlight, and off again. A
right-click offers **Off**, **Highlight** and **Key**.

### Windows

A line for each of the four windows: its number, then **Ellipse**, **Rectangle**,
**Polygon** and **Gradient**. A shape's button turns the window on as that shape, and
pressed again turns it off. A click on a line picks that window, and the Viewer shows its
handles.

The picked window's rows: **Outer softness**, **Inner softness**, **Opacity**, **Roundness**
on a rectangle, **Invert**, **Subtract**, and **Smooth** on a polygon.

**Track back**, **Stop** and **Track forwards** track the picked window, with a bar for how
far the track has got. See [Tracking a window](/use/grading/#tracking-a-window).

### Match

- **Auto balance**.
- **Match to**, the shot or still to match to, then **Match**, and **Compare**, which makes
  it the Viewer's reference.
- **Show chart**, then **Match chart**, and the mean ΔE before and after.

See [Matching](/use/grading/#matching).

### Look

- **Looks**: the library's looks, and **Save look…** at the foot of the list.
- **LUT**: a `.cube` file for the shot's first LUT node, with a clear button, and its **Mix**.
- **Export LUT**: the size, 17, 33 or 65, the pictures it is for, **Video**, **Log** or
  **sRGB**, and **Export…**.
- **CDL**: **Import…** adds a CDL node after the current node, and **Export…** writes the
  current Correction or CDL node.

See [Looks and LUTs](/use/grading/#looks-and-luts) and [CDL](/use/grading/#cdl).

## Other nodes

A node that is not a Correction shows its own rows in place of the pages, under its name.

### CDL

**Space**, then **Slope**, **Offset** and **Power**, a number a channel, then
**Saturation** and **Mix**. See [CDL](/effects/colour/cdl/).

### Colour warper

The mesh over a hue rim, the picture's own colours faint under it, the mesh at rest in
hairline and the moved mesh over it.

- Drag a point to move it, `Shift` for finer. Each drag is one undo step.
- Double-click a point to put it back.
- Right-click the mesh to put every point back.

**Resolution**, **Keep luma**, **Strength** and **Mix** sit beside the mesh, or under it in
a panel under 640 pixels wide. See [Colour warper](/effects/colour/colour-warper/).
