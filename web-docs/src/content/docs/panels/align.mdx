The **Align** panel holds three grids. **Window ▸ Panels ▸ Align** opens it.

<Shot file="align.png" caption="The Align panel, with a layer selected." eager/>

| Grid | A dot does |
| --- | --- |
| **Anchor point** | Moves each selected layer's anchor point to that point of its box. The picture stays where it is. |
| **Composition** | Moves each selected layer so that point of its box meets the same point of the composition: top left to top left, centre to centre. |
| **Selection** | Lines the selected layers' boxes up with the box round them all: left, centre, right, top, middle or bottom. Two or more layers. |

The two buttons under **Selection** distribute the layers across and down, three or more of
them. The outermost two stay and the middles between them are spaced evenly. With `Shift`
held the gaps between the boxes are.

The three buttons under **Composition** are **Fit to comp**, **Fit to comp width** and
**Fit to comp height**. See [Transform](/use/transform/#fit-to-comp).

- A click is one undo step for the whole selection.
- Right-click a grid for **Anchor to centre**, **Centre in composition**, the fit and distribute
  rows, and **Align to the first picked layer**, which lines the selection up with that
  layer's edge instead. **Settings** has the same switch.
- A layer whose Anchor point or Position is keyed keeps its anchor point, and a notice says so.
- A keyed Position takes a key at the playhead under **Composition** and **Selection**.
- A Position driven by an expression stays put.
- The grids are greyed with nothing selected, or only cameras, lights and audio layers.
- Nothing moves unless a Viewer showing the composition is open.
