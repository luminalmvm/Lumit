The **Align** panel holds two grids of nine dots, one for each corner, edge middle and the
centre of a layer's box. **Window ▸ Align** opens it.

<Shot file="align.png" caption="The Align panel, with a layer selected." eager/>

| Grid | A dot does |
| --- | --- |
| **Anchor point** | Moves each selected layer's anchor point to that point of its box. The picture stays where it is. |
| **Composition** | Moves each selected layer so that point of its box meets the same point of the composition: top left to top left, centre to centre. |

- A click is one undo step for the whole selection.
- A layer whose Anchor point or Position is keyed keeps its anchor point, and a notice says so.
- A layer whose Position is keyed stays put under **Composition**.
- The grids are greyed with nothing selected, or only cameras, lights and audio layers.
- Nothing moves unless a Viewer showing the composition is open.
