title: A spinning arc from a circle
description: Draw a circle on a shape layer, trim it to an arc, key the trim, and taper its ends.

---

A [shape layer's](/use/shapes/) modifiers change how its shapes are drawn and leave their
paths as they are.

**1. Deselect everything.** Press `Ctrl+Shift+A`. With no layer selected, a shape tool makes
a shape layer.

**2. Pick the Ellipse tool.** Press and hold the shape button on the
[toolbar](/panels/toolbar/) and pick **Ellipse**.

**3. Set the paint.** On the toolbar, click the word **Fill** and choose **None**, then set
**Width** to 12.

**4. Draw the circle.** Hold `Shift` and drag in the Viewer.

**5. Open the shape.** In the Timeline, twirl the new layer open, then **Contents**.

**6. Add a trim.** Click **Add** on the **Ellipse** row and choose **Trim paths**.

**7. Cut the circle to an arc.** Twirl **Trim paths** open and set **Trim end** to 25.

**8. Key the start of the turn.** Move the playhead to the first frame and click the
stopwatch on **Trim offset**.

**9. Key the end.** Move the playhead a second later and set **Trim offset** to 360. The arc
goes once round the circle.

**10. Taper the ends.** Click **Add** on the **Ellipse** row again and choose **Stroke
taper**. The arc narrows to a point at each end.
