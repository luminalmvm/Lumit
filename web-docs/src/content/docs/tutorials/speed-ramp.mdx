A speed ramp slows a stretch of footage and brings it back to normal speed. On a layer it
is made on the layer's [Retime](/use/retime/), in the graph editor. For clips in the Cut
timeline, see [Speed ramps, coming from Vegas](/tutorials/speed-ramps-from-vegas/).

<Shot file="speed-ramp.png" caption="A speed ramp in the graph editor, seen through the Speed lens." eager/>

**1. Select the layer.** Click the Footage layer's row in the Timeline.

**2. Choose the Retiming workspace.** Choose **Window ▸ Workspace ▸ Retiming**. The
Timeline switches to the graph editor on the speed graph.

**3. Give the layer a Retime.** Press **Enable Retime** on the empty graph, or
`Ctrl+Alt+T`. The graph draws a flat line at 100%.

**4. Add four points.** Double-click the line at the frame where the slow-down begins, at
the frame where the slow stretch begins, at the frame where it ends, and at the frame where
normal speed is back.

**5. Set the slow speed.** Right-click the second point, choose **Set speed…** and type
`25` in **Speed**. Do the same on the third. The line now falls to 25%, runs level, and
climbs back to 100%.

**6. Shape the two slopes.** Right-click the line between the first and second points and
choose **Smooth**. Do the same between the third and fourth.

**7. Land on every frame.** With footage shot at a higher frame rate than the composition,
drag a point towards the dashed **Every frame** line. The point snaps to it, at the speed
where each source frame shows once.

**8. Fill the frames in between.** Below that speed source frames repeat. Choose
**Layer ▸ Flow** to have the frames in between made instead.

**9. Play it.** Press `Space`.

## One slope in one command

**1. Put the playhead where the change of speed starts.**

**2. Press `Ctrl+Alt+R`.** The **Ramp…** box opens.

**3. Type the speed to reach in To.**

**4. Type how long the change takes in Over.** The unit beside it switches between frames
and seconds.

**5. Pick the curve in Ease.**

**6. Press Ramp.** With no point after it, the speed stays there.
