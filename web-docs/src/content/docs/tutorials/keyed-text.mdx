A text layer's words can be [keyed](/use/text/#keyed-words) like any other property. A key
either switches the words or fades into the next key's words.

## Switch the words

**1. Add the title.** Press `Ctrl+T` for the Type tool, click in the Viewer, type *hello*
and press `Enter`.

**2. Open the Source section.** With the layer selected, open **Source** in
[Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/).

**3. Key the words.** Move the playhead to 0 s and click the stopwatch on the **Text** row.
A key appears in the row's lane.

**4. Go to the switch.** Move the playhead to 2 s.

**5. Type the new words.** Select the **Text** row, type *goodbye* and press `Enter`. A
second key appears at 2 s.

**6. Play it.** The Viewer shows *hello* until 2 s, then *goodbye*.

## Fade between them

**1. Right-click the first key.** In the lane, right-click the key at 0 s.

**2. Make it linear.** Choose **Linear**. The words now fade from *hello* to *goodbye*
over the two seconds, and *hello* is gone at 2 s.

**3. Ease it.** Choose **Easy ease** instead to start and end the fade slowly.

**4. Switch again.** Choose **Hold** on the key to put it back to a switch.

## Add a third set of words

**1. Go to 4 s.** Move the playhead there and type *see you* in the **Text** row.

**2. Choose each span.** The span from 0 s to 2 s fades and the span from 2 s to 4 s
switches, since only the first key was made linear. Right-click the key at 2 s and choose
**Linear** to fade that span too.
