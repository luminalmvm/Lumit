[Hologram](/effects/stylise/hologram/) records a layer as a hologram on a film. This makes
a round sticker with depth in it and a backdrop behind it, and tips it to show both.

**1. Start with the subject.** Use artwork or footage over black. Black is empty in a
hologram, and the backdrop shows through it.

**2. Cut it round.** Select the layer, pick the **Ellipse** tool and drag a circle over it
with `Shift` held. The [mask](/use/masks/) is the film's outline.

**3. Make a depth layer.** It is white where the subject is near and black where it is far.

- For footage or a photograph, duplicate the layer with `Ctrl+D`, add **Effect ▸ Utility
  ▸ Depth** to the copy and press **Analyse**. Leave **View** on **Depth**. See
  [Depth](/effects/utility/depth/) for the model it needs.
- For artwork, draw one: a soft white shape on a black solid.

Turn off the depth layer's **Visible** switch.

**4. Add the effect.** Select the subject and choose **Effect ▸ Stylise ▸ Hologram**.
The subject replays in green over a grid. The first frame takes a few seconds while the
shader is built.

**5. Choose the Sticker preset.** On the effect's heading menu in
[Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), choose **Presets ▸ Sticker**. The film is now
a rainbow hologram 30 mm wide, made from 16 views, with a seal on it.

**6. Keep the subject's colours.** In **Subject**, set **Subject colour** to **Full
colour**.

**7. Give it depth.** In **Depth**, set **Depth map** to the depth layer and **Depth scale
(mm)** to 6. White now stands 3 mm nearer than **Subject depth** and black 3 mm further.

**8. Put a backdrop behind it.** Open **Backdrop**. Set **Backdrop** to **None** and
**Backdrop layer** to a layer holding a pattern, with its **Visible** switch off. It sits
at **Backdrop depth (mm)**, 24 mm behind the film.

<Shot file="hologram-sticker-steps.webp" caption="The sticker after steps 4, 5, 7 and 8. The last two have Tilt y at 6." eager/>

**9. Tip the film.** At the first frame, turn on the stopwatch beside **Tilt y** and set it
to -6. Move the playhead two seconds on and set **Tilt y** to 6. The subject slides over
the backdrop as it plays. Past the preset's **View zone** the image is cut.

<Shot file="hologram-sticker-tilt.webp" caption="Tilt y at -6, -3, 0, 3 and 6."/>

**10. Shift the colour.** Keyframe **Tilt x** from -2 to 2. A rainbow hologram's colour
follows the tilt up and down, from blue towards red.

**11. Move the light.** In **Lights**, keyframe **Rig rotation** from -10 to 10. The image
dims as the light leaves the direction **Reference x** and **Reference y** name.
