Puppet bends a layer without cutting it apart: a mesh is laid over everything the
layer draws, pins hold or move spots of it, and the picture bends between them.

## 1. Place pins

1. Select the layer — a cutout character on its own layer works best.
2. Pick the **Puppet position pin** tool (`Ctrl+P`). A wireframe appears over the
   layer: the mesh, cut to the picture's opaque shape.
3. Click to drive a pin in. Put one on each part that should hold still — hips,
   shoulders — and one on each part that will move.

A click outside the mesh, or on a layer with nothing opaque, is refused with a line in
the status area. **Density** and **Expansion** on the options bar set how fine the
mesh is and how far past the edge it reaches; the defaults are fine to start.

## 2. Drag

Drag a pin. The wireframe follows the pointer, and the picture takes the deformation
when you release. Everything between the pins bends rather than stretches — an arm
bends at the elbow instead of smearing — and the pins you left alone hold their spots.

One drag is one undo step; `Escape` abandons a drag in flight.

## 3. Keyframe

A layer with pins grows a **Puppet** heading in the Timeline, a row per pin, a row per
number under each pin. Animate a pin like any other property:

1. Turn on the stopwatch on the pin's position.
2. Move the playhead, drag the pin, repeat.

The keyframes take the same interpolation, easing and graph editing as everything
else. Rename a pin inline on its row; delete it from the row's right-click menu.

## 4. Starch, overlap and bend

The other three pin tools share the strip slot with the first:

- **Starch pin**: stiffens the region round it, so a torso stays rigid while the limbs
  bend. **Amount** sets how strongly; the pin row's value column sets how far it
  reaches.
- **Overlap pin**: says which part draws in front when the picture folds over itself —
  a hand crossing the body passes in front rather than tearing.
- **Bend pin**: with the bend tool, dragging around the pin turns the region about it,
  and `Alt`-dragging resizes it — a hand waving from the wrist, without the wrist
  travelling. Both numbers keyframe like the rest.
