The [3D qualifier](/use/grading/#the-3d-qualifier) keys a node on colours taken from strokes
drawn across the picture.

**1. Open the Colour workspace.** Switch to **Colour** on the strip at the right of the
toolbar.

**2. Pick the shot.** Click its tile in the Shots panel, and put the playhead on a frame
where the colour shows.

**3. Add a node.** Press **+** in the Grade panel's header. The new node becomes current.

**4. Key in 3D.** On the Grade panel's **Qualifier** page, press **3D**, then
**Highlight**.

**5. Stroke what to keep.** Press **Pick**, then drag across the colour on the Viewer. The
rest of the picture turns grey.

**6. Add what it missed.** Press **Add to pick** and stroke across the parts of the colour
still grey.

**7. Leave out what bled in.** Press **Subtract from pick** and stroke across the colours
that should stay as they are.

**8. Check the key.** Press `Shift+H` for **Key**.

**9. Set its reach.** Raise **Size** to take in more colour round each sample, and
**Softness** to soften the key's edge.

**10. Put the tool down.** Press the armed button again, then `Shift+H` to see the picture.

**11. Grade the colour.** On the **Wheels** page, move the wheels and the numbers under
them. The change lands only where the key is.
