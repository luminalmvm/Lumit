A shot with a 24-patch ColorChecker Classic in frame can be corrected from the chart's
published colours. Every shot filmed under the same light then takes the same correction.

**1. Open the Colour workspace.** Switch to **Colour** on the strip at the right of the
toolbar.

**2. Pick the chart shot.** Click its tile in the Shots panel, and put the playhead on a
frame where the chart is flat to the camera and lit as the scene is.

**3. Show the chart.** On the Grade panel's **Match** page, tick **Show chart**. A 6 by 4
grid appears on the Viewer.

**4. Fit the grid.** Drag each corner onto the matching corner of the chart, dark skin at
the top left and black at the bottom right. The middle of each patch drawn on the grid sits
inside its patch.

**5. Match.** Press **Match chart**. The page shows the mean ΔE before and after.

**6. Put the chart away.** Untick **Show chart**.

**7. Grade on top.** Press `Alt+S` and grade the look in the new node, after the chart's.

**8. Copy the grade.** Press `Alt+Shift+C`.

**9. Pick the other shots.** `Ctrl`+click each shot lit the same way in the Shots panel.

**10. Paste.** Press `Alt+Shift+V`. Every picked shot takes the grade.
