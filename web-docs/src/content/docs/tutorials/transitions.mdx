A [transition](/use/cutting/#fades-and-dissolves) runs across the overlap of two clips in the
Cut timeline. The same ones can be keyed between two layers.

**1. Open the edit.** Switch to the **Cut** workspace on the strip at the right of the
toolbar, and open the composition.

**2. Put a whip pan on a cut.** Right-click a picture clip near the end that meets the next
clip and choose **Transition ▸ Moves ▸ Move ▸ Whip left**. The two clips overlap by a second,
and the transition's rows open in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), marked
**Transition**. With no spare media at the cut it is refused.

**3. Set its length.** Right-click the overlap, choose **Length…** and type the length in
frames.

**4. Tune the smear.** In Effect controls, lower **Smear** from 60 for a cleaner pan, or
raise it for a longer streak.

**5. Tune the ease.** Set **Ease** to **Smooth** for an even pan. **Sharp**, which the whip
starts on, is slow at both ends and fast in the middle.

**6. Play it.** Put the playhead before the cut and press `Space`.

**7. Put a flash on another cut.** Right-click a clip at the next cut and choose
**Transition ▸ Hits ▸ Flash ▸ Flash**. The flash peaks in the middle of the overlap, where
one clip gives way to the other.

**8. Set how strong it is.** In Effect controls, **Intensity** is the flash at the middle of
the overlap, and **Colour** is what it flashes to.

## Between two layers

**1. Overlap two layers.** In the **Edit** workspace, drag one layer's bar in the Timeline
until it starts before the other ends.

**2. Select both.** Click one layer's row and `Ctrl`+click the other.

**3. Choose Layer ▸ Transition ▸ Moves ▸ Move ▸ Whip left.** A Move is added to each layer
and keyed across the overlap. The upper layer's **Opacity** is keyed across the crossfade.

**4. Tune both layers.** Select one layer and change **Smear** and **Ease** on its Move in
Effect controls. Do the same on the other.

## Save the look

**1. Save the outgoing side.** Select the layer that starts first, right-click the **Move**
heading in Effect controls and choose **Save preset…**. Name it. The bottom strip reads
**Preset saved**.

**2. Save the incoming side.** Do the same on the other layer, under another name. Each
preset holds its layer's **Side** and keys.

**3. Use them on another pair.** Put any Move between the two layers with
**Layer ▸ Transition**, and move the playhead to where the later layer starts. On each
layer's Move heading choose **Presets ▸** and that side's preset. The keys start at the
playhead and keep the length they were saved with.
