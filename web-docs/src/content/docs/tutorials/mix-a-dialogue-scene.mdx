---
title: Mixing a dialogue scene
description: Route the voice through a bus with a reverb on a send, lower the music behind it, ride the music, and export to a loudness with stems.
sidebar:
  order: 10
---

A scene with a dialogue recording and music, mixed through buses in the
[Mixer](/panels/audio/#mixer). See [Mixing](/use/mixing/) for each part.

**1. Place the sound.** Drag the dialogue recording and the music from the
[Project panel](/panels/project/) into the composition. Each becomes an Audio layer.

**2. Open the Audio workspace.** Switch to the **Audio** workspace on the strip at the right
of the toolbar. The Mixer has a strip for each layer.

**3. Make the dialogue bus.** Select the dialogue layer and press `Ctrl`+`Alt`+`Shift`+`B`.
The new bus's strip stands after the layers, and the dialogue strip's output chip names it.
Double-click the bus's name and type `Dialogue`.

**4. Make the reverb bus.** Right-click the Mixer's empty ground and choose **Insert bus**.
Double-click its name and type `Reverb`. Click the empty slot in its inserts and pick
**[Reverb](/effects/audio/reverb/)**. Click the insert, and in Effect controls set **Dry** to
0 and **Wet** to 100.

**5. Send the dialogue to it.** On the **Dialogue** bus's strip, click the empty slot in the
sends and pick **Reverb**. Drag the send's bar to set its level.

**6. Lower the music behind the dialogue.** Select the music layer. In the
[Audio panel](/panels/audio/#audio-panel), press **Lower behind…** and pick **Dialogue**.
Set **Amount** to −15 dB.

**7. Set the music to Touch.** Click the automation mode chip on the music strip and choose
**Touch**.

**8. Ride the music.** Press `Space`. Hold the music fader, bring it up under the opening
and let go before the first line. The fader glides back to where it was. Press `Space` to
stop. The ride is written as keys, one undo step named **Record automation**. Twirl the music
track open in the Audio timeline to see it on the **Volume** lane.

**9. Set the loudness target.** Right-click the **Master** strip and choose **Loudness
target ▸ −16 LUFS**.

**10. Normalise the mix.** Right-click the **Master** strip and choose **Normalise mix…**.
The status line gives the loudness it reached.

**11. Export with stems.** Choose **File ▸ Export…**. In the **Audio** section, **Loudness**
reads **Target, −16 LUFS**. Tick **A file for each bus** beside **Stems**, and press
**Export**. Beside the file are `<file name> - Dialogue` and `<file name> - Reverb`.
