[Holographic foil](/effects/stylise/holographic-foil/) turns a layer into a sheet of foil
that tips under a set of lights. This puts it on a logo, then prints a picture with its
title in foil.

## A foil logo

The logo is a text layer, a shape layer or artwork with an alpha channel.

**1. Add the effect.** Select the logo layer and choose **Effect ▸ Stylise ▸ Holographic
foil**. The logo is cut from rainbow foil. The first frame takes a few seconds while the
shader is built.

**2. Leave the logo's colour out.** In [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), set
**Print** to **Foil only**. The whole logo is foil, whatever colour it was.

**3. Size the sheet.** Open **Sheet** and set **Sheet width (mm)** to 60. The logo is now
60 mm wide in the world, and its bands are broader.

**4. Pick a Material.** Set **Material** to **Shatter**. The foil breaks into gold shards.
Set it back to **Prism**.

**5. Press the logo in.** Open **Surface** and set **Relief** to **Bevel from alpha**. Set
**Bevel width (mm)** to 1.5 and **Bevel height (µm)** to 180. The letters round over at
their edges and catch the lights there.

<Shot file="holographic-foil-logo.webp" caption="The logo after step 3, as Shatter in step 4, and pressed in at step 5." eager/>

**6. Tip the sheet.** At the first frame, turn on the stopwatch beside **Tilt y** and set
it to -40. Move the playhead two seconds on and set **Tilt y** to -10. The colours sweep
across the logo as it plays.

<Shot file="holographic-foil-logo-tilt.webp" caption="Tilt y at -40, -25 and -10."/>

**7. Swing the lights.** In **Lights**, turn on the stopwatch beside **Rig rotation**, move
the playhead and set it to 60. Every light turns about the view axis.

**8. Sweep one light.** Set **Lighting** to **Custom** and open **Spot A**. Keyframe
**Direction x** from -0.4 to 0.4. That spot alone crosses the logo.

**9. Add the glow.** Open **Output** and set **Highlights** to **Off**. Add **Effect ▸
Stylise ▸ Glow** below Holographic foil and set its **Threshold** to 1.5. The glints bloom
and the rest of the foil does not.

## Wire other layers in

Each row in **Maps** reads another layer. A layer with its **Visible** switch off is still
read.

**1. Emboss a texture.** Set **Height map** to a layer that holds a texture, such as a
solid with [Fractal noise](/effects/generate/fractal-noise/). Its brightness becomes the
height of the surface, in place of the bevel.

**2. Fit the texture.** Set **Repeats** to 3 for a finer grain. Where a seam shows between
the repeats, set **Wrap** to **Mirror**. **Strength** in **Surface** sets how deep it
presses.

**3. Steer the grooves.** In **Grating**, set **Pattern** to **From map**. Set **Direction
map** to a layer of line art or text. The grooves run along its outlines, which show as
bands of their own.

**4. Vary the colour.** Set **Pitch map** to a layer and **Pitch at white (nm)** to 1000.
The white parts of that layer take another colour of the rainbow.

## Foil on a printed picture

**1. Add the effect to the picture.** Select a photograph or artwork layer and choose
**Effect ▸ Stylise ▸ Holographic foil**.

**2. Print it.** Set **Print** to **Opaque print**. The picture is printed over the foil,
and foil shows where the picture is bright.

**3. Make the mask.** Add a text layer with the title in white, place it over the picture
and turn off its **Visible** switch.

**4. Mask the foil.** In **Maps**, set **Foil mask** to the text layer. The title is foil
and everything else is print.

<Shot file="holographic-foil-maps.webp" caption="Top: one text layer as the Height map, as the Foil mask of an Opaque print, and as the Direction map. Bottom: a logo as the Direction map, the text layer as the Pitch map, and a photograph as the Environment."/>

## Export

**1. Check the colour depth.** In **File ▸ Project settings…**, a **Colour depth** of 16
or 32 bpc keeps the glints brighter than white for Glow. At 8 bpc nothing is brighter than
white.

**2. Raise the quality.** Set **Quality** in **Output** to **High**.

**3. Export.** Choose **File ▸ Export…**. See [Export](/use/export/).
