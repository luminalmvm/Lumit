The [node graph](/use/nodes/) can make a parameter follow something else in the
composition, and let one effect hand its work to another. Both are a wire.

<Shot file="graph-panel.png" caption="A layer's chain with a Wiggle driver wired into Glow's Threshold." eager/>

Open the graph from **Window ▸ Node graph**, or switch to the **Nodes** workspace. It
follows whichever layer is selected in the Timeline.

## Make a glow pulse with the music

The composition has a music layer and a layer you want to react to it.

**1. Put a Glow on the layer.** Select it and add **Effect ▸ Stylise ▸ Glow**. It appears
in the graph as a box between Source and Layer out.

**2. Add the driver.** Click empty canvas and press `Tab`. Type `audio` and pick
**[Audio level](/effects/drivers/audio-level/)**.

**3. Point it at the sound.** Pick the music layer on Audio level's **Audio** row. The box
hands out two numbers — **Amplitude**, the whole sound, and **Low**, the bottom end alone.

**4. Expose Glow's parameters.** Click the **E** badge in Glow's header and a socket
appears beside every one of its parameters.

**5. Fit the range.** Press `Tab` again and add a **[Remap](/effects/drivers/remap/)**.
Drag from Audio level's **Low** socket to Remap's **Value** socket, then set **In high**
to about `0.4` and **Out low** / **Out high** to the quiet and loud ends of the glow you
want.

**6. Wire it home.** Drag from Remap's output to Glow's **Intensity** socket. Play the
composition back.

The Intensity row in [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/) now names the driver
where its number was. Any keyframes it had are overridden while the wire is there, and
come back when it is pulled.

## Feed one effect's points into another

The points family produces a **stream** — a set of positions — on a teal socket, and
another effect reads it. That is the only kind of wiring between two effects there is:
the picture itself always runs straight down the stack.

**1. Add the producer.** On a layer, add **Effect ▸ Generate ▸ Grid**. It draws a lattice
of soft discs, and hands the same lattice out on a **Points** socket.

**2. Add the consumer, below it.** Add **Effect ▸ Generate ▸ Clone to points**. It must
sit **after** Grid in the stack — a data wire only runs downstream. Its **Points** socket
is drawn whether the box is exposed or not. Until something is wired in, the box says
**No stream** and draws nothing.

**3. Wire them.** Drag from Grid's Points socket to Clone to points' Points socket.

**4. Choose what gets cloned.** Set **Clone layer** on the Clone to points row to the
layer you want stamped at every point.

**5. Take the lattice out of the picture.** Turn Grid's **Mix** down to zero: the stream
is still handed out while nothing is drawn.

[Connect points](/effects/generate/connect-points/),
[Trail](/effects/generate/trail/), [Scatter](/effects/generate/scatter/) and
[Emit from image](/effects/generate/emit-from-image/) feed or read the same socket.
