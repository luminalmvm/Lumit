---
title: Cleaning up a noisy shot and its sound
description: Take the noise out of a picture with Denoise, then the hiss, hum, clicks and room out of its sound.
sidebar:
  order: 9
---

[Denoise](/effects/blur-sharpen/denoise/) takes camera noise out of a shot from a measurement
of it. The sound has an effect for each fault, all on the same layer.

## The picture

**1. Add Denoise.** Select the footage layer and choose **Effect ▸ Blur & sharpen ▸
Denoise**. Denoise reads the frames either side only as the first enabled effect on the
layer. Where the layer has other effects, drag it above them in **Effect controls**.

**2. Learn the noise.** Put the playhead on a frame with a flat, evenly lit area in it.
Select Denoise in Effect controls and press **Learn**, or `Alt+L`. The square on the Viewer
moves to the patch that was measured, and the display above the controls reads **Quality**.

**3. Move the square if the quality is low.** With the **Selection** tool (`V`), drag the
square onto a flat patch with no edges in it, or drag a corner to make it larger. The noise
is measured again when it is let go.

**4. Check what is removed.** Set **View** to **Removed noise**, or press `Alt+N`. Where
outlines of the picture show in the grey, lower **Luma** or raise **Detail**. Press `Alt+N`
again for the result.

**5. Check the frames either side.** Set **View** to **Frames blended**. White is where
every frame either side was averaged in, and black where none was. Set **View** back to
**Result**.

## The sound

**6. Add Noise reduction.** With the layer selected, choose **Effect ▸ Audio ▸ Noise
reduction**.

**7. Mark a stretch of noise.** Find a second or more where nobody speaks. Put the playhead
at its start and press `B`, then at its end and press `N`.

**8. Learn it.** Select Noise reduction in Effect controls and press **Learn**, or `Alt+L`.
**Adaptive** turns off, and the display draws the profile it learned and reads **Learned**.

**9. Listen to what is removed.** Tick **Noise only**, or press `Alt+N`, and play. Where the
voice is heard in it, lower **Threshold** or **Reduction**. Untick it.

**10. Take out the hum.** Add **Effect ▸ Audio ▸ Hum removal**. The display reads the
frequency it found, such as **Hum at 50.0 Hz**.

**11. Take out the clicks.** Add **Effect ▸ Audio ▸ Click removal**. For the clicks of a
mouth, set **Type** to **Mouth clicks**. The display counts the clicks around the playhead.

**12. Soften the S sounds.** Add **Effect ▸ Audio ▸ De-esser**. Tick **Sibilance only** and
play. Move **Frequency** and **Threshold** until only the S sounds are heard, then untick
it.

**13. Install the voice model.** Open **Edit ▸ Settings… ▸ Addons**, press **Check for
addons**, and install the **Model runtime** and **DeepFilterNet3**. See
[Addons](/use/addons/#voice-isolation).

**14. Isolate the voice.** Add **Effect ▸ Audio ▸ Voice isolation**. Raise **Background**
from -60 to leave some of the room in.
