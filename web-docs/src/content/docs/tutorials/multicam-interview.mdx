---
title: Cutting an interview from several cameras
description: Sync the cameras and the recorder by their sound, then cut between them as it plays.
sidebar:

---

A [multicam comp](/use/multicam/) holds every camera and recorder of one shoot in step. In
the [Cut workspace](/use/cutting/) it is cut by picking angles as the shot plays.

**1. Import the footage.** Drag each camera's files and the recorder's sound into the
[Project panel](/panels/project/).

**2. Make the multicam comp.** Select them all, right-click and choose **New multicam
comp…**. Leave **Sync by** on **Sound**.

**3. Give each camera one angle.** Where a camera stopped and started, set **Angles** to
**Each folder**, and the files in one folder on disk share an angle. Press **Make**. The
status line shows the sync and names any file that did not line up.

**4. Open the edit.** Switch to the **Cut** workspace on the strip at the right of the
toolbar, and open the composition the interview is cut into.

**5. Place the multicam comp.** Drag it from the Project panel onto a picture track. Its
picture lands there and its sound on a sound track, linked. The sound plays the recorder.

**6. Show the angles.** Double-click the multicam clip. The angle viewer opens beside the
composition, with the angle on screen outlined.

**7. Cut as it plays.** Press `Space`, then press `1` to `9`, or click an angle, as the
shot goes. Each press cuts at the playhead and shows that angle from there. The sound stays
on the recorder.

**8. Change a cut's angle.** Right-click a piece in the Cut timeline and pick another camera
from **Angle ▸**. With several pieces selected, all of them change.

**9. Move a cut.** Press `N` for the **Roll** tool and drag the cut between two pieces.

**10. Take a cut away.** Right-click a piece beside a dotted cut and choose **Join**.

**11. Fix a camera that is out of step.** Right-click a piece and choose **Open multicam
comp**. Right-click that camera's track and pick the recorder from **Sync to angle ▸**.
Every cut that shows the camera follows.
