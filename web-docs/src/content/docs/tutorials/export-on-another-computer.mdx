A laptop sends an export to a desktop, and the file comes back to the laptop. Both run
Lumit, and the account has [Lumit Pro](/use/profiles/#lumit-pro).

**1. Sign in on both computers.** On each, press **Sign in** in the
[profile card](/use/profiles/#lumit-account) and use the same account.

**2. Let the desktop take exports.** On the desktop, open **Settings ▸ Account ▸ Computers**.
**Other computers can export here** is on by default.

**3. Tell the desktop where its footage is.** Where the desktop has a copy of the footage,
press **Add folder…** beside **Look for footage in** and choose the folder holding it.
Footage found there is not sent.

**4. Leave Lumit running on the desktop.** With **Settings ▸ General ▸ Keep running in the
background** on, its window can be closed.

**5. Open the export dialogue on the laptop.** Front the composition and choose **File ▸
Export on another computer…**, or press `Alt+M`.

**6. Choose the desktop.** On the **Export on** row, choose the desktop by name. **Ready**
beside it says it has every plugin, font and model pack the export uses.

**7. Choose where the file goes.** On the **File goes to** row, choose **This computer**.
Press **Browse…** and choose where to write it.

**8. Press Export.** The export queue opens, with the export under the desktop's name. Its
status goes through **Checking files**, **Sending …**, **In …'s queue**, **Preparing…**, a
bar while the desktop exports, and **Bringing back …**.

**9. Close the laptop.** Once the status reads **In …'s queue**, the laptop can sleep. The
file comes back when it wakes.

**10. Open the file.** The notice **… is here from …** has **Show**, which opens the file's
folder.

To leave the file on the desktop, choose the desktop's name on the **File goes to** row.
**Bring here**, on the export's right-click menu in the queue, fetches it later.
