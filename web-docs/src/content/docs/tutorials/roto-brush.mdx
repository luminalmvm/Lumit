The [Roto brush](/use/roto-brush/) cuts a moving subject out of a shot from a few scribbles.

**1. Select the footage layer.** Click it in the Timeline.

**2. Pick the Roto brush tool.** Press `Alt+W`. **Size** on the toolbar sets the scribble's
width.

**3. Go to a frame where the subject separates well.** It does not have to be the first
frame.

**4. Drag through the subject.** The layer gains a
[Roto brush](/effects/utility/roto-brush/) effect, and the matte appears when you release.
This frame is the **base frame**.

**5. Mark the background.** Where the matte takes in too much, hold `Alt` and drag through
the background. With no background stroke, the frame's border counts as the background.

**6. Look at the edge.** In [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), set the effect's
**View** to **Boundary** to see the edge drawn over the picture, or to **Matte** to see the
matte itself.

**7. Propagate.** Press **Propagate** on the effect's card. The matte is carried outward
from the base frame in both directions, in the background. The line under the buttons counts
the frames, then says how far the matte reaches. **Cancel** keeps the frames already solved.

**8. Correct a frame.** Move the playhead to a frame where the matte is wrong. Drag through
what it lost, and `Alt`-drag through background it took in.

**9. Propagate again.** Only the frames from the correction outward are solved again.

**10. Soften the edge.** Raise **Refine radius** on the card to widen the soft band round
the edge. Where one spot needs more, press `Alt+W` again for the **Refine edge** tool and
drag over it.

## Start from a tap instead

A segmentation model can make the base frame's matte from a click. It is an
[addon](/use/addons/).

**1. Install the model.** In **Settings ▸ Addons**, install the model runtime and a
**Segmentation** model pack.

**2. Add the effect.** Select the footage layer and choose **Effect ▸ Utility ▸ Roto
brush**.

**3. Ask for the model.** Set **Seed from** to **Segment**.

**4. Click the subject.** Use the Roto brush tool, on the frame to start from.

**5. Take out what it got wrong.** `Alt`-click anything the matte took in by mistake.

**6. Finish with strokes.** Drag strokes where the taps are not enough, then press
**Propagate**.
