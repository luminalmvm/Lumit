The [Roto brush](/effects/utility/roto-brush/) cuts a moving subject out of a shot
from a few scribbles, one frame at a time only where the automatic answer goes wrong.

## 1. Stroke the base frame

1. Add a **Roto brush** effect to the footage layer.
2. Pick the **Roto brush** tool (`Alt+W`). **Size** on the options bar sets the
   scribble's width.
3. Scrub to a clear frame — one where the subject separates well. It does not have to
   be the first frame.
4. Drag through the subject. The stroke draws green, and the matte appears as soon as
   you release.
5. Where the matte grabs too much, hold `Alt` and drag through the background — a red
   stroke, claiming "not the subject".

The frame you stroked first becomes the **base frame**; the effect's card shows it,
and **Set to current frame** moves it. If you draw no background stroke, the frame's
border counts as background.

Set **View** on the card to **Boundary** to see the edge drawn over the picture, or
**Matte** to see the matte itself.

## 2. Propagate

Press **Propagate** on the effect's card. The matte is carried outward from the base
frame in both directions, following the shot's motion, as a background job — roughly a
second per 1080p frame, and you can keep working while it runs. The card shows how far
the span reaches; **Cancel** keeps the frames already solved.

## 3. Correct a frame

Scrub the result. Where the matte leaks or drops a limb:

1. Scribble **across** the wrong region — foreground where it lost the subject, `Alt`
   where it grabbed background. A dab beside the leak is not enough; cover it.
2. Propagate again. Only the frames from the correction outward are re-solved; the
   rest are reused, so a fix late in the shot is quick.

Corrections carry forward, away from the base frame. A leak between the subject and a
same-coloured wall may creep back after a few frames — stroke it again where it does.

## 4. Refine the edge

**Refine radius** on the card sets how wide the softened band round the edge is, and
covers most footage. Where one spot needs more — hair, motion blur, smoke — pick the
**Refine edge** tool (with the Roto brush on the strip) and paint over it; the band
widens exactly where you painted.
