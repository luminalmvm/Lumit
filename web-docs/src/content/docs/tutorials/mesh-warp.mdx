[Mesh warp](/effects/distortion/mesh-warp/) lays a grid over a layer and bends the picture
as the grid's points are dragged in the Viewer.

**1. Add the effect.** Select the layer and choose **Effect ▸ Distortion ▸ Mesh warp**.

**2. Set the grid.** In Effect controls, set **Rows** and **Columns**. A change to either
starts a flat grid.

**3. Select the effect.** Click its heading in Effect controls. The grid appears over the
layer in the Viewer.

**4. Drag a point.** With the Selection tool, drag a point where two lines cross. `Shift`
keeps the drag to one axis.

**5. Curve the lines through it.** Click the point to show its four handles and drag the
end of one. The handle opposite mirrors it.

**6. Turn on Animate mesh.** Move the playhead to where the bend starts and tick **Animate
mesh**.

**7. Set each pose.** Drag a point. Every point and handle takes a keyframe at the
playhead. Move the playhead and drag again for the next pose.

**Reset mesh** puts the grid back flat and removes its keyframes.
