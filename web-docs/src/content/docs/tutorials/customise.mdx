Lumit doesn't have to look the way it shipped. Most of this page lives in
**Edit ▸ Settings… ▸ Appearance**, and every change lands the moment you make it.

<Shot file="customise-looks.jpg" caption="One project, four looks: Gruvbox dark, Desk, Lantern in Arcane dark, and Catppuccin Latte." eager/>

## Pick a colour scheme

**Colour scheme** has 33 built in, dark ones first and light ones under them. Point at one
in the list and the whole window tries it on before you pick it.

<Shot file="customise-schemes.png" caption="The colour scheme list. Gruvbox and Catppuccin are both in there."/>

## Change the accent

**Accent** is the colour that marks what's active: the playhead, the Export button, the
tool in hand. Six swatches sit beside it, and pointing at one previews it.

<Shot file="customise-accents.png" caption="The default spruce, then blue, amber and violet."/>

## Make a theme of your own

**Customise…** opens the theme editor on the theme in use. Every colour in the interface
has a row, and the app repaints as you change one.

<Shot file="customise-theme-editor.png" caption="The theme editor, one row per colour."/>

**Save** keeps it under **Your themes**. Saving from a built-in scheme asks for a name and
makes a new theme, and **Save a copy…** writes the edits under a new name. Closing with
unsaved edits asks whether to keep them.

**Your themes** also duplicates, renames, deletes, exports and imports them. A theme
travels as a `.lumtheme` file, so one can be passed to a friend.

The Viewer's surround is not in the editor. It stays neutral.

<Shot file="customise-appearance.png" caption="Settings ▸ Appearance: scheme, theme, accent and style at the top."/>

## Choose a style

**Shape** changes how the interface is built, not only its colours.

- **Studio** is the default: panels meeting flush, with hairlines between them.
- **Desk** is the same flush panels on a tighter grid, with lower-case labels and the
  toolbar down the left.
- **Lantern** stands each panel in a room as a card, with a gap and a shadow round it.
  **Room** sets it to **Day** or **Night**.

<Shot file="customise-desk.jpg" caption="Desk, in Grey room."/>

<Shot file="customise-lantern.jpg" caption="Lantern, in Arcane dark, in the night room."/>

**Label case** overrides how a style cases its labels: **All caps**, **First letter** or
**Lower case**.

## Move the bars

**Toolbar position** puts the tools along the **Top** or down the **Left**. **Viewer bars**
arranges the Viewer's controls: **Split, top and bottom**, **One bar, at the top**, **One
bar, at the bottom**, or **Deck**, which puts everything about playing in a fixed deck
under the picture. Each can be left to the **Style's choice**.

<Shot file="customise-rail-deck.jpg" caption="Studio with the toolbar on the left and the Viewer's deck under the picture."/>

## Words or icons

**Chrome labels** decides what the bars say. **Icons** is the default. **Words** spells
every button out, and **Icons everywhere** turns panel titles into icons too.

<Shot file="customise-labels.png" caption="The Timeline's foot bar with Icons, then with Words."/>

**Icon set** draws the icons **Regular**, **Engraved** or **Bold**.

<Shot file="customise-icon-sets.png" caption="The toolbar in Regular, Engraved and Bold."/>

Fancy drawing your own? **Custom icons** shows a folder. Put an SVG in it named after the
icon it replaces, such as `razor.svg` or `pointer.svg`, and press **Reload icons**. The row
counts how many it replaced.

## Size and spacing

- **Scale** runs from 75 % to 200 %.
- **Compact** tightens the row spacing throughout.
- **Motion** sets how much controls animate: **Full**, **Minimal** or **None**.
- **Tooltips** turns them on or off.

## Wires

**Wires**, under **Panels**, sets how each wire in a [node graph](/use/nodes/) runs:
**Curved**, **Angled** or **Hanging**.

<Shot file="customise-wires.png" caption="The same wire, Curved, Angled and Hanging."/>

## Shortcuts

**Settings ▸ Shortcuts** lists every command. Click a shortcut to rebind it; a clash is
flagged. **Preset** loads a whole keymap: Lumit's own, **After Effects** or **Vegas**, and
**After Effects shortcuts ▸ Import…** reads your own file from After Effects.

<Shot file="customise-keyboard.png" caption="The keyboard on the Shortcuts page. Filled keys work anywhere, outlined ones in one panel."/>

## Arrange the panels

Drag a panel's tab to move it, and **Window ▸ Workspace ▸ Save as new workspace…** keeps
the arrangement. See [Workspaces and panels](/use/workspaces/).

## Take it with you

Themes, keymaps and workspaces all export and import. With
[Lumit Pro](/use/profiles/#sync), a signed-in profile carries all of it to your other
machines.
