A [shared project](/use/projects/#shared-projects) is edited by several people at once. One
person, the **host**, shares it from their computer, and the others join as **guests** with
an **invite link**.

Host and guests run the same version of Lumit. A shared project holds up to 16 people, the
host included.

## Share the project

On the host's computer, with the project open.

**1. Open the window.** Choose **File ▸ Multi-edit…**. It opens on **Share this
project**.

**2. Say who you are.** Type **Your name**. It is the name the others see.

**3. Set a password.** Type one in **Password**, or leave it empty.

**4. Start.** Press **Start sharing**. The window now shows the **Invite link** row and the
**People** list.

**5. Read the line under the link.** It says who the link works for.

| Line | Who can join |
| --- | --- |
| Checking your connection… | Not known yet. |
| Anyone with this link can join. | Anyone. |
| The relay isn't responding. | People on the host's network. |
| Only people on your network can join. | People on the host's network. See [Reach a guest outside the network](#reach-a-guest-outside-the-network). |

**6. Send the link.** Press **Copy link** and send it to each guest. For a
[friend](/use/profiles/#friends), press **Send to a friend** beside the link and pick them.

## Join it

On a guest's computer.

**1. Open the window.** Choose **File ▸ Multi-edit…** and press **Join a project**. A
click on the invite link in a browser does the same, with the link filled in.

**2. Say who you are.** Type **Your name**.

**3. Paste the link.** Paste it into **Invite link**.

**4. Type the password.** A **Password** row appears for a link that needs one.

**5. Point at the footage.** Press **Choose…** beside **Footage folder** and pick the
folder where this computer keeps the project's footage.

**6. Join.** Press **Join**. The host's project opens in place of the open one.

An invite a friend sent waits at the top of **Join a project**, under their name. Press
**Join** on its row in place of steps 3 and 4.

A footage item that is used in a composition and has no file in the folder arrives as a
[stand-in](/use/projects/#footage-someone-has-not-got).

## Work together

**1. Edit.** An edit made by one person appears for everyone. Each person has a colour, and
what they have selected is marked in it.

**2. See who is here.** Press **Shared** on the bottom strip. **People** lists everyone and
the composition each has open.

**3. Follow someone.** Press the eye on their row. The open composition and the playhead
go where theirs go, and the Viewer wears a frame in their colour. `Esc`, **Stop** on the
frame, or moving the playhead stops it.

**4. Save.** The host saves the project file. A guest's copy is a new, unsaved project.

## Reach a guest outside the network

The line under the link reads *Only people on your network can join*.

**1. Stop.** Press **Stop sharing**, then choose **File ▸ Multi-edit…** again.

**2. Choose a relay.** With [Lumit Pro](/use/profiles/#lumit-pro), tick **Lumit relay**.
Otherwise open **Advanced** and type the address of a [relay](/use/projects/#relays) in
**Relay**.

**3. Start again.** Press **Start sharing**.

**4. Send the new link.** Press **Copy link**. The link sent before no longer works.

A host and guest on the same VPN need no relay. While sharing, open **Advanced**, type the
host's VPN address in **Extra address**, and send the link again.

## Finish

**1. Leave.** A guest presses **Leave** in the Multi-edit window.

**2. Stop sharing.** The host presses **Stop sharing**, which ends it for everyone.
