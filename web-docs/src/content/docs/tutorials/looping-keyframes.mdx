An [expression](/use/expressions/) put on a keyframed property keeps the keyframes under
it. `loopOut("cycle")` repeats them for the rest of the layer.

**1. Show the property.** Select the layer in the Timeline and press `R`. The **Rotation**
row opens under it.

**2. Key the move once.** With the playhead on the layer's first frame, turn on the
stopwatch beside **Rotation**. Move the playhead one second on and set **Rotation** to
`1x +0°`.

**3. Open the expression editor.** `Alt`+click the stopwatch. The editor opens over the
row.

**4. Check the language.** The dropdown under the text reads **JavaScript**. A Rhai
expression does not read keyframes.

**5. Type the loop.** Type `loopOut("cycle")`.

**6. Keep it.** Click away, or press `Ctrl+Enter`. The two keyframes stay on the row's
lane, and an **Expression** line appears under the row.

**7. Play it.** Press `Space`. The turn repeats after the second keyframe.

**8. Change the lap.** Drag the second keyframe along the lane. Every lap takes the new
length.

**9. Ease it.** Select a keyframe and press `F9`.

**10. Open the expression again.** Click the text on the **Expression** line.

## Other loops

| Expression | After the last keyframe |
| --- | --- |
| `loopOut("cycle")` | Starts again from the first keyframe. |
| `loopOut("pingpong")` | Runs back and forth. |
| `loopOut("offset")` | Starts again from where the last lap ended. |
| `loopOut("continue")` | Carries on at the speed it had at the last keyframe. |
| `loopOut("cycle", 1)` | Repeats only the stretch between the last two keyframes. |

`loopIn` does the same before the first keyframe.

## Loop a layer's Retime

**1. Give the layer a Retime.** Select a Footage or Precomp layer and press `Ctrl+Alt+T`.
The **Retime** row has a keyframe at the layer's in point and one at its out point.

**2. Put the loop on it.** `Alt`+click the Retime row's stopwatch, type
`loopOut("cycle")` and press `Ctrl+Enter`.

**3. Make the layer longer.** Drag the end of the layer's bar to the right. After the second
keyframe the layer plays again from the first.

## Take the expression off

Right-click the row and choose **Remove expression**. The keyframes are the property's
animation again.
