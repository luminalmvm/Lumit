title: Cutting an interview by its words
description: Transcribe an interview, then cut it by deleting and moving the words in the Transcript panel.

The [Transcript panel](/use/editing-by-transcript/) shows what a composition says as text,
and cuts the composition when the text is edited.

**1. Put the interview on the Cut timeline.** Open the **Cut** workspace and drag the
footage from the [Project panel](/panels/project/) onto the [Cut timeline](/use/cutting/).

**2. Open the Transcript panel.** Click the **Transcript** tab beside the Project panel.

**3. Transcribe.** Press **Transcribe**. The first time, the **Speech model** window opens.
Press **Install**. See [Addons](/use/addons/#captions-whisper).

**4. Play it.** Press `Space`. The word being said is lit and the page follows it. Click a
word to put the playhead there.

**5. Delete a sentence.** Drag across its words, or triple-click its paragraph, and press
`Delete`. The clips close up on every track.

**6. Take out the filler words.** Press `Ctrl+Shift+F`. Tick the kinds to take, and press
**Delete all**.

**7. Shorten the pauses.** Press `Ctrl+Alt+P`. Set **Longer than** and **Shorten to**, and
press **Shorten all**.

**8. Move a line.** Select its words and drag them to where they belong.

**9. Fix a misheard name.** Press `Ctrl+F` and type it. Open **Replace**, type the right
spelling and press **Replace all**.

**10. Put a cut back.** Choose **··· ▸ Show removed words**, select the struck-through
words and press `R`.

**11. Name the speakers.** Double-click a speaker's name and type the right one.

**12. Export the transcript.** Choose **··· ▸ Export transcript…** and pick a format.
