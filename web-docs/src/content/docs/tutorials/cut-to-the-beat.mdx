Lumit places a [beat marker](/use/markers/) on each beat of a composition's music. Cuts snap
to the markers.

**1. Put the music in the composition.** Drag the song from the
[Project panel](/panels/project/) into the Timeline. It becomes an Audio layer.

**2. Open the Audio panel.** Switch to the **Audio** workspace on the strip at the right of
the toolbar. The [Audio panel](/panels/audio/) is in the right-hand column.

**3. Choose what to listen to.** In **Beats**, set **Source** to the song's layer, or leave it
on **This comp** to hear the whole mix.

**4. Set how keen the detection is.** Raise **Sensitivity** for more beats, lower it for
fewer. To fix the tempo instead, type it into **BPM**, or press **Tap** in time with the song.

**5. Place the markers.** Press **Generate beat markers**. The status line says how many were
placed, and the markers appear on the ruler. Press it again after a change to replace them.

<Clip name="beat-markers" caption="Generate beat markers: the markers land on the ruler and the status line counts them."/>

**6. Move the playhead to a beat.** Switch back to the **Edit** workspace. Hold `Shift` and
drag along the Timeline ruler, and the playhead lands on the nearest marker.

**7. Cut the footage there.** Select the footage layer and press `Ctrl+Shift+D` to split it
at the playhead. Or press `C` for the razor and click the footage bar near a marker: with
the **magnet** on, the cut lands on the marker.

In the [Cut workspace](/use/cutting/) the razor snaps to the same markers with **Snap** on,
and `Ctrl+K` cuts at the playhead.
