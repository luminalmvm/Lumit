A text [animator](/use/text/#animators) changes the letters of a text layer one after
another.

## Use a ready-made animation

**1. Add the title.** Press `Ctrl+T` for the Type tool, click in the Viewer, type the words
and press `Enter`.

**2. Go to where the title should start.** Move the playhead there.

**3. Add the animation.** With the text layer selected, choose **Animation ▸ Animate text ▸
Fade in by letter**. The letters fade in one after another over the next second.

**4. Show its keyframes.** Click in the Timeline and press `U`. The animator's **Range
offset** row appears, with a keyframe at the playhead and another a second later.

**5. Change how long it takes.** Drag the second keyframe along its lane.

**6. Turn it into an exit.** In [Effect controls](/panels/effect-controls/), open
**Animators** and press **Reverse** on the animator's heading. The letters fade out over the
same keyframes.

## Build one from a blank animator

**1. Add a blank animator.** Select the text layer and choose **Animation ▸ Animate text ▸
Blank animator**. It appears under **Animators** in Effect controls, with a range and no
properties yet.

**2. Say what the letters do.** Choose **Add ▸ Property** on the animator and add **Opacity** and
**Position**. Set **Opacity** to 0 and the second **Position** number to 60. Every letter is
hidden, and 60 pixels lower.

**3. Give the range a soft edge.** Set **Shape** to **Ramp** and **Range end** to 25.

**4. Key the start.** Set **Range offset** to −25 and click its stopwatch. The range sits
before the first letter, and every letter is still hidden.

**5. Key the end.** Move the playhead a second later and set **Range offset** to 100. The
range passes over the words, and each letter rises into place as it goes by.
