---
title: Captioning a talking clip
description: Turn the speech in a clip into styled captions, fix them, and export them.
sidebar:
  order: 7
---

Lumit hears the speech in a layer and makes a [caption layer](/use/captions/) from it.

**1. Put the clip in the composition.** Drag it from the [Project panel](/panels/project/)
into the Timeline, and select its layer.

**2. Open Create captions.** Choose **Layer ▸ Audio ▸ Create captions…**.

**3. Install the speech model.** The first time, the **Speech model** window opens. Press
**Install**. See [Addons](/use/addons/#captions-whisper).

**4. Choose how the captions are cut.** Leave **Language** and **Speakers** on **Detect**.
Pick a **Style**, and set **Lines** and **Characters per line**.

**5. Make them.** Press **Create captions**. The status line shows the transcription, and
**Cancel** stops it. The caption layer goes at the top of the stack, and the **Captions**
panel opens on it.

**6. Fix a word.** Double-click a caption in the Captions panel, retype it and press
`Enter`.

**7. Retime a caption.** Twirl the caption layer open in the Timeline and drag the end of a
block.

**8. Split a long caption.** Put the playhead where it should break, click in the Captions
panel and press `Ctrl+Shift+D`.

**9. Pick a word out.** Right-click a word in a caption and choose **Emphasise**.

**10. Move a caption off a title.** Right-click the caption and choose **Raise to top**.

**11. Change the look.** Press **Style** in the Captions panel and pick another.

**12. Export.** Open the [export](/use/export/) dialog and set **Captions** to **Burn in**,
**SRT file**, **WebVTT file** or **In the video file**.
