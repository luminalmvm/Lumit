A scene is graded one shot at a time in the [Colour workspace](/use/grading/). The first
shot sets the look, and the others are brought to it.

**1. Open the Colour workspace.** Switch to **Colour** on the strip at the right of the
toolbar. The Shots panel lists the composition's shots in time order.

**2. Pick the first shot.** Click its tile in the Shots panel.

**3. Balance it.** On the Grade panel's **Match** page, press **Auto balance**. A
Correction is added as node **1**, with the cast taken out and the black and white points
set.

**4. Set the look.** On the **Wheels** page, drag inside a wheel to move its colour and its
bar to move its master. Set **Contrast** and **Saturation** in the row under the wheels.

**5. Add a node for the skin.** Press `Alt+S`. Node **2** is added and becomes current.

**6. Pick the skin.** On the **Qualifier** page, press **Pick**, then drag across a face on
the Viewer. Release, and press **Pick** again to put the tool down.

**7. Clean the key.** Press `Shift+H` twice for **Key**. Under **Finesse**, raise **Clean
black** and **Blur radius** until the face is solid and its edge soft. Press `Shift+H`
again to see the picture.

**8. Hold it to the face.** On the **Windows** page, press **Ellipse** on the first line.
On the Viewer, drag its centre onto the face, a corner to size it, and the outer ring to
soften it.

**9. Track the window.** Go to the shot's first frame and press **Track forwards**. The
window follows the face to the end of the shot.

**10. Grade the skin.** On the **Wheels** page, move **Gamma** and **Gain** for the face.

**11. Grab a still.** Press `Ctrl+Alt+G`. The frame and its grade go into the Gallery.

**12. Go to the next shot.** Press `↓`.

**13. Bring the grade over.** Press `=`. The first shot's nodes take the place of this
shot's grade.

**14. Compare with the still.** Double-click the still in the Gallery. Drag the divider
across the picture.

**15. Match the balance.** Click chip **1** in the Grade panel's header. On the **Match**
page, choose the still in **Match to** and press **Match**.

**16. Track the window again.** Click chip **2**, click the first line on the **Windows**
page, go to the shot's first frame and press **Track forwards**.

**17. Do the rest of the scene.** For each shot, press `↓`, then repeat steps 13, 15 and 16.

**18. Put the reference away.** Press `Ctrl+Alt+C`.
