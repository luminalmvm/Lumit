[Planar track](/effects/utility/planar-track/) follows one flat surface through a shot and
writes its movement onto another layer as a [Corner pin](/effects/distortion/corner-pin/).

The composition has the shot on one Footage layer and the picture to put on the surface on
a layer above it.

**1. Add the effect.** Select the shot's layer and choose **Effect ▸ Utility ▸ Planar
track**. Leave **Follow** on **Surface**.

**2. Go to the footage's first frame.** The corners are placed there, and every later frame
is measured against it.

**3. Place the four corners.** Press the dropper, **Pick**, on the **Upper left** row, then
press on that corner of the surface in the Viewer and release. Do the same for **Upper
right**, **Lower left** and **Lower right**. The Viewer marks each corner while the effect
is selected, and the Selection tool drags a mark.

**4. Mask what crosses the surface.** Draw a [mask](/use/masks/) on the shot's layer round
anything that passes in front. With **Use masks** on, the layer's masks are left out of the
track.

**5. Analyse.** Press **Analyse**. The status counts the frames, then reads *Tracked over*
and the number of frames. A bar above it shows how much of the footage was followed.

**6. Name the layer to pin.** Set **Pin layer** to the picture's layer.

**7. Create the pin.** Press **Create corner pin**. The picture's layer gains a Corner pin
with a keyframe on every frame.

A status of *Tracked 180 of 400 frames* means the surface could not be followed past that
frame. *What is inside the shape is not one flat surface* is a refusal, and nothing was
tracked.
